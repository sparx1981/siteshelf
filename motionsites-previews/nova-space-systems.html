RocketScience

How do we get to space?

It takes speed, not just height. A vehicle must reach roughly 28,000 km/h to stay in orbit, so every kilogram of structure, propellant and payload is a negotiation. Our staged designs shed weight on the way up and deliver payloads exactly where they need to be.

Launch vehicles

From light-lift small-sat carriers to heavy vehicles that send crews beyond low orbit, the NOVA fleet is built around reusable first stages, proven engines and one standard payload interface.
