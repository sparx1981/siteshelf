Build a single-page "Culture Values Grid" About page for an invented worker-owned print shop, "Parcel Hill Press": six principles shown as bright painted tiles; hovering, focusing or tapping a tile shows its story, a painted scene and a quote from a team member in a story panel, and clicking pins the story. Everything is one HTML file.

Canvas & base: Friendly cooperative look. Page #fbf5ea, ink #1c2230 (15.6:1), secondary #4f5668 (7.2:1), panels #ffffff with a 1.5px #1c2230 border at 18px radius and a hard 5px offset shadow in #1c2230 (a printed-sticker feel). Six tile colours, each with ink text above 7:1: coral #ff8a73, mustard #ffcb52, mint #5fdcb4, sky #7bb8ff, lilac #b9a4ff, rose #ff9fc2. Invented shop, people and neighbourhood only.

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the page title (clamp 36 to 72px) and tile titles (24px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for body and UI at 15 to 17px; numerals on tiles in Young Serif at 56px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase.

Imagery & art direction: Each tile is painted in its own colour with a large numeral, a hand-drawn line icon (a broom, a speech bubble, a compass, a seedling, a hand-and-heart, a printing press) and soft paper grain with an offset halftone dot pattern in a darker tint. The story panel shows a painted 16:9 scene for the active value (interior of the print shop for "Everyone sweeps", a market street for "Ask the person who does the job", a sunrise field for "Plant what you cannot harvest", a food table for "Feed the room", an abstract ink wash for "Quiet craft", a harbour for "Leave the door open"), a small painted avatar of the person quoted, and a role line. A banner above the grid shows a painted row of inked paper sheets fanned out like proofs in the six colours. Media slots for real photography: each story scene (1600x900) and the six avatars (400x400), with alt text.

Layout: Header: eyebrow "Parcel Hill Press · How we work", title "Six rules we actually follow", intro line. Below, at 960px and up, two columns: left a 3 by 2 grid of tiles (square, 12px gap); right a sticky story panel (scene, title, story paragraph, quote with avatar, a "Pinned" tag when pinned and an Unpin button). Under 960px the grid is 2 by 3 and the panel sits below it. A filter row above the grid has four chips (All, People, Craft, Place) that dim tiles outside the filter without removing them. A closing band reads "Want to work like this? We are hiring a press operator" with a button.

Copy: Principles (title, one-line rule): 1 Everyone sweeps (Nobody is too senior for the broom); 2 Ask the person who does the job (The best answer sits closest to the work); 3 Plant what you cannot harvest (Do work whose payoff is for someone else); 4 Feed the room (Meetings run on food and fairness); 5 Quiet craft (We would rather be good than loud); 6 Leave the door open (Visitors are future colleagues). Write six original 55-to-70-word stories with specific details (a flooded basement, a mistaken print run, a rota on the fridge) and six one-sentence quotes, each from an invented team member with a role. Messages: "Story pinned: Everyone sweeps." "Story unpinned." "Showing People principles."

Interaction: The grid is a group of six buttons with roving tabindex; Arrow keys move focus in two dimensions (Left/Right within a row, Up/Down between rows, Home and End); focusing or hovering a tile (pointer devices only) updates the panel immediately; clicking, tapping or pressing Enter or Space pins the story (aria-pressed true) so that hover and focus movement no longer replace it until Unpin or until another tile is clicked. The panel is an aria-live polite region that announces only on pin and unpin, not on every hover. Filter chips use aria-pressed, dim non-matching tiles to 35 percent opacity and keep them clickable. A small stagger animation (60ms per tile) fades tiles in on load and the active tile lifts 6px (none under reduced motion). The pinned tile and filter persist in localStorage inside try/catch.

Technique notes: Distinguish hover from touch with matchMedia('(hover:hover)') so tapping on touch devices pins directly. Panel content swaps with a 150ms crossfade by toggling an opacity class between two innerHTML writes; skip under reduced motion. Painted scenes come from a function per value so they are rendered once on first request and cached.

Responsive: 2 by 3 grid under 960px with the panel below; under 420px tiles drop their icon size but keep numerals and titles, with text wrapping to three lines; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1c2230 outline with a white 2px gap. Never rely on colour alone: every tile has a numeral and title and the active tile has a thick border and "Pinned" tag. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
