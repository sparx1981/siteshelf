Opening
14 March · A letter from the workshop

Dear friends of Wren & Lodge,

Eleven years ago we sold our first chair out of a rented garage, to a neighbour who insisted on paying twice what we asked. We took the money, bought a better planer, and promised ourselves that we would never again price a piece below what it truly cost to make well. That promise still sits on a scrap of oak above the door.

This letter is our attempt to explain who we are, because more of you have been asking, and because we think a furniture company should be able to say plainly what it believes.

We would rather make one table that lasts sixty years than sixty tables that last one.

Everything we do follows from that sentence. It is why we use joinery instead of glue where we can, why we sell replacement parts, and why a chair you buy today can be repaired by someone who has never met us.

Milestones

  1. 2014First chair sold from a garage workshop in Dunmore.
  2. 2016Moved into the old tannery with four makers and one very loud bandsaw.
  3. 2019Launched the Repair Programme: free parts for the life of every piece.
  4. 2022Planted our ten-thousandth tree through the Replant Pledge.
  5. 2025Became employee-owned. Everyone who builds a table owns a piece of the company.

What we hold to

    Thank you for sitting with us, for the repairs you've sent in, and for the photographs of chairs at your family tables. We hope to keep earning them.

    Mara Wren & Ellis Lodge, founders
