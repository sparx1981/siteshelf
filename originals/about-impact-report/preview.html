    The Alder Street clean-up

    Two hundred neighbours filled 1,100 sacks in a morning. By lunchtime the bank was visible for the first time in a decade.

    Reeds return to Marl Bend

    Planted as plugs three springs ago, the reed bed now shelters moorhens and slows the winter floods. Volunteers keep the count in a notebook.

    Eleven schools join the river club

    Pupils test water, sketch the bank and write letters to the council. Two classes have already changed a bus route.
