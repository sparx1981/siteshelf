Build a single-page "Impact Report Page" About page for an invented river charity, "Tidewater Trust": headline numbers that count up, charts for litter removed, funding and volunteers, a year switcher (2024, 2025, 2026), and a progress-to-goal tracker with an on-pace marker. Everything is one HTML file with invented data.

Canvas & base: Fresh river palette. Page #f2f7f4, panels #ffffff with a 1px #d3e2da border and 18px radius, ink #12302b (14.6:1), secondary #4a6660 (6.2:1), accent teal #0f7a6e (white text 5.4:1), river blue #2a6fb0 (white 5.1:1), sand #e8c872 (ink text 9.6:1), coral #c8452f for behind-pace states (white 4.9:1). Invented charity and data only.

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans" 800 for the title (clamp 34 to 68px), KPI numerals (clamp 40 to 64px, tabular) and card headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for body and chart labels at 13 to 16px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, teal.

Imagery & art direction: A painted river-valley hero (inline SVG from the scene library's landscape generator in its forest palette at dawn, with a winding river painted in blue and silver, reed silhouettes along the bank, soft mist and a vignette) sits behind the title with a gradient so text stays legible. KPI cards carry small painted icons (a bin bag, a riverbank, a volunteer figure, a school) in flat teal and sand. Three story cards each show a painted scene (a riverbank clean-up at dawn, reeds returning at a bend, a school group by the water) from the landscape, sea and city generators. Charts are drawn in SVG with rounded bars, a soft area under the line and a donut with small gaps. Media slots for real photography: hero (2000x800) and three story images (1200x800), with alt text.

Layout: Hero with eyebrow "Tidewater Trust · Annual impact report", title "A cleaner river, one stretch at a time", a year switcher (segmented control 2024 / 2025 / 2026) and a "Show data as tables" switch. Below: a KPI row of four cards (Litter removed in tonnes, Riverbank restored in km, Volunteers, Schools reached), each with a change versus the previous year. Then a two-column chart row: left "Litter removed by month" bar chart (12 bars), right "Where the money came from" donut with legend (Individuals, Local councils, Businesses, Grants); a full-width "Volunteers by quarter" area/line chart; then "Progress to our 2028 goals", four goal rows each with label, value text, a progress bar, a marker for expected pace (two of three years elapsed in 2026) and a status chip (Ahead of pace, On pace, Behind pace); then three story cards and a transparency note.

"Showing data as tables." "Showing charts." Stories in two sentences each.

Interaction: The year switcher (radiogroup) re-renders KPIs, charts and the highest-month note; KPI numbers count up from the previous value over 900ms (immediate under reduced motion) and bars grow from the baseline over 600ms with 25ms stagger. Each bar and donut segment is focusable (tabindex 0) with an aria-label ("June: 52 tonnes") and shows a tooltip on hover or focus; the line chart has focusable points. Arrow keys move between bars. The "Show data as tables" switch (role switch) swaps every chart for a real table with caption and totals, and announces it. Goal bars animate to their values when they scroll into view once (IntersectionObserver), with the on-pace marker at 66.7 percent; status chips compare progress with the marker (more than 5 points ahead, within 5, or behind). Counters and charts must render correctly without JavaScript animation (final values are always in the DOM text). The chosen year and view persist in localStorage inside try/catch.

Technique notes: Count-up uses requestAnimationFrame with easeOutCubic over 900ms, formatting with toLocaleString('en-GB'); donut arcs are stroke-dasharray segments on one circle (r 70, circumference 439.82) rotated -90 degrees with 3-unit gaps; the line chart path is built with a Catmull-Rom to Bezier conversion for a smooth curve.

Responsive: KPI row 2 by 2 under 900px and single column under 420px; charts stack; bar labels show only every second month under 560px; tables scroll inside their own box; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #12302b outline with white offset. Never rely on colour alone: every chart value is available as text (labels, tooltip, table) and every status chip is worded. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
