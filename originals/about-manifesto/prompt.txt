Build a single-page "Studio Manifesto" About page for an invented furniture and design studio, "Hollow Oak": ten poster-scale manifesto lines that light up one at a time as you scroll, over a fixed painted landscape that moves from night to gold and a painted oak that grows more leaves with every line. Everything is one HTML file.

Canvas & base: Dark and warm. Page #0e1418, cream text #f7f1e6 (16.4:1), dimmed lines #6f767b (3.7:1 on the page, allowed because they are large display type and the full text stays readable), amber highlight #ffb454 (11.2:1), leaf green #6fbf73, deep overlay gradient from rgba(14,20,24,.9) at the left to rgba(14,20,24,.15) at the right so painting never reduces legibility. Invented studio and names only.

Typography: "Big Shoulders Display" 800, uppercase, tight 0.92 leading, clamp 44 to 128px for the manifesto lines; "Work Sans" 400/500/600 for the intro, labels and rail at 14 to 17px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, amber.

Imagery & art direction: Four stacked painted skies fixed behind the content (inline SVG from the scene library's landscape generator, each with its own palette and sun position): night with stars, dawn with a low pink sun, a clear high-sun day (alpine palette) and a gold late-afternoon, cross-fading by scroll progress. In front of them a large painted oak stands at the right: a thick textured trunk, forking branches and five foliage clusters made of overlapping leaf blobs in three greens with light speckle; the clusters appear one after another (scale and opacity) as lines progress, so the tree is bare at line 1 and full at line 10. Soft ground fog and a field silhouette sit at the bottom; fine grain and vignette finish it. Media slots for real photography: optionally a 2400x1400 landscape for the sky layers and a studio workshop photo (1600x1000) in the closing section.

Layout: An intro screen with eyebrow "Hollow Oak Studio · Manifesto", a small headline "Ten things we believe" and a "Scroll" cue. Then ten manifesto sections, each at least 60vh tall with the line left-aligned in a column about 62 percent wide on desktop (full width on mobile); each line contains one key phrase set in amber with an animated underline bar. A fixed rail on the left edge lists ten small tick buttons with the current number ("03 / 10") and clicking a tick scrolls to that line. A closing section "Build it so someone can mend it" with a short paragraph, a button "Visit the workshop" and a small signature row of three painted avatars from the portrait generator (the studio's makers).

Copy: Lines (key phrase in brackets): 1 "We make things that outlast the argument for making them." [outlast]; 2 "Slow is a speed." [Slow]; 3 "We would rather be asked why it took so long than why it broke." [why it took so long]; 4 "Every drawing has a person at the other end of it." [a person]; 5 "Waste is a design flaw, not a by-product." [design flaw]; 6 "We say no to the brief that needs a louder logo." [say no]; 7 "Good materials tell the truth about time." [truth about time]; 8 "We share the plans. Copy us, then improve us." [Copy us]; 9 "Pay people properly, finish properly, apologise properly." [properly]; 10 "Build it so someone can mend it in 2090." [mend it]. Messages: "Line 3 of 10."

Interaction: On scroll (throttled with requestAnimationFrame) compute the active line as the section whose centre is nearest the viewport centre. Active line: full cream, key phrase amber with its underline bar growing to 100 percent over 500ms; lines already passed: cream at 55 percent opacity; upcoming lines: dimmed colour. Sky cross-fade: progress p from 0 to 1 over the whole manifesto maps to opacities of the four skies (triangular blend around 0, 1/3, 2/3, 1). Tree clusters: cluster k is visible when active index is at least 2k (clusters 0 to 4 at lines 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9), scaling from 0.6 to 1 with a 600ms ease. The rail ticks reflect state (current filled, passed outlined); a polite live region announces "Line N of 10" when the active line changes while the user is using the rail or keyboard (not on every scroll). Pressing the keys j and k or Page Down and Page Up moves a line at a time, scrolling the next section's centre into view. Reduced motion: no cross-fade easing (skies switch by threshold), no growth animation (clusters appear instantly), no smooth scrolling.

Technique notes: Sections have scroll-snap-align: center with scroll-snap-type: y proximity on the document so lines settle in the middle without trapping scrolling. The tree is drawn once as SVG with each cluster in its own group; visibility toggles a class that sets transform: scale and opacity with transform-origin at the cluster centre. Skies are separate absolutely positioned SVGs with will-change: opacity.

Responsive: Under 820px the tree shrinks and sits bottom right behind the text with the overlay darker, lines use clamp(40px,12vw,72px), the rail becomes a thin bottom bar of ten small dots with the counter, and the intro and closing keep generous padding; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #f7f1e6 outline with dark offset. All ten lines are real text in reading order inside a main landmark; the painting is decorative (aria-hidden). Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
