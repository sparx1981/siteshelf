Canvas & base: Cream (#fbf7f0) page with a deep green (#12372a) hero band and a terracotta (#d1603d) accent. Cards white, 14px radius.

Typography: Young Serif for headings, Figtree for body.

Layout: Hero with mission line and three stat figures; a 4-up perks grid (CSS shape icons); "Open roles" board with search, department chips (All, Engineering, Design, Operations, Support), a Remote-only switch, and a list of role rows (title, team, location, type) that expand to a description, requirements and an Apply button.

Interaction: Chips, switch and search combine; the count updates and is announced; rows are disclosure buttons (aria-expanded) with only one open at a time; Apply shows an inline confirmation panel. An empty state offers "Clear filters" and a general application link.

Responsive: Perks grid goes 2 then 1 column; role meta wraps under the title on mobile.

Rules: Single HTML file, invented company "Tallow & Pine", prefers-reduced-motion, no external images.
