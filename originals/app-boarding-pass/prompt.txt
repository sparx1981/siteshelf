Canvas & base: A deep navy travel-wallet page #0c1730 with a soft radial lift (#24428a at 30% top-left) and a faint blurred night-sky photograph behind everything at 14% opacity so the page feels like looking out of a cabin window at dusk. Two pass cards, 360px wide, white #ffffff, 24px radius, a layered soft shadow (0 30px 60px -24px rgba(0,0,0,.7)), separated into a main section and a stub by a dashed perforation line with two semicircular notches that match the page colour. Ink #14213d (15:1), secondary #56627a (6.3:1). Status chips: On time #0b5c3d on #dff5ea, Boarding #7a5000 on #fff1cc, Gate changed #a02c1f on #fde6e2. Invented airlines "Kestrel Air" (blue #1f4fd1, white text) and "Ember Airways" (amber #ffb020, #2a1a00 text), invented airports Pennick Bay (PNB), Lantern City (LNT) and Saltmarsh Island (SMI).

Typography: "Outfit" 500/600/700 for city codes (56px, tight tracking), times and headings; "IBM Plex Mono" 400/500 for flight numbers, seat, gate and the sequence line (14 to 16px); "Inter" 400/500 for body copy and captions. Small-caps labels 11.5px with 0.08em tracking in secondary ink.

Imagery & art direction: Each pass header carries a wide destination photograph, 360 by 150px, graded like golden-hour travel photography: Kestrel Air shows a dusk coastline above Lantern City (layered violet ridges fading into peach mist, a low sun just above the horizon, long soft shadows, fine film grain at 18%, gentle vignette); Ember Airways shows a pale morning seascape over Saltmarsh Island (flat water, low sun glitter, a thin horizon haze). Overlay a navy-to-transparent gradient (rgba(12,23,48,.72) to 0) from the bottom so the white airline name and flight number stay above 4.5:1. The back of the pass carries a simple vector terminal map. Build the photographs as inline SVG scenes with gradients, ridges, mist bands, a sun disc with a radial halo and an SVG feTurbulence grain layer; no external files. Media slots for real photography: header image 720x300 (2x), focal point lower-third, with a dark gradient overlay; page backdrop 1920x1080 blurred 24px.

Layout: Left column (desktop): eyebrow "Wayfarer Wallet", headline "Everything for the gate.", a one-sentence lead, a control row (Boost brightness switch, Flip pass, Next pass, Replay updates), a live status strip with a chip and countdown, and an alert area for gate-change banners. Right column: the wallet, a carousel of two passes with two dots beneath. Pass front, top to bottom: photographic header band with airline name and "KA 218 · Economy Plus", route row (PNB, plane glyph, LNT with city names), a 3 by 2 detail grid (Passenger MARLOW / WREN, Date 14 Oct, Departs 10:25, Boarding 09:40, Gate B12 in monospace, Seat 14A), the perforation, and the code block with a decorative PDF417-style barcode built from nine rows of seeded narrow and wide bars, the sequence line "Seq 047 · Group 3" and the caption "Decorative code, not scannable". Pass back: "Flight details" heading, definition list (Aircraft Dash 8 Q400, Terminal 1, Baggage belt, Lounge, Meal "Complimentary tea and shortbread"), a terminal map SVG with gates B10, B12, B14 and a blue "you are here" dot, and a "Share this pass with someone" link.

Copy: Live feed lines in order: "Gate assigned B12", "Gate changed to B14" (banner: "Gate changed from B12 to B14"), "Boarding opens for Group 3", "Final call for flight KA 218". Countdown reads "Boarding in 24 min 10 s", then "Boarding now · gate closes in 9 min". Second pass: Ember Airways EM 604, Business, LNT to SMI, 17 Oct, boarding 15:10, departs 15:55, gate A04, seat 3C, lounge "Harbour Lounge".

Interaction: A simulated feed advances every 7 seconds; the gate value flips like a split-flap (rotateX from -90deg to 0, 500ms), the chip changes colour and text, and a polite live region announces each event. The countdown ticks every second from a fixed target and is recalculated at "Boarding opens". Pointer swipe left or right on the card (60px threshold) or ArrowLeft/ArrowRight with the card focused switches passes with a 300ms slide of a double-width track. Flip rotates the focused pass 180 degrees over 600ms (cubic-bezier(.3,.9,.3,1)) with perspective 1400px, the hidden face inert; Enter or Space on the card flips it. The brightness switch (aria-pressed, remembered in localStorage inside try/catch) turns the page near-black, adds a white 3px ring and glow around the card and renders the barcode in pure black for maximum scanner contrast. Replay restarts the feed.

Responsive: Passes scale to min(360px, 92vw); under 900px the columns stack and centre; controls wrap; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff with navy offset. The barcode is decorative and says so. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (cross-fade instead of flip, no split-flap, no slide). Invented names, flights and passenger details only.
