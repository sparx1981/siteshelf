Canvas & base: Warm kitchen page #fbf3e4 with a cream recipe panel (#fffaf0, 1px #eadbbd border, 28px radius) sized like a tablet on desktop (max 900px) and full-viewport on phones. Ink #2a1f14 (15:1), secondary #6b5a45 (5.9:1). Accent tomato #d6432b (white on it 4.7:1), herb green #2f7d4f for done states, timer amber #f0a020 with dark text. Big step card background #ffffff with a thick 6px left bar in tomato. Invented cooking app "Cookline".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for step titles and numerals (step text size is user-set from 24px to 44px, default 30px, line-height 1.35); "Nunito" 400/600/800 for UI, ingredient list and timers (16 to 18px).

Layout: Header with the recipe name "Lantern Cove Lentil Soup", servings stepper, total time and a progress bar "Step 3 of 8". Main area: the current step in a large card showing the step number badge, a short step title (e.g. "Soften the onions"), the instruction text where any duration phrase ("cook for 8 minutes", "simmer 25 minutes") is turned into an inline timer chip button with a clock icon, plus a small illustration (generated SVG: pan, pot, knife, bowl) per step. Under it: Back and Next big buttons (min 56px high), step dots, and an active timers dock (cards with label, big mm:ss, pause and cancel, a progress ring). A slide-over ingredients drawer opened with an "Ingredients" button lists 9 invented-quantity ingredients as checkable rows (checkbox, quantity scales with servings), plus equipment. A toolbar offers text size A- and A+, a "Keep screen awake" switch and a "Read steps aloud" is NOT included (no audio).

Interaction: Next and Back (buttons, ArrowRight/ArrowLeft keys, swipe left or right on the card with a 60px threshold and pointer events) slide steps with a 250ms transform transition and move focus to the step heading with a polite announcement "Step 4 of 8: Add the lentils". Tapping a timer chip starts a countdown (duration parsed from the text) with a card in the dock; multiple timers run concurrently using a single requestAnimationFrame or interval clock based on timestamps (so background throttling does not drift); at zero the card flashes amber, the page title changes to "Timer done", a banner announces "Onions timer finished" via aria-live assertive and a visual pulse repeats until dismissed (no sound). Timers survive step changes. Pause, resume and cancel are buttons; adding 1 minute is a small +1 button. Servings change scales ingredient quantities (fractions formatted nicely) and the timers are not scaled. The Keep awake switch uses navigator.wakeLock inside try/catch and explains if unsupported. Ingredient checkboxes persist per session in memory. Text size changes persist in localStorage (try/catch).

Responsive: Under 700px the ingredients drawer is a bottom sheet, the dock becomes a horizontal scroll row, Back and Next are full width stacked, and step text defaults to 26px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #2a1f14 with white offset; tap targets at least 48px. Do not rely on colour or sound: finished timers show text "Done" and a banner. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no slide, no flashing pulse; static highlighted card instead). Invented names only.
