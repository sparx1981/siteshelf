Canvas & base: A reader window filling the viewport (max 960px by 700px centred on desktop, full-bleed on phones). Three themes: Paper (page #fbf8f1, ink #231f19, chrome #efe9da), Sepia (page #f1e4c8, ink #3b2a14, chrome #e4d3ae) and Night (page #14161b, ink #d9dce4 at 12:1, chrome #1d2027). Accent coral #d6532f on Paper and Sepia, #ff9d7a on Night. Bookmarks show a ribbon in the accent. Invented novella "The Lantern Keeper's Atlas" by "Isolde Marchetti" (original text written for the demo).

Typography: Reading text switchable between "Lora" (serif, default) and "Inter" (sans) at 16px to 28px (default 20px, line-height 1.7, justified with hyphenation); chapter headings in "Lora" 700; UI in "Inter" 500/600 (14 to 15px).

Layout: A top bar (back arrow, book title, chapter name, buttons for Contents, Bookmark, Aa settings), the page area showing one page on narrow screens and two facing pages on wide screens (above 900px), with comfortable margins and a gutter shadow, and a bottom bar: progress slider with page "Page 12 of 48" and percentage plus time left ("About 14 min left"). The Aa popover holds font size A- and A+ with a numeric readout, theme swatches (Paper, Sepia, Night) and font choice (Serif, Sans) and a line-spacing chip group. The Contents drawer lists 6 chapters with page numbers and a Bookmarks tab listing saved pages with a snippet. Text is about 4000 words of invented prose in 6 short chapters.

Interaction: Pagination uses CSS multi-column layout (column width equal to the page width, column gap equals the gutter) on a horizontally scrollable container advanced by pages with scrollLeft and a 220ms transform or smooth scroll; page count is recomputed when font size, typeface, theme padding or viewport size changes, keeping the reader at the same text position (store a character offset from the first visible text node). Page turns: ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, PageDown, PageUp, Space and Shift+Space, clicks on the right 30% and left 30% of the page, swipe with pointer events (60px threshold), and the slider. Bookmark toggles the current page (aria-pressed, ribbon corner appears); bookmarks and the last position persist in localStorage (try/catch) keyed to the book. Chapter links jump to the first page of the chapter and move focus to the heading. Time left estimates words remaining at 230 words per minute. A polite live region announces "Page 13 of 48" at most once per second. Settings popover traps focus and closes on Escape.

Responsive: Under 900px single page; under 560px the bottom bar condenses (slider only with page text above); touch targets 44px; no horizontal scroll of the document at 390px (only the internal column strip scrolls, hidden scrollbars).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px accent ring with offset. Text content is real selectable DOM text. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant page jumps). Invented book, author and text only.
