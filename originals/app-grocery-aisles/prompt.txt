Canvas & base: A warm market-morning page #fbf6ea with a phone-width list sheet (max 440px on desktop, full-width on phones, #ffffff, 28px radius, 1px #eadfc6 border, soft shadow) beside a left column of editorial copy. Ink #26301f (14:1), secondary #667060 (5.4:1). Brand leaf green #2f7a45 (white text 5.3:1), tomato #d8452a for the add button accent and the swipe-reveal label, butter #f5d77a for highlights. Aisle header tints: Produce #e3f2d6, Bakery #fbe5c4, Dairy #e4eef9, Pantry #f1e4d3, Frozen #dff1f4, Household #ece6f4. Invented list app "Aislewise".

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the headline, aisle names and the basket figure (aisle names 22px); "Nunito" 400/600/700/800 for items, quantities, chips and buttons (17px items, 13px meta). Tabular numerals for counts and prices.

Imagery & art direction: Every aisle header shows a small circular 56px "photograph" and a wide 440 by 110px banner strip behind the title, painted like soft top-down food photography on a warm linen surface with natural window light from the left: Produce (a scatter of tomatoes, limes, leafy greens and a lemon in a wooden crate, deep saturated reds and greens), Bakery (golden loaves, a seeded roll and flour dust on a worn board), Dairy (milk bottles, a butter dish and eggs in blue-tinted light), Pantry (jars, pasta shapes and a sprig of rosemary), Frozen (frosted peas, ice crystals, cool cyan highlights), Household (folded linen, a candle and a green plant). Each has a soft shadow, gentle depth of field (back items softened), 12% film grain and a 20% cream overlay at the top so text stays readable. Build them as inline SVG compositions (circles, ellipses, gradients, blurred shadows, feTurbulence grain); no external files. Media slots for real photography: aisle banners 880x220 (2x), top-down, left-lit, warm white balance, and 112x112 circular crops.

Layout: Left column (desktop): eyebrow "Aislewise", headline "Shop in the order you walk.", a short paragraph, a store switcher chip group (Fernhill Market, Harbour Co-op) that reorders aisles to each store's route, and keyboard hints. Right: the list sheet. Top bar: list name "Saturday shop", basket counter "7 of 18 in the basket" with a thin progress bar, a Shop mode toggle (hides ticked items, aria-pressed) and a Share button. Then six aisle sections, each with a photographic banner header (aisle name, item count, collapse chevron) and item rows: 44px tick circle, item name, quantity chip ("2 kg", "×6"), optional note in secondary text, and a drag handle. At the bottom a sticky add bar: text input "Add an item", Add button, and a scroll-free row of quick suggestion chips ("Oat milk", "Bananas", "Sourdough", "Free-range eggs", "Basil", "Washing-up liquid") that auto-file into the correct aisle; typing also shows an inline aisle guess ("Looks like Dairy").

Copy: Seed items such as Heirloom tomatoes 500 g, Baby spinach, Limes ×4, Sourdough loaf, Seeded rolls ×6, Whole milk 2 L, Salted butter, Free-range eggs ×12, Spaghetti 500 g, Passata, Frozen peas, Washing-up liquid. Empty state "Basket empty. Time to find your first item."; completion "Everything in the basket. Enjoy the walk home."

Interaction: Tap or press Space on the tick circle to toggle (circle fills green with a 180ms check draw, row text strikes through and fades to 55%, the row moves to the bottom of its aisle with a 220ms FLIP). Swipe a row right with a pointer or finger (translate follows the drag; past 90px and released it ticks; the row reveals a green "In basket" backplate), swipe left past 90px to reveal a red "Remove" backplate and delete with an Undo toast. Keyboard: arrow keys move between rows, Space toggles, Delete removes, Alt+ArrowUp/Down reorders within the aisle, and "/" focuses the add input. Shop mode filters ticked items with a height collapse. Switching store reorders the aisle sections with a 300ms FLIP. Adding an item parses quantity from text ("2 kg rice" becomes name Rice, quantity 2 kg) and guesses the aisle from a keyword dictionary. The counter and progress update live; a polite announcement says "8 of 18 in the basket". All state persists in localStorage in try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the editorial column sits above the sheet; the sheet is full-bleed under 520px with the add bar fixed to the bottom safe area; banners crop to 90px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #26301f with white offset. Swipe is never the only way: every row has tick and remove buttons reachable by keyboard. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no FLIP, draw or collapse animation). Invented names only.
