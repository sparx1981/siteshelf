Canvas & base: A full-bleed twilight scene fills the page behind a calm two-panel interface: deep indigo sky #0f1426 grading to #26356b at the horizon, a still lake mirroring the sky and a low band of pine-covered ridges in four overlapping blue-violet layers with atmospheric mist between them. Over this sit panels #171d36 at 92% (1px #2a3358, 24px radius, backdrop blur 10px). Text #eaeefc (14:1), secondary #a0abd4 (7.3:1). Breath colours: inhale cyan #7be0ff, hold violet #b79cff, exhale peach #ffc3a0, shared with the circle, the progress ring and the phase word. Primary buttons cyan #7be0ff with dark text #04202a (11:1). Invented app "Stillwater".

Typography: "Cormorant Garamond" 500/600 for the phase word (40px), the in-circle counter (64px) and the session timer (56px, tabular numerals); "Outfit" 400/500/600 for headings, labels, settings and the log (15 to 17px). Eyebrow 13px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, cyan.

Imagery & art direction: The backdrop should feel like a long-exposure photograph taken at blue hour from the shore: silent water with a faint horizontal sheen, ridges softened by mist, a thin crescent of light along the horizon where the sky turns peach, a handful of stars in the upper third, fine film grain at 15% and a soft vignette. The glowing breath circle is the focal object: three concentric rings (core radial gradient from near-white to the phase colour to deep indigo, a 14%-outset ring at 45% opacity, a 30%-outset ring at 25%) casting a coloured bloom onto the water below it, so the lake appears to breathe light. Build the scene as inline SVG (gradients, ridge paths, mist bands, star dots, a feTurbulence grain layer, a mirrored reflection group with reduced opacity and a slight wave displacement); no external files. Media slots for real photography: full-bleed 1920x1080 lake-at-dusk photograph with a 45% indigo overlay, plus an optional 8-second looping 1080p clip of still water.

Layout: Header (eyebrow "Stillwater", headline "Breathe for a few minutes.", lead "Follow the circle. In when it grows, out when it shrinks."). Two columns above 900px. Left, the stage panel: a 380px square holding the breathing circle (300px at full size) inside a thin 3px progress ring, the phase word beneath (Ready, Inhale, Hold, Exhale, Session complete), the large session time, a summary line ("Calm pattern · 5 minutes"), and Start and End buttons. Right, the settings panel: Duration chips (5, 10, 20 minutes, Custom with a minutes input 1 to 60), Breathing pattern cards (Calm 5-5, Box 4-4-4-4, Relax 4-7-8, each with a tiny waveform diagram), Interval bell (None, Every 2 minutes, Every 5 minutes), switches for Soft chime sound, Gentle vibration and Screen reader cues, then the Session log (date, minutes, pattern, Completed or Ended early) with Clear.

Copy: Pre-roll text "Get comfortable" then a 3-2-1 countdown; completion text "Session complete. Well done."; log seed rows "11 Oct · 10 min · Calm · Completed", "10 Oct · 5 min · Box · Completed", "8 Oct · 20 min · Relax · Ended early (12 min)".

Interaction: A timestamp-driven requestAnimationFrame loop scales the circle 0.55 to 1.0 on inhale and back on exhale with ease-in-out, holds at the current size on hold phases, crossfades colour per phase over 600 to 800ms and counts down the seconds left in each phase inside the circle. Session time and the progress ring update every frame. Start begins after the 3-2-1; the same button pauses and resumes; End needs a second press within 3 seconds (it becomes "Press again to end") and logs "Ended early" with elapsed time. Interval bells flash a white ring over 1.2 seconds and optionally play a 528 Hz sine chime generated with Web Audio (gain ramp .18 to .0001 over 1.2s, inside try/catch) and vibrate 120ms if enabled. Settings are in a fieldset that is disabled while a session runs. Space toggles Start or Pause when no control has focus. Phase words are announced politely only when Screen reader cues is on; the stage is role="timer" with an aria-label that reports time remaining. Sessions persist in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack and the circle scales to min(300px, 76vw); the backdrop crops to the lake; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images or audio files (the chime is generated). [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Colour is never the only phase cue (word and counts always shown). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (the circle holds at 80% with no scaling, a ring fills linearly per phase, no bloom animation, starfield static). Invented names only.
