Canvas & base: Soft evening-lilac page #efeaf7 with a white journal card (#ffffff, 1px #ddd5ee border, 28px radius, soft shadow). Ink #261f3a (15:1), secondary #6a6285 (5.7:1). Five mood colours used for tiles and faces: Radiant #ffcf4a, Good #8fd18a, Okay #8fc7f0, Low #b9a2e8, Rough #f08aa0 (dark ink #261f3a text on all, 7:1 or better); empty days #f3f0fa with a dashed #cfc6e6 border. Accent plum #6b3fb8 (white on it 6.9:1). Invented app "Dayglow".

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for headings and the big date (clamp 30px to 44px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI and entry text (16px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Dayglow", headline "How did today feel?" and a line. Two columns. Left: today's entry card with the long date, a mood picker of five large round buttons each drawn as an inline SVG face with its label under it (Radiant, Good, Okay, Low, Rough), a textarea "A line or two about today" with a live counter (max 280), a tag chip group (Sleep, Work, Family, Friends, Exercise, Weather, Food, Quiet), and a Save entry button with a "Saved" confirmation. Right: the month mosaic: a month title with previous and next buttons and a legend, a 7-column calendar grid (Monday first) of day tiles coloured by mood (the day number small in a corner, a tiny dot when an entry text exists), today outlined, future days dimmed; below, three stat tiles (Current streak, Average mood with a mini face, Most common tag) and a mood distribution bar of five segments.

Interaction: Choosing a mood selects it (radiogroup with arrow keys, aria-checked) and previews it on today's tile; Save writes the entry to localStorage in try/catch (date key YYYY-MM-DD) and updates tiles and stats with a gentle pop on the tile. Clicking or pressing Enter on a day tile (a real button, aria-label "9 October, Good, 2 tags") opens that day's entry in the left card for viewing or editing (past days editable, future disabled); a "Back to today" link returns. Month navigation changes the grid; the previous month is pre-seeded with demo entries so the mosaic looks alive. Streak counts consecutive days ending today (or yesterday) with entries; average uses 1 to 5 values. Delete entry button with confirm via a second press. A polite live region announces saves and navigation.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack (entry first); tiles stay at least 40px; stats become a scroll-free 1-column list under 480px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #261f3a ring with white offset. Mood is never colour-only: faces, names and aria-labels always accompany it. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pop). Invented names only.
