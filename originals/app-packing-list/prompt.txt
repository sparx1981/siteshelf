Canvas & base: A bright, airy travel-planner page #f6f3ec with a white app sheet (#ffffff, 1px #e6dfd0 border, 28px radius, soft shadow 0 40px 80px -40px rgba(40,30,10,.5)) laid over a tall destination photograph that fills the top 340px of the viewport and fades into the page colour with a bottom gradient. Ink #1f2a2e (15:1), secondary #5a666b (5.9:1). Accent ocean teal #0f7f86 (white text on it 5.1:1), sun amber #f2a62b for weather chips, success green #2f8a5b for packed items. Category tints: Clothes #fde8d6, Toiletries #e0f1ee, Tech #e4e8fb, Documents #f4e6f2, Extras #eeeacb. Invented app "Packwise".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for the destination name, headings and the big progress figure (destination 48px, tight tracking); "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for labels, list items and buttons (16px list text, 13px meta); numerals tabular.

Imagery & art direction: Each destination has a hero photograph painted to feel like a travel magazine opener. Pennick Alps: a crisp alpine morning, snow ridges in four layers with cool blue shadows, thin cloud bands, a low pale sun, long fir silhouettes in the foreground. Saltmarsh Islands: a bright coastal afternoon, turquoise-to-teal water with sun glitter, a low flat headland and drifting cumulus. Lantern City: a blue-hour skyline with lit windows, mist between building layers and a soft violet-to-peach sky. All have fine film grain (15 to 20%), a gentle vignette, and a warm highlight; the active photograph cross-fades over 600ms when the destination changes. Build the scenes as inline SVG (gradient skies, layered ridges or buildings, sun halo, mist bands, feTurbulence grain); no external files. Media slots for real photography: destination hero 1600x800 with the horizon on the upper third, plus three 480x320 weather-condition thumbnails.

Layout: Header strip over the photograph: eyebrow "Packwise", destination name as a select styled like a title ("Pennick Alps"), dates "14 to 18 Oct · 4 nights", a weather summary row of five day chips (Mon 6° snow, Tue 4° cloud, Wed 9° sun, Thu 7° rain, Fri 5° wind) each with a small drawn icon. The app sheet below: left side the progress block (ring with "17 of 42 packed" and a Reset link), a Nights stepper (1 to 14) and a Travelling-as chips group (Solo, Couple, Family). Right side the checklist grouped by category cards: Clothes, Toiletries, Tech, Documents, Extras; each category has a header with a mini progress bar and count, collapse chevron, and rows with a 28px checkbox, item name, quantity (scales with nights for clothing like socks and tops), and a delete cross on hover. Above the lists a "Suggested for this weather" panel with chips such as "Waterproof jacket", "Thermal base layer", "Sunglasses", "Umbrella" that add themselves to the right category. A final row "Add your own item" with a text field, category select and Add button.

Copy: Sample items: Passport, Boarding passes, Travel insurance card, Phone charger, Power bank, Adapter (type G), Wool socks, Thermal tops, Waterproof jacket, Walking boots, Toothbrush, Sun cream SPF50, Lip balm. Progress copy: "Nearly there: 3 things left" at 90%; "Packed. Have a wonderful trip." at 100%.

Interaction: Changing destination swaps the photograph, the forecast chips and the suggested items (alpine suggests thermal layers and gloves; coast suggests swimwear and sun cream; city suggests umbrella and a day bag). Nights changes quantities live (socks = nights + 1, tops = nights, underwear = nights + 1) with a small number roll. Checking an item strikes it through with a 200ms line draw, moves it to the end of its category and updates the ring (stroke-dashoffset 400ms) and the category bar. Suggestion chips animate into the list (translate and fade 220ms). Items can be reordered with Alt+ArrowUp/ArrowDown; Delete removes with an Undo toast. Collapsing a category uses aria-expanded. All state persists in localStorage in try/catch. The ring and counts announce politely (throttled).

Responsive: Under 900px the progress block sits above one column of lists; the hero crops to 260px; forecast chips scroll within their own row; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1f2a2e with white offset. Checked state is shown by tick icon and strike-through, not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no cross-fade, roll or slide). Invented names only.
