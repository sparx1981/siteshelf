Canvas & base: A dark, warm listening-room page #14110f with a blurred, enlarged copy of the current episode's cover art as a colour wash behind everything (60px blur, 35% opacity, crossfading between episodes over 700ms) and a soft vignette. Player glass #1d1916 at 82% with 16px backdrop blur, 1px rgba(255,255,255,.12) borders, 28px radius. Text #f6efe6 (14.5:1), secondary #b3a99a (7:1). Accent amber #ffb454 (dark text #2a1700 on it 10:1) for the play button, scrubber fill and active chapter; sage #8fd1a3 for "played" badges. Invented podcast app "Slowcast" with invented shows.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for episode titles and show names (title 30px, 1.15 leading); "Manrope" 400/500/700/800 for controls, queue rows and times (14 to 16px), tabular numerals for time.

Imagery & art direction: Every episode has square cover art (320px) painted like contemporary podcast artwork with an editorial photographic feel: (1) "The Long Tide" a moody dawn shoreline with a lone lighthouse and mist; (2) "Kitchen Table Maths" warm top-down table scene with paper, a pencil and a mug in soft window light; (3) "Quiet Engines" a cool-toned close-up of a vintage motorbike silhouette against a pale sky; (4) "Marginalia" an abstract collage of torn paper in coral, indigo and cream; (5) "Field Notes from Pennick Bay" layered sage and slate hills with a small boat. Each cover has depth, grain at 15%, a gentle highlight sweep, the show name set in the artwork and a thin inner border. The large player cover has a soft reflective shadow beneath it and a slow 40-second parallax drift of the blurred background. Build covers and background as inline SVG (gradients, layered shapes, feTurbulence grain); no external files or audio. Media slots for real artwork: 1400x1400 cover per episode and a 1920x1080 blurred backdrop derived from it.

Layout: Left (desktop): the Now Playing card: 300px cover, show name, episode title, a chapter list chip row, a scrubber (elapsed and remaining times, chapter ticks on the bar, a draggable thumb, a buffered bar), transport controls (previous episode, back 15 s, a 72px amber play or pause button, forward 30 s, next episode), a Speed button cycling 0.8x, 1x, 1.2x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2x with a menu, a Sleep timer button (Off, 15 min, 30 min, 45 min, End of episode) and a volume slider. Right: "Up next" queue, a vertical list of six episodes (36px cover thumbnails, title, show, duration, a drag handle, a three-dot menu with Play next, Move to top, Remove) and a footer "Add an episode" with three suggestions. A top strip shows the sleep timer countdown chip when active.

Copy: Episodes: "The lighthouse that nobody wanted" 42:10 (The Long Tide), "Why fractions feel hard" 28:35 (Kitchen Table Maths), "A carburettor is a conversation" 51:02 (Quiet Engines), "Notes in the margins" 33:48 (Marginalia), "The tide tables of 1952" 24:17 (Field Notes from Pennick Bay), "Slow mornings" 18:40 (The Long Tide). Chapters for the first: Cold open 0:00, The keeper's letter 6:20, The vote 19:45, After the light 34:10.

Interaction: Playback is simulated with a timestamp clock (no audio): Play advances elapsed time at the chosen speed, the scrubber and times update every 250ms, chapters highlight and announce, and when an episode ends the next queue item loads and plays. The scrubber is a role="slider" (aria-valuetext "12 minutes 5 seconds of 42 minutes 10 seconds") with Arrow keys +/-5 s, Shift +/-30 s, PageUp/Down +/-60 s, Home/End; pointer drag seeks with live preview of the time under the thumb. Back 15 and Forward 30 buttons jump; the previous button restarts the episode if more than 3 seconds in, otherwise goes to the prior item. Space toggles play when focus is not on a text control; J and L skip back and forward, N next, ArrowUp/Down on the volume. Queue reordering: drag a row by its handle with pointer events (the row lifts with a shadow and the others slide with a 180ms FLIP), and keyboard users focus the row and press Alt+ArrowUp or Alt+ArrowDown (announced as "Moved to position 2 of 6"). Row menus use the disclosure pattern. Sleep timer counts down and when it reaches zero pauses playback with a gentle 3-second volume-fade animation and a toast; End of episode pauses at the end. Queue and position persist in localStorage in try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the queue sits below the player; cover scales to min(300px, 70vw); transport buttons keep 48px minimum; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images or audio files. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Every drag action has a keyboard equivalent. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no backdrop drift, no FLIP, no fade; instant changes). Invented shows, episodes and names only.
