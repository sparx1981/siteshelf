Canvas & base: A phone-sized app (390 by 780px frame on desktop, full viewport on phones) over a full-bleed city map. The map base is a warm light-grey #e9e6df with parks #cfe3c4, water #bcd9ea, main roads white with #d6d1c4 casing, minor roads #f4f1ea, buildings as slightly darker blocks #ddd8cc with a 1px lighter top edge, the route drawn in indigo #3a3fd8 (5px with white casing) with a pulsing pickup dot and a drop-off pin. A white bottom sheet (#ffffff, 28px top radius, shadow 0 -20px 50px -20px rgba(0,0,0,.4)) holds the booking controls. Ink #14161f (16:1), secondary #5c6275 (6.2:1), accent indigo #3a3fd8 (white on it 7.6:1), success green #0f8a5f, surge amber #b45f06 on #fff1d6. Invented ride service "Hopper Rides" in the fictional Lantern City.

Typography: "Sora" 600/700/800 for headings, fares and the vehicle names (fares 20px tabular); "Inter" 400/500/600 for addresses, meta and buttons (15 to 16px).

Imagery & art direction: The map should read like a polished aerial city plan photographed at golden hour: soft long shadows cast to the east from every block (offset darker rectangles at 10% opacity), tree-dotted parks with dark green stipples, a river with a lighter shoreline highlight and faint ripples, a ring road, a bridge, and a subtle paper grain at 10%. Slight tilt-shift blur at the top and bottom edges of the viewport. Vehicle thumbnails are three-quarter illustrated cars with soft ground shadows and glossy highlights (Mini in tangerine, Comfort in graphite, Group in pearl white with a roof rack). The driver card after matching shows a warm, studio-lit portrait avatar. Build map, cars and portrait as inline SVG (paths, gradients, blurred shadow ellipses, feTurbulence grain); no external files. Media slots for real photography: map backdrop 1170x2532 or a styled map embed, vehicle images 480x300 on transparent backgrounds, driver portrait 256x256.

Layout: Top overlay: a round back button and a "Where to?" title chip. The map fills the screen with the route between pickup (Pennick Quay, 14 Harbour Row) and drop-off (Lantern Central Station). The bottom sheet, from top: a drag handle, two stacked address fields with a connector line (a filled circle for pickup, a square for drop-off) and a swap button; a suggestions list that appears while a field is focused (recent: Home, Work, Kestrel Point Park, Lantern Library, Airport Terminal 1); the trip summary line "4.8 km · 14 min"; a vehicle list with three rows (Mini, 1 to 3 seats, 3 min away, £8.40; Comfort, 1 to 4 seats, 5 min, £11.20; Group, 1 to 6 seats, 7 min, £15.90) each with a car illustration, name, seats icon, ETA and fare, and a selected state (2px indigo border, tinted background); a payment row (card ending 4821, promo "WELCOME5" applied), a note chip and a primary "Request Comfort" button (56px). After requesting: the button turns into a matching state with an animated radar ring and "Finding your driver…", then a driver card (portrait, name Mira Okonkwo, rating 4.9, vehicle "Graphite Comfort · LN24 KRT", ETA 4 min, buttons Call and Message) and the map shows the car icon moving toward the pickup.

Copy: Surge chip "Busier than usual: +10%" can be toggled by a "Rush hour" demo switch which raises fares and ETAs. Cancel link "Cancel request" with a confirm step; completion banner "Mira is arriving in 4 min".

Interaction: Focusing an address field opens the suggestions list; arrow keys move through suggestions, Enter selects, Escape closes; choosing a place recomputes distance and time (deterministic pseudo-distance from the two addresses) and redraws the route with a 500ms stroke-dashoffset animation. Swap exchanges the fields with a 250ms rotate. Selecting a vehicle updates the button label and fare with a number roll; vehicles are a radiogroup (arrow keys). Fares = base + per km + per minute with a Rush hour multiplier. The bottom sheet can be dragged between collapsed (handle and button only) and expanded with pointer events and snaps with a 300ms spring; Space or Enter on the handle toggles it. Request starts a 3-second search (radar rings, announced politely), then reveals the driver card and animates the car marker along the route to the pickup in 6 seconds with requestAnimationFrame; Cancel returns to booking.

Responsive: On phones the frame is the viewport and the sheet respects safe-area insets; the desktop shows the phone frame centred on a soft backdrop; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images or map tiles (the map is generated SVG). [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #14161f with white offset. Selected vehicle shown with border, tick and aria-checked, not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pulse, radar, route draw or car movement; states change instantly). Invented names, plates and addresses only.
