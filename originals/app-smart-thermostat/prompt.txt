Canvas & base: A calm smart-home screen in a tablet-like panel (max 1000px by 680px, 32px radius, deep shadow) over a full-bleed painted living room. The room lighting is tied to the target temperature: cool 16 to 19 degrees gives a blue-grey morning (wall #cdd8e2, shadows #8fa0b4, a pale daylight beam), comfortable 20 to 22 gives a neutral white afternoon, and warm 23 to 28 gives a golden evening glow (wall #f0d6b0, a warm lamp halo, orange window light), interpolated smoothly over 800ms. Interface glass: #0f1318 at 72% with 16px backdrop blur, 1px rgba(255,255,255,.14) borders. Text #f4f6fa (15:1), secondary #aab3c2 (7.8:1). Mode colours: Heat ember #ff8a4c, Cool ice #6fd0ff, Eco leaf #7fe0a0, Off slate #8b94a5. Invented smart-home brand "Hearthly".

Typography: "Outfit" 300/500/600 for the huge target temperature (112px, weight 300, tabular) and headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for labels, schedule and readouts (14 to 16px). Degree symbol set at 40% size, raised.

Imagery & art direction: The backdrop is a photographic living room painted in layers: a plastered wall with soft light falloff, a tall window with a grid of panes and a landscape glimpse (distant hills and sky) whose colour follows the temperature setting, a low sofa in sage green with a draped throw, a side table with a lit lamp whose halo grows as the temperature rises, a monstera plant with large glossy leaves, a rug with a subtle woven pattern, wooden floorboards catching a long diagonal light shaft. Add depth with a shallow-focus blur on the nearest objects, a 14% film grain and a vignette. When Cool mode is active a faint frosty breath of mist drifts near the window; in Heat mode a gentle warm shimmer rises from a radiator under the window. Build the room as inline SVG (gradients, rounded shapes, blurred shadow ellipses, feTurbulence grain, a mix-blend-mode screen light shaft); no external files. Media slots for real photography: living-room hero 1920x1080 in three lighting variants (cool dawn, neutral noon, warm dusk) to cross-fade, plus a 600x600 plant detail.

Layout: Left: a round dial 360px across centred in a glass card: an outer track arc of 270 degrees (from 7:30 to 4:30 o'clock) with tick marks every degree and larger ticks every 5, a coloured value arc from the minimum (16) to the target, a draggable knob, the target temperature in the centre with the mode name beneath, "Now 19.4°" and "Humidity 46%" lines, and small minus and plus buttons flanking the readout. Right: stacked glass cards: Mode selector (Heat, Cool, Eco, Off as a four-button radiogroup with icons), Eco panel (leaf toggle, "Away until" time chips, projected saving "Save about £0.62 today"), Weekly schedule (7 day rows by 4 blocks: Morning 06:30, Day 09:00, Evening 17:30, Night 22:00; each block shows its set temperature as a small pill and colour tint; click or Enter to edit with a mini stepper popover), and an Energy tile (today's estimated kWh and cost, mini bar sparkline for the last 7 days).

Copy: Status line under the dial: "Heating to 21.5° · 38 min", "Holding 21.5°", "Cooling to 22°", "Eco 17° while you're out", "System off". Schedule hint "Click a block to change its temperature."

Interaction: Pointer drag on the knob or anywhere on the arc sets the target in 0.5 degree steps by angle (pointer capture, touch-action none); the dial role="slider" (aria-valuemin 16, aria-valuemax 28, aria-valuenow, aria-valuetext "21.5 degrees, heating") supports ArrowUp/Right +0.5, ArrowDown/Left -0.5, PageUp/PageDown +/-2, Home/End. Minus and plus buttons do the same with press-and-hold repeat. The "Now" temperature drifts toward the target at 0.1 degree per 3 seconds (simulated) and the status text and the energy estimate update; reaching the target switches to Holding. Mode buttons switch behaviour (Eco lowers the effective target by 4 degrees and tints the dial green; Off freezes the drift and greys the dial). Schedule edits update the grid and persist in localStorage (try/catch); the current block is highlighted and, when the clock crosses a block, the target follows it unless manually overridden (an "Override until 17:30" chip appears with a Resume schedule button). Room lighting and the window colour cross-fade with the target. A polite live region announces target changes after 600ms of inactivity.

Responsive: Under 900px the panels stack with the dial first and shrink to 300px; the schedule becomes a horizontally compact 4 by 7 grid with abbreviated days; the backdrop crops to the window and sofa; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff ring with dark offset on the knob, buttons and schedule blocks. Temperature state is never colour-only (numbers, mode names and icons accompany colour). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no cross-fade of lighting, no mist or shimmer, no drift animation; values update instantly). Invented names only.
