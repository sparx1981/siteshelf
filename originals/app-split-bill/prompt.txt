Canvas & base: Friendly café-receipt page #f3efe6 with a main white receipt card (#ffffff, jagged zig-zag bottom edge made with CSS, 1px #e4dcc8 border, soft shadow) and a summary card to its side. Ink #26231c (15:1), secondary #6a6454 (5.6:1). People have fixed colours: Odette #e0553a, Kwame #2f7d4f, Priya #7a4fe0, Ines #d99a1e, Fenn #2a8fb8, each used as a filled avatar with white or dark text at 4.5:1 and as a chip tint. Accent ink #26231c for primary buttons. Invented app "Tabsplit".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for amounts and headings (totals 32px); "Nunito" 400/700/800 for UI and item names (16px); "DM Mono" 400 for line amounts (15px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Tabsplit", headline "Dinner for five, settled." and a line. Two columns. Left, the receipt card: a "Who's here" row of person chips (avatar, name, remove cross) with an "Add person" inline field; an items table of eight dishes at "Marlow's Kitchen" (invented) with name, price field, a quantity, and a row of small person toggle buttons showing who is sharing it (all selected by default for starters, individual for mains), plus "Split evenly" and "Clear" row actions; an "Add item" row; below it the totals block: subtotal, tax rate field (default 20% shown as VAT, applies to prices) and tip choice chips (None, 10%, 12.5%, 15%, Custom with a number input), plus service note. Right, the summary card: "Each person owes" list with avatar, name, items total, tax share, tip share and the amount due (large), a "Paid by" select (who covered the bill), a settle-up section listing simple transfers ("Kwame pays Odette £23.40"), a bar showing each person's share, and "Copy summary" and "Reset" buttons.

Interaction: Everything is computed in integer pence to avoid rounding errors: each item's price is split equally among its sharers with leftover pence distributed one by one in a stable order so shares always sum exactly to the item price; tax and tip are proportionally allocated by item subtotal with the same remainder method, so the grand total equals the sum of person totals exactly. Toggling a person on an item recalculates instantly with a quick number roll; an item with no sharers is flagged "Nobody is paying for this" and the summary shows an unallocated warning. Prices accept pounds with decimals (parsed and clamped), quantities multiply. Settle-up minimises transfers: compute each person's net (paid minus owed), then greedily match debtors to creditors. Person toggles are buttons with aria-pressed and aria-label "Priya shares Margherita pizza". Add or remove people with Enter and the remove button (removal asks for confirmation if they share any item). The summary is a live region (polite) that announces the grand total changes after 600ms. Copy summary produces a plain-text breakdown via clipboard API with fallback. State saved to localStorage in try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the summary card stacks under the receipt; item rows turn into cards with toggles below the name; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #26231c with white offset. Colour is never the only person identifier (initials and names always shown). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no number roll or bar animation). Invented names and prices only.
