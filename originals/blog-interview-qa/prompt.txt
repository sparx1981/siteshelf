Build a single-page "Interview Q&A Article" blog template for an invented craft magazine, "Kiln & Reed Quarterly": a studio-visit interview with a ceramicist, set as questions and answers with painted speaker avatars, a sticky question rail, pull quotes and a highlights filter. Everything is one HTML file.

Canvas & base: Warm clay editorial look. Page #f7efe3, article card #fffaf2 with 1px #e6d6bd border, ink #2b211a (14.5:1), secondary #675646 (6.4:1), terracotta #a8431f (white text 5.9:1; 5.5:1 as text on the page), glaze teal #1f5f5b for the interviewer, soft clay tint #f0dcc4. Invented magazine, studio and people only.

Typography: "Instrument Serif" 400 (italic for quotes) for the title (clamp 40 to 78px), answers (21px, 1.55 leading) and pull quotes (clamp 28 to 44px); "Work Sans" 400/500/600 for questions, labels and UI at 14 to 16px. Questions set in Work Sans 600, small caps feel, teal; answers in serif. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, terracotta.

Imagery & art direction: A painted studio hero (inline SVG, 880x560): clay-plaster wall with subtle trowel marks, a wooden shelf with five glazed pots in different silhouettes (bottle, bowl, jug, tall vase, lidded jar) with gradient glazes in teal, ochre, bone, rust and slate, soft contact shadows, a trailing plant, a window-light wash from the left, grain and vignette; in front, the painted portrait of the ceramicist (portrait generator, apron in rust) framed like a pinned photograph with tape corners. A small painted mug (product generator) and bowl motif decorate the pull quotes. Avatars: interviewer (teal ring) and ceramicist (terracotta ring) from the portrait generator, cropped to circles. Media slots for real photography: hero studio photo (1760x1120) and the portrait (800x1000).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Studio visit · No. 18", title "Hands, Heat and Patience", deck "Odalys Brennick on single firings, slow pricing and the perfect mug", meta row (interviewer avatar and "Priya Venn", date, "9 min read") and the hero. Below, at 1000px and up, a two-column grid: a sticky left rail (width 260px) titled "Questions" with 8 numbered jump links, a "Highlights only" switch and a progress line; and the article column (max 720px). Each exchange: question block (avatar, label "Priya", question text) then answer block (avatar, "Odalys", answer paragraphs); some answers are long and clamp to 3 lines with a "Read more" button; two pull quotes sit between exchanges with a "Copy quote" button. End block with the ceramicist's studio details and a "More studio visits" strip of two painted cards.

Copy: Questions: "How did you end up with your hands in clay?", "What does a normal day look like?", "You fire most pieces only once. Why?", "Do you ever throw away work?", "What makes a mug good?", "How do you price something you made slowly?", "What would you tell someone starting out?", "What is on the shelf right now?". Write 8 original answers (40 to 80 words each) in a warm, specific voice with no real brands. Pull quotes: "A good mug disappears into your morning." and "I price the hours I would be ashamed to hide." Messages: "Quote copied." "Showing 4 highlights." "Showing all 8 questions."

Interaction: The jump rail uses IntersectionObserver or a throttled scroll handler to mark the current question (aria-current, a rust bar); clicking a link scrolls (smooth unless reduced motion) and moves focus to that question heading (tabindex -1). Read more toggles aria-expanded and the clamp. The Highlights only switch (role switch, aria-checked) hides non-highlight exchanges (4 of 8 are flagged with a star), updates the rail and announces the count. Copy quote uses the Clipboard API in try/catch with a polite message. The progress line shows the percentage of the article scrolled. Settings persist in localStorage inside try/catch.

Technique notes: Line clamp uses -webkit-line-clamp with display:-webkit-box; measure whether the answer actually overflows (scrollHeight greater than clientHeight) and hide Read more when it does not. Avatar crop uses an overflow-hidden circle with the portrait SVG scaled to 210 percent and offset so the face is centred.

Responsive: Under 1000px the rail becomes a horizontal chip row that sticks under the top edge (scrolling inside itself only) with the switch at its end; avatars shrink to 40px; hero art is full width; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #2b211a outline with white offset. Never rely on colour alone: speakers are labelled by name text and avatar ring. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no smooth scrolling). Invented names only.
