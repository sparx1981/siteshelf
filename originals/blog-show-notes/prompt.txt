Build a single-page "Podcast Show Notes" episode page for an invented podcast, "Slow Signal": a demo player with a waveform scrubber, a clickable chapter list, a searchable transcript that follows playback, and a resource list. The player is a simulated, silent demo (no audio file): it advances a clock so every feature can be seen working. Everything is one HTML file.

Canvas & base: Warm linen editorial look. Page #f3efe8, cards #fffdf9 with a 1px #ddd5c6 border and 16px radius, ink #231f1a (15.9:1), secondary #5a5348 (7.1:1), accent ember #b83f1c (white text 5.9:1; 5.6:1 as text on the page), deep teal #0d4a4a for the cover and player (cream text #f3efe8 at 9.8:1), highlight yellow #fff0a8 for search marks (ink text 15:1). Invented podcast, host and guest; example.com links only.

Typography: "Bricolage Grotesque" 700 for the episode title (clamp 30 to 54px) and card headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI and transcript at 14 to 16px; "IBM Plex Mono" 500 for timestamps (tabular). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, ember.

Imagery & art direction: Square cover art painted in inline SVG: a deep teal field covered with hand-drawn topographic contour lines (wobbling closed curves in cream and sand at varying opacity, generated from polar waves), a small compass rose, a hand-lettered "SLOW SIGNAL" wordmark in a cream ring, and subtle paper grain with a soft vignette. A waveform of 120 rounded bars (seeded random heights with a gentle envelope) forms the scrubber, bars before the playhead filled ember and the rest sand. Two painted speaker avatars (host with a green scarf, guest with round glasses) from the portrait generator. Each chapter has a small painted thumbnail (landscape, sea, city and interior scenes at 96x64) so the list reads like an album tracklist. Resource cards use small hand-drawn icons (book, map, compass, pen). Media slots for real photography: cover (1400x1400), guest portrait (800x1000) and optional chapter thumbs (480x320).

Layout: Hero: cover art left (up to 340px), right the eyebrow "Episode 42 · 38 min", title "The Quiet Hour With a Cartographer", a two-sentence summary, speakers (avatar, name, role) and the player card: play/pause round button, current time and total, the waveform scrubber, speed button (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x), skip back 15s and forward 30s, and the line "Demo player: no audio". Below, two columns above 980px. Left (sticky): Chapters (6 rows: thumbnail, title, start time, a tiny progress bar for the current chapter) and Resources (5 cards with title, short note, example.com link and Copy link). Right: Transcript card with a search field, match counter ("3 of 7"), Previous and Next match buttons, a "Follow along" checkbox and the transcript list (about 16 lines, each with a timestamp button, speaker name and text).

Copy: Title "The Quiet Hour With a Cartographer", host "Maya Okoro", guest "Ruth Delacroix, mapmaker". Chapters: 00:00 Cold open, 02:10 Why maps lie, 09:45 Drawing the coast, 17:30 Colour and silence, 26:05 Listener questions, 33:40 What to read next. Write 16 original transcript lines about choosing what to leave off a map, with invented book and tool names. Messages: "Link copied." "Jumped to Drawing the coast." "No matches for 'compass'."

Interaction: Simulated clock: playing advances current time by 0.25s x speed every 250ms until 38:40, then stops. The scrubber is a native range input (aria-label "Seek", aria-valuetext "9:45 of 38:40") layered under the waveform so keyboard arrows seek by 5s; clicking a waveform bar seeks. Skip buttons move 15s back or 30s forward. Current chapter and current transcript line derive from the time; the active line has aria-current and a left rule and scrolls into the visible area of the transcript box only when Follow along is on and the user has not scrolled manually in the last 3 seconds. Clicking a chapter or a timestamp seeks and announces "Jumped to ...". Search: case-insensitive, highlights matches with mark, shows count, Next and Previous cycle through matches (Enter and Shift+Enter in the field), scrolling the match into view; an empty result announces "No matches for ...". Escape clears the field. Copy link copies the example.com URL with the Clipboard API in try/catch. A polite live region announces chapter changes during playback (not every second). Speed, follow setting and last time persist in localStorage inside try/catch.

Technique notes: Waveform fill uses a clipPath rectangle whose width follows progress, so one set of bars is drawn twice (sand and ember) rather than recolouring each bar; the playhead is a 2px line. Transcript auto-scroll uses scrollTo on the transcript container, never scrollIntoView on the page, so the page does not jump.

Responsive: Under 980px the hero stacks (cover 240px, then text, then player), the left column becomes a normal section above the transcript, the transcript box gets a max height of 70vh with its own scroll, and the waveform bars thin to 80; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images, no audio. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #231f1a outline with white offset. Never rely on colour alone: the active chapter and line have a rule and aria-current. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no smooth scrolling, no animated progress easing). Invented names only.
