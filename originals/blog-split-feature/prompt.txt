Build a single-page "Split Feature Article" blog template for an invented nature magazine, "Fieldline Review": a long read about a salt-marsh restoration, where a sticky title column with a full-height painted cover stays in view while the article scrolls beside it, with chapter navigation, reading progress, expandable notes and inline painted figures. Everything is one HTML file.

Canvas & base: Editorial and calm. Article side #f6f1e5 (paper) with ink #1f2a24 (14.2:1) and secondary #4d5a52 (6.4:1); title column #1d3d31 (deep marsh green) with cream text #f6f1e5 (11.9:1) and sage #b9d3bf for small labels (7.4:1); accent rust #9f4520 for links, drop cap and rules (6.3:1 on paper). Hairline rules #d9d0ba. Invented publication "Fieldline Review"; invented story and names only.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for the title (clamp 34 to 64px, tight 1.02 leading) and chapter headings (clamp 26 to 38px); "Newsreader" 400 and italic for the article body (reader-controlled 18, 20 or 22px, 1.65 leading, 64 to 68 character measure); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI, labels and captions at 13 to 15px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, sage.

Imagery & art direction: The title column is dominated by a full-height painted cover (inline SVG from the scene library's landscape generator in its dawn palette: a flat salt marsh at first light with winding tidal channels catching pink sky, reed silhouettes in the foreground, a heron, soft mist bands, grain and a vignette) under a green gradient so the title stays legible. The article carries three inline figures, each painted and captioned: (1) a breached sea wall with grey winter water (sea generator, coast palette), (2) sea lavender flowering in the second summer (landscape, forest palette with lavender-pink dabs), (3) an abstract map-like painting of tidal channels in teals and sand (abstract generator). A painted portrait avatar (portrait generator) sits in the byline and again beside a pull quote from the ecologist. Media slots for real photography: the cover (1200x1600 portrait) and the three figures (1600x1000), each with alt text and a caption with credit line.

Layout: Two columns at 960px and up: left column 42 percent wide, position sticky, top 0, height 100vh, padding 40px; right column 58 percent with generous side padding and a 68ch text measure. Left column content from top: eyebrow "Fieldline Review · Long read", title "What the Salt Marsh Remembers", a two-line deck, byline (avatar, "By Ines Calloway", "14 min read · October 2026"), then at the bottom the chapter list (5 items, each a button with a number, the active one marked with a thin rust line and aria-current), a progress ring with percentage, text size buttons (A-, A+) and a "Show all notes" toggle. Right column: five chapters ("A wall gives way", "The first winter", "Mud, slowly", "Lavender", "What stays"), each with an h2, 2 to 3 paragraphs (first paragraph of chapter one has a drop cap), numbered note markers that expand a note card, one figure per chapter as described, one pull quote, and an end-of-article block with author bio and a "Keep reading" row of two painted related-story cards.

Copy: Opening paragraph: "The sea wall at Tern Reach failed on a Tuesday in January, quietly, the way old things do. By Friday there was a new shoreline." Pull quote: "We did not plant the marsh. We stopped standing in its way." Notes such as "Tern Reach lies on the invented Halmer Estuary; tide ranges there reach 5.8 metres." Buttons: "Show all notes", "Hide all notes". Write about 900 words of original, believable prose across the five chapters (restoration, tides, sediment, plants, birds, local fishers), no real people or institutions.

Interaction: Scrollspy with IntersectionObserver marks the chapter whose heading is closest to the top; clicking a chapter button scrolls to it (smooth unless reduced motion) and moves focus to its heading (tabindex -1). The progress ring shows percentage of the article read (scroll position over article height) with stroke-dashoffset and the number as text. A- and A+ change the body size across three steps and persist in localStorage inside try/catch. Note markers are buttons with aria-expanded and aria-controls toggling a card under the paragraph; "Show all notes" expands or collapses every card and flips its label. A thin top progress bar mirrors the ring. External links are none. Keyboard: all controls reachable in reading order; the chapter list uses a nav landmark with aria-label "Chapters".

Technique notes: The sticky column uses height:100vh with overflow hidden and a flex column so the chapter list stays at the bottom; the cover SVG is absolutely positioned with preserveAspectRatio slice. Under 960px the layout becomes one column: the cover becomes a 56vw-tall header with the title overlaid, the chapter list becomes a horizontally scrolling chip row that sticks to the top (scroll inside it only), and the ring moves into that row. Use scroll-margin-top on headings equal to the sticky row height.

Responsive: Single column under 960px, body text 18px minimum, no horizontal scroll at 390px, figures full bleed with captions below.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #9f4520 outline with 2px offset (on green use #f6f1e5). Never rely on colour alone: the active chapter also has a line and aria-current. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no smooth scrolling, no ring easing). Invented names only.
