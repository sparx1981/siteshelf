Canvas & base: Dark ops-console page #0e1116 with a faint dot grid, panels #171c24 (1px #2a313c, 20px radius), text #e9edf3 (15:1), secondary #9aa6b6 (7:1). Danger red #ff4d5e (on dark 5.6:1; button text on red #1a0306), warning amber #ffb020, safe teal #2dd4a6. Invented product "Bunkerline" (a hosting dashboard).

Typography: "Manrope" 600/700/800 for headings and buttons (15 to 17px labels); "IBM Plex Mono" 400/500 for the log and timer readouts (13px).

Layout: Header ("Bunkerline", headline "Some buttons should make you mean it." and a line). A controls row: Hold time slider (500ms to 2500ms, default 1200ms) and a switch "Use click and confirm instead of hold" for accessibility. A three-panel grid, each panel with a title, an explanation of the consequence and one large hold button: (1) Delete project "atlas-api" (red, trash icon), (2) Publish site to production (amber, rocket icon), (3) Reset all settings (teal, refresh icon). A hold button is 72px tall, 280px wide, rounded 18px, with its label left and a 40px circular progress ring on the right that fills clockwise; beneath the label a hint "Hold for 1.2 s". A wide log panel at the bottom lists outcomes with timestamps (Confirmed, Cancelled early at 64%, and so on) and a "Clear log" button.

Interaction: Pointerdown starts a requestAnimationFrame timer; the ring's stroke-dashoffset and a background fill sweep (left to right inside the button) track progress 0 to 1 while the label changes to "Keep holding…" and the button scales to 0.98 with a slight red glow that intensifies. At 100% the button flashes, the label becomes "Done" with a check, a result is logged and an undo toast appears for 4s ("atlas-api deleted. Undo"). Releasing early rewinds the ring over 250ms back to 0 with "Cancelled" in the log (with the percent reached). Keyboard: holding Space or Enter on a focused button does the same (keydown starts, keyup cancels, repeat events ignored); focus is never lost. Alternative path (switch on): the button becomes a normal click that opens a native <dialog> with "Are you sure?" and Confirm and Cancel buttons (default focus on Cancel). A visually hidden live region announces "Hold to confirm, 50 percent" at 25% steps and the final result. Pointer movement away from the button cancels (pointerleave or pointercancel).

Responsive: Panels stack under 860px; the button becomes full width (max 360px) with the ring staying on the right; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no scale or glow; ring still tracks progress linearly; rewind instant). Invented names only; the actions are simulated.
