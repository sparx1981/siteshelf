Canvas & base: Soft off-white showcase page #f4f7f9 with a 2 by 3 grid of white tiles (1px #dde5ea borders, 20px radius) plus one dark tile #0f1b24. Ink #0f1b24 (16:1). Four switchable fill themes set CSS variables: Ocean (fill #0ea5c6, text on fill #031a22), Sunset (fill #ff6a4d, text #2a0a02), Forest (fill #1f8a5b, text #ffffff) and Grape (fill #7c4dff, text #ffffff). Invented design system "Tidepool UI".

Typography: "Sora" 600/700 for button labels (16px) and headings; "Inter" 400/500 for captions and controls (14 to 15px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Tidepool UI", headline "Buttons that fill like water." and a short line. A controls bar: theme swatches (four radio swatches), a Speed slider (300ms to 1400ms, default 700ms) with live value, and an "Auto-play demo" checkbox. Below it the grid of six tiles, each with a caption and one button: (1) Get started, solid pill, blob mode; (2) See pricing, outline pill that fills solid, rising wave mode; (3) Download, square-ish 12px radius, sideways tide mode with an arrow icon; (4) Book a demo, dark tile, light outline, blob mode; (5) a 64px round icon-only Play button, blob mode; (6) a large CTA "Start your free trial" with arrow, wave mode, 64px tall.

Interaction: Blob mode: on pointerenter a circle element (size 320% of the button width) is placed at the exact entry point (CSS variables --x and --y) and scales from 0 to 1 over the speed duration with cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), while its border-radius gently wobbles; on pointerleave it is moved to the exit point and scales back to 0. Wave mode: a fill block rises from the bottom with a moving sine-wave top edge (SVG mask repeating horizontally, sliding 1.2s linear). Tide mode: the fill sweeps in from the left with a rounded leading edge and exits to the right. Label colour switches to the on-fill text colour with a 150ms delay so it changes as the water passes it. Keyboard focus triggers the fill from the centre and removes it on blur, so keyboard users get the same feedback; pressed state scales to 0.97. Touch fills from the tap point and drains on release. Controls: theme swatches update CSS variables live; Speed updates --d live; Auto-play cycles a simulated hover through the six buttons every 1.4s and stops as soon as the user uses the pointer or keyboard on any button or turns it off. Buttons are real <button> elements with visible text and a toast announcing the click ("Get started clicked") in an aria-live region.

Responsive: The grid becomes 2 columns under 980px and 1 column under 560px; the large CTA keeps a 64px height and wraps text only under 360px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Focus ring 3px #0ea5c6 (or #fff on the dark tile) with offset; never remove the outline. Contrast of label on base and on fill at least 4.5:1 in every theme. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (fills appear instantly without wobble or wave slide, auto-play off and its checkbox hidden). Invented names only.
