Canvas & base: Calm light-grey page #eef1f4 with white panels (#fff, 1px #dde3e8, 20px radius) and one dark panel #11181f. Ink #11181f (16:1). Button colours: pay green #12805c (white text 5.0:1), save blue #2457ff (white 6.9:1), send violet #6a3df0 (white 6.3:1); success mint #12a56e, error red #d62f3f (white 5.2:1). Invented payments tool "Settlebook".

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans" 600/700/800 for buttons and headings (clamp 34px to 56px headline); "Inter" 400/500 for captions and status text (14 to 15px).

Layout: Header ("Settlebook", headline "Buttons that tell you what happened.", one short paragraph). A controls strip: "Make the next action fail" switch, a Speed select (Fast 900ms, Normal 1600ms, Slow 2600ms) and a "Reset all" button. A grid of three white panels, each with a title, a caption, a live status line and one button: (1) "Pay £24.00" wide 56px pill with a card icon; (2) "Save changes" 48px 12px-radius button; (3) "Send invite" 48px button with a paper-plane icon. A fourth dark panel shows a compact icon-only round button (56px) cycling through loading, success and error, with a log list below it ("14:02:11 Payment settled") that records every outcome.

Interaction: Pressing a button starts a flow: the label fades out and the button's width animates (measured in px, 320ms) down to a circle the same height while a spinner arc appears (rotating 800ms linear). After the chosen duration the result morphs: success turns the background mint and draws a check mark with stroke-dashoffset (360ms), error turns it red, draws a cross and shakes horizontally (6px, 3 cycles). The button then holds 1.4s and expands back to idle with the original label (for error the label changes to "Try again" until pressed). While busy the button has aria-busy="true" and aria-disabled="true" (not disabled, so focus is kept) and ignores presses; the width and colour changes use transform-free properties only where needed. Outcomes alternate by default (pay succeeds, send fails on the first press and succeeds after that) unless "Make the next action fail" is on. A polite status line under each button and in the dark panel log announces "Paying…", "Payment complete" or "Payment failed, try again". Escape cancels a running flow and returns to idle with "Cancelled".

Responsive: Under 700px the grid is one column; buttons are full width (idle) and shrink to circles centred; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #11181f with white offset (white on the dark panel). Do not rely on colour alone: icons (check, cross) and text status accompany every state. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no width animation or shake; states swap instantly and the spinner becomes a static ring with dots text "…"). Invented names only; amounts and times fictional.
