Canvas & base: Light page #f5f4fb with white cards (#ffffff, 1px #e3e1f2 borders, 20px radius) and a dark showcase card #1b1740. Indigo primary #4a3df0 (white text 6.7:1), teal secondary #0aa89a, amber accent #ffb020 (dark text). Ripple colours are the button's text colour at 22% opacity. Invented UI kit "Ripplekit".

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans" 600/700/800 for buttons and headings (15 to 16px labels, headline clamp 34px to 56px); "Inter" 400/500 for captions.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Ripplekit", headline "Feedback you can feel." and a short line, a controls bar (Colour: Indigo, Teal, Amber radio swatches; Duration slider 300ms to 1200ms; "Unbounded icon ripple" switch), and a 3-column grid of five demo cards: (1) Filled buttons (three sizes: small 36px, medium 44px, large 56px); (2) Outlined buttons with 2px border; (3) Text buttons in a row (Cancel, Learn more, Skip); (4) Round icon buttons (heart, share, bookmark, plus) 48px plus a 64px floating action button with a plus; (5) A settings list with three rows (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Notifications) whose whole rows ripple on press. A sixth dark card shows a "Chip set" of five toggle chips that ripple and toggle selected state.

Interaction: On pointerdown inside any ripple surface a span is appended at the pointer position, sized to cover the furthest corner (diameter twice the max distance), and animates from scale 0 to 1 over the duration (cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1)) while opacity stays at its colour alpha; on pointerup or leave it fades out over 300ms and is removed. Quick taps still complete the expansion before fading. For the icon buttons the unbounded switch lets the ripple extend beyond the 48px button (overflow visible, 120px circle). Keyboard: pressing Enter or Space on a focused button triggers a centred ripple (use the click event with detail === 0 so mouse clicks are not doubled) and a hold ring is not needed; focus-visible shows a 3px outline offset 3px. Chips toggle aria-pressed. List rows are real buttons with role-appropriate labels; a toast reports "Wi-Fi row pressed" in an aria-live region. Colour swatches change --rc (ripple) and the primary fill live.

Responsive: Grid to 2 columns under 980px and 1 column under 600px; list rows stay full width; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Ripple elements are aria-hidden and pointer-events:none; never trap focus. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (replace the expanding ripple by a brief 120ms background flash at 12% opacity; duration control hidden). Invented names only.
