Canvas & base: Deep teal desk page #0f3b3f with a subtle cloth texture (repeating radial dots #ffffff08) and a soft overhead light (radial #1d6b70 at 30%). Postcards are #fbf5e6 stock with a 1px #e0d3b3 edge, 14px radius, a printed stamp and a postmark; five illustrated fronts with distinct palettes. Text on cards #2b2418 (14:1), on the page #eaf6f2. Accent coral #ff6b57. Invented stationer "Wayfarer Postcards".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for headings and card place names (clamp 34px to 60px headline, 26px place names); "Karla" 400/500/700 for body, captions and buttons (16px).

Layout: Two columns on desktop. Left: eyebrow "Wayfarer Postcards", headline "Five places, one pile.", short paragraph, a control row (Fan out / Stack buttons, a counter "Top card: Kestrel Point") and a hint line. Right: a stage 520px wide by 440px tall where five postcards (300px by 200px, landscape) are stacked at the centre with small deterministic rotations (-6, 3, -2, 5, -3 degrees) and 3px offsets. Each card shows a generated SVG illustration of an invented place (Kestrel Point lighthouse, Lantern Cove harbour, Saltmarsh reeds, Wren Valley hills, Heron Bay sunset), a handwritten-style caption area, the place name, a stamp and a postmark ring. On fan, cards rotate about a pivot below the stage (transform-origin 50% 140%) from -28 to +28 degrees with 40ms stagger and rise by 20px when hovered individually.

Interaction: Hovering or focusing within the stack fans it out (transition 520ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.2,.5,1)); leaving collapses it. Clicking or pressing Enter/Space on a card brings it to the front: the cards reorder with a FLIP animation (Web Animations API) and the clicked card lifts, straightens and settles on top; the counter and a polite live region announce "Top card: Heron Bay". Arrow Left/Right move focus through cards while fanned; Home/End jump; Escape collapses. Cards are real buttons with aria-label "Heron Bay postcard, position 3 of 5". Fan out and Stack buttons toggle a persistent fanned state (aria-pressed) for touch devices where hover is unavailable; touch users can also tap the stack to fan it. Drag is not required.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack and the stage scales (CSS transform scale 0.8 under 600px, wrapper height adjusted) so the fanned cards stay within 390px; no horizontal scroll.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ff6b57 outline offset 3px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant fan and reorder, no lift). Invented names only.
