Canvas & base: Near-black page #08080f with a faint aurora gradient (radial violet #4b2fd0 at 20%, radial teal #0ba8a0 at 15%) and noise-free flat panels. Cards #12131f with 22px radius, a 2px (adjustable 1 to 5px) border drawn by a conic gradient, and inner padding 24px. Text #f1f2ff (16:1), secondary #a5a8c8 (7.9:1). Three gradient palettes: Aurora (#7c5cff, #19e6c9, #ff6bd6), Ember (#ff8a3d, #ff3d6e, #ffd23d) and Glacier (#6ee7ff, #7aa2ff, #e8f7ff). Invented design studio "Prismcraft".

Typography: "Sora" 600/700/800 for headings and numerals (headline clamp 36px to 64px, card titles 22px); "Inter" 400/500 for body (15 to 16px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Prismcraft", headline "Borders that never sit still." and one line. A controls bar: Palette radio chips (Aurora, Ember, Glacier), Speed slider (1.5s to 8s per turn, default 4s) and Thickness slider (1 to 5px, default 2px), plus a "Always spin" switch. Below, a 3-column grid of six cards, each with an inline-SVG icon in a 48px rounded tile, a title, two lines of text and a small link row ("Explore ->"). Card topics: Colour systems, Motion library, Icon sets, Type pairing, Layout grids, Handoff kit. The first card is slightly larger (spans two columns on wide screens) and holds a mini pricing line "From £19 / month".

Interaction: Each card's border is a conic-gradient painted on the border-box using a registered custom property --a (@property, syntax "<angle>"), animated 0 to 360deg infinitely with linear timing; the animation is paused at rest and runs while the card is hovered or contains focus (or always when "Always spin" is on). A soft blurred copy of the gradient sits behind the card as a glow whose opacity rises on hover (0.15 to 0.55). Cards lift 4px and the icon tile tilts 6 degrees on hover. Controls update CSS variables live (--g1, --g2, --g3, --dur, --bw). Cards are focusable articles with a real link inside; the link receives focus-visible and the whole card shows the spinning border via :focus-within. Where @property is unsupported the border falls back to a static conic gradient at 0deg (feature check with CSS.supports) and a small note appears.

Responsive: 3 columns above 980px, 2 above 620px, 1 below; the large first card spans the full row on small screens; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline offset 3px on links, chips and sliders. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (borders stay static at a pleasant angle; the Always spin switch and Speed control are hidden). Invented names only.
