Canvas & base: Midnight-violet display table #120d24 with a soft spotlight (radial #3a2a7a at 30%) and a faint reflective floor ellipse. The collectible card is 340px by 476px (5:7), 18px radius, with a 10px gold-foil frame (#e8c36a to #fff0b8 gradient), an inner illustration panel and a text area in cream #fbf4e2 with ink #2a1f12 (13:1). Foil layers: a rainbow conic/linear gradient (hsl rotating) blended with color-dodge over the artwork, a diagonal sparkle texture made from tiny radial dots, and a glare radial gradient at the pointer. Invented collectible game "Moonfield Cards".

Typography: "Unbounded" 600/700 for card names and HP (name 22px); "Outfit" 400/500/600 for attack text, labels and UI (14 to 16px).

Layout: Left: eyebrow "Moonfield Cards", headline "A card that catches the light." and a short paragraph, then a row of three selectable thumbnails (Embermoth, Tidefox, Pebblewyrm) as small foil mini-cards, a Rarity line ("Ultra Rare, 012 / 150") and instructions. Right: the large card on a perspective stage (perspective 1100px). Front face: name, HP, stage badge, an illustration panel (generated SVG creature on a starry or marsh backdrop with a holo mask), two attacks with energy cost dots, damage numbers and a flavour line, plus a weakness, resistance and retreat row, and the set symbol and number at the bottom. Back face: a deep indigo pattern with a central crescent emblem and the stats table (Height, Weight, Habitat, Discovered by) and a short lore paragraph.

Interaction: Pointer movement over the card tilts it up to 16 degrees on X and Y (smoothed with lerp in requestAnimationFrame) while CSS variables --mx and --my move the glare and the rainbow background-position, and the foil layer brightness rises with distance from centre; leaving the card eases it back to rest with a small idle float animation. Click, Enter or Space flips the card 180 degrees over 700ms (the front fades its foil as it turns); a "Flip" button duplicates this for touch. Keyboard: with the card focused the arrow keys tilt it in 6-degree steps so the foil can be explored without a pointer, and Escape resets. The thumbnails are a radiogroup that swaps the creature (name, art colours, attacks, stats) with a quick 300ms fade-through flash. A "Shine" slider (0 to 100) controls foil intensity. The card is a button-like element role="group" with aria-label and a visually hidden description that reads the full card text; the flipped state toggles aria-pressed.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack and the card scales to min(340px, 86vw) with proportional text; tilt uses touch pointer events (touch-action:pan-y); no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffd86b outline offset 4px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no tilt, float or smoothing; flip becomes a cross-fade; sheen shows at a fixed pleasant position). Invented creatures and numbers only.
