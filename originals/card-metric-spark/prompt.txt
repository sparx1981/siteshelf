Canvas & base: Light analytics page #f3f5f9 with white cards (#ffffff, 1px #e1e6ef borders, 20px radius, soft shadow 0 12px 30px -20px rgba(20,30,60,.35)). Ink #121826 (17:1), secondary #576178 (6.4:1). Positive #0f8a5f (white on it 4.6:1), negative #d6334a, neutral #576178. Sparkline strokes in the card's accent (indigo #4a55e8, teal #0f9fa8, violet #8a4fe0, amber #d98a0b, rose #e0457b, green #0f8a5f) with a gradient area fill at 18% to 0%. Invented analytics tool "Tidemark".

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans" 600/700/800 for labels, values (value 34px, 800, tabular numerals) and the period switch; "Inter" 400/500 for secondary text and tooltips (13 to 14px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Tidemark", headline "The numbers, at a glance." and a segmented period switch (Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days) aligned right. A 3-column grid of six KPI cards: Revenue (£48,210), Orders (1,284), Conversion rate (3.8%), Average order (£37.54), Refund rate (1.2%, where down is good), Active users (9,430). Each card has a title with a tiny icon, the big value, a delta pill ("+8.4%" with an arrow, green for good and red for bad, accounting for inverse metrics), a comparison line ("vs previous 30 days"), and a 64px-tall sparkline across the card bottom with a last-point dot. Beneath each card, a visually hidden data table lists the values per day for assistive tech.

Interaction: On load and on period change the sparklines draw in with stroke-dashoffset (700ms ease-out) while values count up (600ms) and delta pills fade in; data are generated deterministically from a seeded random function per metric and period so repeated visits match. Hovering or touch-dragging across a sparkline moves a vertical crosshair with a dot and a tooltip showing the date and value; keyboard users focus the card (tabindex 0) and use Left/Right arrow keys to step through points, Home/End to jump, Escape to clear; the tooltip text is mirrored in a polite live region. The period switch is a radiogroup with arrow keys and updates all cards. Clicking a card toggles a "pinned" outline and shows min, max and average in a footer row (aria-pressed). Number formatting uses Intl.NumberFormat with GBP for currency.

Responsive: 2 columns under 980px and 1 under 620px; the period switch wraps below the headline; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #4a55e8 offset 3px. Delta colours are accompanied by arrows and the words "up" or "down" for assistive tech (not colour only). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no draw-in or count-up; final state at once). Invented names and figures only.
