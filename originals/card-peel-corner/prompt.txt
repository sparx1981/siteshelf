Canvas & base: Warm paper page #f2ece0 with a faint linen texture (repeating-linear-gradient 4px at 3% opacity). Cards are #fffdf8 with 1px #e2d8c4 borders, 22px radius and a soft shadow (0 18px 40px -22px rgba(60,40,10,.5)). Ink #2a241b (14:1), secondary #6a5f4d (5.8:1). The peeled flap shows the card's back in #e7dfcf with a diagonal gradient shading; the coupon revealed under the corner uses #d9482b (white text 4.9:1) or #1f6f54 (white 6.2:1) alternating by card. Invented homeware shop "Marlow & Pine".

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for card titles and coupon values (24px titles); "Karla" 400/500/700 for body, prices and buttons (16px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Marlow & Pine", headline "Peek under the corner." and a short line. A 3-column grid of six product cards, each 4:5 with a generated SVG illustration (linen throw, ceramic jug, brass lamp, oak stool, wool rug, glass vase) at the top, a title, a one-line description, a price and a small bottom-right hint "Peel for an offer". The bottom-right corner of every card is a peelable page corner: at rest a tiny 0px fold; on hover or keyboard focus the card face is clipped along a diagonal and a folded flap (the card's back, shaded) appears, revealing the coupon triangle beneath with a short value ("-20%", "Free delivery", "2 for 1", "-15%", "Free gift", "-10%").

Interaction: Hover or focus-within animates the registered custom property --s (@property, length) from 0 to 84px over 380ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.2,.5,1). A real button "Reveal offer for Linen throw" (the corner hit area, 72px square, bottom-right) toggles an open state on click, Enter or Space that animates --s to 190px and shows a full coupon panel in the revealed triangle area: value, "Use code PINE20", and a Copy code button (clipboard in try/catch with a textarea fallback and a polite "Copied" message). Escape closes the open peel and returns focus to the corner button. Only one card is open at a time. Touch devices use the corner button the same way (tap to open). The flap shading and shadow update with --s; the card does not move or reflow. On load the first card is shown peeled to 84px as a hint until the user presses a key or pointer.

Responsive: 2 columns under 980px and 1 under 600px; the peel size scales with a --k factor (0.85 on small screens); no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #2a241b ring with white offset on the corner button and any inner controls. Offer text is real text (not an image) and is available to assistive tech when open; when closed the corner button's label says what will happen. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no peel transition; the corner jumps to the target size). Invented names only.
