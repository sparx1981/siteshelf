Canvas & base: Warm festive café page #2a1710 (espresso) with a soft spotlight (radial #6b3a1e at 40%) and tiny gold dust specks. Prize cards #fff6e4 (cream) 300px by 200px with 18px radius, a 3px gold edge (#e0b04a) and a dotted ticket perforation on the left. The scratch layer is a silver foil canvas (gradient #cfd3d8 to #8f959c with fine noise and diagonal sheen) with the text "SCRATCH HERE" tiled in a darker silver. Prize text ink #3a1d0e (13:1) on cream, gold #e0b04a accents, red #c4382c for stamps. Invented loyalty scheme "Lucky Lantern Cafe".

Typography: "Gloock" 400 for prize headings (clamp 28px to 36px); "Nunito" 400/600/700/800 for body, progress text and buttons (15 to 17px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Lucky Lantern Cafe", headline "Scratch, sip, smile." and a line explaining there are three tickets today. A row (wrapping) of three ticket cards, each with a heading (Ticket 1, 2, 3), a prize area under the foil (Free flat white; 10% off your next bag; Try again tomorrow - one of the three is a no-prize), a small terms line ("Valid until Sunday. One per customer."), a live progress readout ("Scratched 0%") with a thin bar, and buttons: Reveal prize and Reset. Below the row, a summary line "Tickets scratched: 0 of 3" and a "Reset all" link.

Interaction: Pointer (mouse, touch, pen) drags on the canvas erase foil with a soft round brush (24px radius, destination-out) with smooth interpolation between pointer samples; touch-action:none on the canvas while scratching but page scroll remains possible outside. Progress is sampled by counting transparent pixels on a coarse grid every 120ms; at 55% the remaining foil fades out (400ms) and the prize animates in (scale 0.9 to 1) with a confetti burst (60 particles on a second canvas, 1.2s, skipped when reduced motion is preferred or the prize is "Try again"). The prize text is always in the DOM but marked aria-hidden until revealed; the "Reveal prize" button (real button, always focusable) reveals instantly and announces the prize in a polite live region ("Ticket 2: 10% off your next bag"). Keyboard scratching: the foil area is focusable (tabindex 0, role="img" with a label "Scratch foil, use arrow keys to scratch or press Reveal"); a coin cursor appears, arrow keys move it 20px and scratch, Shift multiplies the step by 3. Reset restores the foil with a quick re-coat animation and reshuffles nothing (prizes are fixed per ticket). Scratch state is not persisted.

Responsive: Cards wrap to a single column under 700px (full width, max 340px, centred); canvases are resized with devicePixelRatio and on resize; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffd36e outline offset 3px. The prize must never be conveyed only by the canvas (it is real text under it). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no confetti, no fades; reveal is instant). Invented names only.
