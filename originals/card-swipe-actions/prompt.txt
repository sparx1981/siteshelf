Canvas & base: Soft cream page #f7f3ea; the list sits in a 440px-wide phone-style panel (#fffdf8, 28px radius, 1px #e4dccb border, soft shadow). Message cards are white with 1px #ece4d3 borders, 18px radius. Action backplates: Snooze amber #ffb020 (dark text #2a1a00, 9:1) and Archive teal #0f8a73 (white 4.9:1). Unread cards show a 6px indigo dot #4a3df0. Ink #1d1a14 (16:1), secondary #6a6354 (5.8:1). Invented mail app "Postbox".

Typography: "Figtree" 500/600/700/800 for sender names, subjects and actions (16px names, 15px previews); "Inter" 400/500 for small meta (13px).

Layout: Page header with eyebrow "Postbox", headline "Clear your inbox with a flick." and a short line, and a legend "Swipe left on a message, or press the Actions button". The panel header shows "Inbox", an unread count badge and a "Reset demo" link. Below it a list of eight message cards (invented senders: Wren Valley Council, Kestrel Rail, Odette Marlow, Lantern Cove Library, Marlow & Pine, Ines Calloway, Saltmarsh Gardeners, Starling Almanac) each with a 44px initials avatar, sender, time, a bold subject, a two-line preview, an unread dot and a small "Actions" icon button (three dots, 44px target) at the right. Behind each card, aligned right, sit two 88px-wide action plates: Snooze (clock icon) then Archive (box icon).

Interaction: Dragging a card horizontally with a pointer or finger translates it (pointer events with setPointerCapture, vertical scroll still works via touch-action:pan-y); releasing past -60px snaps it open to -176px showing both actions (spring 320ms); dragging beyond -260px and releasing commits Archive (the card slides out, the list collapses with a 240ms height transition) and an undo toast appears for 5 seconds ("Archived 'Pool lane closure' - Undo"). Dragging right closes an open card. Only one card is open at a time. Alternatives without gestures: the Actions button toggles the same open state (aria-expanded), the plates are real buttons that become focusable only when open, and keyboard shortcuts on a focused card: Enter opens details (toggle a read state), E archives, S opens the snooze menu, ArrowLeft opens the actions, ArrowRight or Escape closes. Snooze opens a small popover with Later today, Tomorrow morning and Next week, which sets a snooze badge on the card and removes the unread dot. Archiving and snoozing announce via an aria-live region. Undo restores the card at its original position with a reverse animation.

Responsive: Under 480px the panel is full-bleed width with 12px side padding and the action plates are 80px wide; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #4a3df0 offset 3px on cards, buttons and menu items. Cards are list items containing buttons (no nested interactive roles beyond real buttons). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no spring, no collapse animation; state changes instantly). Invented names and messages only.
