Canvas & base: Soft slate page #e9edf3 with a faint radial highlight. Phone-width wallet panel 400px wide (#f6f8fb, 28px radius, 1px #d8dfe9 border, deep soft shadow). Four invented bank cards with gradient faces: Everyday (#2457ff to #5d8bff), Savings (#0e8f6c to #35c79a), Travel (#ff7a45 to #ffb84d, dark text) and Business (#1b1f2a to #3b4256). Card text white (or dark on Travel) at 4.5:1 or better. Panel text #151a24, secondary #5b6678 (5.9:1). Frozen overlay: frosted white at 55% with a snowflake icon and diagonal ice lines. Invented bank "Pocketbank".

Typography: "Manrope" 600/700/800 for headings, balances and card names; "IBM Plex Mono" 400/500 for card numbers and amounts (14 to 16px).

Layout: Left column headline "Your cards, neatly stacked." with a short paragraph (desktop) and the wallet panel on the right. The wallet shows a header ("Wallet", total balance £8,420.50 and an add button), then the card stack: four 200px-tall cards with 22px radius, chip, contactless icon, masked number "•••• 4821", name, expiry and an invented network mark (two overlapping circles). Collapsed cards show only a 64px strip (name, last four, balance) overlapped like a real wallet; the expanded card shows its full face. Below the stack a details panel for the expanded card: balance, available credit or savings goal, a "Freeze card" switch row, a "Show number" button, a "Copy number" button and a list of three recent transactions (invented merchants such as Lantern Bakery, Kestrel Rail, Wren Books) with amounts.

Interaction: Clicking or pressing Enter on a collapsed card expands it: the cards re-flow with a 420ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.1,.4,1) transition of their top offsets (the expanded one is full height, others strips), the details panel cross-fades to the new card's data. Only one card is expanded at a time; clicking the expanded card again collapses all to an even fan. Arrow Up/Down move focus between cards; Home/End jump; each card is a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the details region (role="region" aria-live="polite"). The Freeze switch (role="switch") toggles a frosted overlay on that card with a 300ms fade and a "Frozen" badge; frozen state persists per card in memory and in localStorage (try/catch). Show number swaps the masked number for a full fictional number (4821 0000 0000 4821 style but invented) for 8 seconds then masks again; Copy number uses clipboard with try/catch and a polite "Copied" message.

Responsive: Under 900px the headline sits above the panel; under 440px the panel is full width with 16px padding and card text scales; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #151a24 with white offset (white on dark cards). Do not rely on colour only for the frozen state (badge and icon). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant re-flow and fades). Invented names and numbers only; no real network logos.
