Canvas & base: Warm market page #f9f5ec with a white cart panel (#ffffff, 1px #e8e0cd border, 24px radius). Ink #25201a (15:1), secondary #6b6253 (5.6:1). Stepper control: pill #f3ecda with 1px #d9cfb3 border; buttons #25201a (white icons); disabled-at-limit state uses aria-disabled with 40% opacity and a not-allowed cursor. Accent leaf green #2f7d4f, warning amber #b45f06 (on #fff4e0), delete red #c0352b. Invented grocer "Fernhill Market".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for headings and prices (price 20px); "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for UI (15 to 16px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Fernhill Market", headline "Your basket, your pace." and a line. A two-column layout: left the cart list, right an order summary card. Cart rows (4 items: Sourdough loaf 8 in stock, Heirloom tomatoes 5, Free-range eggs 12, Oat milk 3) each with a 72px generated SVG product tile, name, unit price and weight, a stepper (minus, editable number input, plus; 44px buttons) and a line total. Under each stepper a stock hint ("8 in stock") that turns amber when the quantity reaches the limit. The summary shows subtotal, delivery (free over £40) with a progress bar ("Add £6.30 for free delivery"), total, and a Checkout button; an empty state appears when all items are removed ("Your basket is empty" with an Undo link).

Interaction: Clicking plus or minus changes quantity by 1; pressing and holding repeats: after 400ms it steps every 160ms, accelerating to every 60ms after 12 steps (pointerdown starts, pointerup/leave/cancel and blur stop; keyboard holding Enter or Space on a button also repeats using the same logic with e.repeat ignored natively). At the maximum the plus button gets aria-disabled="true", the control shakes horizontally (4px, 3 cycles, 300ms), the stock hint pulses amber and an aria-live message says "Only 8 in stock". At quantity 1 the minus button becomes a trash icon (aria-label "Remove Sourdough loaf") and pressing it removes the row with a collapse animation and an Undo toast (5s). The number field accepts typing (digits only, sanitised on input, committed on blur or Enter, clamped to 1..stock with a polite message when clamped); ArrowUp/ArrowDown inside the field change by 1 (Shift by 5). All totals recalculate instantly with the number rolling (150ms) and the delivery bar animating; currency with Intl.NumberFormat GBP.

Responsive: Under 860px the summary moves below the list; rows stack the stepper under the name under 520px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Use aria-disabled (not disabled) so focus is not lost at limits. Visible focus 3px #2f7d4f with white offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shake or collapse animation; messages carry the feedback). Invented names only.
