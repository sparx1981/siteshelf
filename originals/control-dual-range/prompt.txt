Canvas & base: Soft warm page #faf6ef with a white filter panel (#ffffff, 1px #eadfca border, 24px radius, soft shadow). Ink #2a2218 (15:1), secondary #6d6250 (5.7:1). Range accent terracotta #c2522d (white text on it 4.6:1). Histogram bars inside the selected range are terracotta, outside are #e5d9c3. Track #e5d9c3, selected segment terracotta. Handles are 28px white circles with a 3px terracotta border and soft shadow. Invented rental site "Hearthstone Stays".

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the panel heading and the big price range (heading 26px, range readout 30px); "Karla" 400/500/700 for labels, inputs and results (15 to 16px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Hearthstone Stays", headline "What does a good night cost?" and a line. A filter panel (max 560px) containing: the heading "Price per night", a live readout "£80 – £420", a histogram of 28 bars (80px tall) representing 600 invented listings, directly above a dual-handle track (the bars align to the track), two number fields (Minimum, Maximum) with a £ prefix and "min" and "max" captions, a results line "212 stays in this range" with the average price, and Reset and "Show stays" buttons. Below the panel, a short grid of three sample stay cards that filter live (cottage, treehouse, lighthouse keeper's rooms with prices) to show effect: cards outside the range dim and are marked "Out of range".

Interaction: Handles are role="slider" elements with tabindex 0, aria-orientation horizontal, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax and aria-valuenow reflecting their own bounds (min handle max = max value minus gap, max handle min = min value plus gap), aria-valuetext "£80 per night", aria-label "Minimum price" and "Maximum price". Pointer drag with setPointerCapture moves a handle in £10 steps (snapping), clicking the track moves the nearest handle there; handles show a floating value bubble above while dragged or focused. Keyboard: Left/Down -10, Right/Up +10, Shift +/- 50 (PageUp/PageDown also 50), Home/End jump to bounds. Minimum gap £20 so handles never cross; when both handles are at the same end the one that can move gets the higher z-index so it is never stuck. The number fields commit on blur or Enter with clamping and a polite message. The histogram bar highlight, count and average update immediately; the count uses a rolling number animation (150ms).

Responsive: Under 560px the panel is full width with 16px padding and the number fields stack; handles keep a 44px invisible hit area; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #2a2218 ring with white inner ring around the focused handle. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no value bubble transition, no rolling number). Invented names only.
