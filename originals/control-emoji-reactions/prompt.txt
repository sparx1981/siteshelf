Canvas & base: Soft social-feed page #f0f2f8 with white post cards (#ffffff, 1px #dfe3ef border, 20px radius) in a 620px column. Ink #1b2133 (15:1), secondary #5a6480 (5.9:1). Reaction chips: #f3f5fb with 1px #dfe3ef border (pill), selected chips tinted by the reaction colour at 14% with a 1.5px border in that colour. Reaction colours: Like blue #2f6bff, Love red #ff3b5c, Laugh amber #f2a900, Wow violet #8a5cff, Sad teal #2a9d9a, Celebrate orange #ff7a2f (all used only with dark text on tints, 4.5:1 minimum). Invented community app "Huddle Square".

Typography: "Figtree" 500/600/700/800 for names, counts and headings (16px, counts 14px tabular); "Inter" 400/500 for post text (16px, 1.55).

Layout: Header "Huddle Square" with eyebrow, headline "React with a flourish." and a line. Three post cards (invented authors Odette Marlow, Kwame Boateng and Ines Calloway) each with a 44px SVG avatar, name, time, a short post (a recipe win, a lost cat found, a sunrise photo described with a generated SVG illustration), and a reaction bar: up to six reaction chips (icon plus count) showing only those with counts above zero, then an "Add reaction" button (smiley with plus) that opens the picker. The picker is a rounded popover with six 44px reaction buttons drawn as inline SVG icons (Like thumb, Love heart, Laugh face, Wow face, Sad face, Celebrate star burst), each with a tooltip label below on hover or focus.

Interaction: Clicking a chip toggles your own reaction of that type (count +1 or -1) with a pop animation (scale 1 to 1.35 to 1, 260ms) and six tiny floating copies of the icon that rise and fade (700ms). Clicking "Add reaction" opens the picker; long-pressing (500ms) any chip also opens the picker anchored to it with a short grow animation; selecting a reaction in the picker adds or toggles it and closes the picker. A user can hold only one reaction per post (choosing another switches it, decrementing the old count). Keyboard: chips and the add button are real buttons with aria-pressed and aria-label ("Love, 12 reactions, you reacted"); Enter or Space toggles; ArrowUp or ContextMenu or Shift+Enter on a chip opens the picker; inside the picker ArrowLeft/Right move (wrapping), Home/End jump, Enter/Space chooses, Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger; outside click closes (event.composedPath). Counts have a rolling number animation (120ms). A polite live region says "You reacted with Love. 13 reactions."

Responsive: Under 520px the bar wraps onto two lines, chips keep 40px height and the picker is a bottom-sheet-style row (full width, 6 buttons evenly spaced); no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images (reaction icons are inline SVG, not emoji characters, so they render everywhere). [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1b2133 with white offset. Pressed state is shown by fill, border and aria-pressed (not colour alone: the selected icon also gets a ring). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pop, floating icons or long-press growth). Invented names only.
