Knob caps are machined aluminium: conic-gradient from #e9e9ee through #8e8e98 with a bevelled rim and a 3px indicator line in amber #ffb020. Value arcs glow amber (#ffb020) for Drive, cyan #3fe0ff for Tone, green #5df29a for Volume and magenta #ff5bd0 for Reverb. Labels #e8e8ee (14:1), secondary #9a9aa6 (6.7:1). Invented audio brand "Tonewheel Audio".

Typography: "Oswald" 500/600/700 for knob labels and the pedal name (uppercase, tracking .08em); "Inconsolata" 400/600 for numeric readouts and the waveform scale (15 to 18px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Tonewheel Audio", headline "Turn it up. Gently." and a short line. A pedal-shaped panel (max 760px) with a name plate "BLOOM / OVERDRIVE", a live waveform screen (an inline canvas 100% by 120px with a soft green phosphor look) and a row of four 120px knobs: Drive, Tone, Volume, Reverb. Each knob has 11 tick marks around a 270-degree sweep (from 7 o'clock to 5 o'clock), numerals 0, 5 and 10 at the ends and centre, an arc track with the value arc glowing, and a digital readout below ("6.4"). Under the panel, a "Presets" row of buttons (Clean, Crunch, Lead, Ambient) and a "Reset all" button, plus hint text about keys.

Interaction: Pointer drag rotates by angle: the knob measures the pointer angle around its centre and updates the value smoothly (clamped to 0 to 10 across the 270-degree sweep with no wrap-around jump; the drag begins from the current value without snapping); holding Shift while dragging gives 5x finer control; the wheel changes the value by 0.1 per notch (Shift 0.02); double-click or double-tap resets to the default. Keyboard (each knob is role="slider" tabindex 0, aria-valuemin 0, aria-valuemax 10, aria-valuenow, aria-valuetext "6.4 out of 10", aria-label): Arrow keys change by 0.1 (Shift 1.0), PageUp/PageDown by 1.0, Home and End jump to 0 and 10, Escape resets. Rotation animates with a 120ms ease when changed by keys or presets, and instantly when dragged. The waveform screen redraws on every change: Drive shapes soft clipping of a sine wave, Tone adds harmonics (brightness), Volume scales amplitude, Reverb adds a decaying echo tail on the right. Presets animate all knobs over 400ms. A polite live region reports "Drive 6.4 out of 10" after changes settle for 300ms.

Responsive: Knobs shrink to 84px under 760px and wrap to a 2 by 2 grid under 480px; the waveform keeps full width; no horizontal scroll at 390px.
