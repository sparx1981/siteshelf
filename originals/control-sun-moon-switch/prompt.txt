Canvas & base: The whole page is themed by a data-theme attribute. Light theme: page #f6f9ff, card #ffffff, ink #14203a (15:1), secondary #4a5a7a (6.8:1), accent #ff9a1f. Dark theme: page #0b1022, card #141b36, ink #eaf0ff (14:1), secondary #9fb0d9 (7.5:1), accent #ffd27a. Theme change cross-fades all colours over 400ms. The switch track is a sky: day gradient (#6cc4ff to #bfe7ff) and night gradient (#10163a to #2a2f6b). Invented weather app "Skyline Daily".

Typography: "Sora" 600/700/800 for headings and the forecast numerals (headline clamp 34px to 56px); "Inter" 400/500 for body (15 to 17px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Skyline Daily", headline "Day, meet night." and a line. A large switch (176px by 80px, fully rounded) centred in a feature row, with caption "Light" and "Dark" labels either side. Inside the track: a 60px sun (radial gradient yellow to orange with soft halo rings) that slides to the right and transforms into a pale moon (#e9edf7 with three darker crater circles and a crescent shadow) while three white clouds drift down and away and ten stars fade and scale in with a staggered twinkle. Below the switch: two smaller variants side by side (a 112px by 52px compact version and a 52px round icon button version where the icon morphs), and a sample forecast card ("Tonight in Pennick Bay", 11°, clear skies, hourly strip of six items with tiny SVG icons) that restyles with the theme. A short paragraph notes the preference is remembered.

Interaction: All variants are real buttons with role="switch" and aria-checked (true = dark) and a visible label (aria-label "Dark mode"). Click, Space or Enter toggles; Left arrow sets light, Right arrow sets dark. The sun to moon morph runs over 600ms with cubic-bezier(.5,.1,.2,1): the knob translates, the moon shadow circle slides over the sun to create a crescent, clouds translate down 60% and fade, stars fade in with 40ms stagger and continuous 3s twinkle. Preference loads from localStorage ("sl-theme") in try/catch, falling back to prefers-color-scheme; changes to the system preference update the theme only if the user has not chosen explicitly. All switches stay in sync. The document colour-scheme meta and the theme-color meta update. A polite live region announces "Dark mode on" or "Light mode on".

Responsive: Under 560px the big switch scales to 144px by 66px, variants stack, the forecast strip scrolls within its container only; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px outline in the current accent with an inner ring offset 3px. State is never conveyed by colour alone (icon morph and the labels). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant swap, stars static, clouds hidden or shown without drifting). Invented names only.
