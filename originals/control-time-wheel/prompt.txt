Canvas & base: Calm dawn page with a vertical gradient from #1b1f4a (top) to #f08a6b (bottom, low horizon) and a soft sun disc behind the picker; the picker card is frosted dark glass rgba(16,18,48,.72) with backdrop blur 14px, 1px rgba(255,255,255,.18) border and 28px radius. Selected row band is white at 14% with 1px white at 40% rules. Text #f5f2ff (14:1), dimmed rows at 45% opacity, accent amber #ffc872 for the AM/PM selection and the Set button (dark text #2a1a00). Invented app "Daybreak Alarm".

Typography: "Outfit" 300/500/700 for the large wheel numerals (36px, selected 44px, tabular) and headings; "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for labels and buttons (15 to 17px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Daybreak Alarm", headline "When should we wake you?" and a line. The picker card (max 420px) contains a title "Set alarm", a row of three wheels side by side: Hours (1 to 12, or 00 to 23 in 24-hour mode), Minutes (00 to 59) and AM/PM (hidden in 24-hour mode), each wheel 200px tall showing five rows (row height 40px) with the selected row in the centre between two hairlines, and top and bottom fade masks. A colon between hours and minutes. Beneath: a summary line "Alarm for 7:30 AM, in 8 hours 12 minutes", a 12h/24h toggle (segmented), a day chooser (Mon to Sun small circles toggling, weekdays pre-selected) and a primary "Set alarm" button, plus a "Saved alarms" mini-list showing the last three set alarms.

Interaction: Each wheel is a scroll container with CSS scroll-snap-type y mandatory and scroll-snap-align center on rows, padded so the first and last rows can reach the centre; scrolling (wheel, drag, touch, keyboard) updates the selected value on scroll end (debounced 80ms) and styles rows by distance from the centre (opacity and scale). Clicking a row scrolls it to the centre. Each wheel has role="spinbutton" tabindex 0 with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow, aria-valuetext ("7 hours", "30 minutes", "AM") and aria-label; ArrowUp/ArrowDown change by 1, PageUp/PageDown by 5 (hours 3), Home/End jump to the ends, and typing digits jumps to a value (two digits for minutes, with a 1.2s buffer). AM/PM toggles with ArrowUp/Down, the letters A and P. Hours wrap from 12 to 1 and minutes wrap 59 to 00 when stepping by keyboard (scroll position follows with a smooth scroll). The summary line updates with a computed time until the next occurrence and is announced politely when the selection settles. Set alarm adds an entry to the Saved list (localStorage in try/catch) with a toggle switch to enable or disable each and a delete button.

Responsive: Under 420px the card is full width with 16px padding; wheel row height stays 40px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffc872 ring around the focused wheel. Do not rely on scroll-only interaction: all values reachable by keyboard and typing. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (instant scroll jumps, no row scaling transitions). Invented names only.
