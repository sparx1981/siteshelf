Interaction: Accordion modules open with animated height (one or several open), with an Expand all / Collapse all button and a running total of lessons and hours. Reviews carousel has previous/next buttons, dots, swipe, and keyboard arrows, pausing autoplay on hover/focus and never autoplaying under reduced motion. The preview button opens a native dialog with a fake video player (play/pause toggling a progress bar). Enrol shows a toast.

Responsive: Pricing card moves above the curriculum on mobile; hero stacks; no horizontal overflow at 390px.
