Canvas & base: Soft sky gradient page (linear-gradient 180deg #eaf4fb to #f7fafc) with white tiles (1px #dbe7f1 border, radius 20px, soft shadow). Ink #11263a, secondary #526a80 (6:1). AQI bands with names and distinct text labels: Good (0 to 50) #2bb673, Moderate (51 to 100) #e6c229, Unhealthy for sensitive groups (101 to 150) #f08a24, Unhealthy (151 to 200) #e0483f, Very unhealthy (201 to 300) #8e3fbf. Band colours use dark text #1a1200 on yellow and orange and white text on the rest for contrast. Invented network "Clearair" with stations in a fictional city "Marlowe".

Typography: "Manrope" 600/700/800 for headings and AQI numbers (tile AQI 44px 800; page title 28px); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 14px.

Layout: Header with wordmark "clearair", a city selector (Marlowe, Northgate, Harbourside; segmented), a pollutant filter (AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃; segmented) and a last-updated chip with a pulsing dot ("Updated 12 s ago"). Hero strip: citywide AQI as a big semicircular gauge (SVG arc with band gradient and a needle that animates to the value), the band name, and a health advice card tailored to the band (for Moderate: "Unusually sensitive people should reduce long outdoor exertion", with icons as text). Below, a grid of 8 station tiles per city (e.g. "Riverside Park", "Market Square", "Ring Road North"). Each tile shows station name, a small coloured AQI circle number with band label, a mini gauge arc, three pollutant bars (PM2.5, NO₂, O₃ with µg/m³ values and WHO guideline tick), and a 24-hour sparkline (SVG polyline with a dot at now and hover/keyboard readout). Selecting the pollutant filter recolours tiles by that pollutant's own index and changes the sparkline data. Clicking a tile expands it into a modal-like detail (use native dialog) with a larger 24 hour chart, forecast for the next 6 hours (bars) and an "Alert me" toggle.

Interaction: Readings drift every 5 seconds by small seeded random steps (animated number tween and needle motion); tiles whose band changes flash a subtle outline once and announce "Riverside Park is now Unhealthy for sensitive groups" in an aria-live region (max once per 10 s). The sparkline is focusable: Left/Right arrows move a readout through hours.

Responsive: Tile grid 4 columns, 2 columns under 1000px, 1 column under 560px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Every band is named in text beside the colour; charts have aria-labels and text values; native dialog gives focus trapping; visible focus 3px #11263a with white halo. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no needle sweep, pulse or flash). Invented names only.
