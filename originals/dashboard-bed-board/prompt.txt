Canvas & base: Clinical light page #f1f6f7, white panels (1px #d7e4e6 border, radius 16px), ink #12292e, secondary #4f6a70 (6:1), teal #0e7c86 primary. Bed status colours with text and icon labels: Occupied #0e7c86 (white text), Discharge pending #e08a00 (dark text #2b1a00), Cleaning #6b5bd6 (white text), Ready #2f9e5a (white text), Blocked #6b7a80 (white text). Invented hospital ward "Ward 6B, Marlowe General" (28 beds in four bays).

Typography: "Sora" 600/700 for headings and numbers (title 26px 700, counts 30px 700); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 14px and bed labels.

Layout: Top bar with ward name, shift chip "Day shift, 07:00 to 19:30", live clock and a Print handover button (toast only). Left main area: a status filter row of chips with counts (All 24, Occupied, Discharge pending, Cleaning, Ready, Blocked; aria-pressed) and the ward map: four bays (Bay A, B, C, D) each a rounded card titled with the bay name and a 2 by 3 grid of bed tiles (24 beds total, plus 4 side rooms as larger tiles in a fifth "Side rooms" group). Each tile shows bed number, an icon and status word, and for occupied beds patient initials (invented, such as "A.K.") with expected discharge ("EDD Tue"). Right sticky panel: occupancy ring (SVG, percentage and "17 of 24 occupied"), live status counts and, when a bed is selected, a "Bed 7" detail card with status, patient initials, age band, notes and action buttons that depend on status: Occupied -> "Mark ready for discharge"; Discharge pending -> "Patient left, send for cleaning"; Cleaning -> "Cleaning complete"; Ready -> "Admit patient" (assigns invented initials) and "Block bed"; Blocked -> "Unblock". Each action moves the bed to the next status with an animated tile transition (scale pop 300ms) and updates counts and ring.

Interaction: Filter chips dim non-matching tiles to 20% opacity; tiles are buttons in a roving-tabindex grid (arrow keys move across the ward, Enter selects). A log list under the detail card records the last 5 changes with times ("09:42 Bed 7 sent for cleaning").

Responsive: Under 1000px the right panel moves below the ward; bays become 2 columns then 1 under 560px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Every status has an icon and text, aria-live for changes, visible focus 3px #12292e with white halo. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pop). Invented initials and names only.
