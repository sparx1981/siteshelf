Canvas & base: Deep navy dashboard page #0c1424 with panels #121c33 (1px #223056 border, radius 18px). Text #e8eefc, secondary #9aa9cc (7:1). Tariff bands: Off-peak #3b82f6 (00:00 to 06:00 and 22:00 to 24:00), Standard #5eead4, Peak #fbbf24 (16:00 to 19:00). Accent yellow #ffd23f for the logo glow and primary actions, saving green #4ade80. Invented product "Ampere Home".

Typography: "Manrope" 600/700/800 for headings and big numbers (KPI 36px 800); "DM Mono" 400/500 for axis labels, tooltips and times (12px); "Inter" is not used. Page title: "Today's energy".

Layout: Top bar with a lightning-bolt glyph logo "Ampere", a range toggle (Today, 7 days) as segmented buttons, and a profile chip. KPI row of four cards: "Used today 14.9 kWh" with a delta chip, "Cost so far £3.34", "Peak usage 3.1 kW at 18:00" and "Carbon 2.9 kg CO₂". Main panel "Hourly usage" with 24 vertical bars (CSS, height proportional to kWh), each coloured by its tariff band, a legend (Off-peak, Standard, Peak), a dashed line marking "now" and a y-axis with 0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 kWh labels. Hovering or focusing a bar shows a tooltip ("18:00 · 1.4 kWh · £0.42 · Peak rate 38p"); bars are buttons navigable with arrow keys. Second panel "Where it goes": an SVG donut of appliances (Heating 38%, Hot water 18%, Cooking 12%, Laundry 9%, Lighting 8%, Other 15%) with a centre label showing the selected slice or the total; legend items are toggle buttons that dim the slice and update the percentages for the remaining items. Third panel "Save money": a suggestion card "Run the dishwasher and washing machine after 22:00" showing the saving ("About £4.60 a month") with a "Schedule it" button that turns into "Scheduled for 22:30" and animates a small arrow moving the load from a peak bar to an off-peak bar in the chart (bar heights change smoothly and cost updates).

Interaction: Today shows 24 bars; 7 days switches to 7 grouped daily bars with peak share split (animated 400ms). Tooltips follow focus and hover without rebuilding the DOM. The donut slices grow on load (stroke-dasharray animation 700ms). Cost counters tween on change.

Responsive: Under 900px KPIs go 2x2, panels stack; chart bars narrow and axis labels show every 6th hour; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Chart bars are buttons with aria-labels; band meaning is also written in the tooltip and legend (not colour alone); visible focus 3px #ffd23f. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no growth animation). Invented names only.
