Canvas & base: Graphite UI #0f1318 with panels #171d25 (1px #2a3340 border, radius 16px). Text #e9eef5, secondary #98a6b8 (7:1). Status colours: Moving #38d39f, Idle #ffb454, Offline #8a95a6, Alert #ff6b6b. Map surface #0b1016 with roads #26313f (8px) and arterial roads #35445a (12px), a river #12304a and park blocks #12261f. Selected route highlighted #38d39f at 4px with a glow. Invented platform "Convoy" for a fictional courier firm "Northline Couriers".

Typography: "Space Grotesk" 500/600/700 for headings and numbers (title 28px 700); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 14px; "Space Grotesk" tabular numerals for speeds and ETAs.

Layout: Top bar with wordmark "convoy", live clock, and a "12 vehicles live" pill with a pulsing dot. Three regions: left list panel (360px), centre map, right detail card (320px) on wide screens (stacked on narrower). The list panel has filter chips (All 12, Moving 7, Idle 3, Offline 2; aria-pressed), a sort select (ETA soonest, Name, Fuel low first) and 12 vehicle rows (van icon in a circle coloured by status, vehicle name like "VAN 07 · Priya", plate "NL 07 KTD", next stop or "Parked at depot", ETA "14 min", fuel bar). Selected row has a green left border. The map is an inline SVG (viewBox 800x520) with a hand-drawn city: river, park blocks, grid of local roads, four arterial routes, six depot/stop markers with labels, and the vehicles as 14px circles (status coloured with white ring) travelling along paths using JS (getPointAtLength) at their own speeds; moving vehicles update every 50ms smoothly. Clicking a marker or a row selects it: map highlights its route and pans slightly (viewBox transition 400ms) and the detail card fills: driver name with initials avatar, vehicle, speed (km/h with tween), fuel %, today's stops completed (4 of 9) as a progress bar, next stop and ETA, and quick actions Call driver and Share ETA (buttons that show a toast).

Interaction: Filtering hides rows and map markers accordingly (opacity .15 on the map for non-matching). Sorting reorders the list with a quick FLIP animation. Keyboard: list rows are buttons (Up/Down move focus, Enter selects), map markers are focusable with aria-labels. Idle and offline vehicles do not move; offline markers have a dashed ring. One vehicle shows an Alert (late by 12 min) with a red badge and shakes slightly once on load.

Responsive: Under 1100px the map goes above the list; under 700px the detail card becomes a bottom sheet style panel under the map; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Status is shown by text and icon as well as colour; map has an aria-label summary and a visually hidden list; visible focus 3px #38d39f. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (vehicles hop to new positions every 2s, no pulse). Invented names only.
