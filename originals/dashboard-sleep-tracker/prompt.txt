Canvas & base: Deep night purple page #0f0b24 with a faint star-dust gradient, panels #181238 (1px #2d2466 border, radius 20px). Text #efeaff, secondary #a99fd6 (7:1). Stage colours with labels: Awake #ff8fa3, REM #7dd3fc, Light #a78bfa, Deep #4338ca. Accent moon yellow #ffe08a. Invented app "Dozeful".

Typography: "Outfit" 500/600/700 for headings and numbers (score 56px 700); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 14px; "Inter" tabular numerals for times.

Layout: Top bar with a crescent-moon wordmark "dozeful" and a profile chip. Title "Last night" with the date and a night selector: seven small day chips (Mon to Sun with scores) as a tablist. Grid: Row 1 left: score card with an SVG ring (0 to 100) animating its stroke to the night's score (e.g. 82) with the label "Good" and a one-line summary ("You slept 7h 24m and woke 3 times"); Row 1 right: stat tiles (Time asleep, Bedtime, Wake time, Sleep efficiency). Row 2: a wide panel "Sleep stages" with a hypnogram: an SVG stepped line over a time axis (22:30 to 06:30) with four horizontal bands labelled Awake, REM, Light, Deep; the area under steps filled by stage colour; hovering or arrow-key focus shows a tooltip with the time range and stage. Row 3: left "Stage breakdown" with four horizontal bars (percentage and duration) and target markers; right "Bedtime consistency" a week chart: for each of 7 nights a vertical capsule from bedtime to wake time on a 21:00 to 08:00 axis with a shaded band for the 7-day average bedtime and wake window, and a note "Your bedtime varied by 46 minutes this week". An insights list with three tips that depend on the selected night (e.g. low deep sleep suggests a cooler room).

Interaction: Selecting a night regenerates the hypnogram from a seeded generator (so each night is stable) and animates the ring, stat counters and breakdown bars (500ms). Hypnogram is built from 30-minute blocks with plausible cycles (Light, Deep, Light, REM repeating, longer Deep early, longer REM late) and random brief awakenings. Tab keys: Night chips follow ARIA tabs (arrows); hypnogram is focusable and arrow keys step by 30 minutes.

Responsive: Under 900px panels stack; hypnogram height 180px; week chart labels abbreviated; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Stages are named in labels and tooltips (not colour alone); the hypnogram has a text summary in aria-label; visible focus 3px #ffe08a. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no ring or bar animation). Invented names only.
