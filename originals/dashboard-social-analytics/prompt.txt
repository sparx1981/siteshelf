Canvas & base: Warm light page #fbf6f1 with white panels (1px #eadfd3 border, radius 18px), ink #231a2e, secondary #6b5d78 (5.9:1), brand magenta #d6246e, accent orange #ff8a3d, plum #5b2a86, up green #1a9c63, down red #d1344a. Invented analytics tool "Chirpwise" covering three invented networks: "Loop", "Frame" and "Chirp" (colours #5b2a86, #ff8a3d, #1fa3a3).

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans" 600/700/800 for headings and numbers (KPI 32px 800; page title 28px); "DM Mono" 400 for axis labels and tooltip numbers (12px); body 14px Plus Jakarta Sans 500.

Layout: Top bar with wordmark "chirpwise", a date preset segmented control (7 days, 28 days, 90 days) and an Export button. Network chips row (All networks, Loop, Frame, Chirp; aria-pressed). Four KPI cards: Reach, Engagement rate, New followers, Link clicks, each with value, percentage delta chip vs previous period (green or red with arrow and text) and a 40px sparkline. Main panel "Reach over time" with an SVG area chart (gradient magenta to transparent), gridlines, x-axis date labels, a dashed grey line for the previous period (toggle "Compare to previous period" switch), and a crosshair with tooltip showing date, reach and previous value. Below, a "Top posts" table with columns Post (title and network dot), Published, Reach, Engagement, Clicks; headers are sort buttons (aria-sort) and each row shows a small inline bar for reach. 10 realistic invented posts (for example "Behind the scenes: our spring launch", "5 tips for tidy spreadsheets", "We're hiring a community lead").

Interaction: Changing preset or network regenerates data with a seeded random so the same selection gives the same numbers; the chart morphs by animating the path (400ms) and KPIs count to the new values. Hover or touch moves the crosshair; the chart is focusable and Left/Right arrows move it day by day, Home/End jump to ends, announcing the value in aria-live. Table sorting toggles ascending and descending with arrow icons. Export shows a toast "Report ready" only.

Responsive: Under 900px KPIs become 2 columns; table scrolls inside its own container; chart height 220px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Chart has role=img with aria-label summary and a visually hidden data table; delta chips include words (up/down); visible focus 3px #d6246e. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no morph or counting). Invented names only.
