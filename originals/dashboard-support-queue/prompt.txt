Canvas & base: Light cool page #f2f5fa, white panels (1px #dce3ef border, radius 14px), ink #15203a, secondary #56647f (6:1), brand blue #2f5bea. Priority colours with text: Urgent #d92d3a, High #e8710a (dark text where needed), Normal #2f5bea, Low #6a7690. SLA states: ok #14935f, at risk #c77a00, breached #d92d3a. Invented help-desk "Helpline" for a fictional shop "Maple & Co".

Typography: "Figtree" 500/600/700 for UI and headings (page title 24px 700); numbers in "Figtree" tabular; body 14px.

Layout: Top bar with wordmark "helpline", a search field (filters tickets by subject or customer) and agent chip "You: Priya N.". KPI row: Open tickets, Median first response, SLA met today (percentage), Unassigned. Left filters sidebar (240px): status list (Open, Pending, On hold, Solved with counts), priority checkboxes, assignee select (Anyone, Me, Unassigned, each agent) and a "Saved views" list. Main: a table of 14 tickets with columns: Priority pill, Subject and customer, Channel icon+text (Email, Chat, Phone), Assignee (avatar initials or "Unassigned" button), SLA clock (a countdown such as "00:42:10 left" or "Breached 12m ago") with a small ring, and Updated. Sort by SLA soonest by default. Right panel "Agent load": horizontal bars per agent (open tickets vs capacity of 8) with colour stepping to amber at 6 and red at 8, and an "Assign to the least loaded" button. Clicking a ticket opens a right drawer with subject, customer details, last three messages in a conversation preview (customer bubble left, agent right), canned reply chips ("Send tracking link", "Offer refund", "Ask for photo") that insert text into a reply box, and buttons Assign, Set pending, Solve.

Interaction: SLA clocks tick every second (accelerated x30 toggle for demo) and change colour state at 20 minutes left and at breach, re-sorting when sorted by SLA. Assigning a ticket via the menu updates the agent load bars with an animated width. Filters and search update the table instantly with an aria-live result count. Table rows are keyboard focusable; Enter opens the drawer; Escape closes it and returns focus.

Responsive: Under 1100px the sidebar collapses into a "Filters" disclosure and the load panel moves below; the table becomes cards under 700px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Drawer is a native dialog or role=dialog with focus management; priority and SLA are conveyed by words as well as colour; visible focus 3px #2f5bea. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no ring or bar animation). Invented names; example addresses only.
