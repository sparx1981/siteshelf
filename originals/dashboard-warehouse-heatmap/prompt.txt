Canvas & base: Industrial charcoal page #14161a with panels #1c1f25 (1px #2c313a border, radius 14px). Text #eceff4, secondary #9aa3b2 (7:1). Heat scale from cool to hot with distinct lightness: #2a3a55 (very low), #3f6a8f, #4fa3a5, #9bd17a, #f5d34f, #f59f3a, #ee5a3a (very high). Accent safety yellow #ffd400. Invented operation "Stockroom" for the fictional distribution centre "Brightwater DC".

Typography: "Big Shoulders Display" 700/800 uppercase for headings and numbers (KPI 44px 800); "Work Sans" 400/500/600 for body 14px; "DM Mono" 400 for cell labels (11px).

Layout: Top bar with wordmark "STOCKROOM" in yellow, site chip "Brightwater DC · Bay 3", a shift selector (Morning 06 to 14, Afternoon 14 to 22, Night 22 to 06; segmented) and a metric selector (Picks per hour, Travel seconds per pick, Congestion; segmented). KPI row: total picks, average pick rate, busiest aisle, congestion hot spots. Main panel "Floor map": a warehouse plan with twelve aisles (A1 to A12) drawn as vertical lanes between racking, each lane made of 20 bay cells stacked top to bottom (like a 12 by 20 grid with gaps for cross-aisles after bay 7 and 14); each cell coloured by the selected metric using the heat scale; the dock doors drawn along the bottom and the pack stations at the top. A legend gradient with min and max values. Hover or focus a cell shows a tooltip with "Aisle A7, bay 12: 38 picks, SKU-4821 Oat cartons". Clicking an aisle label (or a cell) selects that aisle: the selected aisle gets a yellow outline and a right panel "Aisle A7" shows a 24-hour (or shift-hour) bar chart of picks per hour and its top three SKUs with counts. Below the map a "Slotting tips" list: three suggestions such as "Move SKU-4821 from A7 bay 12 to A2 bay 3 to cut travel by 14%" with an "Apply" toggle that updates the map (the moved bay cools down and the target warms slightly, animated).

Interaction: Changing shift or metric re-colours cells with a staggered wave (10ms per column) and animated KPI numbers. Cells are buttons within a grid supporting arrow keys. Data is deterministic via a seeded generator so selections are stable.

Responsive: Under 1000px the aisle panel moves below the map; cells shrink to 14px; labels abbreviate; no horizontal scroll at 390px (map scales down).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Each cell has an aria-label with its value; legend labelled; visible focus 3px #ffd400. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no wave). Invented names only.
