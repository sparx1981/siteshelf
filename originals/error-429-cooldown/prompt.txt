Cards rgba(255,255,255,.06) with 1px rgba(255,255,255,.14) border, radius 22px. Text #f1eeff, secondary #b5acdf (8:1). Accent violet #7c5cff, ring colours: remaining time gradient #7c5cff to #38e1c6, warning amber #ffb84d, success mint #38e1c6. Invented API product "Quillpost".

Typography: "Space Grotesk" 600/700 for headings and the big "429" (clamp 96px to 200px, line-height .9, tracking -.05em, gradient text from #7c5cff to #38e1c6); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 16px; "JetBrains Mono" 400 for request counts and headers at 13px.

Layout: Centred column (max 640px). Header strip with wordmark "quillpost". The big "429" with the ring behind it: an SVG circle ring (300px) whose stroke drains clockwise from full to empty over a 30 second cooldown, with the seconds remaining shown inside beneath "429" as "Try again in 24s". Title "Slow down a little." and body "You've sent 120 requests in the last minute. Your plan allows 100. Take a short breather and we'll let you back in." Below a request meter: a horizontal bar with a marker at the 100 limit and a fill at 120 coloured amber, label "120 / 100 requests this minute", which drains smoothly toward 0 during the cooldown. Controls: a switch "Retry automatically when the timer ends" (default on, role=switch), a primary button "Retry now" (disabled/aria-disabled until the timer ends, then enabled with a pulse) and a secondary "Upgrade plan" link. At zero with auto retry on, the page shows "Retrying…" for 1.2s then success state: a check, "You're back in. Requests are flowing again." and a "Go to dashboard" button; with auto off the Retry button pulses until pressed. A small "Why this happens" panel lists three tips (batch your calls, cache responses, use webhooks) with a copyable header example `Retry-After: 24` in mono with a "Copy" button (clipboard in try/catch).

Interaction: Timer ticks every second (smooth ring animation via CSS transition of stroke-dashoffset), the meter fill drains accordingly, text updates are announced politely every 10 seconds and at 0. A "Restart demo" link below resets everything to test again.

Responsive: Ring scales to 82vw max 300px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Time remaining is available as text (not only the ring); visible focus 3px #38e1c6. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no ring easing or pulse, ring jumps each second). Invented names only.
