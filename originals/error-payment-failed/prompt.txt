Canvas & base: Light warm page #faf8f5, white cards (1px #e8e1d6 border, radius 20px), ink #1e1b16, secondary #625a4d (6.2:1), reassurance green #1f7a4a, alert coral #c9402e (used for the status icon and small labels), brand teal #0f766e primary. Invented shop "Oakhill Home" with a basket of three items.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for headings (title clamp 32px to 52px); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 16px; "DM Mono" 400 for card numbers and reference at 14px.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Oakhill Home" and a secure-checkout chip. Two columns (main 1.4fr, summary 1fr). Main: a status card with a coral circle icon (an x inside, gentle shake once on load), title "Your payment didn't go through.", reassurance "You haven't been charged. Your basket is saved for 10:00." (live countdown) and a reference "Ref OH-7731-4A". Then a "What probably happened" list with three likely reasons (each a row with an icon and short text): "Your bank declined the payment, often a security check", "The card details didn't match (check the CVV and expiry)", "There wasn't enough available balance". Under it "Try again" with a tab-like choice of three methods (radiogroup): Another card, Digital wallet, Bank transfer. Another card shows a form: card number with automatic grouping and brand hint text, name, expiry (MM/YY) and CVC with validation; Digital wallet shows a single "Pay with wallet" button; Bank transfer shows account details with a copy button and a note "Orders ship after payment clears, usually in 1 working day". A primary button "Pay £184.50" processes for 1s, then randomly (first attempt) fails again with "Declined again, try another method" and on the second attempt succeeds with a success state. Right column summary: three items with CSS thumbnails, subtotal, delivery £4.50, total £184.50, and a "Need a hand?" card with a "Chat with us" button that opens a small chat panel (bottom right) with a greeting from invented agent "Priya" and quick replies ("My card was declined", "Change delivery address", "Speak to a person") that append canned answers with typing dots.

Interaction: Card number formatting (groups of 4), expiry slash insertion, inline errors (aria-invalid) on pay; countdown ticks each second and, at zero, shows "Your basket was released. Items are still available, restart checkout." with a button. Chat panel is a dialog-like region with focus management and Escape to close.

Responsive: Under 900px the summary moves below; chat panel becomes full width bottom sheet; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Status uses role=alert on load; fields labelled; visible focus 3px #0f766e. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shake or typing dots animation). Invented names; fake card numbers only (never real).
