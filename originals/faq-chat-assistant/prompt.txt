Canvas & base: Soft lilac page #f1eefb, chat window 560px wide with white surface, 24px radius and shadow, header band #2d1b69 with an avatar dot, user bubbles #5b3df5 with white text, bot bubbles #f3f1fb with ink #1b1535, secondary #6f6890. Invented company "Nimbus Mail" and bot "Pip".

Responsive: Window becomes full-height under 600px.

Rules: No libraries or external images, hidden attribute respected, 44px targets, reduced-motion disables bounce and smooth scroll, invented content.
