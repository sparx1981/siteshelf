Canvas & base: Dark slate page #0e1218, panels #151b24 with 1px #26303d borders and 14px radius, ink #e8edf5, secondary #9aa8bb (7:1), accent cyan #4cc9f0, highlight amber #ffc857. Invented cloud storage product "Vaultline".

Typography: "IBM Plex Sans" 400/500/600 for headings and body (headline clamp 34px to 56px, line-height 1.08, tracking -.02em; section titles 28px 600; questions 18px 500; body 16px); "IBM Plex Mono" 400 for labels and anchors (12px uppercase .08em). Headline: "Help, indexed."

Layout: Top bar with wordmark "vaultline" and a search-looking button "Search help ⌘K" (decorative). Hero strip with headline and sub "Find answers fast. Everything is grouped by topic and every question has a link you can share." Main: two columns (260px sticky index, content). Index: label "ON THIS PAGE" and a list of five topics (Getting started, Syncing, Sharing, Billing, Security) each with a question count; the active topic has a 3px cyan left border, brighter text and a small dot. Content: five sections each with an id, a title and 3 collapsible questions (details/summary styled with a rotating chevron); each summary also has a small "#" anchor button on hover/focus that copies the deep link (location without hash + #id) to the clipboard, with a bottom toast "Link copied". Questions have realistic specific answers (storage limits, file versions for 30 days, link expiry, VAT invoices, 2-step login, etc.). A closing "Still stuck?" card with a button "Contact support" and an email help@vaultline.example.

Interaction: Scroll-spy via IntersectionObserver updates the active index item; clicking an index item scrolls smoothly (scroll-margin-top 90px) and updates the hash; if the URL has a hash matching a question, that question opens on load. Keyboard: index items are links; summary items toggle with Enter/Space; the anchor buttons are focusable and labelled "Copy link to <question>". Clipboard in try/catch with fallback toast "Press Ctrl+C to copy" if it fails.

Responsive: Under 900px the index becomes a horizontally scrolling sticky chip bar below the top bar (position sticky, top 0) and the content becomes full width; no horizontal scroll of the page at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Toast uses role=status; visible focus 3px #4cc9f0. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no smooth scroll or transitions). Invented names; example.com style addresses only.
