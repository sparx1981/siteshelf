Canvas & base: Calm blue-white page #f3f7fc, ink #0e2038, secondary #4f6280 (6.3:1), brand blue #1d5fe0, success green #14a36b, warning amber #e08a00, danger #d64545. Cards white with 1px #dbe4f1 borders, radius 18px. Invented ISP "Brightwire Broadband".

Typography: "Figtree" 500/600/700 for headings and UI (headline clamp 34px to 58px, line-height 1.06, tracking -.02em; question text 26px 700); "Figtree" 400 for body 17px. Headline: "Let's get you back online."

Layout: Header with wordmark "brightwire" and a link "Contact us". Two-column layout: left (main) a card with the current question, large and clear, with answer buttons below (e.g. "Yes", "No", "Not sure") and a progress breadcrumb above ("Step 2 of about 4") with the visited path as chips; right (sidebar) a vertical flowchart map of all nodes drawn as boxes with connecting lines; the current node highlighted in blue, visited nodes in light blue with a check, and the path lines in blue; others grey. The tree: Q1 "Are the lights on your router on?" (No: S1 Power fix; Yes: Q2). Q2 "Is the internet light green?" (Yes: Q3; No: Q4). Q3 "Do all devices lack internet?" (No: S2 Single device fix; Yes: S3 Restart and test). Q4 "Is the internet light red or off?" (Red: S4 Line fault, call engineer; Off: S5 Check cable and filter). Each solution screen shows a title, a numbered list of 3 to 5 steps with concrete wording, an estimate "Takes about 5 minutes", and buttons "That fixed it" (shows a celebration state) and "Still not working" (goes to a Contact card with phone 0800 555 0199 and webchat). Controls: Back (go up one), Restart.

Interaction: Choosing an answer slides the new question in (200ms) and updates the chart path with a drawing line; keyboard: answer buttons are real buttons, the chart nodes are not focusable (decorative with a text alternative via the breadcrumb). Browser-like back works via the Back button. Focus moves to the new question heading after each answer. Number keys 1 to 3 choose answers.

Responsive: Under 900px the chart collapses into a collapsible "Show map" details element below the card; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Question region has aria-live polite; visible focus 3px #1d5fe0. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no slide or line drawing). Invented names and 0800 555 style numbers only.
