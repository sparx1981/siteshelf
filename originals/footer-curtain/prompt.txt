Canvas & base: Content sheet #f3ede2 (warm oat) with ink #1d1a16 (15:1) and secondary #5f574b (6.5:1); the sheet has rounded bottom corners (36px) and a deep soft shadow so it reads as a curtain. The footer behind is charcoal #121212 with cream text #f3ede2 (15:1), muted #a59d8e (7:1) and oat accent #e8d3a8. Invented coffee roastery "Obsidian & Oat".

Typography: "Syne" 700/800 for the enormous footer wordmark (clamp 26px to 96px, tight tracking, uppercase, words kept whole so it wraps as OBSIDIAN & / OAT) and for headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for body and links (16px).

Layout: The sheet holds the whole page: a header, hero ("Roasted slowly. Delivered warmly." with a generated SVG coffee bag illustration), three coffee cards (generated SVG bags in different colours, name, notes, price), a story section with a short paragraph and three numbers, and a closing banner "Your first bag is on us". The sheet ends 0px above the footer and has margin-bottom equal to the footer height. The footer is position fixed at the bottom with z-index below the sheet; it contains the wordmark "OBSIDIAN & OAT" filling the width, a newsletter row (email field and Join button), three link columns (Coffee, Visit, Company), a live local-time clock for the roastery ("Pennick Bay · 14:32"), a Back to top button and a bottom line with copyright and hello@obsidianandoat.example.com.

Interaction: As the user reaches the end of the page, the sheet scrolls up and away revealing the fixed footer. JavaScript measures the footer height (ResizeObserver) and writes it to a CSS variable used for the sheet margin. A progress value (0 to 1) of how much of the footer is revealed drives a parallax: the footer content translates from -40px to 0 and the wordmark letters rise in a stagger (translateY 40px to 0, 40ms per letter) as progress passes 0.2. If the footer is taller than the viewport it falls back to a normal static footer after the sheet (no fixed positioning). Because the footer is visually hidden behind the sheet until the end, focusing any footer link or button with the keyboard scrolls the page to the bottom first so the focus target is visible. The clock updates every 30 seconds with Intl.DateTimeFormat; Back to top smooth-scrolls to the top and moves focus to the page heading. The newsletter field validates inline with an accessible message.

Responsive: Under 700px columns collapse to two, the footer becomes static if it is taller than the viewport, and the sheet corner radius is 24px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. The footer must remain reachable by assistive tech (it stays in DOM order after the sheet and is not aria-hidden). Visible focus 3px #e8d3a8 on dark and #1d1a16 on the sheet, with offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (footer renders as a normal static footer; no parallax or stagger). Invented names only.
