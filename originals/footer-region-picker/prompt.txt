Canvas & base: Fresh-market page #fdf9f1 with ink #26211b (15:1), secondary #615748 (6.6:1), rules #e6dcc8. Footer band #1f2a1f (deep green) with cream text #f6f0e2 (12:1) and muted #b8c3b0 (6.8:1). Accent fig #8c2f5f for the page, leaf #8fd16a on the dark footer. Popover panel #fffdf7 with 1px #d8cdb4 border, 16px radius, shadow 0 24px 60px -20px rgba(0,0,0,.6). Invented grocer "Fennel & Fig Market".

Typography: "Newsreader" 600/700 for headings and the greeting (clamp 36px to 64px); "Manrope" 400/500/600/700 for UI, links and popover (15px).

Layout: A compact hero: logo, greeting line ("Welcome in" translated), headline "Good food, delivered like a neighbour would.", a delivery line ("Delivering to United Kingdom"), and a product row of three cards (Sourdough loaf, Fig preserve, Olive oil) whose prices are shown in the selected currency. The footer has four link columns, a newsletter line and, in the bottom bar, the selector button: a globe icon, the region name, language name and currency code ("United Kingdom · English · £ GBP") with a chevron. The button opens a popover above it (width 440px) containing: a search field "Find your country", a scrollable list of twelve regions (United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, United States, Canada, Australia, Japan) as a radiogroup with flag-less coloured monograms, a "Language" chip group (English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português), a "Currency" select (GBP £, EUR €, USD $, CAD $, AUD $, JPY ¥) that follows the region but can be overridden, and Save and Cancel buttons.

Interaction: The button has aria-haspopup="dialog" and aria-expanded; the popover is role="dialog" aria-label "Region and language", non-modal with a Tab loop (Tab from the last control returns to the first) and Escape to close and refocus the button; outside click closes using event.composedPath. Typing in the search filters regions live with a count announced politely; ArrowUp/ArrowDown move through the visible regions (roving radio) and Enter or Space chooses; choosing a region proposes its default language and currency (chip and select update with a short highlight). Save applies: greeting and headline text switch to the selected language using a built-in dictionary for the six languages, prices re-render using fixed invented exchange rates with correct symbols and Intl.NumberFormat when available, the delivery line updates, document.documentElement.lang is set, and a toast "Preferences saved" appears. Choices are saved in localStorage (inside try/catch) and restored on load. On first load the popover opens once, non-modally, without stealing focus, to show itself (also when reduced motion is preferred).

Responsive: Under 560px the popover becomes a bottom sheet (full width, max-height 80vh, 20px top radius) with a scrim and its own scrolling; link columns collapse to two; the selector button spans the width; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #8c2f5f (3px #8fd16a on the dark footer) with offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no popover slide, no highlight pulse, no toast animation). Invented names only; example.com-style contact details.
