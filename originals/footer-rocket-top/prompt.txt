Canvas & base: Deep space page #070b1f with a subtle star field (generated SVG circles, a few twinkling) and a faint nebula glow (radial gradients #4b2aa8 at 25% and #0f6fd1 at 18%). Cards #101735 with 1px #26306b borders. Text #eef1ff (16:1), secondary #aab4e6 (8:1). Accent flame orange #ff7a2f with yellow core #ffd24a, secondary accent cyan #5ce1ff. Invented space-history publication "Starling Almanac".

Typography: "Outfit" 600/700/800 for headings and the button label (headline clamp 40px to 84px); "Inter" 400/500 for body (17px) and footer links (15px).

Layout: A long single page: a hero (headline "Every launch begins with a countdown.", short paragraph and two buttons), then eight "mission log" cards in a two-column grid, each with a generated SVG mission patch, a year, a title and one sentence (all invented missions: Lantern-1, Kestrel Probe, Meridian Lander and so on). The footer is a dark gradient panel with: a launch pad drawn in SVG (a gantry tower and a pad), a 96px rocket standing on it (white body, orange fins, round window, nose cone), four link columns (Almanac, Missions, Learn, Contact) and a bottom bar with copyright and a status line "All systems nominal". A "Back to top" button with the rocket icon and a label "Launch to top" sits in the footer next to the pad. After the page has scrolled more than 600px a 56px floating round mini launcher (rocket icon, label via aria-label) appears at the bottom-right.

Interaction: Pressing the footer button or the floating launcher starts a sequence: a 3-2-1 countdown readout (700ms per count, shown in a pill beside the pad with role="status"), then ignition (flame flicker animation, pad shakes 2px, 500ms), then liftoff: the rocket (a fixed-position clone of the SVG) travels from its current position up past the top of the viewport over about 1.6 seconds with ease-in, leaving a trail of fading smoke puffs (small circles spawned every 60ms), while window.scrollTo smooth-scrolls to the top in parallel. When the page reaches the top the clone is removed, a toast "Touchdown at the top" appears and focus moves to the page headline (tabindex -1). The real rocket in the footer fades back in after 800ms ("next one is being stacked"). If the user presses the button again mid-sequence it is ignored; Escape aborts the countdown before ignition. The launch is also available with keyboard Enter/Space and announces each stage in an aria-live region.

Responsive: Footer columns collapse to two at 860px and one at 520px; the pad and rocket scale to 72px; the floating button moves 16px from the edges; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #5ce1ff with dark offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no countdown, flame, smoke or travel; the page jumps to the top instantly and the status line announces it; stars do not twinkle). Invented names only.
