Canvas & base: A short outdoor-gear brand page (invented "Horizon Outfitters") with a calm cream body #fbf6ee and ink #231a16 (15:1) that ends in a tall footer scene. The footer sky is a vertical gradient driven by scroll progress through the footer (0 to 1): at 0 it is a bright afternoon (#8fd3ff to #ffe9b8), at 0.5 golden hour (#ffb36b to #ff7a59 to #6b3fa0) and at 1 deep dusk (#2a1a4d to #0f0a24 to #ff6a4d at the horizon). Layered hills in three silhouettes (#5a2e5f, #3b1f4a, #1d1030) with a slight parallax offset. Footer text is cream #fff4e6 with a dark backing gradient so contrast stays at least 4.5:1 at every scroll position.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for the footer headline and column titles (headline clamp 36px to 72px); "DM Sans" 400/500/600 for links and small print (16px, 14px).

Layout: Above the footer, a brief page: hero statement "Gear for the long way home.", three product teasers (tent, boots, stove) as simple generated SVG cards. The footer is 100vh tall (min 640px) with: the sky and sun (a 180px glowing disc with a soft halo ring) behind the hills; a large headline "See you at golden hour." near the top; four link columns (Shop, Trails, Company, Help) with five links each and a newsletter field with a Subscribe button; and a bottom bar with copyright, a language note and social text links (Instagram, Journal, Trail Radio). Stars (generated SVG circles with twinkle) fade in after progress 0.55; a thin crescent moon appears after 0.8. A "Sunset" readout under the headline shows the current progress as a time ("4:12 pm" to "8:47 pm") for delight.

Interaction: As the footer scrolls into view, progress = how much of the footer is visible (0 to 1). The sun translates from high in the sky down behind the hills (translateY with an ease), the sky gradient interpolates between three stop sets in JavaScript (lerp colours, set CSS variables), hill layers shift 0 to 24px at different rates, and the star opacity and moon opacity follow progress. The newsletter form validates the email inline with an accessible message and a success state (no network). Links have a warm underline sweep on hover and visible focus. A "Pause the sky" toggle button (aria-pressed) freezes the scene at the current state for users who dislike motion.

Responsive: Columns collapse to two at 900px and one at 520px with the headline reducing to 36px; the sun shrinks to 120px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffd27a with dark offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (the scene renders statically at golden hour, no twinkle, no scroll-linked movement; the Pause control is hidden). Invented names only; example.com-style contact details.
