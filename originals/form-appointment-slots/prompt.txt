Canvas & base: Soft teal-grey page #eef4f4, white cards with 1px #d6e3e3 borders and radius 22px, ink #10292b, secondary #4f6b6d (6:1), teal #0f8b8d primary, deep teal #0b5f61, amber #f2a900 for the hold timer, error #c0392b, busy slots #e4ecec. Invented clinic "Linden Physiotherapy".

Typography: "Sora" 600/700 for headings and times (page title clamp 32px to 52px, line-height 1.08, tracking -.02em); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 16px. Title: "Book your physio appointment."

Layout: Two-column on desktop (main 1.5fr, summary 1fr sticky). Main: step 1 "Choose a day": a horizontally scrollable strip of the next 10 weekdays as cards (weekday abbreviation, date number, month, a small availability line such as "6 slots") with previous/next arrow buttons; step 2 "Choose a time": slots grouped under Morning, Afternoon and Evening headings as pill buttons (09:00 to 18:30 every 30 minutes, treatment length 45 minutes noted), some marked unavailable (aria-disabled, strikethrough, tooltip "Taken"); step 3 "Your details" appears after a slot is selected: name, mobile, reason select (Back pain, Knee injury, Sports injury, Posture, Other) and an optional note, plus a Confirm button. Summary panel: appointment type "Initial assessment, 45 min, £60", selected day and time, therapist "with Dr Ines Moreau" and the hold timer card: "Slot held for 04:59" with a draining bar (amber under 60s, red under 20s).

Interaction: Selecting a slot starts a 5 minute hold (timer ticks each second; demo can run at normal speed). Changing slot restarts the timer. When time runs out the slot is released, selection cleared, a polite alert "Your hold expired. Please choose a time again." appears and focus moves to the time grid. Day strip supports arrow keys (roving tabindex) and the grid supports arrow keys across slots. Confirm validates (name 2+ chars, mobile 10+ digits), then shows a confirmation card with a tick, the booking reference (LP-xxxx), "Add to calendar" button (shows a disabled-looking toast "Calendar file downloaded" simulation) and "Book another".

Responsive: Under 900px summary moves below and hold timer becomes a sticky bar at the bottom while a slot is held; slots keep 3 per row; no horizontal scroll at 390px (only the day strip scrolls).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. aria-live announces selection and timer milestones (every minute and at 10s), visible focus 3px #0b5f61. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no bar easing). Invented names only.
