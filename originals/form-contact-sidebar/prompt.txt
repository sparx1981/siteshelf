Canvas & base: Cream page #f5efe3, ink #14120e, secondary #5d564a (6.3:1), vermilion accent #e4472b, deep green #1e3a2f for the side panel, hairlines #14120e at 15%. Inputs are underline-style (no boxes). Invented creative studio "Harbourside Studio" in Plymouth.

Typography: "Instrument Serif" 400 and italic for headings (title clamp 44px to 108px, line-height .95, tracking -.02em); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 16px and labels (uppercase 12px, .12em tracking). Title: "Let's talk about your project." with "talk" in italic vermilion.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Harbourside" (serif) and links Work, Studio, Contact. Two columns (form 1.4fr, panel 1fr) with 56px gap. Form: Topic select as a row of radio pills (New project, Support, Press, Say hello), name, email, budget select (only when New project is chosen; options Under £10k, £10 to 25k, £25 to 60k, £60k+), message textarea (min 5 rows), and a "Send message" button with an arrow that slides on hover. Panel (green): "Office hours" with a live status pill ("Open now · closes 17:30" with a pulsing green dot, or "Closed · opens Monday 09:00" with a grey dot) computed from the viewer's local time against Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:30 Europe/London approximated by local time, a weekly hours table with today highlighted, a reply-time estimate card that changes with the topic (New project: "Usually within 4 working hours"; Support: "Within 1 working hour"; Press: "Same day"; Say hello: "Whenever we can, usually a day"), and contact details (hello@harbourside.example, +44 1752 555 0148, address).

Interaction: Topic pills are a radiogroup; changing topic crossfades the reply estimate and toggles the budget field (height transition). Inline validation on submit (name 2+, valid email, message 20+ characters with a counter "12 / 20 minimum") with errors under each underline and focus on the first error. On success the form replaces itself with "Message sent. Reference HS-4821" and a summary; the estimate card remains. The status pill updates every minute.

Responsive: Under 900px stack (panel after form); pills wrap; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Labels visible, errors in aria-describedby, status pill with text (not colour alone), visible focus 3px #e4472b. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pulse or crossfade). Invented names; example addresses only.
