Canvas & base: Warm tomato-cream page #fff1e0, white card with 2.5px #2a1810 border and 6px 6px 0 #2a1810 shadow, radius 28px. Ink #2a1810, secondary #6b4a3a (6.2:1), tomato #e8452c, sun #ffb627, basil #2e9e5b, plum #7a3b8f, sky #3b8fd9. Faces are 64px SVG circles with simple features that animate on hover/selection. Invented restaurant "Sunday Supper".

Typography: "Baloo 2" 700 for headings (title clamp 32px to 52px, line-height 1.05) and chip labels; "Nunito" 400/700/800 for body 17px. Title: "How was your supper?"

Layout: Centred card max-width 640px. Step 1: question "How did tonight feel?" with five face buttons in a row (Awful, Not great, Okay, Good, Delicious) with labels beneath; each face has its own colour (tomato, peach, sun, basil, sky) and an expression drawn with SVG (mouth curve and eyes). Step 2 (appears after a face is chosen with a slide-down): a follow-up question that depends on mood and 6 multi-select chips: Awful/Not great: "What went wrong?" (Cold food, Slow service, Noisy room, Wrong order, Price, Cleanliness); Okay: "What would make it better?" (Bigger portions, Faster service, More choice, Quieter room, Better value, Nicer music); Good/Delicious: "What did you love?" (The food, Our server, The atmosphere, The cocktails, The desserts, The value). Step 3: a textarea "Anything else?" with a counter 0/300, an optional email field with note "Only if you'd like a reply", and a Send feedback button. Success: the chosen face enlarges and bounces with floating hearts or sparks (CSS), message "Thank you, we read every note." and, for Awful or Not great, "A manager will reply within 24 hours if you left an email."

Interaction: Face buttons are a radiogroup with arrow keys; selected face scales 1.15 with a ring and the others fade to .5. The follow-up block animates in (max-height transition 300ms). Chips toggle with aria-pressed and a pop animation. The Send button is disabled (aria-disabled) until a face is chosen. Submitting shows a 700ms loading dots state first.

Responsive: Faces shrink to 52px under 480px and wrap labels; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Labelled controls, aria-live for the follow-up heading, visible focus 3px #2a1810 with white halo. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no bounce or floating). Invented names only.
