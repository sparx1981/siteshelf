Canvas & base: Warm off-white page #faf7f1, white card with 1px #e6dfcf border, radius 24px and a soft shadow, ink #1d2a24, secondary #5b6b62 (5.9:1), brand green #1f6b4c, accent clay #d1673b, error #c0392b, success #1f8a4c. Invented broker "Hearth & Key Mortgages".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for headings (page title clamp 34px to 56px, step title 28px); "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for body 16px, labels 14px 600, helper text 13px. Title: "Find out what you could borrow."

Layout: Centred column max 720px. Above the card a progress rail of four numbered steps (1 About you, 2 The property, 3 Your income, 4 Review) with connecting lines that fill green as steps complete, labels hidden under 560px except the current one. Card contents per step: (1) full name, email, phone (UK format hint), (2) property value (£, formatted with commas), deposit slider from 5% to 60% (default 15%) with the £ amount and LTV shown ("Loan £255,000 · LTV 85%"), property type select (House, Flat, Bungalow), first-time buyer radio (Yes, No); (3) employment status radio cards (Employed, Self-employed, Retired), gross annual income, term in years slider 5 to 35 (default 25); (4) review list of all answers with an "Edit" link per group, an estimated monthly payment line (repayment at 4.6%) and a required consent checkbox. Buttons: Back (ghost) and Continue (solid green); the final button reads "Send my enquiry". Success screen: animated tick, "Thanks, Priya. A broker will call you within one working day." and the estimated payment recap.

Interaction: Continue validates the current step, sets aria-invalid and inline errors beside each field (never alerts), moves focus to the first error; valid advances with a 250ms slide. Property value and income inputs reformat with thousands separators while typing. Enter submits the step. Edit links in review jump back to that step and return to review after Continue. Progress is kept in memory when going Back.

Responsive: Card padding shrinks under 560px, the rail becomes compact dots with the current label below; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Labelled fields, aria-describedby for errors, aria-current=step on the rail, visible focus 3px #1f6b4c. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no slide). Invented names; example addresses only.
