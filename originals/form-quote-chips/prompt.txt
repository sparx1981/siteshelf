Canvas & base: Pastel studio page #f4f0ff with a faint noise-free dotted grid (radial-gradient #d9d0f5 1px at 22px), ink #1a1530, secondary #5a5278 (6.2:1), electric violet #6c3cff primary, lemon #f5e642 highlight, mint #bff3dc, coral #ff7a66 for the rush tag. Cards white with 2px #1a1530 borders and 5px 5px 0 #1a1530 shadows, radius 20px. Invented studio "Fieldwork Creative".

Typography: "Epilogue" 700/800 for headings and chips (title clamp 36px to 64px, line-height 1, tracking -.03em); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 16px. Title: "Tell us what you're making."

Layout: Two columns (form 1.5fr, brief card 1fr sticky). Form sections as numbered groups: 1 "What are we making?" single-select big chips (Brand identity, Website, Mobile app, Campaign, Packaging); 2 "What do you need?" multi-select chips that change by project type (e.g. for Website: Design, Development, CMS setup, Copywriting, Photography, SEO; for Brand identity: Logo, Guidelines, Naming, Illustration, Stationery, Motion), each chip shows a small +£ hint on hover; 3 "When do you need it?" single-select chips (Rush: 2 weeks +25%, Standard: 6 weeks, Relaxed: 12 weeks -10%) with a coral "Rush" tag; 4 "Budget" range slider £5k to £100k step £1k with the value in a bubble above the thumb; 5 name and email fields. The brief card is a live sentence: "Hi, I'm {name}. I need a {type} with {deliverables} in about {weeks} weeks, with a budget around {budget}." with fields highlighted in lemon as filled and placeholders "…" in muted grey otherwise; below it an estimate range "Estimated: £14k to £19k" computed from the selections (type base: Brand 8k, Website 12k, App 28k, Campaign 15k, Packaging 9k; each deliverable +1.5k to +4k; timeline multiplier) and a note whether it fits the chosen budget ("Within your budget" in mint or "Above your budget by about £4k" in coral). Buttons: "Copy brief" and "Send request".

Interaction: Chips are toggle buttons (aria-pressed) with a pop animation and a lemon fill on selection; changing project type clears incompatible deliverables with a polite announcement. The brief updates in real time. Copy uses the clipboard inside try/catch with a "Copied" state. Send validates name and email and at least a project type, then replaces the form with a thank-you and the final brief.

Responsive: Under 960px brief moves under the form (no sticky); chips wrap; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Chip groups have group labels, brief has aria-live polite (debounced), visible focus 3px #6c3cff with white halo. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pop). Invented names; example addresses only.
