Simplify: a separate "Loupe" button on each frame (a small magnifier icon appearing on hover/focus) opens the enlarge view; clicking the frame itself marks.

Interaction: The Enlarge view is a native dialog showing the frame at 900px wide with its number, a faux exposure line ("f/5.6 · 1/250 · ISO 400"), mark toggle, prev/next buttons and a caption field (contenteditable-free: a text input saving a caption per frame in memory). Keyboard: arrow keys move focus between frames in the sheet grid; M toggles mark on the focused frame; Enter opens the loupe.

Responsive: Under 800px the strips become 3 frames per row (each strip splits in two rows), sprocket holes continue; no horizontal scroll at 390px.
