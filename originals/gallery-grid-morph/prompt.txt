Canvas & base: Off-black page #0e0e10, tiles with 1px #26262b borders and radius 14px, text #f2f0eb, secondary #a3a09a (7.5:1), accent warm white #f2f0eb and a tiny amber #ffb45a for the active index. Artworks are generated SVG compositions in eight palettes (no external images). Invented studio "Frame by Frame".

Typography: "Instrument Serif" 400 and italic for headings (title clamp 44px to 110px, line-height .95, tracking -.02em; detail title 56px); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 15px and meta in uppercase 12px with .14em tracking.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Frame by Frame" and filter chips (All, Warm, Cool, Mono; aria-pressed) that filter tiles by palette family. Hero title "Twelve frames, closely looked at." with a short sub. A responsive grid (4 columns desktop, 3 tablet, 2 mobile; 16px gap) of 12 tiles (aspect 4/5) each showing generated art, a small index number "01" at top left and the title at the bottom on a gradient. On selecting a tile a detail layer opens: dark scrim (rgba 0,0,0,.82) with a two-column layout: the artwork large on the left (max 70vh height) and, on the right, title, artist, year, medium, dimensions, a 3-sentence description, an "Add to wishlist" heart toggle and "Prev" and "Next" buttons plus counter "04 / 12".

Interaction: The shared-element morph uses FLIP: measure the tile's rect, create the detail artwork at its final rect, animate its transform from the tile rect to the final rect over 520ms with cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) using the Web Animations API, while the tile itself becomes invisible and the scrim and meta fade in with 120ms stagger. Closing (Esc, close button, or clicking the scrim) reverses it back to the tile's current rect (even if the grid reflowed). Next and Prev swap the artwork with a crossfade and slide of 240ms and update meta; arrow keys also navigate; focus is trapped in the detail layer and returns to the originating tile. Filtering reflows tiles with a FLIP animation (300ms).

Responsive: Under 800px the detail becomes a single column (art on top, meta below, scrollable); no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Detail layer is role=dialog with aria-modal and aria-labelledby; tiles are buttons with aria-labels; wishlist toggle has aria-pressed; visible focus 3px #ffb45a. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no morph or reflow animation; instant open and close). Invented names only.
