Canvas & base: Midnight page #0a0b14 with a faint radial spotlight (circle at 50% 45%, rgba(110,90,255,.22), transparent 55%) and a circular floor reflection under the ring (ellipse gradient). Text #f1f0ff, secondary #a6a3cc (7.5:1). Accent coral #ff7a6b. Artworks are generated SVG compositions (no external images): 12 pieces in palettes of 3 colours with a mix of sun-and-hills, concentric arcs, geometric blocks and waves. Invented gallery "Ring Room" featuring invented artists.

Typography: "Syne" 700/800 for the title (clamp 36px to 80px, line-height 1, tracking -.03em) and artwork titles; "Inter" 400/500 for body and captions at 15px; counter in "Syne" 700.

Layout: Top bar with "RING ROOM" wordmark and counter "03 / 12". Title "Twelve pieces, one orbit." with a 44ch sub. The stage (height 56vh, min 380px) uses perspective 1400px and a rotating container with 12 tiles (220px by 290px, radius 12px, subtle border) placed around a cylinder with transform rotateY(i*30deg) translateZ(radius) where radius = tile width / (2 * tan(15deg)) (about 410px). Back tiles are dimmed (opacity .4 and slightly blurred). Below the stage a caption block shows the front piece's title, artist, year and medium, and controls: previous and next round buttons, an auto-rotate toggle (aria-pressed) and a range slider labelled "Rotation speed". A reflection of tiles under the ring is not required.

Interaction: Pointer drag rotates the ring horizontally with inertia (velocity decay .95 per frame) and snaps to the nearest tile when slow; arrow keys (Left/Right) rotate one tile with a 600ms ease; auto-rotate (default on) turns at 6 degrees per second and pauses while hovering, dragging or focusing the stage; clicking the front tile opens a lightbox (native dialog) showing the piece large with title, artist, year, medium and a description; clicking a side tile rotates it to the front first. The front tile index drives the caption and counter with a 200ms fade.

Responsive: Under 700px tiles are 150px by 200px with radius computed accordingly (about 280px); caption below; no horizontal scroll at 390px (stage overflow hidden).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Tiles are buttons with aria-labels (title, artist, "piece 3 of 12"); stage has role=group with aria-roledescription=carousel; live region announces the front piece; visible focus 3px #ff7a6b. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no auto-rotate, no inertia, instant snapping). Invented names only.
