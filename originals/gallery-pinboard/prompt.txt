Canvas & base: A cork-free pinboard: a large board surface #e7ece9 (soft sage-grey) with a fine dot grid (radial-gradient #c5d0ca 1px at 24px) and a 10px rounded frame in #2d3a35 with an inner shadow; page background #1b2421. Cards are paper-white #ffffff or tinted notes (#fff3b0 yellow, #ffd9d2 coral, #d4f0e2 mint, #dbe5ff blue) with soft shadows, 2px radius and a rotation between -4 and 4 degrees. Pins are 14px circles in #e4482b, #2d6cdf or #1f9d63 with a highlight. Art cards use generated SVG compositions (no external images). Invented moodboard "Seaside Cafe, opening spring".

Typography: "Caveat" 600 for handwritten note text (22px) and board title; "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for UI (14px to 16px); "DM Sans" 700 for card captions at 13px.

Layout: Top toolbar over the board: title "Seaside Cafe moodboard" (editable via a button that shows an input), an "Add note" button (adds a yellow note at a free spot with editable text via a small prompt-free inline textarea), "Add swatch" button (adds a colour swatch card), a "Snap to grid" toggle (24px), a "Tidy up" button (arranges cards in a neat grid with a 500ms animation), a "Reset" button (restores the starting layout) and a card count. The board is 1200px by 760px max, scaling to the viewport width (min 360px) with overflow hidden inside a frame; it contains 14 starting items: 6 art cards (with captions), 4 notes with short text ("Terrazzo floor?", "Oat flat white, always", "Light from the east", "Blue door!"), 2 colour swatch cards with hex values and 2 small photo-style cards (art) rotated.

Interaction: Pointer drag moves cards (clamped to the board); the dragged card lifts (scale 1.04, larger shadow) and comes to the top z-order; releasing drops it with a subtle 150ms settle. Keyboard: cards are focusable (tabindex 0, role=group, aria-label "Card: title. Use arrow keys to move"); arrow keys move 8px (Shift 32px), Enter toggles a "pick up" mode announced in a live region, Delete removes the card (with an undo toast), "]" brings forward, "[" sends backward. Layout positions (percentages of board size) persist in localStorage inside try/catch and restore on load. Double-click on a note edits its text in place.

Responsive: Under 700px the board keeps its aspect and scales via CSS transform (scale = container width / 1200) with pointer maths accounting for the scale; toolbar wraps; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Live region announces moves ("Moved Blue door by 8 pixels right"); visible focus 3px #2d6cdf with white halo. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no lift or tidy animation). Invented names only.
