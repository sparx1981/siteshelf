Build a single-page "Brick Breaker" game for an invented arcade, "Starlight Arcade": a paddle, a ball and rows of glossy bricks, with power-ups, three level layouts, lives, a saved high score and full keyboard, pointer and touch control. It is drawn on a canvas, runs at 60 fps with delta-time movement, and needs no assets.

Canvas & base: A painted night-sky backdrop on the page (deep indigo #0b1030 to violet #2a1a5a to a rose horizon #c2477a at the bottom) with three parallax star layers and a distant crescent moon; the playfield is a rounded 4:3 panel (logical 800x600) with a 1px #3a3f8a border and a soft inner glow. Brick colours by row (top to bottom): coral #ff6b6b, amber #ffb347, lemon #ffe066, mint #6be3a8, sky #5fc8ff, violet #a78bfa; hard bricks are steel #9aa8c4 with a bolt pattern and need two hits. Text #f3f0ff (15:1), secondary #b9b4de (8.6:1). Ball #ffffff with a cyan #7de8ff glow; paddle gradient #e8eeff to #8da0d8 with a bright top edge; power-up capsules in mint, amber and coral with a letter. Invented game title only.

Typography: "Sora" 600 for the title and overlay headings (clamp 28 to 48px); "Inter" 400/500/600 for the HUD and instructions at 14 to 16px; "JetBrains Mono" 500 for score and lives (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, amber.

Imagery & art direction: Bricks must look like polished glass candy, not flat rectangles: each brick is a rounded rect (4px radius) with a vertical linear gradient from a lighter tint to the base colour, a 1px lighter top edge, a darker 1px bottom edge, a soft white specular highlight (a short rounded bar in the top-left at 35% opacity) and a faint outer glow in the brick colour (shadowBlur 6). The ball leaves a fading 8-point trail; the paddle has a glossy highlight, rounded ends and a soft reflection on the floor of the panel. Breaking a brick spawns 10 to 14 small shards (rotating rectangles in the brick colour) with gravity and fade, a brief flash on the brick's neighbours and, for hard bricks, a spark. The backdrop stars twinkle slowly and shift by 1 to 3 px with the ball's x position (parallax). Overlays (title, paused, level clear, game over) use a frosted glass panel with large type and a primary button. A subtle CRT-free vignette frames the panel. Media slots for real photography: an optional 1920x1080 night-sky photograph for the page background with a 50% indigo overlay.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Starlight Arcade" and title "Brick Breaker". HUD above the canvas: Score, Lives (as small heart or ball icons plus the number), Level and High score. The canvas below, centred, scaling to fit (max 800 px wide, aspect 4:3). Under it: a controls row with Pause, Sound (off by default) and Restart buttons and a short instruction line ("Move with the arrow keys, A and D or drag. Space launches."). Overlays sit over the canvas.

Copy: Title overlay "Press Space or tap to start"; Level clear "Level N cleared. Bonus +N"; Game over "Game over. Score N. Best N." with "Play again". Power-up letters and names: W "Wide paddle" (12 seconds), S "Slow ball" (10 seconds), + "Extra life".

Interaction: Fixed logical size 800x600 scaled by CSS and devicePixelRatio (cap 2). Paddle moves with Left and Right arrows or A and D (acceleration with a maximum speed), by pointer movement over the canvas and by touch drag; Space, Enter, click or tap launches the ball when it rests on the paddle. The ball reflects off walls, the paddle (the bounce angle depends on the hit position from -60 to +60 degrees, speed increases 2% per paddle hit up to a cap) and bricks (resolve against the nearest edge; hard bricks need two hits); losing the ball costs a life and resets to the paddle. Three layouts: Level 1 full six rows of ten bricks, Level 2 a pyramid with gaps, Level 3 a checker of normal and hard bricks. Each level raises base ball speed by 8%. About 18% of broken bricks drop a power-up capsule falling at 120 px/s; catching it applies the effect with a visible timer bar under the HUD. Score is 10 per brick (20 for hard), plus a combo bonus for consecutive hits without touching the paddle. P or Escape toggles pause and the game auto-pauses when the tab loses focus. High score is saved in localStorage inside try/catch. Optional Web Audio blips (short sine and square tones with gain ramps, inside try/catch) play on paddle, brick and power-up events only when Sound is on. State changes (level cleared, life lost, game over, power-up) are announced politely through an aria-live region; the canvas has an aria-label summarising the game and a visually hidden status line holds score and lives.

Responsive: The canvas scales to the viewport width minus 24px (down to 320 px); controls wrap; the paddle is draggable anywhere on the canvas width on touch; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries, audio files or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset on all buttons. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shards, no trail, no parallax or twinkle; gameplay unchanged). Invented names only.
