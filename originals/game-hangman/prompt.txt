Build a single-page "Hangman Words" game for an invented puzzle site, "Wordlantern": guess the hidden word one letter at a time before the picture is complete. It should feel friendly rather than grim: a wooden gallows on a painted dusk hillside and a small paper-lantern character whose light dims with each wrong guess. Everything runs in the page; the word lists are original.

Canvas & base: A painted dusk hillside fills the page (sky #1f2a5a to #c2477a to #ffb48a at the horizon, soft rolling hills in #3a2f5a and #241c40, a few fireflies); the game panel is a glass card rgba(24,20,48,.82) with a 1px #4a4488 border and 24px radius. Letter keys #f3ecff on #2c2660; correct keys mint #7de0b0 with dark text, wrong keys coral #ff8a7a at 55% with a strike line; lantern amber #ffc247. Text #f6f0ff (15:1), secondary #bdb2dc (8.7:1). Invented site name only.

Typography: "Fredoka" 500/600 for the word display, keys and headings (word letters 44px, keys 22px); "Nunito" 400/600/700 for UI at 15 to 16px; tabular numerals for counters. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, amber.

Imagery & art direction: The scene is the star. Draw in inline SVG a wooden gallows (an upright post, a crossbeam with a diagonal brace and a hanging hook, a ground plank) with wood grain lines, knots and a soft shadow, standing on a grassy hill with tufts, a path and a low stone wall; behind it, layered hills, a crescent moon, stars and a few warm windows of a distant village. The character is a round paper lantern with a face (two dot eyes, a small smile that becomes a worried then wobbly mouth), a tiny cap, stick arms and legs; it is built in seven reveal stages tied to wrong guesses (1 rope and hook, 2 lantern body, 3 face, 4 left arm, 5 right arm, 6 left leg, 7 right leg) while its glow radius and brightness fade from 100% to 25% as stages advance; on a win the lantern lifts off the gallows and floats upward glowing brightly with a ribbon of sparks; on a loss the lantern sits down on the ground, dim but safe (the game is gentle: "The lantern is resting. Try again!"). Fireflies drift slowly in the foreground and turn brighter on correct guesses. 12% film grain and vignette. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 dusk landscape photograph for the backdrop with a 45% indigo overlay.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Wordlantern" and title "Hangman Words". Two columns above 900px: left the scene (aspect 4:3) with a wrong-guess counter ("Wrong: 2 of 7") and a lives row of seven small lantern icons dimming; right the game: a category chip row (Animals, Food, Places, Science) plus a Random option, the word display (letters in boxes with underscores, hyphens shown), a hint line ("Category: Animals · 7 letters"), the on-screen keyboard (QWERTY, 3 rows), and the buttons Hint (reveals one letter, costs 1 wrong guess), New word and Reset streak. A scoreboard shows Streak, Best streak, Wins and Losses.

Copy: Word lists (original, 24 each, lowercase): Animals such as heron, badger, lemur, otter, falcon, gecko; Food such as pancake, noodle, ginger, pretzel, saffron, brioche; Places such as harbour, meadow, canyon, glacier, island, lagoon; Science such as orbit, magnet, fossil, quartz, enzyme, galaxy. Messages: "Well done! The word was HERON." and "The lantern is resting. The word was HERON. Try again!"

Interaction: Choosing a category starts a new word. Pressing a physical letter key (a to z) or an on-screen key guesses it once; correct letters fill all matches with a 200ms pop, wrong letters add a stage, play a gentle shake on the keys row and dim the lantern. Hint reveals the first unrevealed letter and counts as a wrong guess (disabled when only one wrong guess remains). Win when all letters are revealed (streak +1, update best, record in localStorage inside try/catch); lose at 7 wrong guesses (streak resets). New word never repeats the last 5 words. Keys already used are disabled with aria-disabled and an accessible name that includes the result ("A, correct" or "Q, wrong"). The word display is a live region summarised for screen readers ("Word: h _ r o n. 4 letters left. Wrong guesses 2 of 7."), updated politely after each guess, and the keyboard is reachable by Tab with arrow-key movement. The game never traps focus; Escape does nothing.

Responsive: Under 900px stack the scene above the game with the scene at 16:10; keys become 9 columns with 44px minimum touch size; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Correct and wrong keys are distinguished by text states (a tick or a strike and the accessible name), not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shake, no float-up animation, no fireflies drifting). Invented names only.
