Build a single-page "Lights Out" game: the classic toggle puzzle where pressing a lamp flips it and its four neighbours, and the goal is to turn every lamp off. It runs entirely in the page, with generated solvable levels, a real solver for hints and a moody painted night-room setting.

Canvas & base: A dim, painted night room fills the page: deep indigo wall #151a3a to #0c1026, a tall window with a crescent moon and stars, a wooden desk at the bottom with a faint reflection, a pool of warm lamp-glow on the desk, a hanging string of bulbs and a vignette. The game board sits on the desk in a dark walnut frame (#3a2414 with a 2px #6b4528 bevel), cells in #1a1630. Lit lamps glow amber #ffc247 with a warm halo; unlit lamps are dark glass #2a2540 with a faint rim highlight. Text #f3ecff (14.9:1), secondary #b6acd0 (8.4:1), success mint #7de0b0, hint cyan #5fd8ff. Invented game title only.

Typography: "Sora" 600 for the title and level label (clamp 32 to 56px); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 16px; "JetBrains Mono" 500 for counters (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, amber.

Imagery & art direction: Each cell is a painted glass lamp built in inline SVG or layered CSS: a round bulb with a radial gradient (white-hot core #fff3c4, amber #ffc247, deep orange edge #e0792a) when lit, a soft outer glow (box-shadow 0 0 30px rgba(255,194,71,.55)) and a specular highlight arc; when unlit a dark glass sphere with a cool blue-grey rim light and a tiny filament line. Flipping animates a 200ms glow fade and a tiny scale (1.0 to 0.94 to 1.0). The brass lamp base (a small rounded cap) sits under every bulb. The room's ambient light level follows the number of lit lamps: more lamps lit means a warmer glow on the desk and wall; when all are off the room drops to moonlight blue and a few fireflies (6 small dots drifting slowly) appear. A level-complete overlay uses a gentle star burst of 20 small stars that fade out over 1.4 seconds. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 dim room or desk photograph as a backdrop with a 55% indigo overlay.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Puzzle", title "Lights Out" and a one-line rule ("Press a lamp to flip it and its neighbours. Turn every lamp off."). Game area centred: HUD row with Level, Moves, Best (per level), and a Lamps lit count; the board (3 by 3 at levels 1 and 2, 4 by 4 at levels 3 and 4, 5 by 5 from level 5, up to level 10; board width min(86vw, 480px)); controls under the board: Undo, Restart level, Hint, New level (skips to a different puzzle of the same size), and Previous or Next level buttons. A small legend below ("Hint highlights a lamp that is part of a perfect solution").

Copy: Win message "All dark. Well done in N moves." with "Best: M" and a Next level button; hint message "Try the highlighted lamp." Status lines announce state.

Interaction: Generate a level by starting from all off and applying a seeded random set of presses (3 + level presses, avoiding pairs that cancel) so every puzzle is solvable. Pressing a lamp toggles it and its up, down, left and right neighbours inside the grid and increments Moves. Undo pops the last press (Moves decrements). Hint runs a Gaussian elimination over GF(2) on the press matrix to find a minimal-weight solution (try the nullspace combinations for 3x3, 4x4 and 5x5) and highlights the first lamp from the current optimal solution with a cyan pulsing ring for 2 seconds; using hints is counted ("Hints used: 2") and shown. Solved when all lamps are off: show the win overlay, record the best moves per level in localStorage inside try/catch and unlock the next level. Keyboard: the board is role="grid" with cells role="gridcell" and aria-pressed (lit); roving tabindex with Arrow keys to move focus, Space or Enter to press; U undoes, R restarts, H hints. Each cell has an aria-label such as "Row 2, column 3, lit". Announce the move result and lit count politely ("Lamp pressed. 7 lamps lit.") and the win. Touch targets are at least 56px on mobile (the board scales to fit).

Responsive: Under 700px the HUD becomes two rows, the board takes 92vw, controls wrap into a 3 by 2 grid; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with a dark offset ring. Lit or unlit is never colour only: lit lamps show a small sun glyph in the centre, unlit show a dot. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pulse, no star burst, no fireflies, instant state changes). Invented names only.
