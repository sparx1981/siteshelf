Build a single-page "Code Breaker" game (Mastermind) for an invented game room, "Brass & Marble": crack a hidden four-marble code in ten guesses using feedback pins. Six colours, optional duplicates, history of every guess and a hint. Everything runs in the page.

Canvas & base: A painted games-room table fills the page (green baize #1f5a3a with a soft lamp pool, a mahogany rail along the top and bottom, a brass ash tray, a pair of dice and a deck of cards in the corners, 12% grain, vignette). The game board is a warm walnut panel #5a3a22 with an inlaid lighter wood border, brass corner caps, and a hidden-code shield at the top (a brass plate with a keyhole that lifts at the end). Marble colours (all distinct in luminance and each carries a shape glyph): Ruby #e63946 with a circle, Amber #ffb703 with a triangle, Emerald #2a9d5c with a square, Sapphire #3a6fe0 with a diamond, Amethyst #9b5de5 with a star, Pearl #f1f1f1 with a plus. Feedback pins: black #14141a for the right colour in the right place, white #fafafa for the right colour in the wrong place. Text #fdf6e3 on dark (14.8:1). Invented brand only.

Typography: "Playfair Display" 700 for the title (clamp 32 to 52px); "Figtree" 500/700 for UI at 14 to 16px; "DM Mono" 500 for numbers and the attempt counter. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, brass #e0b04a.

Imagery & art direction: Marbles are the signature illustration. Each is a circle with a radial gradient (light centre-left, saturated body, darker rim), a crisp white specular highlight arc at the upper left, a subtle inner swirl (a translucent curved stroke) and a soft drop shadow on the wood; empty slots are recessed wells (dark radial gradient with an inner shadow and a thin brass ring). Feedback pins are tiny glossy spheres in a 2 by 2 cluster beside each row. The board has a brass-plated hinge detail and rivets, rows separated by thin routed grooves, and the current row is lifted slightly with a soft glow and a brass arrow marker. The hidden-code shield is a brass plate with an engraved pattern; at game end it slides up to reveal the four code marbles with a short sparkle. A palette tray at the bottom looks like a velvet-lined tray with six marbles sitting in shallow dishes. Add a gentle table-lamp light gradient across the baize. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 games-table photograph for the backdrop with a 40% green overlay.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Brass & Marble" and title "Code Breaker". Left or centre: the board with 10 guess rows (newest at the bottom or top: choose bottom-up with row 1 at the bottom is classic; use top-down for accessibility: row 1 at the top), each row showing four slots and a 2 by 2 feedback cluster plus a row number. Above the rows the shield. Right (or below on mobile): the palette tray (six marbles as buttons with names), controls: Submit guess, Clear row, Hint, New game, and toggles Allow duplicates, Show shapes. A status area states the attempts used and a legend for the pins. On win or loss an overlay summarises.

Copy: Legend: "Black pin: right colour, right place. White pin: right colour, wrong place." Messages: "Cracked it in 6 guesses!", "Out of guesses. The code was Ruby, Amber, Pearl, Sapphire." and "Hint: position 3 is Emerald."

Interaction: Pick a marble from the palette (click, tap or digits 1 to 6) to place it in the first empty slot of the current row; clicking a filled slot removes it; clicking an empty slot selects it (a brass ring) so the next palette pick goes there. Submit (Enter or the button) is enabled when all four slots are filled; it computes exact matches then colour-only matches (standard algorithm that respects duplicates), adds the pins (sorted black first), locks the row and advances. Ten guesses maximum. With "Allow duplicates" off the code has four different colours and the game prevents submitting a guess with repeats (a gentle message). Hint reveals one position of the code in a small marker on the shield and counts as a used hint (shown in the result). Keyboard: Tab to the palette and slots; arrow keys move between slots in the current row; 1 to 6 place colours; Backspace clears the focused slot or the last filled slot; Enter submits; N starts a new game. Each row is a group with an aria-label that includes the colours and the feedback after submission ("Guess 3: Ruby, Amber, Pearl, Sapphire. 2 right place, 1 right colour."); announce the feedback politely after each guess. Persist best (fewest guesses) and the win count in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 760px the palette tray sits below the board in a single row, marbles scale to 44px, the rows compress to 52px height; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Colour is never the only cue: every marble carries its shape glyph and an accessible name (toggle "Show shapes" hides the glyphs visually only when the visitor opts out). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shield slide, no sparkle, no row lift). Invented names only.
