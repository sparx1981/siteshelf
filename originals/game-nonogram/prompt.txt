Build a single-page "Nonogram Puzzle" game for an invented craft-puzzle studio, "Stitch & Solve": fill cells on a 10 by 10 grid so each row and column matches its number clues, and a hidden picture appears as coloured cross-stitches when you solve it. Three pictures (Heart, Cat, House). Everything runs in the page.

Canvas & base: A painted craft-table background (warm linen #e9dcc4 with a soft window-light gradient, a wooden embroidery hoop frame, a pair of scissors, three thread spools, a needle with a trailing thread and a tomato pincushion at the edges). The puzzle is an aida-cloth square inside a wooden embroidery hoop: cloth cream #f6ecd6 with a fine woven grid texture (small cross-hatch lines at 10% opacity), hoop wood #b98a52 with a brass screw. Filled cells are drawn as cross-stitches (an X made of two thread strokes with round caps and a slight shadow) in thread colour #3a4f6b; marked cells are small pale circles #b9a98a; satisfied clues turn #8a8070 with a strike; error cells (after Check) get a red thread #c0392b stitch with an "x" marker. Ink/text #2b2218 (14.6:1). Thread palette for the reveal: orange #e8803a, dark brown #4a2e18, red #d9453a, cream #fff3d6, blue #3a6fe0, brown #8a5a30. Invented studio name only.

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the title (clamp 30 to 50px); "Figtree" 500/700 for UI and clue numbers at 14 to 16px; "DM Mono" 500 for the timer and clue digits (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, #8a4b1f.

Imagery & art direction: The hoop and cloth are the artwork. Draw the inner hoop as an oval or round ring with inner and outer bevels, a metal tightening screw and a soft contact shadow, the cloth with a visible weave (a pattern of tiny 3px squares in two tones), slightly puckered edges (a subtle radial shading) and a few loose threads. Each filled cell is a pair of crossing thread strokes (stroke width about 22% of the cell, round caps, slightly different lengths and angles per cell for a hand-stitched feel) with a soft drop shadow so it looks raised. When the puzzle is solved the black-and-white stitches recolour into the picture's thread colours with a sweeping shimmer, the clues fade and a small label "Stitched!" appears with the picture name; a needle with thread glides across once. Thread spools and scissors sit on the table with soft shadows. 12% grain and a vignette. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 craft-table photograph for the backdrop and a 1200x1200 aida cloth texture.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Stitch & Solve" and title "Nonogram Puzzle". Toolbar: Picture select (Heart, Cat, House), Mode toggle (Fill or Mark) as a segmented control, Undo, Hint, Check, Reset and Timer. Main: the grid in the hoop with column clues above (stacked numbers, bottom-aligned) and row clues to the left (right-aligned), cell size 40px on desktop, scaling with the viewport; a small preview thumbnail of the live pattern in the corner. Under the grid a status line ("34 of 50 stitches placed").

Copy: Pictures defined as 10 by 10 bitmaps: Heart (red), Cat (orange with dark eyes), House (red roof, cream walls, blue windows, brown door). Messages: "Looks good so far. No wrong stitches." (Check), "3 stitches are wrong." and "Stitched! Heart finished in 04:12."

Interaction: Compute row and column clues from the picture. Click or tap a cell to toggle Fill (empty to filled to empty); in Mark mode toggle a mark; right-click or a long press (350ms) marks regardless of mode. Press and drag across cells to paint a line: the first cell's new state is applied to all cells the pointer passes over (limited to the same row or column as the start). A line's clue digits grey out and strike through when the filled cells in that line match its clue exactly. Check highlights wrong stitches (filled where the picture is empty) and reports a count; Hint places one correct stitch; Undo steps back through actions; Reset clears. The puzzle is solved when the filled cells equal the picture's filled cells (marks are ignored). Keyboard: the grid is role="grid" with roving tabindex; arrow keys move; Space toggles fill; X toggles mark; Backspace clears; U undoes; the focused row and column clues are highlighted. Each cell has an aria-label ("Row 3, column 7, filled" or "empty" or "marked"). Announce Check results and completion politely; announce the clue satisfaction of the current row or column briefly ("Row 3 complete"). Timer starts on first action and stops on solve; best times per picture saved in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 700px the cells scale to fit (minimum 28px), clue font drops to 12px, toolbar wraps, the table props (spools, scissors) hide; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #2b2218 outline with white offset on buttons; the focused cell shows a thick outline. Fill, mark and error are distinguished by shape (X stitch, small circle, red stitch with a marker), not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no shimmer, no needle glide). Invented names only.
