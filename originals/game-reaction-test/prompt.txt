Build a single-page "Reaction Time Test" for an invented club, "Pole Position Labs": a motorsport-style start-lights test. Five red lights come on one by one; after a random pause they all go out and you react as fast as you can. Five attempts give an average and a best time, with false-start detection and a simple result chart. Everything runs in the page.

Canvas & base: A painted race-track scene fills the page: a vivid dusk sky (#1b2c5a to #e8744a at the horizon), a grandstand silhouette with a crowd of tiny coloured dots, floodlight towers, an asphalt straight in perspective with a white start line and chequered grid boxes, rumble strips in red and white, and an overhead gantry across the top of the playfield. The control panel below is a dark glass card rgba(14,18,36,.86) with a 1px #3a4270 border and 22px radius. Light colours: lamp off #3a1418, lamp on #ff2a2a with a warm glow, GO state mint #5dffa6, false start amber #ffb020. Text #f4f6ff (15.8:1), secondary #b3bbe0 (8.5:1). Invented club name only.

Typography: "Orbitron" is not available, so use "Unbounded" 600 for the big time readout and headings (clamp 40 to 96px for the readout); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 16px; "JetBrains Mono" 500 for the attempt table (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, #ffb020.

Imagery & art direction: The gantry is the hero illustration, drawn in inline SVG: a dark steel truss spanning the width with five lamp pods, each pod a rounded rectangular housing (#1a1d2e) containing two stacked circular lamps with a recessed bezel, a glossy lens (radial gradient with a white specular spot) and a glow bloom (blurred circle) when lit; slim support towers at both ends with bolts and cable runs, a small digital sign in the middle reading "READY" or "WAIT" or "GO" in a 7-segment style, and soft light spill onto the track below when lamps are lit. The track below is painted in perspective: converging asphalt lines, a white start line, grid boxes, tyre skid marks and subtle heat shimmer near the horizon (a faint animated gradient). When the lights go out the whole scene gets a quick mint flash in the sign and a brief shutter-like vignette; on a false start the sign flashes amber with the text "FALSE START" and the track gets a soft red tint. 12% film grain and a vignette over the whole scene. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 start-line photograph for the backdrop with a 45% navy overlay.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Pole Position Labs" and title "Reaction Time Test". The gantry scene (aspect 16:7) with a large central button area: "Start" (or "Click, tap or press Space when the lights go out"). Under the scene: the big readout (the last time in milliseconds, or the state message), attempt dots (5 circles filling as attempts complete), the average and best readouts, an attempt table (number, time, note such as "Early" or "Fast") and a bar chart of the five times with a dashed average line. A rating line ("Faster than most. Typical is about 250 ms.") appears after five attempts, plus Reset and Try again buttons.

Copy: States: "Press Start", "Lights are coming on...", "Hold...", "GO!", "Too early. Wait for the lights to go out." and "Time: 237 ms". Ratings by average: under 200 ms "Lightning. Elite territory.", 200 to 249 "Faster than most.", 250 to 299 "Right around typical.", 300 to 399 "A little slow, try again.", 400 and over "Warm up first, then try again."

Interaction: Start begins an attempt: lights illuminate one every 800 ms (five lamps), then hold for a random 700 to 3000 ms and all lamps go out at the same moment; record performance.now() at that frame (use requestAnimationFrame to timestamp after the DOM update) and measure on the next pointerdown, mousedown, touchstart or keydown (Space or Enter). Responses before the lights go out are false starts: show "Too early", do not count the attempt and restart that attempt after 1.5 seconds. After five valid attempts compute the average and best (also saved as personal best in localStorage inside try/catch), render the chart (SVG bars, height proportional with a 100 to 500 ms axis) and the rating. The page prevents double-triggering by ignoring input for 150 ms after a result. The primary button is a real button; Space and Enter on the page also work when no button has focus. All states are announced politely (but the lamp sequence is not announced lamp by lamp: announce "Lights coming on" once and "Go" once so the cue is available as text). Respect that some visitors cannot see the lamps: provide an optional "Use sound cue" switch (default off) that plays a short beep (Web Audio inside try/catch) at lights-out, and show a clear text cue "GO!" in the sign.

Responsive: Under 700px the scene crops to 16:9 centred on the lamps, the chart stacks under the table, buttons are full width with 52px minimum height; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries, audio files or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. State is conveyed in words as well as colour. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no flash, no shimmer, no vignette pulse; timing and logic unchanged). Invented names only.
