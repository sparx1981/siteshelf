Build a single-page "Mini Sudoku" game for an invented puzzle press, "Pencil & Page": a six-by-six sudoku (digits 1 to 6, boxes two rows by three columns) with a generated puzzle that has exactly one solution, pencil notes, conflict highlighting, hints, undo, a timer and three difficulties. It runs entirely in the page.

Canvas & base: A painted wooden desk fills the page (warm walnut #7a5230 to #4a2e18 with long grain lines and a soft lamp glow from the upper left); the puzzle sits on a cream paper sheet #fbf5e6 tilted -1deg with a soft shadow, paper fibre texture and a printed header. Ink #1f1b16 (16:1) for printed givens, pencil blue-grey #3a4f6b (8.4:1) for the player's entries, notes #6a7a90 (4.6:1 at 12px bold), selected cell #fff1b8 with a 3px #1f1b16 outline, peer highlight #f3ead2, same-digit highlight #ffe08a, conflict #ffd5cf with a small "!" marker and #a8281c digit, success green #2f7a4a. Controls on a cream card with a #d8cdb2 border. Invented brand only.

Typography: "Newsreader" 600 for printed givens and headings (cell digits 34px); "Caveat" 700 for the player's pencilled digits (38px) and the tiny notes (13px, 700); "Figtree" 500/700 for UI at 14 to 16px; "DM Mono" 500 for the timer. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, #8a4b1f.

Imagery & art direction: The desk is a painted still life in inline SVG: wood planks with grain lines and knots, a hexagonal yellow pencil with a brass ferrule and pink eraser lying diagonally at the lower right with a long soft shadow, a pencil shaving curl, a white block eraser, a mug of tea with a faint steam curl at the upper right, and a pair of round reading glasses at the lower left; 12% film grain and a vignette. The paper sheet has a printed masthead "Pencil & Page · Mini Sudoku" with a small ornament (three diamonds), a thin double border around the grid, fine 1px inner grid lines in #1f1b16 at 55% and 3px box borders, and a slightly rough ink effect on printed digits (a very small SVG displacement filter at 0.6 scale). Pencil digits have a slight random rotation (-2 to 2 degrees) per cell for a hand-written feel. A completion stamp ("Solved!") appears as a rotated rubber stamp in green with a rough edge. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 desk photograph for the backdrop and a 1200x1200 paper-texture scan for the sheet.

Layout: Page centred. Masthead eyebrow "Pencil & Page" and title "Mini Sudoku". A toolbar above the sheet: Difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) segmented control, a New game button and the timer. The sheet holds the 6x6 grid (max 460px square, 72px cells at full size) with boxes of 2 rows by 3 columns. Below the grid: a number pad of six large digit buttons (with remaining counts as small badges, for example "3 left"), then action buttons Notes (toggle), Erase, Undo, Hint and Check. A status line reports progress ("26 of 36 filled").

Copy: Difficulty clue counts: Easy 22, Medium 18, Hard 14. Messages: "Looks good so far." (Check, no mistakes), "2 cells conflict." (Check, with conflicts), "Solved in 03:12. Nicely done." and "Hint: the cell in row 3, column 4 must be 5."

Interaction: Generate a full solved grid by randomised backtracking, then remove cells one at a time (symmetric where possible) keeping a unique solution by counting solutions up to 2 with a solver; stop at the clue target for the chosen difficulty. Given cells are locked. The selected cell is chosen by click, tap or arrow keys (roving tabindex on the grid, role="grid", cells role="gridcell" with aria-labels such as "Row 2, column 5, given 4" or "empty, notes 2 and 5"). Digits 1 to 6 place a number (or toggle a note when Notes is on or when Shift is held); Backspace, Delete or Erase clears; N toggles notes; Z or Undo reverses the last action (a history stack). Entering a digit removes that note from peers automatically. Conflicts (duplicate in row, column or box) show the "!" marker and the red digit immediately, and the Check button reports a count. Hint fills one empty cell with the solution value and counts hints used. The number pad disables a digit once six of it are placed correctly. The timer starts on first input and pauses when the tab is hidden; solving shows the stamp, the time and best time per difficulty saved in localStorage inside try/catch. Announce selected-cell moves and results politely without chattering (only after Check, Hint, conflicts appearing and completion).

Responsive: Under 560px the grid fills the viewport width minus 24px with 52px minimum cells, the number pad becomes a row of six 44px+ buttons, actions wrap in a 3 by 2 grid; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1f1b16 outline with white offset on buttons; the selected cell has the thick outline. Conflicts use a text marker and a changed digit colour, never colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no stamp animation, no cell transitions). Invented names only.
