Build a single-page "Whack A Mole" game for an invented garden-games site, "Tuft & Burrow": thirty seconds, nine holes, moles that pop up and hide, a combo multiplier and decoy garden gnomes you must not hit. It runs in the page with painted artwork, no assets.

Canvas & base: A painted sunny lawn fills the page (sky #8fd0f0 to #e8f6ff, rolling green hills in three greens, a white picket fence along the back, a scarecrow and a wheelbarrow at the edges, a few flowers and butterflies); the game board is a 3 by 3 grid of soil mounds on a lawn patch inside a rounded 22px panel with a thin #2f6b3a border. Mole fur #8a5a3a with a pink nose #f2a0b0 and cream belly #e8d2b0; gnome hat #d9453a; soil #6b4528 to #3f2814; grass greens #6fbf4a, #4a9a3a and #2f7a2a. Text #1c2a16 on light panels (14.6:1) and white on dark scoreboards. Accent sun #ffcf3d. Invented site name only.

Typography: "Baloo 2" 600/700 for the title, scores and big countdown (clamp 32 to 64px); "Nunito" 400/600/700 for UI at 15 to 16px; tabular numerals for score and timer. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, #2f6b3a.

Imagery & art direction: The artwork carries the charm. Each hole is a painted soil mound in inline SVG: an elliptical dark opening with a soft inner shadow, a ring of lighter raised soil with small stones and grass tufts, and a front lip drawn in a separate layer so a mole can rise from behind it (clip the mole to the hole's upper half). The mole is a cute character: round head, small dark eyes with a white glint, a pink star-shaped or round nose with two nostrils, whiskers, tiny ears and paws gripping the edge; variants: normal (brown), golden (bonus, shimmering gold with sparkles, 3x points), and when whacked a dazed face (spiral eyes, stars circling). The decoy is a garden gnome with a red pointed hat, white beard and rosy cheeks. The hammer is a cartoon wooden mallet that swings on press with a small impact burst (star shape), dust puffs and a floating "+10" or "Oops -10" in a bouncy font. The background has gentle parallax (clouds drifting). Add 10% film grain and a soft vignette. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 garden or lawn photograph for the backdrop with a 25% white overlay.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Tuft & Burrow", title "Whack A Mole". HUD: Time left (large), Score, Combo (x1 to x5), Best. Difficulty selector (Easy, Normal, Hard) as a segmented radio group. The board (3 by 3) with each hole's number badge (1 to 9, mapped to the number pad layout 7-8-9 / 4-5-6 / 1-2-3). Start button and a Pause button. A short legend: "Hit moles. Golden moles are worth 3x. Do not hit gnomes."

Copy: Messages: "Ready? Press Start or Space", "Time! You scored N", "New best!" Difficulty text: Easy (moles stay up 1.2 s), Normal (0.9 s), Hard (0.6 s with two at once).

Interaction: A 30 second timer; moles pop up at random empty holes every 600 to 1100 ms (Easy), 450 to 850 ms (Normal) or 300 to 650 ms with up to two at once (Hard) and stay for the difficulty's duration; 12% are golden (3x points) and 12% are gnomes. Hitting a mole scores 10 times the combo multiplier (combo increases by 1 for each consecutive hit up to 5 and resets on a miss or a gnome hit); hitting a gnome costs 15 points and resets combo; clicking empty ground counts as a miss. Pointer, touch and keyboard all work: click or tap a mound, or press the digit keys 1 to 9 (number-pad layout) or move focus with arrow keys on the grid and press Space. Each hole is a button with an aria-label that states its content ("Hole 5, mole up", "Hole 2, empty", "Hole 7, gnome up") updated live. The custom hammer cursor follows the pointer on fine-pointer devices (cursor CSS or an absolutely positioned element) and the default cursor remains on touch. Pause stops timers. At time zero show the result, save the best score per difficulty in localStorage inside try/catch and announce the result politely. Announce only start, pause, end and new best (not every pop) to avoid noise; add a visually hidden running score line that can be read on demand.

Responsive: The board scales with the viewport (max 560px), holes are at least 80px square on mobile, HUD wraps into two rows; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries, audio or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1c2a16 outline with white offset. Mole type is conveyed by shape and by the accessible name, not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (moles appear and disappear without sliding, no hammer swing, no floating text animation, clouds static). Invented names only.
