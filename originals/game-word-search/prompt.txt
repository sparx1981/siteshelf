Build a single-page "Word Search" game for an invented puzzle press, "Seaside Puzzles": a 12 by 12 letter grid hiding a list of words in all eight directions, found by dragging across the letters (or by selecting a start and an end with the keyboard). Three themes change the word list and the painted border art. Everything runs in the page.

Canvas & base: A soft pastel puzzle-page background with a painted themed border (see imagery); the puzzle sheet is cream #fffaf0 with a 1px #e6d9c0 border, 22px radius and a soft shadow. Ink #1d2433 (15.4:1), secondary #5a6478 (5.8:1). Theme accents: Ocean teal #1f7a8c and coral #ff7a59, Garden green #3a7a4a and blossom pink #e8668b, Kitchen tomato #d0452e and butter #f2c14e. Found-word capsules are translucent theme colours at 55% opacity with a 2px darker outline, one colour per word from a 6-colour cycle. Cell hover #eef5f7, selection highlight #ffe58a. Invented brand only.

Typography: "Fredoka" 500/600 for the grid letters (clamp 18px to 28px, uppercase) and headings; "Nunito" 400/700 for UI and the word list at 15 to 17px; "DM Mono" 500 for the timer. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, theme accent.

Imagery & art direction: Each theme has a painted border and corner illustration in inline SVG: Ocean uses rolling wave bands in three teals along the bottom, a scattering of shells (spiral, scallop, starfish), a small sailboat and a lighthouse silhouette in a top corner, with soft gradient sky; Garden uses overlapping leaves in three greens, flowers (five-petal in pink and yellow), a watering can and a butterfly with a dotted flight path; Kitchen uses a gingham edge in tomato and cream, a wooden spoon, a whisk, a bowl of lemons and a herb sprig. The art sits behind and around the sheet (never under the letters), with 10% grain. The grid letters sit on subtle ruled paper. Found-word capsules are drawn as rounded SVG rectangles rotated to the word's direction over the letters with a soft shadow, and a small star pops when a word is found. A tiny painted icon for each word appears beside it in the list (a shell next to "shell", a flower next to "tulip", and so on, drawn as simple 2-colour glyphs where possible). Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 themed backdrop and 400x400 word illustrations.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Seaside Puzzles" and title "Word Search". Toolbar: Theme (Ocean, Garden, Kitchen) as a radio group, New puzzle, Hint (reveal the first letter of an unfound word), Timer and "Found 0 of 10". Main: two columns above 860px: left the grid (max 560px square) with a transparent SVG overlay for capsules and the live selection line; right the word list (10 words, two columns inside the card) where found words are struck through and tinted with the capsule colour and show a tick glyph. On mobile the list sits below the grid.

Copy: Word lists (10 each, 4 to 9 letters): Ocean - shell, anchor, lagoon, dolphin, harbour, current, seaweed, island, tide, coral; Garden - tulip, daisy, compost, trowel, pollen, lavender, hedge, seedling, orchard, mulch; Kitchen - whisk, ladle, skillet, spatula, saucepan, colander, grater, tongs, oven, pepper. Messages: "Found SHELL." and "You found all 10 words in 02:41."

Interaction: Generate the puzzle by placing each word (longest first) in a random one of eight directions at a random free position (allowing overlaps where letters match), fill empty cells with random letters weighted toward common letters, and ensure every word fits. Pointer interaction: pointerdown on a cell starts a selection; while dragging, snap the selection to the straight line (horizontal, vertical or diagonal) from the start through the pointer's current cell, showing a rounded highlight capsule; releasing checks the selected letters (also reversed) against unfound words; a match fixes the capsule in a theme colour, strikes the word through and announces "Found SHELL". Touch dragging prevents page scroll on the grid (touch-action none). Keyboard: the grid is role="grid" with roving tabindex cells (aria-label "Row 3, column 5, letter E"); arrow keys move focus; Space or Enter on a cell sets the start (announce "Start set at row 3, column 5"), then moving focus along a line and pressing Space or Enter again sets the end and checks the word; Escape cancels the selection. Hint highlights the first letter of a random unfound word with a pulsing ring for 3 seconds and counts hints used. Timer starts on first interaction, pauses when hidden, and completion shows a summary with the time and best time per theme saved in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 860px stack the list below the grid; the grid scales to the viewport width minus 24px with a 26px minimum cell; the painted border art simplifies to the bottom band only; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1d2433 outline with white offset; the active cell during keyboard selection also shows an arrow marker. Found state uses strike-through and a tick as well as colour. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no star pop, no pulsing hint ring). Invented names only.
