Interaction: Hovering or focusing the primary button makes nearby stars attract towards it (velocity nudged proportional to inverse distance) and draws extra lines from the button's centre to the 12 nearest stars; leaving releases them. Responsive: Star count drops to 50 under 700px; headline 12vw; no horizontal scroll at 390px.
