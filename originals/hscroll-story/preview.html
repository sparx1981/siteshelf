A short story in five chapters

The long way round

01 / 05

Leaving early

The road out of town was empty at dawn, and the map on the passenger seat had one line drawn across it in pencil. We were not going anywhere in particular. That was the whole idea.

02 / 05

The warm hours

By noon the windows were down and the radio had given up. We stopped for peaches at a roadside stand and ate them on the bonnet, watching the heat shimmer over the fields like water.

03 / 05

Into the green

The road bent into a forest so dense the light turned green. We drove with the engine off down the last hill, listening to the tyres whisper and a river somewhere below us.

04 / 05

The sea, at last

Then, with no warning at all, the land ended. The whole sky turned the colour of a blue we had no word for, and the pencil line on the map finally touched the edge of the page.

05 / 05

Home after dark

We drove back through the night with the windows up and nothing to say. It had not been the shortest way, and that was exactly why we would remember it.
