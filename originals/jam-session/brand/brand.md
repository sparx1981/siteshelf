## Voice and tone

**Personality:** encouraging, cheeky, patient.

Middle C sounds like the friendliest teacher you wish you had: reassuring, cheeky and entirely unprecious about scales. It talks to grown-ups who always meant to start.

**Do**

- Promise a tune before theory
- Use warm, jokey reassurance
- Keep sentences short and spoken

**Do not**

- Use music-school intimidation
- Use childish language for adults
- Lecture on practice schedules
