Interaction: Tabs follow the ARIA tabs pattern and swap snippets instantly. Copy writes the snippet to the clipboard inside try/catch and shows "Copied" for 1.5s. Before the first send the response pane shows the empty state "Press Send to see a response". Cards lift on hover. All interactive items have visible focus (3px #7c83ff).

Responsive: Under 820px panes stack; stats become two columns; no horizontal scroll at 390px (code lines scroll inside their own container).
