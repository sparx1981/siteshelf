Canvas & base: Ivory page #f8f6f0, deep navy #14213d for text and dark bands, secondary #5a6478 (5.9:1), brass accent #b8872b (used for lines and icons, with navy text on brass fills), risk red #c0392b, caution amber #d98a0b, ok green #2f7d4f. Document paper white #ffffff with a soft shadow and 1px #e5dfd0 border. Invented product "Clause & Co", contract review software for small legal teams.

Typography: "Spectral" 500/600 and italic for headings and the contract text (headline clamp 40px to 80px, line-height 1.04, tracking -.01em; contract body 15px/1.7); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI and body 17px. Headline: "Read every clause. Sign with confidence."

Layout: Header with wordmark "Clause & Co" (serif, brass ampersand) and links Product, Security, Pricing and a navy button "Book a demo". Hero left-aligned in two columns: left headline, 48ch sub "Clause & Co flags risky terms, explains them in plain English and tracks every contract from first draft to renewal.", buttons "Try the demo" (navy) and "See a sample report". Right: the interactive document card: header "Master Services Agreement, Harbour Ltd", filter chips (All, Risky 3, Missing 1, OK 2; aria-pressed) and six numbered clauses (1 Term, 2 Payment, 3 Liability, 4 Termination, 5 IP ownership, 6 Governing law) with 2 to 3 lines of realistic invented legal text each; risky clauses get a red left border and a small "Risky" tag, missing a dashed amber "Missing: data protection" block, ok green tick. Under the document a panel "Plain English" showing the explanation of the selected clause (e.g. for 3 Liability: "You are liable for unlimited damages. Ask to cap this at 12 months of fees."), plus a suggested rewrite button "Copy suggested wording". Below: a horizontal lifecycle timeline (Draft, Review, Sign, Renew) with dates and a progress bar, three feature cards and a navy CTA band.

Interaction: Filters dim non-matching clauses to 30% (200ms) and update a live count. Clicking or pressing Enter on a clause selects it (brass outline), scrolls the explanation into the panel with a typewriter-free fade, and highlights the key phrase with a marker background. Copy suggested wording uses the clipboard inside try/catch and shows "Copied". The timeline stage buttons can be clicked to preview each stage's summary; the progress bar animates to the chosen stage over 500ms.

Responsive: Under 900px the hero stacks (document below), timeline becomes vertical; no horizontal scroll at 390px.
