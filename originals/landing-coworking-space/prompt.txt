Canvas & base: Warm off-white page #faf6f0, ink #231a14, secondary #6e5f52 (5.6:1), accent tangerine #f26a1b, deep plum #3b1f3f for the dark band, cards white with 1px #eadfd2 borders and 20px radius. Free availability pips #2f9e5b, busy pips #e3d6c6. Invented coworking space "Hatch Rooms" in a converted print works in Manchester.

Typography: "Syne" 700/800 for headings (clamp 32px to 64px, line-height 1.05, tracking -.03em); "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 17px and UI. Headline: "Rooms that make work feel like a good idea."

Layout: Header (wordmark "hatch" with an orange arch glyph, links Spaces, Membership, Visit, button "Book a tour"). Hero: left headline, 48ch sub "Light, quiet, well-run space for freelancers and small teams. Desks, private offices, meeting rooms and a podcast booth, all on one key fob.", buttons "Book a tour" and "See spaces"; right a stacked photo-free collage built from CSS blocks (arches, circles in orange, plum, sand). A "Spaces" section with four tabs (Hot desk, Private office, Meeting room, Podcast booth). Each tab swaps a detail card: title, short copy, price (from £18 a day, £420 a month, £22 an hour, £15 an hour), three amenity chips and an availability grid for today: a row of 10 hourly cells (8am to 5pm) coloured free or busy, hover/focus tooltip "10:00, free". Next: "Membership" three plan cards (Day pass, Flex, Resident) with a radio-style picker highlighting the choice and updating a summary line. A plum band with stats and a "Book a tour" dialog.

Interaction: Tabs follow the ARIA tabs pattern (arrow keys, Home/End); the panel crossfades in 250ms. Availability cells are buttons that toggle a selection for booking (aria-pressed) and update "2 hours selected, £44". The tour dialog is a native dialog with showModal: name and email fields with validation, date chips (next five weekdays) and time chips (10:00, 12:00, 15:00); Confirm shows a success state "See you on Thursday at 12:00".

Responsive: Under 860px the hero stacks, tabs scroll horizontally in their own container, availability cells shrink; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus, labelled form fields, aria-live for selection summary. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no crossfade or float). Invented names only.
