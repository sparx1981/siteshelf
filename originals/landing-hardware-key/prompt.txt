Interaction: The key floats (translateY 8px, 5s) and tilts toward the pointer. Tap buttons trigger the staged animation over about 1.4s (log lines appear every 350ms). A Reset link clears the logs. Table rows highlight on hover and focus within.

Responsive: Under 860px stack columns and frames; table scrolls inside its own container; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Status text in aria-live, never colour alone. Visible focus 3px #1fe08a. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no floating, instant log). Invented names and example domains only.
