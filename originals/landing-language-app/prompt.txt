Canvas & base: Warm cream page #fff6e9, ink #2a1f3d, secondary #6a5b7f (5.9:1), coral #ff6a5c, indigo #4a3aff, sun #ffc933, mint #2fc9a0. Cards white with 2px #2a1f3d borders and 4px hard offset shadows (4px 4px 0 #2a1f3d), radius 20px, for a sticker-like feel. Invented app "Parley", a speaking-first language app.

Typography: "Bricolage Grotesque" 800 for headings (clamp 42px to 92px, line-height .98, tracking -.03em); "Nunito" 400/700/800 for body 17px and UI. Headline: "Talk first. Textbook later."

Layout: Header with wordmark "parley" (Bricolage 800 with a speech-bubble glyph), links Languages, Method, Pricing and button "Start free". Hero: left column with headline, 44ch sub "Parley gets you speaking real sentences on day one, with short voice lessons and a patient tutor that never sighs.", buttons "Start speaking" (indigo) and "See how it works". Right: a 480px play-area with eight floating phrase bubbles (rounded rectangles in coral, indigo, sun, mint, white) each showing a phrase in the chosen language; below the area, language chips: Spanish, Japanese, Italian, Korean. Under the hero: a two-card row, a "Streak" card (flame glyph, "12 day streak", week dots Mon to Sun with 5 filled) and a "Daily goal" card with a circular progress ring "8 of 10 minutes". Then a three-step method strip and a footer CTA.

Interaction: Bubbles drift with individual float animations (6 to 10s ease-in-out alternate, small x/y offsets). Clicking or pressing Enter on a bubble flips it (rotateX 180 over 500ms) to its English translation and a small speaker button; clicking again flips back. Switching language chips cross-fades the bubble set (200ms out, new phrases in with a pop). The ring animates its stroke from 0 to 80% on view. The streak week dots can be clicked to toggle for demo, updating the count text. Hover lifts bubbles and increases the shadow offset to 6px; focus ring 3px #4a3aff with offset.

Responsive: Under 860px stack columns; bubbles area becomes 360px tall, bubbles wrap into a 2-column grid without drifting; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Bubbles are buttons with aria-pressed and aria-labels describing language and translation. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no drifting, instant flips). Invented names only.
