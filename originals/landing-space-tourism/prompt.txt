Invented operator "Perigee Lines", suborbital and orbital tourism.

Typography: "Unbounded" 500/600 for headings (clamp 34px to 80px, line-height 1.05, tracking -.02em, uppercase for small labels at 12px with .2em tracking) and "Inter" 400/500/600 for body 17px. Headline: "The Earth looks better from here."

Layout: Fixed top bar with wordmark "PERIGEE LINES" and links Flights, Timeline, Book; button "Reserve a seat". Hero: giant moon (min 420px circle, radial-gradient #f2efe6 to #8f8a82, with 7 crater circles in darker tones and soft inner shadows) positioned right and moving up slower than the scroll (parallax factor .35); left side label "Orbital tourism, 2027 departures", headline, 46ch sub "Three days in low Earth orbit. Interaction: Moon translateY = scrollY * -0.3; star layers translate at .05, .1, .2 of scroll. Timeline line height equals scroll progress through the section; each card gains .in class via IntersectionObserver. Booking controls update summary live with aria-live. All buttons have hover glow and a 3px #ffb347 focus ring.

Responsive: Moon shrinks to 300px and moves behind the headline at 40% opacity under 860px; timeline becomes single column; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus, labelled controls, aria-live for the booking summary. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no parallax or drawing, cards visible). Invented names only.
