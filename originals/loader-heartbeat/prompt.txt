Canvas & base: Dark clinical monitor page #04100d with a fine green grid (1px lines at 12% every 24px, heavier every 120px) and a subtle CRT vignette. The trace is phosphor green #3dffa0 (on dark 14:1) with a soft glow and a brighter leading dot; the heart icon and bpm digits in #ff5a6e. Secondary text #8ec9ae (8:1). Panels #08201a with 1px #134334 borders. Invented patient-data platform "Pulse Ledger" (all data fictional).

Typography: "VT323" 400 for the large bpm readout and trace labels (readout 96px); "Space Grotesk" 500/700 for headings and controls (15 to 17px).

Layout: A full-width monitor card (max 980px, 16px radius) with a top status strip ("PULSE LEDGER · Bay 4", a green dot "Connected", a clock), a canvas trace area 280px tall, and a right-hand column (on desktop) with: a big beating heart icon, the BPM readout "72", a small "SpO2 98%" and "Rate steady" tag, and the loading block ("Loading patient chart... 37%") with a thin progress bar and rotating steps (Authenticating, Fetching vitals, Syncing labs, Almost ready). Below the card: controls: BPM slider (40 to 180, default 72), a Rhythm select (Normal sinus, Athletic, Fast, Slow), a Sweep speed select, and a Pause or Play button.

Interaction: A canvas draws a scrolling ECG waveform generated from a PQRST template (P wave, QRS spike with a sharp R, T wave) repeated at the chosen BPM: the trace scrolls left at a constant pixel speed while new samples enter on the right with a bright leading dot and a fading green tail; the heart icon scales 1.0 to 1.18 and a faint ring pulses in time with each R peak; the BPM readout eases to the slider value with a slight jitter of plus or minus 1 for realism (disabled when paused). The loader progress advances from 0 to 100 over about 12 seconds then holds and restarts after 2 seconds; the step text changes at 25% bands. Changing Rhythm alters template shape (athletic: lower rate and taller T, fast: shorter PR interval). The monitor region is role="progressbar" aria-label "Loading patient chart" with aria-valuenow updated every 5%, and the heart/bpm are aria-hidden; one polite announcement at 100%. No audio is played (a visual-only monitor).

Responsive: Under 760px the right column moves below the canvas (canvas height 200px); readout shrinks to 72px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline offset 3px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (the trace is drawn static as a repeating pattern, the heart does not scale, the readout shows a fixed value and progress steps without easing). Invented names and data only; not for medical use.
