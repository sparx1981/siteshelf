Layout: Header with eyebrow "Graphite & Gum", headline "Drawn while you wait." and a line. The drawing card centres an SVG canvas (360 by 240) showing the current shape being drawn: House, Star, Cat face, Lighthouse (four hand-built SVG paths composed of several strokes each), with a pencil that follows the stroke tip. Below the canvas: the counter "0%" in Caveat, a caption ("Sharpening pencils...", "Sketching outlines...", "Adding shadows...", "Nearly done...") that changes by progress band, and a thin pencil-line progress bar. Controls under the card: Shape radio chips (House, Star, Cat, Lighthouse), Speed slider (2s to 10s per cycle, default 5s), a Pause or Play button and a Replay button.

Interaction: Each cycle has three phases: draw (about 65% of the time) strokes appear in order via stroke-dasharray/dashoffset while the pencil rides each stroke's tip using getPointAtLength and rotates slightly; hold (10%) with a tiny pencil wobble; erase (25%) where the pencil flips to its eraser end and sweeps over the drawing right to left, strokes fading by clip-path or opacity in reverse order with small eraser crumb dots falling. The percentage counts 0 to 100 over the draw phase then resets while erasing. Then the next shape starts automatically (loop). Changing shape restarts immediately. The whole animation is a progressbar: role="progressbar" aria-valuemin 0 aria-valuemax 100 aria-valuenow updated every 5% and aria-label "Sketch loading"; no continuous aria-live spam (a polite message at 100% only: "Sketch complete"). Pause (aria-pressed) freezes time; Replay restarts the current shape.

Responsive: Under 520px the card is full width (max 420px) with the canvas scaling by viewBox; controls wrap; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #d9482b with white offset.
