Canvas & base: Deep marine page #06263a with a soft light shaft (linear-gradient 160deg rgba(120,220,255,.12) to transparent) and floating bubbles in the background (generated tiny circles rising slowly). Vessel outlines in #d9f4ff at 3px with a faint inner glow; water in two layers: back wave #1aa6d6 at 70% and front wave #4cd0f5 with highlights; labels #eaf8ff (14:1), secondary #9fc7d8 (7:1). Invented hydration brand "Brookline Water".

Typography: "Fredoka" 500/600/700 for the large percentage and headings (percentage 72px); "Nunito" 400/600/700 for body and controls (15 to 17px).

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Brookline Water", headline "Fill it up." and a line. The stage shows a 260px by 320px SVG vessel centred on a soft platform with reflection. The percentage ("0%") sits centred over the vessel in white with a subtle text shadow (swapping to dark ink #06263a when the water level passes beneath the text for contrast). Below: a status line ("Filling... 42%"), then controls: Vessel chips (Drop, Beaker, Heart, Round flask), a Level slider (0 to 100) that sets the target, a Mode switch "Auto-fill loop" (default on: fills 0 to 100 over 6 seconds, holds 1s, drains, repeats), and Speed select (Calm, Medium, Rolling) for the wave animation speed and height.

Interaction: The water is two SVG paths (sine waves 2x the vessel width) clipped by the vessel shape via clipPath; each wave animates translateX (back 6s, front 4s, linear loop) and the whole water group translates vertically to the level with eased smoothing (requestAnimationFrame lerp). A few bubbles rise inside the water. In auto mode the level follows the loop and the slider tracks it; moving the slider manually switches to manual mode (the switch turns off) and the water eases to the chosen level. When the level reaches 100 a short celebratory splash (ripple rings above the vessel top and a "Full!" tag) plays. The vessel container is role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuetext ("42 percent full") and aria-label "Water level"; updates are throttled to every 5 percent and a polite live message announces "Full" at 100.

Responsive: The stage scales with the viewport (SVG viewBox); controls wrap under 560px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffe27a with dark offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (waves are static flat curves, level changes without easing, no bubbles or splash; auto-loop off by default). Invented names only.
