Canvas & base: A calm financial tool on #f4f6f3 with white cards (1px #dfe4dc border, 16px radius, shadow 0 1px 2px rgba(20,40,30,.05)). Ink #17261f, secondary #5b6b62, accent deep green #0f6b4a, interest colour amber #d98a1c, grid lines #e6ebe4. Invented brand "Fernlane Finance"; all figures are illustrative.

Typography: "Figtree" (400, 500, 600, 700) for interface text and "Fraunces" (600) for the large monthly payment figure. Use tabular-nums for every number. Labels 13px 600 uppercase tracking .06em in secondary colour; payment figure clamp(44px,7vw,68px).

Layout: Two columns at 1100px max width. Left card "Your loan" with three inputs, each a number field paired with a range slider: Loan amount (£5,000–£1,000,000, default £250,000), Interest rate (0.1–15%, step 0.05, default 4.9%) and Term (1–40 years, default 25). Below them a segmented control for "Repayment" versus "Interest only". Right column: a result card with the big monthly payment, three small stats (Total repaid, Total interest, Payoff date), a donut chart of principal versus interest with a centre label of the interest share, then a balance-over-time area chart and a collapsible "Year by year" table.

Interaction: Everything recalculates live as sliders move or numbers are typed; the number field and slider stay in sync and clamp to range, with the field showing a gentle error state when empty or out of range. Payment uses the standard annuity formula, handling zero rate. Interest-only shows interest per month and the balance as a flat line. The balance chart is hand-drawn SVG with a hover or arrow-key readout (vertical guide line, dot, tooltip with year, balance, interest paid so far). The donut animates its arc on change unless reduced motion is set. The table lists year, opening balance, interest, principal, closing balance and sits inside a details element.

Responsive: Below 860px stack to one column with the result card first. Use minmax(0,1fr) grids so nothing overflows at 390px. Sliders get 44px touch height. Charts scale with viewBox.

Rules: Single HTML file with a Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. Add [hidden]{display:none!important}. Currency formatting via Intl.NumberFormat with try/catch fallback. Label every control, announce the monthly payment via an aria-live polite region, show a small "Illustrative only, not financial advice" note. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
