Canvas & base: A calm coastal-walks app called "Tidewalk" shown inside a phone frame (390 x 780px, 36px radius, 10px #0e2a33 bezel) centred on a #e9f1f2 page on desktop; on screens under 560px the frame becomes the full viewport with no bezel. App background #f7fbfb, cards #ffffff with 1px #d6e6e8 borders, text #0e2a33, secondary #4c6b72 (5.6:1). Accent sea teal #0f8b8d, secondary accent sand #f0b86b. Sheet background #ffffff with a soft top shadow.

Typography: "Sora" 600/700 for headings and tab labels; "Inter" 400/500 for body (15px). Tab labels 11px, 600.

Layout: Inside the frame, a scrolling screen area with a status-bar strip and a screen per tab (Today, Routes, Saved, You), each with a generated SVG coastline illustration header and a short list of cards. A 72px bottom tab bar holds five items: Today, Routes, a raised 60px round centre button (sand with a dark plus icon that rotates 45 degrees when the sheet is open), Saved, You. The active tab shows a teal pill indicator that glides between tabs. The centre button opens a bottom sheet sized to 88% of the frame height with a 40px drag handle, a title "Plan a walk", a 2 by 3 grid of quick actions (Start now, Tide times, Add a stop, Invite a friend, Download map, Safety tips) and below it a list of three suggested routes with distance and time.

Interaction: The sheet has three snap states: closed (off-screen), half (about half its height, scrim at 30%) and full (top at the status bar, scrim at 50%). Dragging the handle or header with a pointer follows the finger exactly, then on release snaps to the nearest state using position plus velocity, with a 320ms cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1) transition; dragging down from half closes it. The handle is role="slider" with aria-valuemin 0, aria-valuemax 2, aria-valuenow and aria-valuetext (Closed, Half open, Fully open); ArrowUp/ArrowDown change state, Home closes, End opens fully, and Escape closes and returns focus to the centre button. The sheet content scrolls only when fully open. Clicking the scrim closes. Tabs switch screens with a 200ms cross-fade and are a proper role=tablist with arrow-key roving focus; the centre button is not part of the tablist. While closed the sheet is inert and hidden from assistive tech. Quick actions show a brief toast inside the frame. On first load, after 700ms, the sheet rises to half height once as a hint (skipped under reduced motion).

Responsive: Under 560px the frame fills 100dvh and 100vw with safe-area padding at the bottom; the quick-action grid stays 3 columns on desktop and 2 on narrow screens; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #0f8b8d with white offset; tap targets at least 44px; icons aria-hidden with text labels. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no glide, instant snaps, no cross-fade). Invented names only.
