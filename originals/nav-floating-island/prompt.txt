Canvas & base: Soft off-white page #f3f1ec with generous sections (min-height 90vh each) in alternating tints (#f3f1ec, #e9efe8, #f0e9f2, #e8eef5). Ink #15171c. The island is a floating pill fixed 16px from the top, centred, background rgba(21,23,28,.92) with backdrop blur 14px, text #f6f4ef (15:1), border 1px rgba(255,255,255,.12), shadow 0 14px 40px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.45). Accent #ff6a3d. Invented studio "Orbit Studio".

Typography: "Sora" 600/700 for the logo and headings (page headings clamp 40px to 88px); "Inter" 400/500/600 for nav items and body (15px).

Layout: Collapsed island is 280px wide by 52px: logo "orbit" with a small orange dot at the left, the current section name in the centre in a fade-swapping label ("Home", "Work", "Studio", "Journal", "Contact"), and a round menu button at the right. The expanded island is 520px wide and grows downward to about 300px: a two-column grid of links with small descriptions (Home, Work, Studio, Journal, Contact, Careers), a search field and a primary orange button "Start a project". Page sections: Home hero (headline "We shape calm digital products."), Work (three cards), Studio (paragraph and stats), Journal (three article rows), Contact (large email link and form-free CTA).

Interaction: Clicking the menu button (aria-expanded, aria-controls) or pressing Enter/Space expands the island with a spring-like width/height transition (450ms cubic-bezier(.3,1.3,.5,1)); content fades in with stagger; Escape or outside click (event.composedPath) closes it; focus stays inside while open (Tab cycles, Shift+Tab reverse) and returns to the button on close. Scrolling down more than 80px shrinks the collapsed island to a 52px circle showing only the logo dot and the menu button (width 112px); scrolling up restores the full pill. The current section name updates via IntersectionObserver. Link clicks smooth-scroll and close the island. The island also expands automatically when the button receives keyboard focus via Tab (no, only on activation).

Responsive: Under 560px the island is 92vw collapsed and expanded a single column; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Navigation landmark labelled; aria-current on the active link; visible focus 3px #ff6a3d with white offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no spring, instant transitions, no auto-shrink). Invented names only.
