Canvas & base: A light studio page #f5f2ea with a hero and two sections behind the menu; ink #1a1814. The overlay is full-screen #14120f with a right-hand preview panel whose colour changes per link (Work #ff5a3c, Studio #3c6bff, Journal #14b88a, Careers #ffcc3c, Contact #b58cff) and text in #f5f2ea on the dark side (16:1). Invented design studio "Marrow & Finch".

Typography: "Syne" 700/800 for overlay links (clamp 44px to 116px, line-height 1, tracking -.04em, uppercase) and the preview number; "Inter" 400/500 for small text (15px) and labels (12px uppercase .14em tracking).

Layout: Fixed header over the page: wordmark "Marrow & Finch" at left and a "Menu" pill button at right (with a two-line hamburger icon). The overlay has two columns: left a numbered list of five links ("01 Work", "02 Studio", "03 Journal", "04 Careers", "05 Contact") each with a short descriptor at the right of the number, plus footer row with email hello@marrowfinch.example, social text links (Instagram-like invented "Pixelwall", "Notes") and city "Bristol, UK"; right an image-free preview panel (full-height rounded rectangle) showing a huge number matching the hovered link, a title, one sentence, and a pattern made of CSS shapes unique to each link (circles for Work, stripes for Studio, waves via radial gradients for Journal, grid dots for Careers, concentric rings for Contact).

Interaction: Opening reveals the overlay with clip-path: circle(0 at the button centre) to circle(150% at the button centre) over 700ms cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1); links rise from translateY(110%) inside overflow-hidden lines with 60ms stagger starting 250ms in; footer row and preview fade in at 600ms. Closing reverses over 500ms. Hovering or focusing a link dims the other links to 35% and changes the preview colour, pattern and copy with a 300ms transition. Focus is trapped in the overlay (Tab/Shift+Tab loop), Escape closes and returns focus to the Menu button, the page behind gets inert and body scroll is locked. Clicking a link scrolls to the matching section (or closes with a toast "Opening Work…") and closes the overlay.

Responsive: Under 800px the preview panel moves above the links at 22vh height; link size 12vw; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Menu button has aria-expanded and aria-controls; overlay is role=dialog aria-modal; links in a nav list; visible focus 3px #ffcc3c. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (overlay fades in 150ms, no stagger or clip). Invented names only.
