Overview

Orchids reward patience. They like bright, indirect light, steady humidity and a pot that drains quickly. Most problems are watering problems.

Care

Water when the roots turn silver-grey, usually once a week. Soak for ten minutes, drain fully and never leave the pot standing in water.

Light

An east-facing window is ideal. Leaves should be light green; dark green means more light would help, yellow means less.

Soil

Use bark, not soil. Repot every two years in fresh bark so air can reach the roots.
