Canvas & base: Warm paper page #f6f1e7 with a faint horizontal ruled-line texture (repeating-linear-gradient every 32px, #e6dccb at 40%). Header strip #123a2e (deep green) with cream text #f6f1e7. Panels #fffdf8 with 1px #dccfb8 borders and a soft shadow 0 30px 60px -24px rgba(18,58,46,.45). Text #1b2a24, secondary #5b6b63 (5.3:1). Accent brass #b9852c, highlight mark background #f3dfa6. Invented organisation "Fernhill Library".

Typography: "Fraunces" 600/700 for the wordmark and group titles; "Figtree" 400/500/600 for everything else (15px, 17px in the input).

Layout: Sticky green header with the wordmark, three links (Catalogue, Events, Visit) and a wide search combobox (max 640px) with a magnifier icon, a clear button and a keyboard hint badge "/". Below the header a calm landing section (headline "Find your next good read", short paragraph, three feature cards) so there is something to scroll. The suggestion panel drops 8px under the input at the same width and has: (a) when the input is empty, a "Recent" group with up to five past searches (clock icon, text, a remove cross) and a "Popular right now" group of chips; (b) when typing, results grouped under Books, Events, Help and Branches with 2 to 3 items each, every item showing a small inline-SVG glyph, a title with the matching letters wrapped in <mark>, a one-line meta, and a right-aligned type tag. A row of filter chips (All, Books, Events, Help) sits at the top of the panel. A footer line explains the keys. A selected result is shown in a "You opened" card under the hero.

Interaction: Focusing the input or pressing "/" anywhere opens the panel. The input is role="combobox" with aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-autocomplete="list" and aria-activedescendant; the panel is role="listbox" with role="group" sections and role="option" rows. ArrowDown/ArrowUp move the active option through all groups, wrapping at the ends, and scroll it into view; Home/End jump to the first and last; Enter chooses the active option (or runs a plain search for the typed text if none is active); Escape first clears an active option, then closes the panel, then clears the text; Tab closes the panel. Hover sets the active option without rebuilding the DOM. Results rank prefix matches above substring matches, with a 120ms debounce, and an empty state "Nothing for '…' — try fewer words" appears with a suggestion. Chosen searches are stored as recents in localStorage (inside try/catch), de-duplicated, newest first, each removable by its cross, with a Clear all link. Outside click (event.composedPath) closes the panel. On load, after 500ms, the input is pre-filled with "lant" and the first result is active to show the panel at work (skipped under reduced motion). A polite aria-live line announces the number of results.

Responsive: Under 720px the header links collapse away and the search spans the full width on its own row; the panel becomes full width with a max height of 70vh and scrolls inside; tap rows are at least 48px tall; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #b9852c with white offset; the active option also has a 2px brass inset bar and tinted background. Icons aria-hidden. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no panel slide or row stagger). Invented names, titles and authors only; example.com-style contact details.
