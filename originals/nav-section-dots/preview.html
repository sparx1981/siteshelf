    Halcyon Atelier · Pennick Bay

    Made slowly, by hand.

    We throw, glaze and fire small batches of tableware in a converted net loft. Each piece carries the thumbprint of the person who made it.

    See the collection
    The process

    Clay, fire, patience.

    Twelve days from wet clay to a finished bowl: wedging, throwing, a slow dry, a bisque firing, three layers of glaze and a long cool-down in the kiln.

    The collection

    Eleven shapes, nine glazes.

    Bowls that sit comfortably in two hands, jugs that pour without drips and cups with a lip you will not forget. Glazes are mixed from local ash and sea-clay.

    The studio

    Light from the north.

    Six wheels, two kilns and a long table where we pack orders. Visitors can watch the throwing on Saturday mornings; the kettle is always on.
