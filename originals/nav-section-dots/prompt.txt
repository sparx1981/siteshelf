Canvas & base: A single long scrolling page for an invented ceramics studio "Halcyon Atelier" made of five full-height sections that alternate light and dark: Intro (cream #f4eee4), Process (charcoal #1c1b1a), Collection (clay #d9b99b), Studio (deep blue #14233a), Visit (cream #f4eee4). Text on light #231f1b (14:1), on dark #f4eee4. Accent terracotta #c4623a on light, #f0a37f on dark.

Typography: "Cormorant Garamond" 500/600/700 for large headings (clamp 44px to 120px, tight leading); "Work Sans" 400/500 for body (17px) and the tooltip labels (13px, uppercase, 0.12em tracking).

Layout: Each section fills at least 100vh with generous padding, one big heading, a short paragraph and a generated SVG illustration (vessels, kiln, glaze swatches, studio windows, a map pin) drawn with a seeded random function. A fixed navigation rail sits vertically centred on the right edge (20px inset): five 12px dots spaced 22px apart. The active dot expands into a 12px by 38px pill with an inner fill that rises from bottom to top as the user scrolls through that section (0 to 100%). The rail's colours adapt: whichever section is under the vertical centre of the viewport decides whether dots are dark ink or cream, with a 300ms colour transition. Hovering or focusing a dot slides out a label ("Intro", "Process", "Collection", "Studio", "Visit") to its left in a small pill with a 6px arrow. A tiny counter "02 / 05" sits above the rail.

Interaction: Clicking a dot smooth-scrolls to its section (instant under reduced motion) and moves focus to that section heading (tabindex -1). The rail is a nav with an ordered list of links; the current link has aria-current="location". Arrow Up/Down while a dot is focused move focus to the previous or next dot; Home/End go to the first and last; number keys 1 to 5 jump directly from anywhere. Section headings reveal with an IntersectionObserver (fade and rise 24px). Scroll handlers use requestAnimationFrame throttling and compute the current section from the viewport middle so it also works when the page is shorter than expected.

Responsive: Under 700px the rail shrinks to 9px dots inset 8px from the edge, tooltips are hidden and the counter is removed, while section padding reduces and the heading scale uses the lower clamp bound; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px outline in the contrasting accent with a 2px offset on the dots; dots have 44px invisible hit areas. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no smooth scroll, no reveal translate, no pill growth animation). Invented names only.
