Canvas & base: Editorial magazine page for an invented journal "Northlight Journal". Page #faf7f2 (warm white), ink #1c1a17 (16:1), secondary #5d574e (6.4:1), rules #e3dccf. Header over the hero starts transparent on a dark hero image area (#14202b with a generated SVG skyline of mountains and stars) with cream text; once scrolled it becomes frosted cream (rgba(250,247,242,.86) with 14px backdrop blur, 1px #e3dccf bottom rule, soft shadow) with ink text. Accent ember #d0542b (5.1:1 on cream).

Typography: "Playfair Display" 700/800 for the wordmark and article headings; "DM Sans" 400/500/600 for navigation, tagline and body (17px article body at 1.7 leading).

Layout: A fixed header, 128px tall at the top of the page and 60px when condensed. Expanded state shows, centred, the full wordmark "Northlight Journal" at 34px with a small tagline below it ("Stories from the long light"), left a date and edition ("Issue 41 · Autumn"), right a Subscribe button. Condensed state shows a left-aligned monogram "N" in a 36px ember circle, the nav links (Features, Essays, Field Notes, Archive) inline in the centre and a smaller Subscribe button; the tagline fades out and the wordmark shrinks and slides left as it swaps to the monogram. A 3px ember progress line sits along the bottom edge of the header showing how far through the page the reader is (scaleX from 0 to 1, origin left). Below the header: a 100vh hero with a big title "The last good winter" over the generated skyline, then a readable article column (680px) with four sections, a pull quote, and a footer.

Interaction: The header condenses after 60px of scroll and expands again only below 20px (hysteresis to avoid flicker); transitions are 350ms cubic-bezier(.3,.9,.3,1) on height, padding, font size, opacity and background. Active nav link updates to the section in view (IntersectionObserver) with an ember underline that slides. Progress uses requestAnimationFrame-throttled scroll handling. A skip link appears on focus. On mobile the nav links collapse into a "Menu" button with aria-expanded that opens a dropdown panel under the header (focus moves to the first link, Escape closes and returns focus, outside click closes using event.composedPath).

Responsive: Under 760px the header is 84px tall expanded and 56px condensed, the date/edition hides, the Subscribe button becomes an icon-sized pill, and the wordmark is 26px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #d0542b with cream offset (on dark hero use a #fff offset). Header contrast stays at least 4.5:1 in both states. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (condense instantly, no sliding underline, no progress easing). Invented names only.
