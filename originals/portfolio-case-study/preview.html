Overview

“I stopped checking three apps. I just check Meridian, and it’s right.”

Meridian serves 140,000 weekly riders across four counties. The old app answered the wrong question: where is the bus on a map, not whether I will make my connection.

I led the redesign from research through launch, reframing the product around a single promise — a departure time you can plan your morning around.
