Canvas & base: Sunny page #fff4d9 with a faint dotted pattern (radial-gradient #ecd9a6 1.5px at 20px grid), ink #261a05, secondary #6a5527 (6.4:1), cherry #e6355b, cobalt #2742ff, mint #19b37a, gold foil #f4c542. Cards white with 2.5px #261a05 borders and 5px 5px 0 #261a05 shadows, radius 20px. Invented product "Pip", a bite-sized habit app.

Typography: "Bricolage Grotesque" 800 for headings and prices (headline clamp 36px to 70px, line-height 1, tracking -.03em); "Nunito" 400/700/800 for body 17px. Headline: "Scratch it. Save it." Sub: "You've got one lucky coupon. Scratch the card to see your discount, then watch the prices drop."

Layout: Centred hero text, then a coupon strip: a ticket-shaped card (width 420px, notch cut-outs at both sides) with a scratch surface (silver foil gradient with the text "SCRATCH HERE" and sparkle) over the hidden code area showing "PIP-25" large with "25% off your first year". Below a progress line "Scratched 0%" and buttons "Reveal with keyboard" and "Apply code" (disabled until 55% scratched or revealed). Then three plan cards (Seed £0, Sprout £6 per month, Grove £14 per month) with price blocks; when the code is applied Sprout and Grove show the old price struck through, the new price in cherry with a "-25%" sticker that stamps in (rotate and scale), and a line "Code PIP-25 applied for 12 months". A "Remove code" link restores prices. Footer note "One coupon per customer. Terms apply."

Interaction: The foil is a canvas drawn with a silver gradient and noise-free pattern; pointer events erase with destination-out brush (radius 22px, round, smooth strokes between samples) for mouse and touch (touch-action none while scratching on the canvas only). Progress is computed by sampling alpha every 150ms (grid of 40x20 points). At 55% the remaining foil fades and confetti (24 CSS squares with random trajectories, 900ms) bursts, and Apply code enables. "Reveal with keyboard" instantly clears the canvas, announces the code via aria-live and focuses Apply. Apply updates prices with a 400ms tween and announces totals.

Responsive: Coupon scales to 92vw max 420px; plans stack; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. The code text is real DOM text under the canvas (readable by assistive tech); canvas aria-hidden. Visible focus 3px #2742ff. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no confetti, no stamp animation, instant tween). Invented names only.
