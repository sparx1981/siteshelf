Layout: A slim top bar (logo "STRIDE", nav, a cart button with a count badge). Main: a two-column product section. Adding the same variant increments quantity. Escape and the scrim close the drawer. The heart toggles.

Responsive: Stacks to a single column below 900px with the gallery first; the size grid keeps 5 columns; the drawer is full width on mobile.
