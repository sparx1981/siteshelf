Canvas & base: A bright boutique product page on #ffffff with soft grey panels #f3f1ed, ink #1b1a18, secondary #75706a, accent deep green #1e5a45 and sale red #c8322a. Invented store "Larkspur & Thread" selling one invented item, the "Harbour Wool Overshirt". Product images are generated CSS and inline SVG illustrations of the shirt in four colourways, drawn with simple shapes, no external images.

Typography: "Cormorant Garamond" (500, 600, 700) for the product name at clamp(34px,5vw,52px) and "Jost" (400, 500, 600) for UI. Price 24px tabular-nums.

Layout: A header with logo and a bag button showing a count. Two columns: left gallery with a vertical thumbnail rail (5 views: front, back, detail collar, detail cuff, flat lay) and a large 4:5 stage; right details with breadcrumb, name, rating stars with count, price (with struck-through original price), colour swatches (Moss, Ink, Rust, Oat), size buttons (XS to XXL, some out of stock and struck), a quantity stepper, Add to bag, and an accordion for Details, Fabric & care, Delivery.

Interaction: Hovering the stage on pointer devices shows a lens and a 2.2x magnified view that follows the cursor; on touch or click the stage opens a full-screen lightbox (native dialog) with arrows, thumbnails, pinch-less zoom toggle and Escape to close. Choosing a colour re-tints every view instantly and updates the label. Sizes with no stock are disabled with a tooltip "Out of stock"; picking a size updates availability text ("Only 3 left"). Add to bag requires a size (shows an inline error otherwise), animates the bag count and shows a small confirmation. Thumbnails use roving tabindex with arrow keys. Accordion uses buttons with aria-expanded.

Responsive: Below 900px the thumbnail rail becomes a horizontal row beneath the stage and the details stack; no overflow at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file with a Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. Add [hidden]{display:none!important}. Alt text or aria-labels on all views, visible focus, prefers-reduced-motion, invented names only.
