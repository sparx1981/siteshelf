Canvas & base: A light analytics workspace on #f3f5f9 with white panels (1px #e2e7ef border, 16px radius), ink #0f172a, secondary #64748b, accent blue #2563eb, funnel gradient stops from #2563eb to #7c3aed, success #16a34a, danger #dc2626. Invented product "Pipelane".

Typography: "Inter" (400,500,600,700) at 14px; large numbers 28px at 700 with tabular-nums.

Layout: Header with title, a period segmented control (7d, 30d, 90d), a segment select (All, SMB, Mid-market, Enterprise) and an Export button. Row of four KPI tiles (Pipeline value, Win rate, Avg deal size, Sales cycle) each with a delta pill and mini sparkline. A two-column area: left a funnel chart of six stages (Leads, Qualified, Demo, Proposal, Negotiation, Won) drawn as centred horizontal bars with stage name, count, conversion % from previous stage, and an animated width; right a win-rate line chart (SVG) with a hover crosshair and tooltip. Below, a regional leaderboard (bars with rank and revenue) and a deals table with sortable columns, stage chips and probability bars.

Interaction: Changing period or segment recomputes deterministic data and animates the funnel widths, KPI numbers and chart (instant under reduced motion). Hovering or focusing a funnel stage highlights it and shows drop-off details in a side caption; arrow keys move between stages. Table columns sort on click with aria-sort. Export builds a CSV Blob and triggers a download with a toast. Live region summarises changes.

Responsive: Layout stacks below 980px; table scrolls inside its panel; no page overflow at 390px.
