Build a single-page "Bundle Builder" for an invented outdoor-and-home brand, "Daybreak Supply": the shopper chooses any three products from a grid, watches them drop into a painted gift box, and unlocks trio pricing with a progress ring and a running savings readout. Products, prices and discounts are invented.

Canvas & base: Warm cream page #f7f1e5, ink #201b15 (15.9:1), secondary #675c4c (5.9:1), accent spruce #1f5e45 (white text 7.9:1), savings amber #e0912a, danger clay #b24a26. Cards #fffdf8 with a 1px #e6dcc6 border and 16px radius; selected cards get a 3px spruce outline and a tick badge. Prices in GBP, tabular numerals. Invented brand only.

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the headings, the savings figure and the box label (clamp 32 to 56px for the title); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 14 to 16px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, spruce.

Imagery & art direction: Nine painted product renders (inline SVG with a soft contact shadow and 10% grain on muted backdrops in oat, sage, clay and slate): a trail bottle, a camp mug and a canvas tote, each in three colourways chosen from a palette (Olive, Rust, Slate, Sand, Navy). The signature illustration is the gift box in the bundle panel: a kraft-brown box seen at a three-quarter angle with a dark interior showing three numbered slots, a loose lid leaning to the side with a woven ribbon and a bow, a paper tag reading "Pick 3" that changes to a savings amount when complete, a shadow on a pale surface and a sprinkle of tissue paper. When an item is chosen its small render animates (translate and scale, 500ms) from its card into the next free slot; when three are in, the lid slides on, the ribbon ties and a short confetti burst of 24 small rectangles in brand colours falls over 1.2 seconds. Add a soft spotlight vignette behind the box. Media slots for real photography: 800x800 product photographs per colourway, and a 1200x900 gift-box photograph with transparent background for the bundle panel.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Daybreak Supply" and a bag count. Title block: eyebrow "Build a bundle", title "Pick any three, save a fifth" and a lead sentence. Two-column layout above 960px: left the product grid (3 columns, 9 products, each card with image, name, colour name, price, and a Select checkbox button "Add to bundle"), right a sticky bundle panel with the gift box illustration, a progress ring ("2 of 3") around the count, three slot rows (thumbnail, name, remove button), the price summary (items total, bundle saving, bundle price) and a primary "Add bundle to bag" button (disabled until three are chosen, with the hint "Choose 1 more item"). A fixed bottom bar on mobile repeats the ring, savings and the button.

Copy: Prices: Trail Bottle (Olive, Rust, Slate) GBP 28 each, Camp Mug (Sand, Navy, Rust) GBP 18 each, Canvas Tote (Olive, Slate, Sand) GBP 34 each. Trio discount 20% off the three items; when only two are chosen, show a teaser "Add one more to save GBP 15.20". Messages: "Your bundle is complete. You save GBP 16.00." and "Bundle added to bag."

Interaction: Selecting a card toggles it into the first free slot (maximum three). When three are selected the unselected cards become aria-disabled with the tooltip text "Your bundle is full. Remove one to swap." (focus stays reachable) and clicking one offers "Replace" by swapping out the oldest pick through a small inline button. Savings update live with 300ms number tweens: discount = 20% of the three items' total, teaser for two items = 20% of the cheapest remaining average. The progress ring (SVG stroke-dasharray) animates 0, 1/3, 2/3, full; the box lid closes when full and opens when an item is removed. Remove buttons in slots and re-selecting a card both remove. "Fill with favourites" selects a suggested trio (bottle, mug, tote) in one action; "Clear" removes all. Add bundle to bag changes the bag count (+3), shows "Bundle added to bag." and resets the selection after 800ms. All state changes are announced politely ("Trail Bottle (Rust) added, 2 of 3 chosen"). Selection persists in localStorage inside try/catch. Respect that the animation can be skipped.

Responsive: Under 960px the bundle panel becomes an in-flow card above the grid (compact box illustration) plus the fixed bottom bar; the grid is 2 columns; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #201b15 outline with white offset. Progress is stated as text and numbers ("2 of 3"), never colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (items appear in slots instantly, no confetti, no lid animation). Invented names only.
