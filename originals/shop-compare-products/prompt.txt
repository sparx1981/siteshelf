Build a single-page "Compare Products Tray" for an invented audio brand, "Resonance Audio": a grid of six headphones, a tray that collects up to three for comparison, and a sticky comparison table with a differences-only switch and best-value highlights. All products, specs and prices are invented.

Canvas & base: Light grey-blue page #eef1f5, ink #14202b (15.7:1), secondary #52606d (6.4:1), accent indigo #3a47c9 (white text 7.5:1), best-value green #1c7a4f, caution amber #b8741a. Cards #ffffff with a 1px #d8dee6 border and 16px radius; the comparison table is white with 1px #e1e6ed row lines and a sticky header; the tray is a floating bar #14202b with white text. Prices in GBP.

Typography: "Sora" 600 for headings and product names (clamp 30 to 52px for the title); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI and table text at 14 to 16px; "IBM Plex Mono" 500 for spec values (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, indigo.

Imagery & art direction: Six painted headphone renders (inline SVG with soft contact shadows, subtle highlights on the headband and ear cups and 10% grain) in six colourways on cool studio backdrops (mist, sage, sand, ink, blush, sky): graphite, silver, sand, midnight blue, black and mint; where the renderer allows, vary the pad colour and add a small badge on the cup. The page opens with a painted banner (1400x240) in inline SVG: sound waves as concentric arcs and a waveform ribbon in indigo and teal over a pale gradient, with a faint dot-grid and a headphone silhouette at the right. Rating stars are inline SVG. Media slots for real photography: 800x800 product photographs and a 1920x300 banner.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Resonance Audio". Banner with eyebrow "Compare", title "Find the pair that fits" and a lead sentence. Filter row: Sort select (Featured, Price low to high, Rating) and a result count. Product grid: 3 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile; each card has the render, name, a one-line description, price, rating (stars and number) and a "Compare" checkbox button (a labelled toggle). The floating tray (fixed at bottom, appears when at least one product is selected) shows up to three slots with thumbnails and remove buttons, a hint ("Add 1 more to compare"), a Clear button and a "Compare now" button (enabled from 2 products). "Compare now" scrolls to or opens the comparison section: a table with the products as columns (image, name, price, Add to bag, Remove) and rows for Price, Rating, Battery life (hours), Weight (g), Noise cancelling, Driver size (mm), Water resistance, Codecs, Wireless range (m), Colours, Warranty (years). A "Show differences only" switch hides rows where all selected products match; best value cells (lowest price, longest battery, lowest weight, highest rating) get a green "Best" badge and tinted background.

Copy: Products (name, price, rating, battery, weight, ANC, driver, water, codecs, range, colours, warranty): Studio One 129, 4.5, 38 h, 260 g, Yes, 40 mm, none, "AAC, SBC", 10 m, 3, 2; Open Air 179, 4.6, 24 h, 310 g, No, 50 mm, none, "AAC, SBC, LDAC", 12 m, 2, 3; Pocket On-Ear 69, 4.2, 30 h, 190 g, No, 32 mm, IPX4, "SBC", 10 m, 4, 1; Aurora ANC 299, 4.8, 45 h, 280 g, Adaptive, 42 mm, IPX4, "AAC, SBC, LDAC, aptX", 15 m, 3, 3; Monitor Pro 219, 4.7, 0 h wired, 340 g, No, 45 mm, none, "Wired only", 0 m, 1, 5; Kids Fold 49, 4.3, 28 h, 170 g, No, 30 mm, IPX5, "SBC", 10 m, 5, 1.

Interaction: Toggling Compare on a card adds it to the first free tray slot; the fourth attempt is refused with a polite message ("You can compare up to 3. Remove one to add another."). The tray has role="region" aria-label="Compare tray"; its remove buttons carry names such as "Remove Aurora ANC from comparison". Compare now (enabled at 2 or more) reveals the comparison section below the grid and moves focus to its heading, with the table having proper <th scope> headers, a caption and a sticky first row and first column on scroll. The differences-only switch (role="switch") hides identical rows (and announces how many rows are shown). Best badges are text ("Best") plus a tinted cell, never colour only. Sort reorders the grid with a 200ms FLIP animation; selections persist across sorts. Selected products persist in localStorage inside try/catch. Remove from the table also removes from the tray and unchecks the card. Add to bag in the table shows an inline "Added" message.

Responsive: Under 760px the tray becomes a compact two-line bar, the comparison table scrolls horizontally inside its own container with sticky first column, the grid is one column; no horizontal scroll on the page at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #14202b outline with white offset. Differences and best values are conveyed with words and icons, not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no FLIP). Invented names only.
