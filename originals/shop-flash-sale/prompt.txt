Build a single-page "Flash Sale Countdown" for an invented shop, "Ember & Oak": a time-limited sale with a ticking countdown, live-feeling stock bars, sold-out states, per-customer limits and a short cart hold. It should feel exciting without being stressful or deceptive: the numbers are honest simulations and the page says so. Products, prices and counts are invented.

Canvas & base: Deep charcoal-plum #1b1320 page with a warm hero, ember orange #ff6a2b, gold #ffc247, cream #fff3df (14.8:1 on the dark), secondary #cbbfd0 (8.9:1), success mint #6fe0a8, sold-out grey #8b8296. Cards #261b2e with a 1px #3d2e4a border and 18px radius; sale price in gold, original price struck through in secondary. Buttons ember with dark text #1b1320 (7.4:1). Invented brand only.

Typography: "Bricolage Grotesque" 700/800 for the hero title and the countdown digits (clamp 36 to 96px, tabular numerals); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 16px; "JetBrains Mono" 500 for stock counts and the hold timer. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, gold.

Imagery & art direction: The hero is a painted sunburst poster built in inline SVG: a radial burst of 24 alternating wedges in ember and deep plum fading toward the edges, a large glowing sun disc (gold to ember radial gradient) off-centre behind the countdown, scattered spark dots and tiny stars, a halftone dot ramp along the bottom, 14% film grain and a vignette so it feels printed. Behind the product grid keep a faint warm gradient. Six products are painted renders (inline SVG, soft shadows, 10% grain) on dark-friendly colour fields, using the available product helpers: a Field Watch (watch), a Wool Throw (a folded blanket block with woven stripes), a Trail Boot (sneaker), a Steel Kettle (bottle shape), a Camp Mug (mug) and a Carry Tote (bag). Sold-out cards desaturate to 35% and gain a diagonal "Sold out" ribbon. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x520 hero image, and 800x800 product photographs.

Layout: Top strip with brand and a small "Flash sale ends in 02:14:38" repeated in compact form once the hero scrolls out of view (sticky). Hero: eyebrow "Autumn flash sale", title "Up to 40% off, for one afternoon", the large countdown (hours, minutes, seconds in three blocks with labels), a one-line honesty note ("Stock counts are simulated for this demo."). Product grid: 3 columns on desktop with six cards, each with image, name, sale price, struck original price and saving badge (for example "-30%"), a stock bar with text ("7 of 40 left"), a limit note ("Limit 2 per customer") and an Add to bag button. A cart panel (right-aligned drawer or bottom bar on mobile) shows reserved items with a hold timer ("Held for 09:42"), subtotal and a checkout button.

Copy: Products and prices: Field Watch 79 (was 115), Wool Throw 59 (was 85), Trail Boot 99 (was 140), Steel Kettle 34 (was 49), Camp Mug 12 (was 18), Carry Tote 29 (was 42). Initial stock out of 40: 14, 22, 6, 31, 9, 18. Sold-out text: "Sold out. Get notified if it returns." with a Notify me button that shows "We will email you (demo)".

Interaction: The countdown ends 2 hours 14 minutes 38 seconds after load (a fixed simulated sale end, stored in localStorage so a reload continues) and updates each second inside an element with role="timer" that is not announced every tick; instead a polite live region announces at 60, 10 and 1 minutes remaining and at the end. Every 3 to 7 seconds a random available product loses 1 to 3 units (the stock bar width and count animate over 400ms, the bar turns gold under 50% and ember under 20%) and a small toast says "Someone just bought a Camp Mug" (invented, anonymous, auto-dismiss in 4 seconds, max one visible); at zero the card transitions to the sold-out state over 500ms. Add to bag respects the limit of 2 per product, decrements stock, starts a 10-minute cart hold shown in the cart panel (when it expires the items release back into stock with a message), updates the subtotal and announces "Field Watch added. Held for 10 minutes." Remove buttons release items immediately. When the sale ends all Add buttons become aria-disabled with the text "Sale ended" and prices revert to the original with the message "This sale has ended." Honesty: a visible note explains that stock movement is simulated. No fake urgency copy beyond real countdown and counts.

Responsive: Under 760px the grid is 2 columns then 1 at 420px, the countdown digits scale with the viewport, the cart becomes a bottom sheet toggled by a bag button; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with a dark offset. Stock level is stated in words and numbers, never colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pulsing, no bar animation, no toast slide). Invented names only.
