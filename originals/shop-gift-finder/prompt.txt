Build a single-page "Gift Finder Quiz" for an invented gift shop, "Giftwise": a short quiz about the recipient (who, occasion, budget, interests, style) that narrows a grid of gift cards by tags, scoring and re-ordering them live and explaining why each gift matches. All products, tags and prices are invented.

Canvas & base: Soft blush-cream page #fbf3ee, ink #231820 (16:1), secondary #6b5a62 (5.9:1), accent plum #6a2c5a (white text 9.3:1), highlight coral #e5603f, tag fills #f3e3ea, match-green #2f7a5a. Cards #ffffff with a 1px #ecd9df border and 18px radius; quiz panel #fff with a plum top rule. Prices in GBP. Invented brand only.

Typography: "DM Serif Display" 400 for headings and the question text (clamp 32 to 56px for the page title, 28px for questions); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 15 to 16px; tag text 12px 700. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, plum.

Imagery & art direction: The page opens with a painted gift-wrapping scene banner (1400x260, inline SVG): a pale blush table, a stack of three wrapped boxes in plum, coral and sage paper with satin ribbons and bows tied in different styles, loose curled ribbon, a roll of kraft paper, a pair of scissors, a few pressed flowers and soft shadows with window light, 12% grain. Each gift card's image is a painted product render (headphones, mug, tote, bottle, watch, sneaker and rounded gift-box shapes recoloured from the palette) on a tinted backdrop with a small wrapped-bow badge in a corner and a soft contact shadow. Recipient choices carry tiny painted icons (a heart, a friend pair, a parent, a colleague badge, a child star, a mirror). Media slots for real photography: 800x800 product photographs and a 1920x300 gift-wrap banner.

Layout: Banner with title "Find the right gift" and a lead. Two columns above 960px: left the sticky quiz panel (480px) with a progress indicator ("Question 2 of 5", five dots), the current question as large text with answer buttons, Back and Skip buttons and a summary of answers as removable chips ("Partner", "Birthday", "Under GBP 60"); right the result area: a sentence ("Showing 12 gifts, best matches first"), sort select (Best match, Price low to high, Price high to low) and the grid of 16 gift cards (3 columns) showing image, name, price, tags and a match meter (a thin bar with the percentage and words such as "Great match"); cards that do not meet the budget fade out with a short transition and are removed from tab order. Each card has a heart button to shortlist and a "Why this gift" line (for example "Matches: cooking, thoughtful, under GBP 60"). A shortlist drawer button shows the count.

Copy: Questions: 1 "Who is it for?" (Partner, Friend, Parent, Colleague, Child, Myself), 2 "What is the occasion?" (Birthday, Anniversary, Thank you, Housewarming, Just because), 3 "What is your budget?" (a range slider GBP 10 to 200, step 5, with quick buttons Under 30, 30 to 60, 60 to 100, 100 plus), 4 "What are they into?" (multi-select chips: Cooking, Outdoors, Reading, Tech, Cosy, Music, Plants, Travel; up to 3), 5 "What style suits them?" (Practical, Playful, Luxe). Gifts: 16 items such as Pour-Over Set GBP 38 (cooking, practical, partner, friend, thank you), Trail Flask GBP 28 (outdoors, practical), Listening Headphones GBP 119 (music, tech, luxe), Linen Throw GBP 64 (cosy, housewarming), Herb Garden Kit GBP 24 (plants, cooking, playful), Pocket Atlas GBP 22 (travel, reading), Leather Passport Wallet GBP 45 (travel, luxe), Sunrise Alarm Clock GBP 59 (tech, cosy) and eight more with tags drawn from the same lists and occasion fits.

Interaction: Answering a question advances to the next with a 250ms slide; Back returns and preserves answers; Skip leaves it unscored. Every answer immediately rescored the grid: score = 40% interests overlap, 20% recipient fit, 15% occasion fit, 15% style fit, 10% budget closeness; items above the budget ceiling are hidden (the budget is a hard filter), others are sorted by score with a FLIP animation (200ms). Match meter text: 80 and over "Great match", 55 to 79 "Good match", under 55 "Worth a look". Answer chips can be removed to relax a filter and the quiz jumps to that question. Heart toggles add to a shortlist drawer (a native dialog) listing items with remove buttons and a total; the count persists in localStorage inside try/catch. "Start again" clears everything. Announce result count and top match politely after each answer ("12 gifts. Best match: Pour-Over Set, 92 percent."). Keyboard: answers are real radio and checkbox inputs styled as buttons with arrow-key support; the slider is a native range with a labelled output.

Responsive: Under 960px the quiz becomes a collapsible top panel (a "Refine" disclosure) above the results, the grid is 2 columns then 1 at 420px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #231820 outline with white offset. Match quality is stated in words and numbers, never colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no slide, no FLIP, no fade). Invented names only.
