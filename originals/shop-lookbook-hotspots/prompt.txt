Build a single-page "Lookbook Hotspots" shopping page for an invented home brand, "Hearth & Hollow": one beautifully styled room scene with pulsing hotspots on the furniture; each hotspot opens a small product card with price and Add to basket. All products, prices and names are invented.

Canvas & base: Warm off-white #f7f2ea page, ink #231d17 (15:1), secondary #6c6153 (5.8:1), accent terracotta #b8532d (white text on it passes AA at 4.8:1 for 16px bold) and sage #6f8a6c for tags. Cards #fffdf8 with a 1px #e6dccb border, 16px radius and a soft shadow 0 18px 40px rgba(60,40,20,.18). The scene is a 16:10 rounded (22px) image area with a faint inner shadow. Prices in GBP. Invented brand only.

Typography: "DM Serif Display" 400 for the page title (clamp 36px to 64px) and product names in cards (22px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI, body and prices at 14 to 16px; tabular numerals for prices. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, terracotta.

Imagery & art direction: The scene is a hand-painted interior built in inline SVG (1200x750 viewBox, preserveAspectRatio slice) that must feel like a styled editorial photograph: a warm cream wall with a long soft shaft of afternoon light falling from a tall arched window (a parallelogram gradient from #fff3d0 at 55% to transparent, screen blend), a walnut floor with plank lines and a woven rug (ellipse with concentric terracotta, cream and sage bands and a fringe), a deep sage sofa with rounded arms, three cushions (mustard, cream, terracotta) and a knitted throw, a brass floor lamp with an arc and a linen shade glowing warm, a round oak side table holding a ceramic vase with dried grasses and a small stack of books, a large trailing plant in a terracotta pot, a gallery of three framed abstract prints on the wall in different sizes (painted colour fields), a rattan pendant light, and a skirting board. Add soft cast shadows (blurred dark ellipses and parallelograms), 12% film grain, a subtle vignette and a slight depth blur on the far wall. Product thumbnails in the cards and the Shop-the-look strip use painted product renders (a lamp, a cushion, a vase, a rug swatch, a framed print, a plant pot) generated as small inline SVGs with soft shadows on a warm backdrop. Media slots for real photography: a 2400x1500 styled-room photograph for the scene (hotspot positions are stored as percentages so they carry over), and 800x800 product photographs.

Layout: Header: wordmark "Hearth & Hollow", links (Living, Kitchen, Bedroom), a basket button with a live count and total. Intro: eyebrow "The Reading Room", title "A quiet corner, lit well", a lead sentence and a toggle "Show hotspots" (on by default). Scene section: the room image with six hotspots (Sofa, Floor lamp, Rug, Gallery print, Vase, Plant). Beside or beneath it (on mobile) a "Shop the look" list with the six products, each row showing thumbnail, name, price and Add button, whose rows highlight the matching hotspot on hover or focus. Total of the look and a "Add the whole look" button with a bundle saving of 10%.

Copy: Products and prices: "Mossgreen Sofa" GBP 1,240; "Brass Arc Lamp" GBP 289; "Terracotta Weave Rug" GBP 215; "Colour Field Print No. 3" GBP 64; "Dried Grass Vase" GBP 42; "Trailing Pothos in Clay Pot" GBP 38. Each has a short line ("Three-seater, stain-resistant weave, ships in 2 weeks").

Interaction: Hotspots are buttons (44px hit area, a 16px dot with a pulsing 36px ring animation every 2.4 seconds) positioned by percentage inside the scene. Click, tap or Enter opens a popover card anchored near the hotspot (flipping to stay inside the scene, becoming a bottom sheet under 700px) with thumbnail, name, price, short line, a quantity stepper (1 to 5) and Add to basket. Only one card is open at a time; Escape or clicking elsewhere closes it and returns focus to the hotspot; arrow keys move focus between hotspots. The card is role="dialog" non-modal with aria-labelledby, and the basket button shows an aria-live polite count update ("Brass Arc Lamp added, basket total GBP 289"). The Show hotspots toggle hides the dots and rings (and also stops the pulse), and pressing H toggles it. The basket drawer is a native dialog listing items with remove buttons, subtotal, and the bundle saving when all six are present. Basket contents persist in localStorage inside try/catch. Technique notes: hotspot coordinates live in one data array shared by the SVG scene, the buttons and the list, so replacing the scene image never requires changing code.

Responsive: Under 900px the Shop-the-look list moves below the scene, hotspot dots stay 44px hit areas, the card becomes a bottom sheet; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #231d17 outline with white offset (on the dark scene use white with dark offset). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pulse, no card animation). Invented names only.
