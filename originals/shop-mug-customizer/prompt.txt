Build a single-page "Mug Customizer" for an invented maker, "Wheel & Kettle Co.": a visitor designs a ceramic mug in the browser, sees it rendered with convincing shading, rotates it to check the printed text, and watches the price update live. Everything is drawn in inline SVG and CSS; products, prices and names are invented.

Canvas & base: Soft warm kitchen backdrop #f3e6d3 with a painted counter scene (see imagery), page text #261e17 (15:1), secondary #6a5d4f (5.9:1), accent kettle-blue #2b5a8a (white text 7.6:1) for primary buttons and terracotta #b8532d for highlights. Panels #fffbf3 with a 1px #e5d8c1 border and 20px radius. Mug colour options: Cream #f4ead5, Sage #8aa386, Terracotta #c9683a, Cobalt #2f56d4, Ink #1c1c24, Mustard #e0a43a, Blush #e8b4b0, Sky #9cc7e0. Print colours: Ink #1c1c24, Cream #fff6e4, Gold #d4a237, Terracotta #b8532d, Cobalt #2f56d4. Prices in GBP.

Typography: "DM Serif Display" 400 for the heading and the price total (clamp 32 to 56px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 14 to 16px; the printed mug text can be set in "Caveat" 700, "DM Serif Display", "Unbounded" 600 or "Permanent Marker" depending on the Font option. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, terracotta.

Imagery & art direction: The product must read like a studio photograph, not a flat icon. Draw the mug as an SVG cylinder: a body with an elliptical top rim (outer rim lighter, inner ellipse dark brown coffee with a soft highlight), a bottom ellipse, horizontal shading gradient (edges 35% darker, a soft highlight band at about 30% from the left, a thin specular slit at 72%), a thick handle drawn as a stroked path with an inner darker edge and a highlight along its top, a contact shadow under the base (blurred ellipse) and a faint reflection on the counter. Gloss finish adds the sharp highlights; Matte replaces them with a soft gradient and a fine speckle texture. The pattern and printed text sit on the cylinder: patterns are SVG <pattern> fills (stripes, dots, waves, stars, checks) translated by the rotation angle; the text is set on a gently curved baseline and compressed horizontally by cos(angle) as it turns around the side, fading out beyond 80 degrees. The stage behind the mug is a painted kitchen: a peach plaster wall with a soft window-light shaft, a row of white subway-tile backsplash, a wooden shelf with three jars and a trailing plant, a marble counter in perspective with faint veins and a linen cloth corner, and gentle steam wisps rising from the mug (three curved paths animating upward over 6 seconds, off for reduced motion). Add 12% grain and a subtle vignette. Media slots for real photography: a 2000x1500 kitchen-counter background photograph, and a 1200x1200 blank-mug studio shot with a transparent background to put under the generated print layers.

Layout: Header: wordmark "Wheel & Kettle Co.", a basket count. Two columns: left the stage (aspect 4:3) with the mug, a Rotate slider beneath it (-120 to 120 degrees, also drag the mug horizontally) and "Reset view"; right the options panel as grouped fieldsets: Size (Small 8 oz, Regular 11 oz, Large 15 oz; the mug scales 0.88, 1.0, 1.1), Mug colour (swatches), Handle (Same colour or Contrast), Pattern (None, Stripes, Dots, Waves, Stars, Checks) with Pattern colour swatches, Finish (Gloss, Matte), Text (input up to 20 characters), Font (select), Print colour (swatches), Quantity stepper (1 to 12). Below the options, a price card: itemised lines (Mug, Pattern, Print, Matte finish, Quantity discount), the total in large type and "Ships in 4 to 6 days". Buttons: Add to basket (primary), Save design, Copy design code.

Copy: Default design: Regular 11 oz, Sage, Stripes in Cream, Gloss, text "Good morning", Caveat, Ink. Prices: Small 9, Regular 12, Large 15; Pattern +1.50; Text print +2.50; Matte finish +1.00; discount 10% for 3 to 5 mugs, 15% for 6 or more.

Interaction: Every option re-renders the SVG immediately (CSS custom properties and attribute updates, no full redraw flicker) and recalculates the price with a 300ms number tween. Dragging the mug with pointer events (setPointerCapture) changes the rotation angle (1 degree per 2 pixels); the slider and the mug are linked and the slider is the keyboard route. Text longer than 20 characters is trimmed with a counter ("14/20"); an empty text field removes the print charge. Swatch groups are radiogroups with arrow-key support and visible selection rings. Add to basket confirms inline ("2 x Regular, Sage, Stripes added") and updates the header count. Save design stores the options in localStorage inside try/catch and restores them on next visit; Copy design code copies a short code such as "WK-3F9A-2C" derived from the options to the clipboard inside try/catch and announces it politely. The price total is aria-live polite but debounced to 700ms; the mug SVG has an aria-label describing the current design ("Regular 11 ounce sage mug with cream stripes, printed Good morning in Caveat").

Responsive: Under 900px stack the stage above the options, the stage keeps a 4:3 ratio and a sticky mini price bar appears at the bottom; swatches wrap; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #261e17 outline with white offset. Colour choices are always named in text (aria-labels and visible captions). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no steam, no number tween, instant rotation). Invented names only.
