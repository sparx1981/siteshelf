Build a single-page "Order Confirmation" thank-you page for an invented shop, "Salt & Slate": after checkout the customer sees an animated tick, a warm painted scene of their order being packed, an order summary, a three-step delivery timeline with a changeable delivery slot, and calm next steps. All data is invented and kept in the page.

Canvas & base: Soft warm white #faf6ee, ink #1f1a14 (16:1), secondary #675c4b (5.9:1), accent sea-green #1d6e5a (white text 6.4:1), highlight sand #e8cf9a, alert terracotta #b5492a. Cards #ffffff with a 1px #e8dfcc border and 18px radius. Prices in GBP with tabular numerals. Invented brand, customer "Ines Varga", order number "SS-72915" and example.com contact details only.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for the headline (clamp 34 to 60px) and totals; "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 15 to 17px; "DM Mono" 500 for order numbers and dates. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, sea-green.

Imagery & art direction: A painted top-down scene (full width, 280px, 22px radius) shows the order being packed on a scrubbed oak table: a kraft box open at the centre with tissue paper folds, a stoneware mug and a clay planter nested in shredded paper, a folded linen cushion beside it, a roll of twine, a small handwritten-style "Thank you" card with a pressed flower, a pencil, a brass scissors, a cup of tea with a faint steam ring, window light falling diagonally with soft shadows and a few scattered crumbs of paper; 12% grain and a vignette. The tick is an SVG circle (80px) that draws its stroke in 600ms, then the check path draws in 400ms, then 12 small dots burst outward and fade (800ms). Order items use painted product renders on warm backdrops. The delivery timeline has three nodes with small painted icons (a box, a van, a house) drawn as inline SVG line illustrations. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x420 packing-table photograph and 400x400 product photographs.

Layout: Header "Salt & Slate" and a quiet link "Continue shopping". Hero: the painted scene with the tick overlapping its bottom-centre edge, then a centred headline "Thank you, Ines" and sub "Order SS-72915 is confirmed. A receipt is on its way to ines.varga@example.com." Two columns above 900px: left Order summary (three item rows with thumbnail, name, variant, quantity, price; subtotal, delivery, discount line, total; payment method "Card ending 4242"), Delivery address; right Delivery timeline (Order confirmed today, Packed Tuesday, Out for delivery and Delivered on the chosen slot) with a "Change delivery slot" select (Thursday morning, Thursday afternoon, Friday all day) and a calendar strip of the next 7 days highlighting the chosen day, a Gift note card (textarea up to 120 characters with counter and Save note), and "What happens next" with three short bullets. Buttons row: Track order (primary), Download receipt (secondary), Continue shopping (link).

Copy: Items: Stoneware Mug x2 GBP 32.00, Clay Planter GBP 36.00, Linen Cushion GBP 42.00; delivery GBP 0.00 (free over GBP 60), discount "WELCOME10" -GBP 11.00, total GBP 99.00. Messages: "Delivery slot updated to Friday, all day." and "Gift note saved."

Interaction: On load the tick animates once (respecting reduced motion, where it is shown complete) and the order number types in letter by letter in DM Mono. Changing the delivery slot updates the timeline dates, the calendar highlight and the estimate line with a 300ms highlight flash and announces the change politely. The gift note validates length, shows a live counter and saves in localStorage inside try/catch. Download receipt builds a small plain-text receipt as a Blob and triggers a download named "receipt-SS-72915.txt" (no network); Track order scrolls to the timeline and focuses its heading; the "Continue shopping" link is a no-op demo. Copy order number button copies "SS-72915" to the clipboard inside try/catch with an announcement. The page sets focus to the main heading on load for screen-reader users and exposes the tick as a decorative image with the heading carrying the confirmation.

Responsive: Under 900px stack the columns, the painted scene reduces to 180px with object-position centred on the box, the timeline becomes vertical; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1f1a14 outline with white offset. Success is communicated with words as well as the tick. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (tick static and complete, no typing, no burst, no flashes). Invented names only.
