Build a single-page "Quick View Modal" shop for an invented store, "Parcel & Pine": a grid of products where a Quick view button opens an accessible dialog with a gallery, variant pickers and Add to bag, without leaving the page. Products, prices, stock and names are invented.

Canvas & base: Warm bone page #f5f0e6, ink #1c1915 (15.7:1), secondary #675d4f (5.9:1), accent pine #234d3a (white text 9.6:1), sale terracotta #b24a26 (on white 5.3:1), star amber #e0a03a. Cards #fffdf8, 1px #e4dac7 border, 16px radius. The dialog is #fffdf8 with a 24px radius, a #1c1915 backdrop at 55% and a soft shadow. Prices in GBP.

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the page title (clamp 34 to 60px) and the dialog product name (30px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for all UI at 14 to 16px; tabular numerals for prices and stock counts. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, pine.

Imagery & art direction: Eight products drawn as painted inline-SVG renders with real depth: a waxed canvas tote, a leather satchel, a watch, studio headphones, a canvas sneaker, a stainless flask, a stoneware mug and a beeswax candle in a jar, each on a muted studio backdrop (oat, sage, clay, slate) with a soft contact shadow, fabric or material highlights and 10% grain. Each product has colourways that recolour the render live (for example Olive, Navy, Sand, Terracotta). The dialog gallery has four views per product: Front (the main render), Back (the render mirrored with a slightly different shadow), Detail (the render scaled 1.9x focused on a texture area, with a soft vignette) and In use (the render placed on a small painted lifestyle scene: a hallway with a coat rail, a wooden bench or a window-lit table). The main image is large (aspect 4:5) with a subtle slow zoom on hover that follows the pointer (transform-origin tracks the cursor, max 1.6x), thumbnails are 64px squares. Add a small "New" or "Low stock" ribbon in the corner where relevant. Media slots for real photography: 1000x1250 product photographs for each view, and a 1920x300 seasonal banner photograph above the grid.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Parcel & Pine", a bag button with live count. Title block: eyebrow "Autumn edit", title "Quiet things for wet days", a filter row (All, Bags, Everyday, Home) as toggle buttons. Product grid: 4 columns desktop, 2 on mobile; each card has the image (4:5), name, price, colour dots and a visible Quick view button (shown on hover and focus on pointer devices, always visible on touch). Dialog layout (two columns above 760px): left the gallery with main image and four thumbnails; right the details: name, star rating (4.6 with 128 reviews), price (with a struck-through price for sale items), a short description, Colour (radiogroup of swatches with names), Size (radiogroup of chips; unavailable sizes are disabled with strike-through and a note), stock message ("Only 3 left in Olive / Large"), quantity stepper (1 to 9), Add to bag (primary) and a "View full details" link, plus a free-delivery line.

Copy: Products and prices: Waxed Canvas Tote 49 (sale from 62), Leather Satchel 165, Everyday Watch 120, Studio Headphones 119, Canvas Court Sneaker 78, Steel Flask 32, Stoneware Mug 16, Beeswax Candle 24. Descriptions of two short sentences each. Delivery line "Free delivery over GBP 60, returns within 30 days".

Interaction: The dialog is a native <dialog> opened with showModal() (focus is trapped natively; fall back to a role="dialog" aria-modal overlay with a manual focus trap if showModal is unavailable). Opening moves focus to the dialog's heading (tabindex -1), Escape or the Close button or a backdrop click closes it and returns focus to the Quick view button that opened it. Swatches and sizes are radiogroups with arrow-key navigation (roving tabindex) and visible selection; choosing a colour recolours all four gallery views and the thumbnails immediately and updates the stock message; choosing a size updates the stock message; disabled sizes explain why through aria-describedby ("Out of stock in Olive"). Thumbnails are buttons with aria-current; Left and Right arrows on the main image area change the view. The quantity stepper clamps to available stock. Add to bag is disabled until a size is chosen, with the hint "Choose a size"; on success the dialog stays open for 600ms showing "Added to bag" then closes and the header count increments (announced politely, for example "Waxed Canvas Tote, Olive, Small added. Bag has 2 items."). Filters hide non-matching cards with a short fade and update a polite result count. The bag count persists in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 760px the dialog becomes a full-height bottom sheet (slides up 250ms), the gallery shows the main image with horizontally scrollable thumbnails, Add to bag sticks to the bottom of the sheet; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1c1915 outline with white offset. Colour is always named (swatch aria-labels and a visible selected-colour caption). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no slide-up, no hover zoom animation, no fade). Invented names only.
