Build a single-page "Size Finder Assistant" for an invented knitwear brand, "Fieldnote Knitwear": a short set of friendly questions (height, build, fit, usual size, optional chest measurement) that recommends a size with a confidence meter, explains why, and shows the garment on a painted figure. The model is a transparent rule set in the page; nothing is sent anywhere.

Canvas & base: Soft oat page #f4efe6, ink #1e1b17 (15.8:1), secondary #675d4f (5.8:1), accent moss #2f6b4a (white text 6.2:1), highlight butter #f2c14e, caution clay #b8532d. Cards #fffdf8 with a 1px #e5dccb border and 18px radius; the result card is slightly elevated with a soft shadow. Chart row highlight #e6f1e8. Invented brand and product only ("Merino Crew").

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for headings and the big recommended size (clamp 64px to 120px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for body, labels and form text at 15 to 17px; "DM Mono" 500 for measurements and the chart numbers (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, moss.

Imagery & art direction: The right-hand result card is dominated by a painted fitting-room scene in inline SVG (aspect 4:5): a soft studio wall in oat gradient, a wooden floor line with a long shadow, a full-length figure drawn as a stylised, friendly silhouette with painted shading (head with simple face-less oval and hair shape, neck, shoulders, torso, arms, legs, shoes) in a warm skin tone from a small palette, wearing the Merino Crew drawn on top (a knit texture made from fine repeating rib lines, a ribbed neckline, cuffs and hem, a muted moss colour with a subtle highlight). The figure's height scales with the entered height (reference 175 cm), its shoulder and waist width scale with the Build choice (Slim, Regular, Athletic, Broad), and the sweater's length and looseness follow the Fit choice and the recommended size (a Fitted garment hugs the torso, Relaxed adds a drop shoulder and a longer hem). A thin height ruler with tick marks stands beside the figure and a small label shows the entered height. Add 12% grain and a soft vignette, with a faint window-light parallelogram on the wall. Media slots for real photography: a 1200x1500 model photograph per size to replace the painted figure, and a 1200x1200 flat-lay of the garment.

Layout: Two columns above 900px: left a stacked set of five question cards, each with a number badge and a one-line helper; right a sticky result card. Questions: 1 Height (range slider 150 to 205 cm with a cm or ft-in toggle and a numeric input synced to it), 2 Build (four illustrated radio cards with tiny torso silhouettes: Slim, Regular, Athletic, Broad), 3 Fit (Fitted, True to size, Relaxed as three segmented radio buttons), 4 Usual size in other brands (select: XS, S, M, L, XL, I am not sure), 5 Chest measurement (optional number input 70 to 140 cm with a link "How to measure" that reveals a short instruction). Result card: the painted figure, "We suggest" with the big size, a confidence meter (a 5-segment bar plus a percentage and a word High, Medium or Low), a one-sentence explanation, a bullet list of reasons, a size chart table (XS to XXL with chest and body length in cm, the suggested row highlighted and the runner-up marked "Also possible"), and an Add button "Add size M to bag".

Copy: Example explanation: "At 178 cm with a regular build, M fits best. Choose L if you like layers underneath." Reasons: "Your height points to M or L", "Regular build keeps chest near 100 cm", "True to size fit adds no extra ease", "Your usual size is M elsewhere". Footer note: "Sizes run true to our size chart. Free exchanges within 30 days."

Interaction: Maintain a model: estimated chest (cm) = 0.52 x height + build offset (Slim -6, Regular 0, Athletic +6, Broad +10), replaced by the measured chest when one is entered; fit adds ease to the target (Fitted -3, True 0, Relaxed +4); a usual size nudges the target toward that size's chest midpoint by 25%. Score each size by closeness of its chest midpoint to the target using a Gaussian, normalise the scores, pick the highest as the recommendation and the second as "Also possible" if its share is above 30%. Confidence = the top share mapped to 5 segments and a percentage; if the top two are within 10 points, say "Between M and L" and set confidence to Medium or Low. Every input updates the result live with a 250ms number or figure transition (the figure scales smoothly; the size letter crossfades). The height unit toggle converts values without losing precision; ft-in input accepts "5'10" or "5 10" and validates. Invalid input shows an inline message and keeps the last good result. Announce a changed recommendation politely ("We suggest M, confidence high"). Add to bag shows an inline confirmation with the chosen size. Persist answers in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the result card moves to the top as a compact sticky summary (size, confidence, small figure) and expands to the full card with a "See details" disclosure; build cards become a 2 x 2 grid; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1e1b17 outline with white offset. Confidence is stated in words and numbers, never colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no transitions). Invented names only.
