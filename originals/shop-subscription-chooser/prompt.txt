Build a single-page "Subscription Box Chooser" for an invented coffee club, "Slow Roast Club": the shopper chooses roast, bag size, grind, delivery frequency and extras; a painted coffee bag updates live, the price shows subscribe-and-save, and a calendar shows the next deliveries with skip and pause controls. All data is invented and kept in the page.

Canvas & base: Warm cream page #f7f0e4, espresso ink #261a12 (16.2:1), secondary #6a5a4a (5.7:1), accent roast brown #7a3f1d (white text 7.9:1), crema #e8c58f, fresh green #3a6b3a for savings. Cards #fffaf0 with a 1px #e6d8c0 border and 18px radius. Prices in GBP, tabular numerals. Invented brand only.

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the page title, section headings and the big price (clamp 32 to 56px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 15 to 16px; "DM Mono" 500 for dates and weights. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, roast brown.

Imagery & art direction: The left stage shows a painted kitchen counter scene (inline SVG, aspect 4:5): a warm plaster wall with a soft window-light shaft, a wooden shelf with a small plant and two jars, a marble counter in perspective, and in the foreground a standing kraft coffee bag (rounded rectangle with a folded top, crimp lines, a valve, a label panel in cream with the club mark, a roast-level stripe and a handwritten-style tasting-note line) whose label colour, stripe and note text change with the chosen roast (Light: apricot #e89a4a, notes "Citrus, honey, jasmine"; Medium: caramel #b5702c, notes "Hazelnut, toffee, orange"; Dark: espresso #4a2a18, notes "Cocoa, smoke, black cherry"). Beside the bag a scatter of painted coffee beans (30 to 40 small ellipses with a centre crease, colour matching the roast) and a ceramic cup with steam when extras include a brew cup. The bag scales with the chosen size (250 g 0.85, 500 g 1.0, 1 kg 1.15) and a small paper tag shows the grind. Extras appear as painted objects on the counter: a ceramic dripper, a pack of paper filters, a tiny brew-guide booklet. 12% grain and a soft vignette. Media slots for real photography: a 1200x1500 counter-and-bag photograph, and 600x600 extras photographs.

Layout: Two columns above 960px: left the sticky stage; right a vertical flow of choices as grouped radio cards: 1 Roast (Light, Medium, Dark with a tiny three-bean intensity icon and one-line notes), 2 Bag size (250 g, 500 g, 1 kg with price per 100 g), 3 Grind (Whole bean, Filter, Espresso, Cafetiere), 4 Frequency (Every week, Every 2 weeks, Every month, with a small "Best for 2 cups a day" hint), 5 Extras (checkboxes: Ceramic dripper GBP 18 one-off, Paper filters x100 GBP 4 each delivery, Brew guide booklet free). Beneath: the price card (per delivery, per month equivalent, subscribe-and-save badge showing the discount %, a one-off total for extras) and the calendar card: a month view for the current and next two months with delivery days marked, a legend and buttons "Skip next delivery", "Pause for a month" and "Resume". A primary "Start my subscription" button and a note "Cancel any time. No commitment."

Copy: Base prices per bag: 250 g GBP 9.50, 500 g GBP 17.00, 1 kg GBP 31.00; Light and Medium same, Dark GBP 0.50 less per bag. Discounts: weekly 15%, fortnightly 12%, monthly 8% off the bag price. Delivery is free over GBP 15, otherwise GBP 3.20. Delivery weekday defaults to Thursday and the first delivery is the next Thursday after 3 days.

Interaction: Every choice updates the painted stage (bag colour, notes, size, tag, beans, extras) with a 300ms crossfade or scale, the price card (per delivery = bag price x (1 - discount) + delivery fee + recurring extras; monthly equivalent = per delivery x deliveries per month) with number tweening, and the calendar (delivery dates computed from the frequency and the chosen weekday; skipped dates shown struck through and greyed; paused months blank). "Skip next delivery" toggles the first date as skipped; "Pause for a month" blanks the next 30 days; "Resume" restores. Choosing the weekday is a small select in the calendar card (Mon to Sun). Calendar cells are buttons that can toggle skip on future deliveries, with aria-labels such as "Thursday 23 October, delivery, skip" and a polite announcement on change. Persist choices in localStorage inside try/catch. Start my subscription shows a confirmation panel summarising the plan and the first delivery date.

Responsive: Under 960px the stage becomes a 16:10 banner at the top, the choice cards stack in a single column, the calendar shows one month at a time with previous and next month buttons; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #261a12 outline with white offset. Delivery days are marked with a dot and text (not colour alone). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no crossfades, no number tween, no steam). Invented names only.
