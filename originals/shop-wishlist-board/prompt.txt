Build a single-page "Wishlist Board" for an invented shop, "Fieldnote Goods": shoppers collect products into several named lists on a board, move items between lists by dragging (or with a menu), see price-drop badges on items whose price fell since they saved them, and share a list by link. Products, prices and names are invented; nothing leaves the page.

Canvas & base: Light paper #f6f1e8, ink #1f1b16 (15.6:1), secondary #675d50 (5.9:1), accent forest #1f6b52 (white text 6.3:1), price-drop coral #c63d2f (on white 5.4:1), highlight honey #f2b84b. Cards #fffdf8 with a 1px #e5dccb border and 16px radius, board background #efe7d8 with a very faint dotted grid. Prices in GBP, tabular numerals. Invented brand only.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for headings and list names (clamp 30 to 52px for the page title, 22px for list names); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 14 to 16px. Badges 12px 700 caps, 0.06em tracking. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, forest.

Imagery & art direction: Make the products look photographed. Generate each product as a painted inline-SVG render on a coloured studio backdrop with a soft contact shadow, a gentle highlight and 10% grain: a canvas tote bag, an insulated bottle, a trail sneaker, a field watch, studio headphones and a ceramic mug, each in two colourways (12 products), with backdrops in muted tones (oat, sage, clay, slate, butter, dusty blue). Product images appear as 1:1 thumbnails (72px) in board cards and as 4:5 tiles in the shop grid. The page opens with a painted banner (a flat-lay desk scene in inline SVG: a wooden surface, a notebook, a pencil, dried flowers and a mug, seen from above with soft shadows and a window-light gradient) 160px tall behind the heading. Media slots for real photography: 800x1000 product photographs and a 1920x320 flat-lay banner photograph.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Fieldnote Goods", a "Wishlists" badge showing the total saved count. Banner with eyebrow "Your wishlists", title "Things worth waiting for" and a one-line summary ("9 items across 3 lists. 2 have dropped in price."). Board: a horizontally scrollable row of list columns (each 300px) plus a final "+ New list" column; each column has an inline-editable name, item count and total, a Share button, a menu button (Rename, Delete list), and a stack of item cards. Below the board a "Save more from the shop" grid of the 12 products (4 columns on desktop) with a heart button on each tile that opens a small "Save to..." popover listing the lists (with checkmarks for lists that already contain it) and a "New list" field.

Copy: Default lists "Birthday ideas" (3 items), "Home office" (3 items), "Someday" (3 items), with 2 items showing price drops ("Price drop: GBP 12 less since you saved it", old price struck through). Empty list text "Nothing here yet. Drag an item in or save one from the shop." Share panel text "Anyone with this link can view this list" with a generated link such as https://example.com/wishlist/k7f2q.

Interaction: Item cards can be dragged between columns with pointer events: after a 6px move a ghost card follows the pointer, the column under it highlights, and releasing drops the item at the end of that list (animated with a 200ms FLIP move); on touch, long-press 250ms starts a drag. Every card also has a "Move to list" select (labelled with the item name) so keyboard and screen-reader users can move items, and Remove and "Add to bag" buttons. The heart popover toggles an item in or out of each list (checkbox rows), supports Escape to close and returns focus to the heart. List names edit in place (Enter saves, Escape cancels, max 24 characters); Delete list asks for confirmation inside the menu and moves its items to the first remaining list. Share opens a popover with a read-only input holding the link and a Copy button (clipboard in try/catch, polite announcement). Price drops are computed by comparing each saved item's snapshot price with the current catalogue price; current prices for a few items are lower than their snapshots by design. Everything persists in localStorage inside try/catch, and the summary line and the header badge update live. Announce moves, saves, removals and copies in an aria-live polite region.

Responsive: Under 760px the board becomes a vertical stack of lists (each collapsible with a disclosure button), dragging is replaced by the Move select, the shop grid becomes two columns; no horizontal scroll at 390px outside the board's own scroller.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1f1b16 outline with white offset. Price drops are conveyed with text and an icon, not colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no FLIP, no ghost easing). Invented names only.
