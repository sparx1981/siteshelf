Canvas & base: Midnight navy page #0a1226 with a faint radial spotlight (#1e3a8a at 30%) behind the centre card and a subtle reflective floor line. Cards #121c3a with 1px #2a3a6e borders, subtle top highlight and 22px radius. Text #f1f4ff (15:1), secondary #a9b6dd (8:1). Accent gold #f2c14e used for stars, quote marks and the active dot. Invented company "Oakhaven Studios" (a product-design agency).

Typography: "Playfair Display" 700 for the large quote marks and the names; "Inter" 400/500/600 for quote text (18px on the centre card, 16px on sides) and roles (13px).

Layout: A centred section with heading "Clients say it best" and a short subline. Below, a 3D stage (height 430px, perspective 1400px) holding seven quote cards 380px wide by 350px tall, each with five gold stars, the quote (3 to 5 lines), a divider, a 44px generated SVG avatar circle (initials on a coloured gradient), a name and a role with invented company. The centre card sits at scale 1; neighbours are rotated about the Y axis by 38 degrees (left cards face right, right cards face left), translated 300px sideways per step with 130px depth drop, scaled to 0.86, dimmed to 0.68 opacity and lightly blurred (1.5px); cards two or more steps away fade to 0.25 and stack behind. Below the stage: previous and next round buttons, seven dots and a live counter "3 / 7".

Interaction: Pointer drag or touch swipe moves the carousel with the finger (translate in 1:1 pixels, 300px per card) with momentum and snaps to the nearest card on release; fast flicks advance up to two cards. Clicking a side card brings it to the centre. Arrow buttons and Left/Right keys step; Home/End jump; the stage is focusable with role="group" aria-roledescription="carousel"; each card is role="group" aria-roledescription="slide" aria-label "n of 7", and non-centre cards have aria-hidden="false" but tabindex -1 so only the centre card links are tabbable. Mouse wheel horizontal movement also steps (debounced). Autoplay is off by default with a visible "Autoplay" toggle (aria-pressed) that advances every 5 seconds and stops on hover, focus or drag. A polite live region announces "Slide 3 of 7: Name, Role".

Responsive: Under 700px card width becomes min(78vw, 320px), sideways spacing 0.72 of the card width, rotation 30 degrees, stage height 440px; no horizontal scroll at 390px (stage overflow hidden with clip so shadows do not create scroll).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #f2c14e with navy offset. Transforms and opacity only for movement; will-change on cards. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no momentum, instant snap, no blur, autoplay disabled and the toggle hidden). Invented names only.
