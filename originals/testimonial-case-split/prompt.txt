Canvas & base: Bright editorial page #fbfaf6 with ink #14171a (17:1) and secondary #50575e (7.4:1). Left column a soft panel #f1efe7 with 1px #e2dfd2 rules; right column white #ffffff card with 1px #e2dfd2 border and a thick 6px top bar in the active client's colour. Four invented clients, each with its own accent: Arcwell (#2457ff blue), Nimbus Grain (#e0702b orange), Fathom Labs (#0f9d7a green), Tallow & Rye (#b0344f raspberry). Invented vendor "Ledgerline" (finance operations software).

Typography: "Newsreader" 500/600 italic and roman for the big quote (clamp 28px to 44px, 1.2 leading); "Figtree" 500/600/700 for client names, labels and metrics (metric numerals up to 46px, 700, tabular); small caps eyebrow at 13px with 0.14em tracking.

Layout: A section titled "Results our customers can point at" with a short intro. Two columns: left (360px) is a vertical list of four large tab buttons, each with a hand-built SVG client logo mark (geometric, invented), the client name, an industry line ("Logistics", "Food manufacturing", "Marine research", "Hospitality group") and a right-arrow that slides on hover; the active tab has a coloured left bar, white background and slight shadow. Right (fluid): a testimonial panel with an oversized quote, a person row (generated SVG portrait circle with initials, name, title), three metric tiles (large number counting up, short label, small delta arrow) and a "Read the case study" link with an underline that draws in.

Interaction: The tab list is role="tablist" aria-orientation="vertical"; ArrowUp/ArrowDown (and Left/Right) move between tabs with automatic activation, Home/End jump, roving tabindex. Selecting a tab cross-fades the panel (opacity and 14px upward slide, 320ms) and updates the top bar colour, then counts up the three metrics from zero over 900ms with ease-out (numbers formatted with prefixes and suffixes such as 38%, 4.2 days, £1.2m); each tab panel is role="tabpanel" with aria-labelledby. Counting respects reduced motion by showing final values. Auto-rotate every 8 seconds only until the user interacts, with a visible small progress bar under the active tab; hovering the panel pauses it. A "Next story" button for touch users cycles tabs.

Responsive: Under 860px the layout stacks; tabs become a horizontal scroll-snap row of compact chips (logo plus name) above the panel, tablist aria-orientation switches to horizontal, metrics stack to one column under 520px; no horizontal scroll on the page at 390px (only the chip row scrolls).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px outline in the active client colour with white offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no cross-fade slide, no count-up, no auto-rotate, no link underline animation). Invented names only; metrics are fictional.
