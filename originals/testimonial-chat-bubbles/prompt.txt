A chat window card #ffffff (24px radius, 1px #dcdffa border, shadow 0 40px 80px -40px rgba(54,48,140,.5)) with a header strip #3a31a8 holding the channel name "#love-notes", a green "online" dot and member count. Message bubbles: incoming #f1f2fd with ink text #1d1b3a; highlighted message (the longest quote) #3a31a8 with white text; reactions in pill chips #fff with 1px #dcdffa borders. Accent indigo #3a31a8, mint #19b394, coral #ff7a59. Invented product "Pebble Payroll".

Typography: "Plus Jakarta Sans" 500/600/700/800 for names, headline and bubbles (16px bubbles, 13px meta); "DM Mono" 400 for timestamps and the typing label (12px).

Layout: Two columns on desktop. Left: eyebrow "Loved by small teams", headline "Payroll people actually message us about." (clamp 36px to 64px), a short paragraph, three stat tiles (4.9 average rating, 12,000 teams, 3 min median reply), and the control row (Replay, Pause or Play, Show all). Right: the chat window, 560px wide, 620px tall, with a scrolling thread. The thread holds six messages from invented people (Odalys Fenn, bakery owner; Tobias Reyes-Wu, studio manager; Anneke Prill, charity treasurer; Jomo Adeyemi, café owner; Sunniva Berg, dentist; Hector Valli, bike-shop owner), each with a coloured initials avatar, name and role, one to three sentences of quote, a time such as 09:14, and a "Seen" double tick on the last message of each person. One bubble is highlighted. Between some messages a small date separator "Today" and a system line "Pebble Support joined the channel" appear.

Interaction: When the window enters view (IntersectionObserver, threshold 0.35) the thread opens with the date separator and the first two messages already present (as if the channel was live), then plays the rest: for each message a three-dot typing indicator with the person's name appears for 700 to 1100ms (proportional to quote length), then is replaced by the bubble, which pops in (scale 0.92 and translate 12px to rest, 260ms), and the thread auto-scrolls smoothly to keep the newest message visible. After the last message, reaction chips (heart 24, party 11, thumbs-up 9) bounce in on the highlighted bubble and a "Pebble Support" reply bubble ends the thread. Controls: Replay clears and restarts; Pause or Play freezes the sequence (aria-pressed); Show all immediately reveals every message. Clicking a reaction chip toggles it and updates its count with aria-pressed. The thread is role="log" with aria-live="polite" and aria-relevant="additions"; typing indicators are aria-hidden with a visually hidden "Odalys is typing" status. Users scrolling the thread manually pause auto-scroll until the next message.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack with the headline first and the chat window full width (height 560px); stat tiles become a row of three small tiles; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ff7a59 with white offset. All quotes must be invented and plausible, never attributed to a real company. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (all messages visible immediately, no typing indicators, no pop or bounce). Invented names only.
