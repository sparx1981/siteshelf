Canvas & base: Warm cork-free craft wall: page #efe6d4 with a faint dot grid (radial-gradient #d9cdb4 1px every 22px) and soft vignette. Note cards in paper tones (#fff6a8 yellow, #ffd6e0 pink, #cfeedd mint, #cfe3ff blue, #ffe0b8 peach, #ffffff white) with 2px 6px 18px rgba(80,60,20,.25) shadows, a strip of translucent masking tape (rgba(255,255,255,.55) with a slight rotation) on top of each. Ink #2b2418 (13:1) for note text, #5a4e3a for small print. Accent tomato #d9482b for doodles and the primary button. Invented business "Little Orchard Nursery" (a plant nursery and garden shop).

Typography: "Caveat" 600/700 for note text (clamp 24px to 28px) and signatures; "Kalam" 400 for the form fields and the dialog quote; "Inter" 400/500/600 for page headings, buttons and small print (15px).

Layout: Header: eyebrow "Pinned up by our customers", headline "Notes from the garden gate" in Inter 800 (clamp 36px to 64px) with a hand-drawn SVG underline squiggle, and an "Add your note" button. Below, a 3-column masonry-like grid (CSS columns, 18px gap) of nine invented notes with different paper colours, random but fixed rotation between -4 and 4 degrees, tape strips, a name and place signature ("— Gwen, Hollin Row") and a small doodle (heart, spiral, sun, leaf, star, arrow) as inline SVG. A tenth card is a dashed-border "Pin yours here" placeholder. A footer line explains notes are moderated.

Interaction: Hovering or focusing a card straightens it to 0 degrees, lifts it with a bigger shadow, raises z-index and scales to 1.04 over 220ms. Pressing Enter or clicking opens a native <dialog> with the note enlarged (Caveat 38px), the signature, the doodle and Previous/Next/Close buttons; arrow keys move between notes when the dialog is open; Escape closes and returns focus. The "Add your note" button opens a second <dialog> with a short form (name, place, message limited to 140 characters with a live counter, paper colour radio swatches). Submitting adds a new card at the top of the wall with a drop-in animation (translate -30px, rotate -8deg to its resting angle), saved to localStorage (inside try/catch) and marked "Yours" with a delete icon. Cards are buttons inside a list; the new-note dialog traps focus and uses showModal. A tiny "Reset wall" link clears saved notes.

Responsive: 3 columns above 980px, 2 above 620px and 1 below; dialogs become full-width bottom sheets under 620px; no horizontal scroll at 390px (rotation is clipped by padding on the wall).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Handwriting is only for short notes; all UI text stays Inter for legibility. Visible focus 3px #d9482b with white offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no tilt change, lift or drop-in animation; notes keep a fixed small rotation). Invented names only.
