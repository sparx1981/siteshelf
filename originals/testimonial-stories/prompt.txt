Canvas & base: Deep plum page #1a1020 with a blurred magenta glow behind the phone card. Each story panel has its own vivid gradient background: coral to magenta (#ff7a5c to #c2307d), teal to indigo (#16b3a6 to #3b3bb3), amber to rust (#ffb84d to #d4531f), lime to forest (#b6e04a to #1f7a4d), sky to violet (#4cb8ff to #6a3fd0), rose to plum (#ff8fb3 to #7a2a6a). Text on panels is white or very dark (#160b1a) chosen per gradient for at least 4.5:1. Invented tutoring service "Lumen Learning".

Typography: "Bricolage Grotesque" 600/700/800 for quotes (clamp 26px to 34px inside the card, tight leading) and stats; "Inter" 400/500/600 for names, roles and UI (14 to 16px).

Layout: Two columns on desktop. Left: eyebrow "Real families, real results", headline "Six stories from the kitchen table." and a short paragraph, with a "Watch again" button and a note "Tap right or left, hold to pause". Right: a story card 390px wide by 700px tall with 28px radius and deep shadow. Inside: segmented progress bar at the top (six 3px bars, 4px gaps), a header row (28px initials avatar, name, role, time "2d ago", a pause or play icon button and a mute-free close-less control), a large quote in the middle, a bottom area with a big stat ("A in GCSE Maths in 8 weeks", "42 minutes saved a night", etc.) and a small "Read more" pill. Decorative SVG shapes (circles, arcs, sparks) float behind the text using generated geometry. Six invented stories: parents, a teacher and two students.

Interaction: Stories auto-advance every 5.5 seconds; the active segment fills left to right with a CSS animation driven by a variable duration; completed segments are full, upcoming empty. Tapping or clicking the right 60% of the card advances, the left 40% goes back; the first story Back restarts it and the last story Next ends in a "Watch again" end panel. Pressing and holding (pointerdown longer than 180ms) pauses the timer and animation; releasing resumes. Keyboard: ArrowRight/ArrowLeft move, Space or K toggles pause, Home/End jump, Escape pauses. A visible play or pause button gives a non-gesture way to pause (WCAG 2.2.2). The card is a section with aria-roledescription="carousel", each panel is role="group" aria-label "Story 2 of 6"; only the active panel is exposed (others hidden and inert). Auto-advance stops permanently once the user has used the keyboard or pause button until they press play. The timer also pauses when the tab is hidden or the card is not at least half visible (IntersectionObserver).

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack and the story card becomes 100% wide (max 420px) with height min(78vh, 700px); no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS/JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px white ring with dark offset on the panels' controls. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no auto-advance, no progress animation, manual next and previous only, no floating shapes movement). Invented names only.
