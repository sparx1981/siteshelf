Build a single-page "Gantt Chart Lite" for an invented project, "Pennick Greenhouse Build": a compact planning view with a task table on the left and a scrolling timeline on the right where bars can be moved and resized, dependencies push later tasks, the critical path is highlighted, and the scale zooms between days, weeks and months. All tasks, people and dates are invented and the data lives in the page.

Canvas & base: Page #f1f4ef, ink #1a2420 (15.5:1), secondary #5c6a63 (5.4:1), panels #ffffff with a 1px #d9e0d8 border and 16px radius. Group colours with white-text AA pairings: Groundwork clay #a8532a, Structure pine #1f6b52, Glazing teal #1d6f86, Fit-out plum #7a3f8a. Today line coral #e0463a; critical path outline #1a2420 dashed 2px; weekend columns #f6f8f5; grid lines #e5eae3. Dependency arrows #5c6a63 at 1.5px. Invented project and names only.

Typography: "Sora" 600 for the title (clamp 28 to 44px) and group headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for table text and bar labels at 13 to 15px; "IBM Plex Mono" 500 for dates and durations (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, pine.

Imagery & art direction: A painted banner (1400x220, 22px radius) opens the page: a greenhouse in progress on a meadow at morning, built in inline SVG: a pale sky with soft clouds, rolling green hills in three tones, a half-glazed greenhouse frame (white timber ribs, some panes filled with reflective blue-green glass and a few empty), a small digger, stacked pallets, a wheelbarrow, flowers in the foreground and a long soft shadow, 12% film grain and a gentle vignette. Owner avatars are small painted portraits (30px circles generated from seeds with varied skin tones and hair). Bars have rounded 8px ends, a lighter unfilled part and a darker progress fill, a subtle inner highlight and a soft shadow; milestones are diamonds with a white outline. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x300 site-progress photograph for the banner and 60x60 team portraits.

Layout: Banner with eyebrow "Project plan", title "Pennick Greenhouse Build" and a summary line ("12 tasks · 16 weeks · critical path 11 weeks"). Toolbar: zoom buttons (Days, Weeks, Months) as a segmented control, a Critical path toggle, "Today" scroll button and a Reset plan button. Main card: a left table (sticky, 300px: group headers, task name, owner avatar, duration) and a right scroller with a two-row header (months over weeks or days), 12 task rows of 42px, bars, milestones, dependency arrows in an SVG layer, weekend shading and a Today line. Beneath: a status line that reports the last action in words and a legend.

Copy: Tasks (group, name, start day, days, owner, depends on): Groundwork - Survey and marking 0, 3, Ines; Excavation 3, 5, Tomas, after Survey; Foundations 8, 8, Tomas, after Excavation; Structure - Timber frame 16, 12, Dara, after Foundations; Roof beams 28, 6, Dara, after Timber frame; Glazing - Order glass 4, 20 (lead time), Mira; Install glazing 34, 10, Mira, after Roof beams and Order glass; Seal and test 44, 4, Mira, after Install glazing; Fit-out - Benches and shelving 40, 12, Jonas, after Roof beams; Irrigation 44, 10, Jonas, after Benches; Electrics 44, 8, Vesna, after Roof beams; Handover 56, 0 (milestone), Ines, after Seal, Irrigation and Electrics. Project start Monday 5 January; Today is day 20.

Interaction: Bars are focusable role="slider"-like buttons with aria-labels (name, start date, end date, progress, owner). Drag a bar body with pointer events (setPointerCapture) to move it, snapped to whole days; drag the left or right 10px handle to resize (minimum 1 day). After every change, enforce finish-to-start dependencies: each task's start is at least the latest end of its predecessors; tasks that would start too early are pushed later, cascading, and the status line says "Pushed 3 later tasks". Moving a task earlier than its predecessors is clamped with a short message. Recompute the critical path (the longest chain) and outline those bars when the toggle is on. Zoom changes pixels per day (day 38px, week 14px, month 5px) with the viewport centre kept stable, header rows relabel, and arrows are redrawn from measured positions. Keyboard: Tab to a bar; Left and Right move it by one day; Shift+Left and Shift+Right resize its end by one day; Alt+Left and Alt+Right resize its start; Escape cancels the current drag. Reset plan restores the original data. Changes are announced politely (for example "Install glazing moved to start 12 Feb. Pushed 3 later tasks."). State persists in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the table narrows to names only (owner avatars hidden), the timeline scrolls horizontally inside its own container, the banner reduces to 140px; no horizontal page scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1a2420 outline with white offset. Group is never colour-only (group name appears in the table and in each bar's label tooltip), critical bars carry a dashed outline plus a "Critical" word in their aria-label. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no bar transitions). Invented names only.
