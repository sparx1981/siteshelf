Build a single-page "Order History Stepper" for an invented homeware shop, "Kiln & Co.": an order-tracking page that shows where a parcel is without using a map. A painted route strip with a delivery van gives the sense of place; a five-step stepper with expandable event logs gives the detail; an ETA card shows a delivery window and countdown. All data is simulated in the page.

Canvas & base: Warm page #f6f0e6, ink #211c16 (15.6:1), secondary #675d4e (5.8:1), step-complete pine #1f6b4a (white text 6.4:1), step-current amber #e29a2b with ink text, step-upcoming #cdbfa6 outline, alert coral #c4452f. Cards #fffdf8 with a 1px #e6dac5 border and 18px radius. Invented brand, order number "KC-20418" and names only.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for the page title (clamp 30 to 52px) and the ETA headline (28px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI at 14 to 16px; "DM Mono" 500 for timestamps, order numbers and tracking codes. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, pine.

Imagery & art direction: The hero is a painted route strip (full width, 240px, 22px radius) in inline SVG that replaces a map: on the left a brick warehouse with a loading door and stacked crates, on the right a cosy terraced house with a lit window, a hedge and a doorstep with a welcome mat; between them a gently curving road with dashed centre line, three intermediate stops (a sorting depot, a bridge over a stream, a village green with a tree) and small trees, lamp posts and clouds; golden-hour sky gradient (#9ccbe8 to #fde3b5), soft hill silhouettes, 12% film grain. A detailed delivery van (cream body, pine stripe, rounded roof, wheels with hubcaps, a small "K&Co" lettering) travels along the road according to progress, with a faint dust trail and a soft shadow. Item thumbnails in the order summary are painted product renders (a mug, a planter and a linen cushion) on warm backdrops. The courier is a painted portrait avatar with a name and rating. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x420 landscape panorama replacing the painted route strip, 400x400 product photographs and a 120x120 courier portrait.

Layout: Header with wordmark "Kiln & Co." and a "Help" link. Title block: eyebrow "Order KC-20418", title "Your parcel is on its way". Route strip hero with a progress label under it. Main two columns above 900px: left the vertical stepper with five steps (Ordered, Packed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered), each step a button that expands to show its events (time, place text and a short description) with a chevron; right a stack of cards: ETA card (headline "Arrives Thursday, 12:00 to 15:00", a window bar showing the 3-hour slot on a 08:00 to 20:00 axis with a moving "now" marker, a live countdown), the courier card (avatar, name, rating, "Message courier" button), the delivery address (invented, with example.com email) and the order summary (three items with thumbnails and prices, subtotal, delivery, total). A "Demo" control group at the bottom of the left column: "Simulate next step" and "Reset".

Copy: Events: Ordered "12 Oct, 09:14 - Order placed"; "09:15 - Payment confirmed"; Packed "12 Oct, 14:02 - Items picked at Pennick warehouse"; "15:40 - Packed in recycled box, label printed"; Shipped "13 Oct, 06:30 - Collected by Parcel Line"; "11:20 - Arrived at Saltgate depot"; Out for delivery "14 Oct, 07:45 - Loaded on van 12"; "09:10 - Courier Mira S. is 14 stops away"; Delivered "Arrives Thursday 12:00 to 15:00" (pending). Items: "Stoneware Mug x2 GBP 32", "Clay Planter GBP 36", "Linen Cushion GBP 42".

Interaction: Progress (0 to 4) drives everything: step states (done with a tick, current with a pulsing amber ring, upcoming outline), the connecting line fill (animated 500ms), the van position along the SVG road (using getPointAtLength on the road path, animated over 1.4 seconds with ease-in-out and the van rotating to follow the tangent), the progress label ("Step 3 of 5: Shipped") and the ETA card (before Out for delivery it shows "Arrives Thursday"; at step 4 a live countdown and the window marker move; at step 5 it reads "Delivered at 13:42, left in a safe place"). Each step is a disclosure button (aria-expanded) controlling a region with its events; the current step is expanded by default and only one stays open at a time unless Shift is held. "Simulate next step" advances progress by one with the animations above and announces the change politely; Reset returns to step 3. Keyboard: Up and Down arrows move between step buttons, Enter or Space toggles. The countdown ticks once per second inside a polite live region that only announces every 5 minutes of remaining time. Progress persists in localStorage inside try/catch.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack with the route strip first, the stepper then the cards; the strip keeps a 16:5 ratio with the van scaled; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #211c16 outline with white offset. Step state is never colour-only (tick glyph, "Current" word, and aria-current="step"). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (van jumps to position, no pulse, no line animation). Invented names only.
