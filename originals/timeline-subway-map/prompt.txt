Build a single-page "Subway Map Timeline" for an invented project, "Harbour Library Redesign": a project plan drawn like a metro map, where each coloured line is a workstream, each station is a milestone, interchanges are shared milestones, and time runs left to right. All data is invented.

Canvas & base: Page background #efe8d8 (map paper). The map sits in a rounded card (#f7f1e3, 1px #d8ccb0 border, 22px radius). Behind the lines, paint a faint city map in inline SVG: a meandering river (#bcd8e6) with a harbour bay at the lower left, four park blobs (#cfe3c0), a pale street grid at 8% opacity, a ring road curve and a few small building footprints (#e5dac2), all at low contrast so the lines dominate. Four line colours with AA label pairings: Discovery amber #e9a93c (dark text on labels), Design cobalt #2f56d4, Build pine #1f7a58, Launch vermilion #c4532f. Ink #1c1812 (16:1), secondary #5e5444 (6.2:1). Done stations are filled with their line colour; upcoming stations are paper-white with a coloured ring; interchanges are larger with a black ring.

Typography: "Figtree" 500/600/700 (a clean humanist sans in the spirit of transit signage) for all labels (station names 14px 700, dates 12px 500 tabular) and the page; "Young Serif" 400 for the page title (clamp 28 to 40px) and the detail panel title (24px). Line badges use 12px 700 caps in coloured rounded rectangles. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, #c4532f.

Imagery & art direction: Lines are drawn exactly like a classic transit diagram: 10px strokes with round caps and joins, running only horizontally and at 45 degree diagonals (each segment between two stations runs horizontally and then turns through a single 45 degree diagonal), parallel lines separated by a consistent gap where they share track, interchanges drawn as a white capsule with a thick black outline covering both lines. Stations have a white 3px halo so they stay legible on crowded areas. Add a subtle paper texture (feTurbulence at 5% opacity), a map frame with fine tick marks along the top and a title block at top left reading "Harbour Library Redesign - Project line map" with a small compass rose. Each station's detail panel carries a painted 480x260 illustration generated from a seed (an interview room, an audit floor, a concept sketch wall, a prototype table, a build site, a launch crowd) as inline SVG with grain, plus a painted portrait avatar for the owner. Media slots for real photography: 960x520 milestone photographs, 96x96 owner portraits and an optional hand-drawn base map image behind the lines.

Layout: Header with eyebrow "Project line map", title, and a status line ("Now: Prototype, 54% of stations reached"). Toolbar: a Map or List tab pair, and line filter chips (All lines, Discovery, Design, Build, Launch) as toggle buttons with line-coloured dots. Main area on desktop: the map (scrolls horizontally inside its own container if the viewport is narrow) and a right-hand detail panel (320px) showing the selected station. Station labels are drawn beside each station (above or below as specified in data), never overlapping lines. A pulsing "You are here" pin sits at the last reached station of the Design line. List view: for each line a section with its stations in date order (name, date, status chip Done, In progress or Upcoming). A legend below lists the four lines with their date ranges.

Copy: Stations (id, name, date, line, grid position): Discovery - Kick-off 12 Jan (1,1), Interviews 02 Feb (3,1), Content audit 28 Feb (5,2), Insights 20 Mar (7,2) which is an interchange with Design; Design - Insights, Concepts 15 Apr (9,3), Prototype 10 May (11,3), Usability test 04 Jun (13,4) interchange with Build; Build - Architecture 10 Mar (5,5), Components 22 Apr (8,5), Content migration 18 May (10,4), Usability test, Beta 30 Jun (15,4) interchange with Launch; Launch - Beta, Staff training 15 Jul (17,5), Public opening 12 Aug (19,5). Each station has a two-sentence description, an owner name (invented) and a status derived from a fixed demo date of 24 May.

Interaction: Draw the map from data in JavaScript: build each line's path by connecting its stations with a horizontal run and a single 45 degree diagonal (require dx >= |dy|), draw interchange capsules where a station belongs to two lines. Stations are real buttons (aria-label "Prototype, Design line, 10 May, done") positioned over the SVG with a roving tabindex: Left and Right move to the previous or next station along the current line, Up and Down switch the current line at an interchange, Enter or click selects and fills the detail panel (aria-live polite). Filter chips dim non-selected lines to 15% opacity (200ms) and keep their stations visible but inactive, All lines resets. Selecting an interchange in the panel offers "Also on the Build line" buttons that jump to the neighbouring station on that line. Map and List are a tablist with arrow-key switching; the List view contains the same information in an accessible nested list. Remember the last selected station and view in localStorage inside try/catch. Respect hover: a station highlights its line and shows a tooltip card with name and date after 150ms.

Responsive: Under 760px default to List view; Map view scrolls horizontally in its own container (min-width 1100px inside), the detail panel moves below the map, filter chips scroll horizontally; no horizontal page scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1c1812 outline with paper offset. Status never relies on colour alone (Done has a tick glyph in the list and panel, In progress a half-filled glyph, Upcoming an open ring). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no pin pulse, no dimming transition). Technique notes: keep the geometry in a single data array and generate both SVG and HTML from it so the map and list can never disagree. Invented names only.
