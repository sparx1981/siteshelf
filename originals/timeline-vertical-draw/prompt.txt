Build a single-page "Self-Drawing Timeline" for an invented lighting studio, "Lanternworks": a vertical company-history page where a hand-drawn-feeling path draws itself down the page as the reader scrolls, and milestone cards with illustrations slide in beside it. All names, dates and numbers are invented.

Canvas & base: Warm paper #f6efe2 with a very faint 3% grain; ink #1c1812 (16:1); secondary #6a5f4e (5.6:1). The path is a 4px rounded stroke in terracotta #c4532f with a lighter, undrawn "ghost" path behind it at #e4d6bd (3px, dashed 2 9). Milestone nodes are 18px circles filled paper with a 4px terracotta ring that fills when reached. Accent warm gold #e9a93c for the glowing head and highlights, pine #1f6b52 for tags. Cards are #fffaf0 with a 1px #e4d6bd border, 18px radius and a soft shadow.

Typography: "Young Serif" for the hero title (clamp 44 to 92px), milestone titles (28px) and the sticky year counter; "Figtree" 400/500/700 for body (17px / 1.6) and tags (12px caps, 0.14em tracking). Big outlined year numerals behind each card use "Young Serif" at 140px with a 1.5px #d9c8a8 stroke and transparent fill.

Imagery & art direction: A painted dusk hero (full width, 82vh) opens the page: layered violet and amber ridges, a low sun, a still lake with sun-column reflections and a single paper lantern glowing mid-frame, 14% film grain and a vignette, with the title "Twelve years of small lights" in cream over it and a "Scroll to follow the path" cue with a bobbing arrow. Each of the nine milestones has a painted 560x340 illustration generated as inline SVG from a seeded generator, matching its story: a two-desk garage studio with a kettle (interior), the first lantern product on a cream backdrop (product shot with soft shadow), a harbour studio at golden hour (coastal landscape), a team of twelve as simple painted portraits in a row (portrait faces, varied skin tones, hair and clothing), a remote-working evening (interior with a window glow), a greenhouse of lights (forest palette), a prize night (product with gold accents), a night street of lit windows (city, night palette), and a speculative "next" scene (abstract colour field). Add 12% grain, a rounded 14px frame and a soft inner shadow so they feel like printed photographs. Media slots for real photography: 1120x680 images per milestone, and a 1920x1080 hero photograph.

Layout: Sticky top bar (blur backdrop) with "Lanternworks" wordmark, a thin progress bar showing page progress and a live year counter that updates to the milestone nearest the viewport centre. Below the hero, a central spine: on desktop (above 860px) the path winds through the centre of a 1000px column and cards alternate left and right of it, each linked to its node by a short connector; on mobile the path runs along the left edge (x = 22px) and every card sits to its right at full width. Final block after the last node: a closing panel "Next: the long dusk" with a quiet call-to-action button "Sign up for the 2027 letter".

Copy: Milestones (year, title, text): 2014 "Two desks and a kettle" - we start in a garage with a borrowed soldering iron and a lot of tea; 2016 "The first lantern ships" - 400 hand-finished lamps leave for twelve towns; 2017 "A studio by the harbour" - north light, salt air and a bigger bench; 2019 "Twelve of us" - the team doubles and learns to say no politely; 2020 "Together, apart" - we build a remote workshop kit and ship it to every desk; 2021 "The greenhouse" - a glass room for testing light on plants; 2023 "The Pennick Prize" - recognised for lamps that last twenty years; 2025 "One million evenings lit" - counted in a small ceremony at the quay; 2026 "What comes next" - slower, warmer and repairable by default. Each card also has a tag (Studio, Product, Place, People, Craft, Research, Award, Milestone, Future) and a one-line stat such as "400 lamps".

Interaction: Build the path in JavaScript from the measured vertical positions of the nodes (rebuilt on resize and when fonts load): smooth cubic Bezier segments that swing about 36px left and right of the spine between nodes. On every scroll frame (requestAnimationFrame, passive listener) compute progress = (scrollY + 0.6 * viewportHeight - pathTop) / pathHeight, clamp to 0 to 1, set stroke-dasharray to the full length and stroke-dashoffset to length * (1 - progress), and place a glowing 10px gold head at getPointAtLength(progress * length). A node ring fills when the head passes it and its card animates in once (IntersectionObserver at 25% visibility): translateX 40px to 0 and opacity 0 to 1 over 600ms with a 120ms stagger for the illustration, direction depending on side. Cards are real article elements in an ordered list so the structure reads in order without the animation; the SVG path is aria-hidden. The sticky year counter has aria-hidden and the progress bar uses a visually hidden polite status only on keyboard-triggered jumps. A "Jump to" select in the top bar scrolls to a milestone (smooth, or instant under reduced motion). Under prefers-reduced-motion the full path is drawn, all cards are visible, no head glow and no sliding.

Responsive: Under 860px switch to the left-edge path layout, hide the outlined year numerals behind cards (show the year as a tag on the card), reduce hero to 70vh; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1c1812 outline with paper offset. Technique notes: path drawing uses SVG getTotalLength and dashoffset only, no scroll-jacking; keep scroll handlers passive and do layout reads once per frame. Invented names only.
