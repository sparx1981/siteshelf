Build a single-page "Cipher Playground" tool for an invented puzzle-and-learning site, "Cipherdesk": encrypt and decrypt text with three classic ciphers (Caesar, Vigenère, Atbash) while a painted brass cipher wheel turns to show exactly which letter becomes which, with a step-by-step explanation and a letter-frequency chart. This is for learning and puzzles only. Everything runs in the page.

Canvas & base: A "detective's study" look. Page #16110c with a faint leather grain, panels #231a12 with a 1px #4a3a28 border and 16px radius, ink #f5ead2 (14.6:1), secondary #cdbf9f (9.3:1), brass #d6ab4c (dark text #1d150c on it 8.7:1), changed-letter teal #66d0b6, parchment #efe3c8 used inside the output card with dark ink #2b1f12. Invented brand "Cipherdesk" with a tiny keyhole logomark.

Typography: "Cormorant Garamond" 700 for the title (clamp 32 to 58px) and card headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 15px; "IBM Plex Mono" 500 for text, keys and the wheel letters in the explanation (tabular). Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, brass.

Imagery & art direction: A painted banner (inline SVG: dark walnut desk top with grain, a pool of lamplight, a sealed envelope with a red wax seal, a brass skeleton key, and a faint compass-rose line drawing, with light vignette and film grain) sits behind the title. The star of the page is the cipher wheel, an illustrated brass instrument: two concentric rings on a dark baize disc, outer ring fixed with the plain alphabet and inner ring turning, each engraved with 26 letters, bevelled brass gradients, tiny rivets, a centre boss with a small keyhole, and a soft cast shadow. The active letter pair is marked by a glowing teal bridge across both rings. Output text sits on a parchment card with torn-paper edges and a faint ink stain. Media slots for real photography: optionally one 1600x420 desk photograph for the banner.

Layout: Banner with eyebrow "Cipherdesk", title "Cipher Playground", one-line intro. Two columns above 980px. Left: mode selector (Caesar, Vigenère, Atbash as a segmented radio group), direction toggle (Encrypt, Decrypt), the option for the chosen cipher (Caesar: shift slider 1 to 25 with number; Vigenère: key field letters only; Atbash: no option), the Message textarea, the Result card (parchment, with changed letters highlighted teal-on-dark chips only inside a "Show changes" view or underline), and Copy, Swap (move result into input) and Sample buttons. Right: the cipher wheel (square, up to 460px), a step player (Previous, Play walk-through, Next with "Step 3 of 12"), an explanation line ("H moves forward 3 places to K") and a steps table of the first 12 letters (Letter, Rule, Result). Below, full width: a Letter frequency chart (26 bars for the result, the tallest highlighted, labelled with counts).

Copy: Intro "Pick a cipher, type a message, and watch the wheel turn." Explanations: Caesar "Each letter slides forward 3 places in the alphabet." Vigenère "A repeating key decides a different shift for each letter: the key letter E means shift 4." Atbash "The alphabet is mirrored: A becomes Z, B becomes Y, and so on." Sample text "Meet me by the old clock tower at midnight." with key "LANTERN", shift 3. Messages: "Result copied." "Key must use letters only." "Moved the result into the message box."

Interaction: Letters only are transformed; case is preserved; digits, spaces and punctuation pass through unchanged and do not advance the Vigenère key. Caesar encrypt: (p + k) mod 26; decrypt: (c - k) mod 26. Vigenère: shift for each letter is the next key letter (A=0), encrypt adds, decrypt subtracts. Atbash: 25 - p (its own inverse; the direction toggle is disabled with a note). Wheel: outer ring fixed; the inner ring is drawn with the alphabet starting at the top and rotated by -shift x (360 / 26) degrees; in Atbash it is drawn reversed; the active step letter index is marked at its angle. Rotation accumulates (shortest path) and animates with a 700ms ease with slight overshoot (instant under reduced motion). The step player walks through letters of the message: Next and Previous change the step; Play advances every 900ms and stops at the end; the wheel turns to each step's shift (Vigenère changes shift at every letter). The steps table highlights the current row (aria-current=step). The frequency chart counts only letters in the result and updates live. All text updates on input; a polite live region announces the explanation of the current step when the user moves between steps. Copy uses the Clipboard API in try/catch. State persists in localStorage inside try/catch.

Technique notes: Letter placement on the wheel uses transform="rotate(i x 360/26) translate(0,-r)" so every glyph stands upright relative to the ring. To avoid the long way round, store the previous total rotation and add the signed shortest delta ((target - current + 540) mod 360 - 180). The glowing bridge is a rounded rectangle rotated to the active index, spanning both rings, drawn above the rotating group so it never moves with it.

Responsive: Under 980px the wheel moves above the controls (max 360px), the steps table scrolls inside its own box, the frequency chart bars narrow to 8px with only every second label; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #f5ead2 outline with dark offset. Never rely on colour alone: changed letters are also underlined, and every step is written out. State plainly that these ciphers are not secure and are for learning only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
