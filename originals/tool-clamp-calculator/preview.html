The quiet hour before the ferry

By Marit Olsen · 6 min read

Mist lies on the water until the first boat pulls away, and for a few minutes the harbour belongs to the gulls. We walked the length of the quay with coffee, counting masts and arguing gently about the weather.

“Everything looks larger when the light is low.”

By the time the sun cleared the ridge the town had put on its working face again, and the fog turned to a thin gold haze above the roofs.
