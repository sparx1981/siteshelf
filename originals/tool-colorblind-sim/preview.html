Edit six colours and see what changes for people with protan, deutan, tritan or no colour vision. An approximation to guide design, not a medical test.

Severity 100%

At 100% the three colour types are the full “-opia” forms (protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia). Lower values give the milder “-anomaly” forms. Monochrome is always complete.

Tip: Add labels or patterns so colour is never the only signal.
