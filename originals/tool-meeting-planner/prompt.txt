Build a single-page "Meeting Time Planner" tool for an invented scheduling site, "Meridian Desk": choose up to five cities and a date, see everyone's working hours on a 24-hour overlap grid, jump to the best slot and copy a ready-to-send invite line. Everything runs in the page using the browser's Intl time-zone data.

Canvas & base: A clear, airy "flight board" look. Page #eaf1f8, panels #ffffff with a 1px #d3deec border and 16px radius, ink #10213a (15.7:1), secondary #4b5d78 (6.9:1), accent blue #1f5fd1 (white text 5.9:1). Grid cell colours with text always present: working hours #bfe8d4 (ink text), shoulder hours (early 07 to 09, late 17 to 21) #fde8b8, night #1e2b4a (white text). Best-slot highlight is a 3px blue outline plus a "Best" flag. Invented brand "Meridian Desk" with a tiny meridian-line logomark.

Typography: "Manrope" 700/800 for the title (clamp 30 to 52px) and city names; "DM Sans" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 15px; "DM Mono" 500 for times and grid numbers (tabular numerals). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, blue.

Imagery & art direction: Each city row starts with a painted skyline thumbnail (inline SVG from the scene library's city generator, 120x56) whose palette follows that city's current local time of day: dawn (05 to 08), day (08 to 17, the "coast" palette), gold (17 to 19), dusk (19 to 22) and night (22 to 05), so the stack of skylines reads like a miniature sunrise-to-midnight gradient down the page. A header banner shows a painted "terminator" band: an SVG strip of the globe seen from above with a soft day-to-night gradient, a thin meridian line and a pin for each selected city, the pins positioned by longitude. Selected-time cards show the city, local time in large type and a small sun or moon glyph. Media slots for real photography: optionally one 1600x360 aerial photograph for the banner.

Layout: Banner with eyebrow "Meridian Desk", title "Meeting Time Planner", short intro and the terminator strip. Controls bar: date input, working hours (start and end selects, default 09:00 to 17:00), city picker (select plus Add button, max 5), Jump to best slot button. The overlap grid: a horizontally scrollable table region with a sticky first column (skyline thumb, city, UTC offset) and 24 hour columns in the first city's local time, each header an hour button; rows show local hour numbers in each cell. Under the grid: the Selected slot panel (a card per city with local time and date, plus "next day" or "previous day" tags) and the Best slot summary with Copy invite.

Copy: Default cities London, New York, Tokyo. Summary: "Best slot: 14:00 to 15:00 London, 09:00 New York, 23:00 Tokyo (late)". When none overlaps: "No hour works for everyone. Closest: 13:00 London, with Tokyo at 22:00." Invite text: "Tue 14 Oct, 14:00 London / 09:00 New York / 23:00 Tokyo". Messages: "Invite line copied." "Added Sydney." "Removed Tokyo."

Interaction: City data: name, IANA zone, longitude (London Europe/London, New York America/New_York, Los Angeles America/Los_Angeles, Sao Paulo America/Sao_Paulo, Lagos Africa/Lagos, Nairobi Africa/Nairobi, Cairo Africa/Cairo, Berlin Europe/Berlin, Dubai Asia/Dubai, Mumbai Asia/Kolkata, Singapore Asia/Singapore, Tokyo Asia/Tokyo, Sydney Australia/Sydney, Auckland Pacific/Auckland). For the chosen date, compute the instant of local midnight in the first city, then for each of 24 slots and each city compute local minutes via Intl.DateTimeFormat formatToParts (handles half-hour zones and daylight saving). A slot is Working when it falls fully inside the working hours, Shoulder when it starts 07:00 to 08:59 or 17:00 to 20:59, else Night. Best slot: first the longest run of slots that are Working for all cities (report start to end); otherwise the single slot with the lowest penalty (Working 0, Shoulder 1, Night 3 per city). Clicking or pressing Enter on an hour header selects that slot; Left and Right arrows move between headers (roving tabindex); the selected column is outlined for all rows. Cities can be removed with a labelled button and reordered with up and down buttons; the first city defines the column hours. State (cities, date, hours) persists in localStorage inside try/catch. Copy uses the Clipboard API in try/catch with a polite live announcement; changing the selected slot announces "Selected 14:00 London, 09:00 New York, 23:00 Tokyo".

Technique notes: Offset lookup: offset = Date.UTC(parts) - instant, rounded to a minute, applied twice around a daylight-saving boundary when finding local midnight. Skyline palettes are chosen by local hour, not by the grid state, so the thumbnails remain correct when the user picks a different slot (they show the selected slot's local time).

Responsive: Under 760px the grid scrolls horizontally inside its own region (cells 30px wide, sticky city column 118px), controls stack, skyline thumbs shrink to 72x36; no horizontal scroll of the page at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #10213a outline with white offset. Never rely on colour alone: every cell shows its local hour number and a legend explains the three states in text. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented brand only; real city names are fine.
