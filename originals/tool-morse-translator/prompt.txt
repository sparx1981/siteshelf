Build a single-page "Morse Translator" tool for an invented signalling-history site, "Lampwick": translate text to Morse and Morse back to text, then play the message as silent light flashes from a painted lighthouse lamp with a moving timeline playhead. There is no audio at all. A tap pad lets you key your own Morse and see it decoded. Everything runs in the page.

Canvas & base: A calm night-harbour look. Page #07121f, panels #0f2236 with a 1px #26415e border and 16px radius, ink #e8f1fa (15.6:1), secondary #a9bdd2 (8.9:1), lamp amber #ffd166 (dark text #1a1204 on it 12.1:1), signal teal #5fd0c4 for decoded text, alert coral #ff9a8a. Invented brand "Lampwick" with a small lantern logomark.

Typography: "Space Grotesk" 600 for the title (clamp 30 to 54px) and headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 15px; "JetBrains Mono" 500 for Morse strings and codes (tabular). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, amber.

Imagery & art direction: A wide painted night-harbour banner (inline SVG: starry gradient sky with a crescent moon, layered dark ocean from the scene library's sea generator in its night palette with moonlit glints, a small sailing boat silhouette, and a striped red-and-white lighthouse on a rock at the right with a gallery rail and a glowing lantern room). A soft light beam is a translucent polygon from the lantern sweeping left across the water; it is OFF by default and fades on for every dot and dash during playback, with the lantern glow and a sparkle on the waves in sync. The same flashes drive a large round "signal lamp" panel (brass bezel, glass dome, radial glow, reflector rings) so the signal is readable without the banner. Reference chart tiles are drawn as little lamp icons: round bulbs for dots, long capsules for dashes. Media slots for real photography: optionally one 1600x420 night-lighthouse photograph for the banner.

Layout: Banner with eyebrow "Lampwick", title "Morse Translator" and the line "Silent signals: light only, no sound." Two columns above 1000px. Left: Translator panel with a mode toggle (Text to Morse, Morse to text), an input (textarea max 140 characters with counter), the output as plain text in a monospace block and as a glyph row where each letter is a group of bulbs and capsules and words are separated by wider gaps, buttons Copy, Swap (use output as input, flip mode) and Sample; then the Playback panel: the signal lamp, status chip (Idle, Dot, Dash, Gap), a timeline strip with a playhead, Play/Pause/Stop buttons, Speed slider 3 to 6 WPM (default 5) with an opt-in checkbox "Allow faster speeds (up to 20 WPM)", and a Loop checkbox. Right: the Reference chart (A to Z, 0 to 9 and common punctuation; each tile shows the character, its code in text and lamp glyphs; clicking or pressing a tile adds that character to the text input) and the Tap pad: a big round key button you press and release (pointer or Space/Enter) with timing-based dot/dash detection, a live Morse line, decoded text and a Clear button.

Copy: Default input "SOS meet at the harbour". Messages: "Copied Morse." "Playing at 5 WPM." "Unknown symbol skipped: #". Hints: "Separate letters with a space and words with a slash." Sample Morse: "... --- ... / -- . . - / .- - / - .... . / .... .- .-. -... --- ..- .-.".

Interaction: Table: ITU Morse for A to Z, 0 to 9 and . , ? ' ! / ( ) & : ; = + - _ " $ @. Text to Morse: uppercase, letters joined by one space, words by " / ", unknown characters skipped with a polite note. Morse to text: normalise dots and dashes typed as middle dots or en dashes, split on spaces and slashes, unknown codes show a question mark. Timing: unit ms = 1200 / WPM; dot 1 unit lit; dash 3; gap inside a letter 1 dark; between letters 3; between words 7. Playback builds an event list of (lit, duration) and runs it with requestAnimationFrame against performance.now so pausing and resuming keep position; the lamp, beam, status chip, active glyph group and playhead update from the same clock. Reduced motion: the beam fades are removed (the lamp still switches state instantly), the faster-speed option is disabled, and playback remains available because it is user-triggered. Tap pad: pointerdown starts timing, pointerup ends it: shorter than 2 units is a dot, otherwise a dash; the unit follows the speed slider; a silence of 3 units after a release commits the letter and 7 units adds a word space; Space and Enter work as the key (keydown/keyup) and the button supports pointer capture. Copy uses the Clipboard API in try/catch with a polite live announcement. State (mode, text, speed) persists in localStorage inside try/catch.

Technique notes: Timeline strip: width = total units x 10px; bars are absolutely positioned divs (lit segments only) inside a scroll container; the playhead is a 2px line moved by transform from elapsed ms; auto-scroll keeps it 40 percent from the left. Beam fade uses opacity only. All lamp states are also text (the status chip), so nothing depends on colour or brightness alone.

Responsive: Under 1000px the columns stack with the Playback panel first after the translator; the banner lighthouse stays visible at right while the title sits above the water; the reference chart is 4 tiles per row; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images, no audio, no vibration. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #e8f1fa outline with dark offset. Photosensitivity safety (WCAG 2.3.1): at the default range of 3 to 6 WPM a dot plus its gap lasts at least 400ms, so the lamp never flashes more than 3 times per second; faster speeds are opt-in behind a labelled checkbox with the warning "Fast speeds flash quickly; stop if uncomfortable", and the flashing area stays small (the lamp panel and the banner beam). Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
