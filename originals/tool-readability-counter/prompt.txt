Build a single-page "Readability Counter" tool for an invented writing-tools site, "Plainpage": write or paste text and see words, characters, sentences, reading and speaking time, a reading-ease score with grade level on a dial, and a sentence map that flags the long sentences. Everything runs in the page; nothing is uploaded.

Canvas & base: A bright writer's-desk look. Page #f6f1e6 with a subtle paper grain, editor and panels #fffdf8 with a 1px #ded5c0 border and 16px radius, ink #1c1a17 (16.8:1), secondary #5b564b (6.9:1), accent teal #0f6b66 (white text 6.6:1), warning amber text #8a4f00 on tint #fbe9c7 (6.3:1), alert red #a3201a on tint #fadbd6 (6.6:1), good green #1f6b3a. Invented brand "Plainpage".

Typography: "Lora" 600 for the title (clamp 30 to 52px) and the editor text (17px, line-height 1.7, so it feels like a manuscript); "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 15px; "IBM Plex Mono" 500 for numbers (tabular). Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, teal.

Imagery & art direction: A painted desk-top banner (inline SVG: warm wood desk seen from above, an open lined notebook with a handwritten margin note, a sharpened yellow pencil, a stoneware mug rendered by the scene library's product generator with a soft contact shadow, and a pair of round glasses, all with grain and a gentle window-light gradient) sits behind the title. The reading-ease dial is an illustrated brass gauge: a half-circle face with coloured arcs (hard, medium, easy), tick marks, a needle with a tiny counterweight and a glass highlight. Stat tiles carry small hand-drawn icons (a pencil, a clock, a megaphone, a ruler). Media slots for real photography: optionally one 1600x420 overhead desk photograph for the banner.

Layout: Banner with eyebrow "Plainpage", title "Readability Counter" and the one-line promise "Write plainly. See where readers will stumble." Two columns above 980px. Left: the editor panel (label "Your text", textarea min 340px, character limit 20,000 shown as a thin bar, buttons Sample (plain), Sample (dense), Clear, Copy stats). Below it, the Sentence map: your text re-rendered as sentences, each with a left colour bar (green up to 20 words, amber 21 to 30, red 31 or more) and its word count in a small tab; clicking a sentence selects it in the editor. Right (sticky): the dial with the grade readout ("Grade 7, fairly easy"), a target grade select (Grade 6, 8, 10, 12) that shows "On target" or "2 grades above target", six stat tiles (Words, Characters, Sentences, Paragraphs, Reading time, Speaking time), and a Flags list ("3 long sentences", "1 very long sentence", "Average sentence 18 words").

Copy: Placeholder "Start typing, or press Sample to see how it works." Messages: "Stats copied." "Sentence 4 is 34 words. Try splitting it after 'because'." Dial labels: Very easy, Easy, Fairly easy, Standard, Fairly hard, Hard, Very hard. Samples: a friendly 150-word note about a community garden (plain) and a dense 120-word policy paragraph (dense).

Interaction: Update on every input (debounced 120ms). Words = whitespace-separated tokens containing a letter or digit. Sentences split on . ! ? followed by space or end, ignoring common abbreviations (Mr., Dr., e.g., i.e.). Syllables per word: lowercase, strip non-letters, count vowel groups (aeiouy), subtract a trailing silent e, minimum 1. Flesch reading ease = 206.835 - 1.015 x (words/sentences) - 84.6 x (syllables/words); Flesch-Kincaid grade = 0.39 x (words/sentences) + 11.8 x (syllables/words) - 15.59, clamped 0 to 18 for the display. Reading time at 225 words per minute, speaking time at 150, shown as "1 min 12 s" and "under 1 min". The needle eases to the new reading-ease value (700ms spring-like overshoot, instant under reduced motion). The sentence map highlights the sentence containing the caret, and Enter or Space on a focused sentence selects its range in the editor. Copy uses the Clipboard API in try/catch; a polite live region announces the grade after typing pauses (700ms) and after Copy. The text is saved in localStorage inside try/catch (never sent anywhere).

Technique notes: Needle angle = -90 + (clamp(ease, 0, 100) / 100) x 180 degrees; animate with requestAnimationFrame using a critically damped spring (stiffness 170, damping 26) so it settles without bounce, or a CSS transition with cubic-bezier(.3,1.4,.5,1). The sentence map keeps character offsets for each sentence so selection is exact.

Responsive: Under 980px the right column moves above the editor (dial and tiles in a 2-column grid, not sticky); the textarea is at least 260px high; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1c1a17 outline with 2px offset. Never rely on colour alone: sentence tabs show the word count and the flag list is written text. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Invented names only.
