Build a single-page long-form article, "The Last Lamplighter of Saltgate", set like a printed magazine feature. The goal is beautiful, correct typography on the web: a proper drop cap, hanging punctuation, pull quotes that hang their opening quotation mark into the margin, sidenotes, balanced headlines, small caps, old-style numerals and a baseline rhythm. A small "Type settings" panel lets the reader change the typography live. All people, places and text are invented.

Canvas & base: Paper #fbf6ea with a faint 3% grain; ink #1b1713 (16:1); secondary #675d4c (5.8:1); rule colour #d9ccb0; accent oxblood #8f2d1f for the drop cap, pull-quote marks, links and small caps labels. Hero illustration area on a #1b1713 field. Cards and the settings panel use #fffaf0 with a 1px #e3d7bc border, 16px radius. Invented publication name "Saltgate Review".

Typography: "Playfair Display" 700/800 (and 700 italic) for the headline (clamp 38px to 84px, line-height 1.02, text-wrap: balance) and the pull quote; "Spectral" 400 / 400 italic / 600 for body at 19px/1.65 with hyphens: auto, text-wrap: pretty, font-variant-numeric: oldstyle-nums, font-feature-settings "liga","kern","onum"; "Work Sans" 500/600 for the kicker, byline, captions and settings at 12 to 14px, with letter-spacing .12em on small-cap labels. Small caps (font-variant-caps: all-small-caps) introduce each section's first three words.

Imagery & art direction: The hero is a painted wide illustration (1400x640) in inline SVG: a harbour town at lamp-lighting time, a dusk sky going from teal to rose, rows of crooked rooftops, a stone quay with a line of old gas lamps lit one by one in warm gold with soft halos and reflections in dark water, a lone figure with a long pole at the nearest lamp, 14% film grain and a vignette. A second inset illustration (a 360x440 painted portrait of an elderly lamplighter in a flat cap, grey stubble, kind eyes, warm lamplight on one side of the face) is placed as a float in the text with a caption; a third small plate (an interior of a workshop with a lamp pole and oil can) sits between sections. All three carry captions in Work Sans italic with a credit line "Illustration". Draw a thin double rule (3px / 1px) under the byline and a decorative fleuron (three small diamonds) between sections. Media slots for real photography: a 1400x640 hero photograph, a 720x880 portrait and a 1100x560 workshop photograph.

Layout: A top bar with the publication name, a reading-progress hairline and a "Type settings" button. Header block: kicker (small caps) "Features · Saltgate", headline with balanced wrapping, a deck (italic, 22px, 2 lines), byline "By Dara Okoye · Photographs and illustration by the Review studio · 12 October" and the hero. Body: a centred column with a 60ch measure (switchable); first paragraph has a 3-line drop cap in Playfair 800 oxblood using CSS initial-letter: 3 with a @supports fallback to a float (font-size 4.4em, line-height .8, padding-right .08em); the first line is in small caps. Then paragraphs, a subheading, a pull quote that spans the column with a hanging opening quotation mark (positioned in the left margin using hanging-punctuation where supported and a negative text-indent of .45em as the fallback), a sidenote in the margin on screens above 1100px (and inline below, as a bordered aside), the portrait float on the left, the workshop plate, a closing paragraph ending with an oxblood end mark (a small square). A "Keep reading" card at the end.

Copy: Write about 9 original paragraphs (about 700 words) on the last man who lights a street by hand, covering his route, his pole and key, the gas company that shut down, a retirement that did not happen, a schoolchild who asks questions, and why the town keeps him. Pull quote: "I do not light the street for the people who walk it. I light it for the ones who look out of their windows." Sidenote: "A lamplighter's round in Saltgate takes 41 minutes and 112 steps."

Interaction: The settings panel (a native dialog or disclosure) has: Text size (A- and A+, 17 to 24px), Measure (Narrow 52ch, Comfortable 60ch, Wide 70ch), Columns (single or two-column body with a thin rule on screens above 1000px), Hyphenation (on or off), Hanging punctuation (on or off), Drop cap (Classic 3-line, Raised, Off) and Baseline grid overlay (a repeating line every 1.65 x font size, aria-hidden, pointer-events none). Changes apply immediately through CSS custom properties and classes on the article, are persisted in localStorage inside try/catch and announced politely ("Two columns on."). The progress hairline follows scroll through a passive scroll handler with one requestAnimationFrame per frame. The sidenote is linked to its marker with aria-describedby; the pull quote is a blockquote with a cite line. Technique notes: use real hanging punctuation and balanced wrapping where the browser supports them and graceful fallbacks otherwise; never fake bold or italic, load only the weights used.

Responsive: Under 760px the body is 18px, the portrait stops floating and sits full-width above its paragraph, columns and sidenote margins are disabled (the sidenote becomes an inline aside), the headline drops to 40px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1b1713 outline with paper offset. Invented names only.
