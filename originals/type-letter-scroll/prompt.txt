Build a single-page "Letter Scroll Reveal" long-read for an invented journal, "The Slow Hour": an essay called "On Slow Mornings" in which every letter fades, rises and sharpens one after another as each paragraph scrolls into view. It is a reading experience first; the motion is a quiet reward, never an obstacle.

Canvas & base: Warm paper #f7f1e6 with a faint 3% grain; ink #1d1a16 (16:1); secondary #6b6253 (5.7:1); accent apricot #d9692b used for the drop rule, links and the progress line; plate frames #fffaf0 with a 1px #e6dac2 border. The hero is a full-bleed painted dawn that dissolves into the paper at its bottom edge. Invented publication and author names only.

Typography: "Instrument Serif" 400 for the hero lede (clamp 48px to 120px, line-height .98, italic on the last two words) and section headings (clamp 30 to 44px); "Newsreader" 400 / 400 italic / 500 for body at 20px/1.7 with a 62ch measure; "Inter" 500/600 for the byline, the progress label and the replay button at 13 to 14px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, apricot.

Imagery & art direction: The hero is a painted early-morning scene in inline SVG: pale violet sky grading to peach and cream, a low sun with a wide soft halo, two ridges in dusty blue, a mist band and a still lake with a long vertical sun column, 12% grain, and a cream gradient at the bottom so the first paragraph sits on paper. Between sections place three painted plates (each 1100x560, 14px radius, soft shadow, 12% grain, a one-line caption in italic): a sunlit kitchen with a kettle and two cups (interior), a walking path through morning mist (forest palette landscape), and a breakfast table from above with bread, fruit and a cup (food). Add a small hand-drawn sun glyph next to the byline. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 hero photograph of early light on water, and three 1100x560 plate photographs.

Layout: A slim sticky header (blur backdrop) with the wordmark "The Slow Hour", a reading-progress line (2px apricot) along its bottom edge and a "Replay letters" button at the right. Hero (80vh): eyebrow "Essay · 7 min read", the lede "Slow mornings are a kind of wealth." split into letters that reveal automatically on load over about 1.6 seconds, a byline "By Mira Solt, 12 October" with the sun glyph, and a quiet scroll cue. Article column: 62ch wide, centred, six paragraphs of original text, two subheadings, a pull quote in large italic with a thin apricot rule, and the three plates breaking the column to 1100px wide on desktop. A closing line "Thank you for reading slowly." and a small subscribe row (email input and button, with inline success message).

Copy: Write six original paragraphs about unhurried mornings: the kettle before the phone, the light moving across a kitchen table, walking before deciding anything, the small pleasure of a task done without an audience, what mornings teach about attention, and an invitation to try twenty minutes. Pull quote: "Nothing important was ever hurried into existence before breakfast."

Interaction: Split each paragraph, heading and the lede into words (inline-block, so words never break mid-word) and letters (inline-block spans, aria-hidden), keeping the original sentence in a visually hidden span so screen readers read normal text. Letters start at opacity 0, translateY(0.55em) rotate(2deg) and blur(3px); when the paragraph is 35% visible (IntersectionObserver, fire once) each letter transitions to its natural state over 520ms with transition-delay = min(index, 90) * 14ms so even long paragraphs finish within about 1.3 seconds. The hero lede runs on load with a 40ms per-letter stagger. The reading-progress line width tracks (scrollY / (documentHeight - viewportHeight)) in a passive scroll handler with one requestAnimationFrame per frame. "Replay letters" resets and re-reveals the paragraphs currently in view. The subscribe form validates a basic email pattern, shows an inline error or "Thank you, the next hour is yours." and announces politely. Under prefers-reduced-motion all text is visible immediately with no transforms or blur, and the Replay button is hidden. Technique notes: letters use only opacity, transform and filter; no layout changes occur during the reveal because each letter occupies its final space from the start.

Responsive: Under 760px the body drops to 18px, plates become full-bleed with 10px radius and shorter 16:10 crops, the lede scales to 52px, header shows only the wordmark and progress line; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1d1a16 outline with paper offset. Never hide the real text from assistive technology. Invented names only.
