Build a single-page "Outline Marquee Type" landing page for an invented type foundry, "Nocturne Type Co.": five rows of giant words scroll in opposite directions, alternating between outlined and solid letters, and the motion responds to how fast the visitor scrolls. It is a loud, poster-like hero followed by a short quiet page.

Canvas & base: Near-black #0e0b14 page with a full-bleed painted night city behind the marquee, darkened by a #0e0b14 overlay at 62% so the letters stay dominant: a violet-to-magenta dusk sky, layered towers with warm lit windows, a few neon sign glows in #ff3d8b and #3fd0e0 and a faint haze band. Letter colours: outline cream #fff3df (2px stroke), solid amber #ffb347, solid hot pink #ff3d8b, a cyan #3fd0e0 outline row and a cream solid row. Secondary text #bdb4d6 (8.5:1). Invented studio name only.

Typography: "Archivo Black" for the marquee words (uppercase, clamp 84px to 152px, line-height .92, letter-spacing -0.01em) and the section headings; "Space Grotesk" 400/500/700 for UI and body at 15 to 18px; "DM Mono" 500 for small labels. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, #ffb347.

Imagery & art direction: The painted city must read like a long-exposure night photograph: towers in three depth layers (#2a1b4a, #1a1033, #0d0820) with tiny warm window dots in seeded random positions, two glowing neon sign rectangles reflecting as soft blurs on the wet street at the bottom, rain-streak highlights, 14% film grain and a heavy vignette, all built as inline SVG so no external image is needed. Marquee words sit on top with mix-blend-mode normal; outline rows carry a soft glow (drop-shadow 0 0 24px in the stroke colour at 35%). A slim chromatic fringe (text-shadow 3px 0 #ff3d8b, -3px 0 #3fd0e0 at 40%) appears on every row while scroll speed is high. Below the hero, three painted specimen tiles (Aa on gradient fields) and a footer with a faint city skyline strip. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 night-street photograph for the hero backdrop with a 62% dark overlay.

Layout: Top bar: wordmark "Nocturne Type Co.", links (Fonts, Licensing, Journal) and a "Pause motion" toggle button. Hero (min 100vh): the five marquee rows stacked and vertically centred, with a small intro card pinned bottom-left (eyebrow "Foundry", sentence "Loud letters for quiet grids.", buttons "Browse fonts" and "Read the licence"). Marquee texts (each repeated until the track is at least 2x viewport wide): row 1 outline "TYPE FOR THE NIGHT SHIFT -", row 2 solid amber "LOUD LETTERS - QUIET GRIDS -", row 3 outline cyan "NOCTURNE TYPE CO. -", row 4 solid pink "SET IN BLACK - SOLD IN DAYLIGHT -", row 5 outline cream "OPEN ALL HOURS -". After the hero: a "Fonts" list of five invented families with a one-line description and a hover that flips outline to fill, then a footer.

Copy: Fonts: "Streetlight (display, 3 weights)", "Last Tram (grotesque, 5 weights)", "Neon Ledger (mono, 2 weights)", "Midnight Serif (text, 4 weights)", "Kiosk Pro (poster, 1 weight)". Footer line: "Made after dark, in a rented room above a bakery."

Interaction: Each row moves with a requestAnimationFrame loop: x += direction * (baseSpeed + boost) * dt where baseSpeed is 40 to 90 px per second per row and boost = min(|scrollVelocity| * 0.9, 900) px per second (scroll velocity measured from window.scrollY deltas and smoothed with a factor 0.12). The track is two identical copies and x wraps modulo the width of one copy so the loop is seamless; widths are measured after document.fonts.ready and on resize. Rows skew horizontally by skewX = clamp(scrollVelocity * 0.012, -8, 8) degrees, lerped at 0.1 per frame. Hovering or focusing a row slows it to 15% speed and fills outline letters with their colour (200ms); touch has no hover, so rows simply keep moving. The Pause motion button (aria-pressed) stops all rows and the skew; this satisfies the requirement to let people stop moving content. Under prefers-reduced-motion the rows render static: each row shows its phrase once, wrapped, with no animation, and Pause is hidden. The marquee region is aria-hidden except for one visually hidden heading that reads "Type for the night shift. Loud letters, quiet grids."

Responsive: Under 700px the rows reduce to 64px type, the intro card moves below the marquee, the top bar collapses to the wordmark and Pause button; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Technique notes: transform-only animation (translate3d and skew) with will-change on tracks, no layout reads inside the frame loop. Invented names only.
