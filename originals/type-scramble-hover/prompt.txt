Build a single-page "Scramble Text Hover" index for an invented digital studio, "Glyphwork": a dark, typographic menu where every link scrambles through random glyphs and then resolves letter by letter into its real label when hovered or focused, set over a faint field of glyphs that brightens around the pointer. It should feel like a terminal that has learned manners.

Canvas & base: Near-black #07090c with a deep green-teal atmosphere painted behind everything: two huge blurred radial fields in #0f5e54 and #3b1d6e at 45% opacity, a CRT-style scanline overlay (repeating 2px line at 4% white), a soft vignette and 10% film grain. Text mint #d9ffe8 (16:1); secondary #8fb3a3 (7.4:1); accent signal green #4dffa0 for scrambling glyphs; accent magenta #ff4fa3 for the active row marker. Panels (controls) rgba(10,16,18,.78) with a 1px #1f3a33 border and 14px radius. Invented studio name only.

Typography: "JetBrains Mono" 500/700 for the glyph field, scrambling links and controls; "Unbounded" 600 for the page title and the big link labels (clamp 32px to 84px, uppercase, line-height 1); "Inter" 400/500 for small descriptions at 15px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, #4dffa0.

Imagery & art direction: The background glyph field is the artwork. Draw it on a full-bleed canvas: a grid of monospace characters (14px cells) in #4dffa0 at 6% opacity, each cell holding a random glyph; glyphs within 180px of the pointer rise to 55% opacity and swap rapidly (every 60 to 120ms) and the brightness falls off with a smooth cosine; when idle, 12 random cells per second flip glyphs and a slow diagonal band of brightness drifts across the canvas every 14 seconds. Overlay the atmosphere gradients, scanlines and vignette so the field feels like a monitor in a dark room. Next to the menu show a 280px square generative "glyph portrait": a circular emblem built from 9 concentric rings of tiny glyphs rotating at different speeds in alternate directions, with a bright core glyph that changes on link hover. Media slots for real photography: an optional 1920x1080 low-light server-room or street photograph with a 70% black overlay in place of the atmosphere gradients.

Layout: Left-aligned content in a 1160px container. Header: wordmark "Glyphwork" and the tiny line "Interfaces, identities and odd little tools". Hero title "Say it in glyphs" using the same scramble on load. Main: a two-column area; left the menu of six giant links numbered 01 to 06 (Selected work, Studio, Services, Journal, Careers, Contact), each with a thin rule, a small right-aligned description and an arrow that slides in on hover; right the glyph portrait and a controls panel with Charset (Letters, Symbols, Binary, Blocks), Speed (slow, normal, fast) and a "Scramble all" button. Footer note: "Hover, focus or press S to scramble."

Copy: Descriptions: Selected work "Twenty-four projects, one honest case study each"; Studio "Eleven people, two dogs, one very good kettle"; Services "Identity, product design and small useful software"; Journal "Notes on type, tools and craft"; Careers "Two open roles, no trick questions"; Contact "hello@example.com, replies within a day".

Interaction: Implement a scramble engine: for a target string, each character i resolves after delay = i * stagger + random(0, jitter) where stagger is 38ms at normal speed (60 slow, 22 fast) and jitter is 120ms. A timer ticks every 40ms; unresolved characters show a random glyph from the chosen charset (Letters A to Z and digits; Symbols !<>-_\/[]{}=+*^?#; Binary 0 and 1; Blocks the characters of a block-element range), resolved characters show the true letter and take the normal colour, unresolved ones show signal green. Spaces never scramble. Hovering or focusing a link starts a scramble that resolves in about 600ms; leaving resets instantly without scrambling; re-entering mid-scramble restarts cleanly. Pressing S scrambles every link in sequence, and "Scramble all" does the same. The accessible name of each link is its real label (aria-label) while the scrambling spans are aria-hidden, so assistive technology never reads random characters. The charset and speed are native radios and a select, persisted in localStorage inside try/catch. Under prefers-reduced-motion nothing scrambles: labels are plain text, the glyph field is a static single frame and the portrait does not rotate.

Responsive: Under 860px the columns stack (menu first, portrait and controls after), link labels drop to 34px, the glyph field cell grows to 18px and idle flips halve; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset and a magenta marker. Technique notes: draw the canvas at device-pixel ratio capped at 2, only redraw dirty cells where practical, throttle to 30 fps. Invented names only.
