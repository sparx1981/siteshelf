Build a single-page "Handwriting Signature" experience for an invented firm, "Lantern & Pine Studio": a letter of engagement lies on a desk and a pen writes the visitor's signature on the signature line, stroke by stroke, with a moving nib, ink that settles, and a final flourish. The visitor can type their own name, change the ink, flourish and speed, replay, and copy the result as SVG. Everything is invented and runs locally.

Canvas & base: Full-bleed painted walnut desk, seen from above: warm brown wood (#6b4528 to #3f2814) with long soft grain lines, a pool of warm lamp light from the upper left (radial gradient #ffe2a8 at 35%), a darker vignette at the corners, 12% film grain. The letter is cream paper #fbf6ea with a subtle fibre texture, tilted -1.2 degrees, a soft contact shadow and a slightly lifted right edge. Ink choices: blue-black #1b2a6b, black #111418, sepia #5a3a1e, emerald #0f5a46. Document text #2a2620 (13:1) and secondary #6b6253. Controls on a rounded panel #fffaf0 (1px #e3d7bc, 16px radius). Invented firm and people only.

Typography: "Caveat" 700 for the signature (size 112 to 128 in SVG units, so it feels handwritten); "Newsreader" 400/500/italic for the letter body at 15px/1.6; "Inter" 500/600 for UI at 14px; letter heading in "Newsreader" 600 small caps. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, #8a4b1f.

Imagery & art direction: The desk is the picture. Paint it in inline SVG: wood planks with grain drawn as 40 long slightly curved translucent lines in two browns, a feTurbulence texture at 6%, a warm lamp-light radial gradient, a brass fountain pen lying at the upper right (a long rounded body with a darker cap, a gold clip, a tapered gold nib with a slit, soft highlights and a long shadow), a coffee cup ring stain (two overlapping thin brown circles at 12% opacity), a paper clip and a small wax-seal stamp in vermilion. The letter itself is a card with a printed header (a small hand-drawn lantern-and-pine logo mark in two colours), a title "Letter of Engagement", a date, recipient block, three short paragraphs of invented text and a signature block with a line, a small "X" marker and the typed name set beneath in Inter. The signature layer is a separate SVG sitting on the line with a mix-blend-mode multiply so the ink picks up the paper texture. Add an ink-settle effect: while a stroke is fresh its opacity is 0.82 with a faint wider blur at 20%, then settles to 1.0 and a crisp edge once the stroke is done. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 overhead desk photograph in place of the painted desk, and a 600x300 scanned paper texture for the letter.

Layout: Page split on desktop: the letter (max 720px) fills the left two thirds, centred on the desk; the control panel floats at the right (300px). Letter content top to bottom: logo and "Lantern & Pine Studio", "Letter of Engagement", date "12 October", "To: Alder & Finch Ltd", three paragraphs, "Signed," and the signature line (full width, 1.5px ink at 40%) with "X" at its left and, below it, the typed name and "Authorised signatory". Panel: eyebrow "Signing desk", a Name field (max 18 characters, default "Mira Solt"), a Sign or Replay button, Speed slider (0.5x to 2x), Ink swatches (four radio buttons as colour chips), Flourish select (Swoosh, Loop, Double rule, None) and a "Copy SVG" button. After signing, a green "Signed" stamp with the time appears at the signature block's right.

Copy: Paragraph text about a three-month brand-and-website engagement with scope, timing and a fee line using "GBP 18,500" and example.com contact details. Stamp: "Signed 12 Oct, 10:42". Status line: "Signature written."

Interaction: Render the name as SVG <text> in Caveat, rotated -4 degrees, and reveal it with a mask whose white path is a thick brush (stroke width about the font size, round caps) following a gentle sine wave from the first to the last letter; animate its stroke-dashoffset from the full length to 0 with an ease-in-out curve over 2.4 seconds divided by the Speed setting. A small nib (an SVG group) follows the brush path using getPointAtLength, rotating with the path tangent and lifting slightly at each wave crest. When the name finishes the chosen flourish (a hand-built stroked path: swoosh, loop, or double rule) is drawn with stroke-dasharray over 0.9 seconds, then the "Signed" stamp fades in. Use an feTurbulence plus feDisplacementMap filter (scale 1.4) to roughen edges so the ink does not look vector-perfect. Changing the name rebuilds the brush path from getComputedTextLength() and re-signs automatically after 400 ms of no typing. Sign or Replay restarts from empty. Ink choice recolours instantly. Copy SVG serialises a standalone SVG of the signature (text, flourish and the font-family note) to the clipboard inside try/catch and announces "SVG copied". The signature SVG has role="img" and aria-label "Signature of Mira Solt"; completion is announced politely once. Technique notes: drive the animation with one requestAnimationFrame loop and a single progress value so name, nib and flourish can never drift out of sync; compute dash lengths with getTotalLength after fonts are ready.

Responsive: Under 900px the panel moves below the letter, the letter fills the width with 18px padding, the signature scales with the SVG viewBox; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1b2a6b outline with white offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (the finished signature appears instantly with no nib and no animation; Replay shows a 300ms fade). Invented names only.
