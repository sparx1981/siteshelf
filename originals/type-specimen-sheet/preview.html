Typography is the craft of endowing human language with a durable visual form. A good typeface does its work quietly: it carries the writer's voice to the reader without asking to be noticed. Look closely at the curve of a lowercase a, the bite where a stem meets a bowl, the way an italic leans without losing its balance. These small decisions repeat across thousands of words, and together they set the temperature of a page. Set a paragraph at the size you would actually use, read it for a minute, and ask whether you would still be comfortable at the end of a chapter.
