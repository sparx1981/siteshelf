Build a single-page "Variable Weight Wave" typography piece for an invented type studio, "Inkwell & Ox": one huge sentence set in a variable serif, where the weight of every letter swells and shrinks in a smooth travelling wave that follows the pointer, a finger, a slider, or an idle drift. It is a design piece first and a tool second.

Canvas & base: A deep ink-violet poster background (#10142e) with large soft colour fields painted behind the text, as if risograph inks had been printed with slight misregistration: a vermilion #ff5a3c field top-left, an amber #ffb347 field lower right, a cobalt #2f56d4 field behind the centre and a violet #7a3fa0 field lower left, each a big blurred radial shape at 55 to 75% opacity, overprinted with a paper-grain layer at 14% (feTurbulence) and a fine halftone dot pattern at 6% across the bottom third. Text cream #fff3df (14:1), secondary #c9c2e8 (8:1). Controls sit on a glass strip rgba(16,20,46,.7) with a 1px #3a4286 border and 18px radius. Invented studio name only.

Typography: "Fraunces" via Google Fonts as a variable font (Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,100..900) in roman for the sentence at clamp(40px, 8.6vw, 128px), line-height 1, letter-spacing -0.01em, centred; "Space Grotesk" 400/500/700 for UI at 13 to 15px; "DM Mono" 500 for readouts. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, #ffb347.

Imagery & art direction: The poster feel is the point. Build the background as inline SVG with four large blurred radial gradients (feGaussianBlur 60), a misregistered duplicate of the cobalt field offset 6px in a slightly different hue, an SVG noise layer and a dotted halftone ramp (circles whose radius grows toward the bottom edge) so it reads as a printed sheet rather than a screen gradient. Beneath the sentence draw a live "weight meter": a smooth filled curve across the full sentence width showing each letter's current weight (100 to 900) in cream with a 2px line and 20% fill, with tiny tick labels 100, 500, 900 at the left. Under that, a specimen ladder of nine "Aa" samples at weights 100 to 900 in a row; the sample closest to the average live weight is underlined in amber. Add registration marks (small circled crosses) in two corners and a tiny caption "Fraunces, variable, weight 100 to 900" in DM Mono. Media slots for real photography: an optional 1920x1080 paper-texture or ink-bleed photograph for the backdrop with a 40% violet overlay.

Layout: Eyebrow "Inkwell & Ox · Specimen 07", then the sentence "Weight is just another way to move." with each word an inline-block and each letter its own span (aria-hidden) while the h1 carries the full text in a visually hidden span. Under it the weight meter, then the ladder, then a glass control strip with: Wave width (1 to 8 letters), Maximum weight (500 to 900), Resting weight (100 to 500), Speed (0.3 to 2.0), Optical size (9 to 144), a Wave position slider (0 to 100%, keyboard route to scrub the wave by hand), an Auto ripple switch and a Reset button.

Copy: Lead line under the eyebrow: "Move your pointer through the sentence." Hint line: "Or let it drift. Or use the Wave position slider."

Interaction: Each letter's target weight is resting + (max - resting) * exp(-(d / sigma)^2) where d is the horizontal distance from the letter centre to the wave centre and sigma is the Wave width in letter-widths. Every frame (requestAnimationFrame) each letter eases toward its target with a critically-damped factor of 0.16 (current += (target - current) * 0.16), and the value is written as font-variation-settings "wght" and "opsz" on that letter (and font-weight for fallback). The wave centre follows pointer movement over the sentence block (pointermove, also touch with touch-action: pan-y), otherwise drifts left to right on a loop (centre = (t * speed * 180) mod (width + 4 sigma) - 2 sigma) when Auto ripple is on. After the pointer leaves, auto drift resumes after 1.5 seconds. The Wave position slider overrides the centre and switches Auto off while it is being used. The weight meter, ladder underline and a polite status sentence ("Peak weight 900 at the letter w") update from the same data; the status is announced only when sliders change (debounced 600ms), never during animation. Cache letter centres on resize and when document.fonts.ready resolves. Technique notes: letters keep their layout slots stable by measuring each letter's width at weight 500 and setting it as a minimum inline width, so neighbours do not jitter while weights change.

Responsive: Under 700px the sentence wraps at word boundaries across 3 lines, sliders stack in a two-column grid, the ladder scrolls inside its own row; no horizontal page scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no idle drift, no easing: weights jump to targets only while the pointer or slider is actively used). Invented names only.
