The quiet craft of reading slowly
Why the best typography is the kind you never notice, and how to choose it anyway.


A typeface is a voice. Set the same sentence in two faces and a reader will tell you, without hesitation, which one sounds more trustworthy, more playful, more expensive. The words never changed; the air around them did.


Pair for contrast, not for conflict.


The safest pairings give each face a job. Let the display face announce, and let the text face carry the load for hundreds of words without tiring the eye. When both try to be interesting, neither is.
