Reading is a design decision

Type scales give a page rhythm. Every size is a multiple of the last, so headings feel related to body text rather than arbitrary.

Choose a ratio, then trust it

A small ratio feels calm and editorial. A large one feels loud and poster-like. Most interfaces live between 1.2 and 1.333.
