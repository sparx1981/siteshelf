Build a single-page "Agenda Strip" widget for an invented calendar app, "Lantern Calendar": a horizontal timeline of one day with calendar lanes, event blocks, a live "now" line and smooth drag-to-scroll. All data is generated locally; names, places and events are invented.

Canvas & base: Page background #f4efe6 (warm paper). The widget is one card (max 1120px, 24px radius, 1px #e2d9c8 border, shadow 0 24px 60px rgba(60,40,10,.16)) with a painted header band on top and the strip below on #fffdf8. Text #1f1a14 (16:1), secondary #6b6253 (5.8:1). Calendar colours with AA text pairings: Work cobalt #2f56d4 (white text), Personal terracotta #c4532f (white text), Travel pine #1f7a58 (white text); the now line coral #e8453c; grid lines #eadfcb. Invented brand and people only.

Typography: "Young Serif" 400 for the date heading (clamp 30 to 44px); "Figtree" 400/500/700 for UI, event titles (15px 700), times (12.5px 500, tabular numerals) and lane labels. Eyebrow 12px, 0.16em tracking, uppercase, #c4532f.

Imagery & art direction: The header band (180px tall, full card width) is a painted early-morning landscape: peach and pale-violet sky (#2a3a78 to #f2a6a0 to #fff3d6), a low sun, two soft ridges in dusty blue, a mist band, 12% grain and a cream gradient fading into the card at its bottom edge so the date heading sits on a calm area. Build it as inline SVG. Events with a place get a 56x56 painted thumbnail (a tiny inline-SVG landscape or interior generated from a seed: harbour, studio, station, restaurant) at the left of the block when the block is wide enough. A block's top edge carries a 3px calendar-colour bar; blocks have 10px radius and a soft shadow. The now line is 2px coral with a pill label on top and a faint 24px glow. Media slots for real photography: a 1600x360 morning-light header photograph, and 112x112 location thumbnails per event.

Layout: Header band content: eyebrow "Lantern Calendar", heading "Thursday 15 October", a status sentence ("5 events today. Next: Design review in 24 min."), and on the right Previous day, Next day, Today, zoom out (-) and zoom in (+) buttons plus calendar filter chips (Work, Personal, Travel) with checked state. Strip: a sticky left lane-label column (96px) and a scrolling timeline from 07:00 to 22:00. A sticky top ruler shows hours (every hour a label, half-hour ticks). Three lanes, each as tall as needed: overlapping events in a lane stack into sub-rows so nothing overlaps visually. Default scale 120px per hour (zoom 70 to 240 in 10px steps). Beneath the strip, a one-line legend and a hint "Drag to scroll, arrow keys to move between events."

Copy: Example events (Thursday): Work: "Stand-up" 09:00-09:15, "Design review" 11:00-12:00 (Studio 3), "Roadmap sync" 11:30-12:30 (overlaps, shows in a second row), "Focus block" 14:00-16:00; Personal: "Lunch at the harbour" 12:30-13:30 (Harbour Cafe), "Pottery class" 18:30-20:00 (Kiln Room); Travel: "Train to Lantern City" 16:30-18:10 (Pennick Central). Other days generate 3 to 6 events from seeded templates so Previous and Next day show different content.

Interaction: The now line uses the real time if it falls between 08:00 and 20:00; outside those hours it shows a labelled demo time of 10:40 so the widget always looks alive; it advances every 30 seconds. "Today" scrolls the strip so now sits at 35% of the viewport width. Pointer drag on the timeline scrolls horizontally with setPointerCapture; on release it continues with inertia: velocity v (px per ms) is sampled over the last 80 ms and decays exponentially, v *= exp(-dt / 325), stopping under 0.02 px/ms; under reduced motion the inertia is skipped. Zoom changes the hour width through a damped spring (stiffness 170, damping 26, mass 1, integrated per frame with semi-implicit Euler) while keeping the time at the viewport centre fixed. Each event is a button: Enter or click opens a native dialog with title, time, place, thumbnail, notes and "Close"; focus returns to the block. Left and Right arrow keys on a block move focus to the previous or next event in time order (roving tabindex, one tab stop for the strip); the strip itself scrolls the focused block into view. Filter chips hide and show lanes with a 200ms height transition. Announce the next-event sentence politely when it changes.

Responsive: Under 760px the lane labels collapse to 28px colour tabs with a rotated label, the default scale is 90px per hour, header buttons wrap, and the dialog becomes a bottom sheet; no horizontal page scroll at 390px (only the strip scrolls).

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #1f1a14 outline with white offset. Calendar is never conveyed by colour alone (lane label plus a small calendar word in each block's second line). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no inertia, instant zoom, no glow pulse). Invented names only.
