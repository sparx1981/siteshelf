Build a single-page "Battery Health Ring" widget for an invented phone called the "Nimbus N2": a dashboard that shows the current charge, the battery's long-term health, a charge history you can scrub, and a list of tips you can tick off. Charging and draining are simulated in the page; nothing is real.

Canvas & base: The page background is a deep teal-green painted gradient, built as soft overlapping radial blobs in #2fe0a0, #1b8a6a, #0e4a52 and #7affd0 on #071a1c, blurred and darkened with a #04100f overlay at 55% so it reads as a calm bokeh-like wash. A glass card (rgba(9,32,34,.78), 1px #1f5a5c border, 28px radius, backdrop blur 16px) holds the widget in a two-column layout. Text #eafff6 (15:1), secondary #9cc9bd (7.5:1). Charge colour shifts by level: above 40% mint #4df0b0, 20 to 40% amber #ffc857, below 20% coral #ff7a6b; health ring cyan #5fd8ff; charging accent electric lime #c8ff5a. Invented brand "Nimbus" only.

Typography: "Outfit" 600/700 for the big percentage (clamp 56 to 96px, tabular numerals) and headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 16px; "JetBrains Mono" 500 for small values and axis labels. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, mint.

Imagery & art direction: Make the left column feel like a product shot: a rendered Nimbus N2 phone in inline SVG, standing slightly rotated (-4deg) on a soft surface with a long blurred contact shadow and a faint reflection. The phone has a deep graphite frame with a 1px highlight edge, a gently curved glass reflection streak across the screen, a punch-hole camera and a thin side button; its screen shows a miniature dark home screen (clock, two widget tiles, a row of app dots) and a charging glow that rises from the bottom edge when plugged in. Around the phone sit the two concentric rings (outer = charge, inner = health) at 340px diameter with round caps, the outer ring animated with a soft gradient sweep while charging. A tiny lightning-bolt glyph sits at the ring's top when plugged in. Add 10 to 14 drifting light motes behind the phone when charging. Media slots for real photography: a 1200x1600 phone-on-desk product photograph with transparent or dark background to replace the SVG phone, and a 1920x1080 ambient background.

Layout: Left column: eyebrow "Nimbus N2 · Battery", the phone with rings, the big percentage inside the outer ring centre-bottom of the phone group, status line ("Charging · 38 min to full" or "On battery · 7 h 10 min left"), and a switch group: Plugged in, Low Power Mode, Optimised charging (caps at 80% overnight). Right column: a stat row of four tiles (Maximum capacity 91%, Charge cycles 312, Temperature 31 C, Health "Good"), a history card with range tabs (24 hours, 7 days) and a sparkline area chart with a draggable cursor and readout ("Tue 14:00 · 63% · charging"), then the "Tips to extend battery life" list: five items with checkboxes and an estimated gain ("+22 min per day"), and a total "Estimated gain: +48 min per day" that sums ticked tips.

Copy: Tips: "Turn on Low Power Mode below 20%" (+22 min), "Lower screen brightness to Auto" (+18 min), "Switch off background refresh for 3 apps" (+14 min), "Use Optimised charging overnight" (health, +0.4% per month), "Keep the phone out of direct sun" (health). Health text: "Good - 91% of original capacity".

Interaction: Charge is simulated at one tick per second: plugged in adds 0.4% per tick (0.15% when above 80% with Optimised charging on, and it holds at 80% overnight), unplugged removes 0.08% per tick (0.05% in Low Power Mode). Time estimates scale from the rate (for example 100% / 0.4 per tick maps to 85 real minutes). The ring, number, colour, phone screen glow and status line update together; at 100% the status reads "Fully charged". The history chart shows seeded sawtooth data with the live value appended. The cursor is a role="slider" (arrow keys move 1 point, Home/End jump) and dragging with pointer events updates the readout. Ticking tips adds to the estimated gain total with a short count animation. Switch states persist in localStorage inside try/catch. Announce meaningful state changes politely (charging started, low battery, fully charged), never every tick.

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack, the ring group shrinks to min(340px, 84vw), tiles go two columns, the chart keeps a 2.2:1 ratio; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Level is never colour-only (number, status words, and a low-battery icon below 20%). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no drifting motes or gradient sweep; values still update). Invented names only.
