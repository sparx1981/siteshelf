Build a single-page "Currency Converter Ticker" widget for an invented service, "Mintly Rates": a converter with a live-looking ticker, a swap button, a 30-day sparkline and favourite pairs. All rates are generated sample numbers that drift every few seconds; nothing is real and no network is used.

Canvas & base: A deep indigo-to-teal painted backdrop (#0b1030 base with soft blurred blobs in #3b5bdb, #2a9d8f and #7a3fa0, darkened by a #070b22 overlay at 50%), a ticker bar across the very top (#0a0f2e, 1px #232c63 bottom border) and one glass card (rgba(14,20,56,.82), 1px #2e3a80 border, 26px radius, backdrop blur 14px) centred below. Text #eef1ff (15:1), secondary #a9b3e0 (8:1). Up movement mint #4df0b0, down movement coral #ff7a6b, accent gold #ffd36b. Brand "Mintly Rates" is invented.

Typography: "Sora" 600 for the result figure (clamp 36 to 64px, tabular numerals) and headings; "Inter" 400/500/600 for UI at 14 to 16px; "IBM Plex Mono" 500 for rates, deltas and the ticker. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, gold.

Imagery & art direction: The card's visual centre is a pair of painted banknotes drawn in inline SVG, one for the source and one for the target currency, fanned slightly (-6 and +5 degrees) with soft shadows. Each note is 300x150 with rounded 10px corners, a base colour from its currency (USD pine #2e7d5b, EUR cobalt #3b5bdb, GBP violet #7a3fa0, JPY vermilion #c4452f, AUD amber #d89a1e, CAD crimson #c0392b, CHF red #d0342c, INR saffron #c8702a, BRL teal #2a9d8f, ZAR green #3a8a3a), a guilloche pattern built from 12 overlapping sine-wave paths and concentric rings at 25% white, a large currency symbol in a ring on the left, the code and a made-up denomination on the right, a fine inner border at 6px inset, a diagonal sheen gradient, an invented serial number in small mono caps and a tiny "SAMPLE" watermark so it is clearly not real currency. No real portraits, logos or national symbols. On swap, the notes exchange places with a 450ms arc and fade-through. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 abstract finance or city-night photograph for the backdrop, and optional currency artwork 600x300 per currency.

Layout: Ticker bar: a continuously scrolling row of 10 pairs (USD/EUR 0.9214 +0.12%, GBP/JPY, EUR/CHF and so on) duplicated for a seamless loop, each with a coloured arrow and delta; the bar pauses on hover and on focus within it, and has a visible Pause button. Card: eyebrow "Mintly Rates", heading "Convert money", then a row: Amount input (large, with currency symbol prefix), From select, a round Swap button, To select. Quick amount chips (10, 100, 1,000, 5,000). The banknote pair sits beneath, then the result block: "1,250.00 USD = 1,151.75 EUR" with the figure large, the rate line "1 USD = 0.9214 EUR · 1 EUR = 1.0853 USD" and "Updated 4 s ago". Below, a sparkline card for the pair over 30 days with high, low and change, and a "Favourite pairs" row of chips (star toggle button for the current pair, chips load a pair when pressed, with a remove x).

Copy: Footnote "Sample rates for demonstration, not for trading." Empty and error states: an empty amount shows "Enter an amount"; letters are ignored; negative numbers show "Amounts must be positive"; amounts above 1,000,000,000 are capped with "Large amount: showing capped value".

Interaction: Ten currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, INR, BRL, ZAR) each hold a sample value per USD and a seeded 30-day history that random-walks within about 3%. Every 5 seconds each rate drifts by up to 0.06%; the result figure tweens to its new value over 400ms (ease-out), a changed rate flashes its delta mint or coral for 600ms, and the "updated" counter resets. Changing amount, From or To recalculates instantly; Swap exchanges From and To, rotates its icon 180 degrees and animates the notes. Numbers are formatted with Intl.NumberFormat (JPY with no decimals). The star toggles the current pair into a favourites list (max 6), persisted in localStorage inside try/catch; chips are buttons with aria-labels such as "Load GBP to JPY"; remove buttons are real buttons. The sparkline path draws in over 600ms when the pair changes and its hover and keyboard cursor (role="slider", arrow keys) reads out day and rate. Copy result copies "1,250.00 USD = 1,151.75 EUR" to the clipboard inside try/catch and announces it. The result region is aria-live polite but debounced to 800ms after typing stops.

Responsive: Under 760px the input row stacks (amount, then From, Swap, To in a 3-column row), notes shrink to 240px wide, the ticker font drops to 13px, the sparkline keeps a 2.2:1 ratio; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Direction is never colour-only (arrows and signs). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (the ticker is static and scrollable by hand, no note arcs, no draw-in, tweens are instant). Invented brand only; all rates are samples.
