Build a single-page "Departure Flip Board" for an invented station, "Pennick Central": a mechanical split-flap departures board whose characters physically flip through the alphabet to reach new destinations, times and statuses. Everything is simulated; destinations, services and carriers are invented.

Canvas & base: Full-bleed painted dusk behind the board, as if seen through the station's tall windows: a violet-to-amber sky (#1b1740, #4b2a78, #c2477a, #ff9a62) with a skyline of layered building silhouettes whose windows glow in warm dots, then a polished concourse floor along the bottom that reflects the board as a soft, blurred band of amber. A darkening overlay at 35% keeps the board dominant. The board is a deep-black enclosure #14110f with a brass frame (a gradient from #d8b56a to #8a6a30 with an inner 2px #3a2a10 shadow line), four hex-head screws in the corners, and a header strip. Tiles are #1d1a17 with a 1px #0a0908 hairline through the middle, characters cream #f4ecd8 (14:1). Status colours on the status cells only: On time cream, Boarding amber #ffc247, Delayed coral #ff7a6b, Last call white on #c23b30 background, Departed grey #8a8378, Cancelled coral with strikethrough hairline. Invented carriers: KA, PN, LX, HR.

Typography: "Oswald" 500 for tile characters (the cell is 0.78 em wide by 1.4 em tall, so letters feel condensed and tall), "Space Mono" 700 for the header clock and small captions, "DM Sans" 500/700 for controls at 14 to 16px. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, brass #d8b56a.

Imagery & art direction: Paint the scene like a golden-hour photograph through glass: a dusk sky with a low sun glow behind the skyline, three layers of towers with tiny lit windows, a faint window mullion grid over the whole scene (thin 1.5px dark lines every 360px) and soft lens bloom around the sun, 12% film grain, vignette. Build with inline SVG using seeded random rectangles for buildings and windows. The brass frame must feel tactile: top-lit gradient, a light 1px highlight on the top edge, a darker bevel at the bottom, and a drop shadow below. The flaps themselves should look real: each tile has a top half and bottom half with a subtle vertical gradient (lighter at the hinge), a rounded 3px corner, and a thin dark gap at the centre line; during a flip the moving flap darkens slightly. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 station-at-dusk photograph in place of the painted scene, and an optional looping clip of a rainy platform.

Layout: Max width 1020px. Header strip inside the brass frame: a small pictogram (train front, inline SVG), the title "DEPARTURES" in Oswald caps, "Pennick Central" and a live clock HH:MM:SS in Space Mono on the right. Column captions row: Time, Destination, Service, Gate, Status. Then 9 board rows, each made of flap tiles in groups: Time 5 characters (HH:MM), Destination 14, Service 6 (e.g. KA 204), Gate 3 (e.g. B12), Status 10. Below the frame: a control row with Pause updates (or Resume), Shuffle now, a Sound switch (soft clack, off by default) and an Announce updates switch (off by default), plus a small progress line "Next update in 8 s".

Copy: Destinations from an invented pool: LANTERN CITY, HARBORD REACH, PENNICK BAY, NORTHHAVEN, EQUA LANDING, PORT AUSTRELLE, SALT MARSH, VELLA ISLAND, COPPER RIDGE, OAKENFORD, KESTREL HILL, ASHWICK, TIDEMOUTH, BRAMBLEDOWN, STONEGATE. Statuses: ON TIME, BOARDING, DELAYED, LAST CALL, DEPARTED, CANCELLED. Times are the next departures in 7 to 13 minute steps starting from the current time.

Interaction: Each tile is a split-flap built from four layers (top static, bottom static, flipping top flap, flipping bottom flap). To reach a target character a tile steps through the sequence " ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ0123456789:" one position at a time, each step a 140 ms two-phase rotateX flip (old top falls 0 to -90 degrees, then new bottom falls 90 to 0), using the Web Animations API; very long runs skip ahead so no tile flips more than 14 times. Tiles start with a stagger (column x 30 ms + row x 70 ms) so changes ripple across the board. Every 8 seconds the board either (a) rotates: the top row departs, the rest move up and a new destination flips into the last row, or (b) updates one or two statuses (for example Boarding to Last call to Departed, with colour changes). Pause stops the cycle, Shuffle now forces an immediate rotation. The progress line counts down. The sound switch plays one very short filtered-noise click per changing row using Web Audio inside try/catch (no audio files), at low gain. The whole board is one role="table" with rows and cells carrying aria-labels that always show the final text (the tile layers are aria-hidden); with Announce updates on, a polite live region says "Board updated. Next departure 14:30 to Lantern City, gate B12, boarding." Shuffle now always announces the next departure once.

Responsive: Under 760px each row becomes two lines (Time + Status on the first, Destination + Gate on the second, Service hidden), tile width is computed from the viewport so everything fits; no horizontal scroll at 390px; 44px minimum height for controls.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries, audio files or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. Status is always conveyed in words, never colour alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (tiles swap instantly with no flip and no stagger; the countdown still runs). Invented names only.
