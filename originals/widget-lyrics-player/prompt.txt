Build a single-page "Lyrics Now Playing" widget for an invented song, "Paper Lanterns" by the invented artist Maren Vale from the album "Low Tide Letters": a now-playing card with painted cover art and a lyrics panel that follows the song karaoke-style. Playback is simulated with a timer (no audio files), all lyrics are original text written for the demo.

Canvas & base: The page background is the album cover itself, scaled to 140% and blurred (60px) with saturation 1.4, darkened by a #0b0d22 overlay at 62% so the colours of the cover set the mood: coral #ff8a5c, amber #ffcf6b, cobalt #5b8cff, mint #2fe0c0 on deep navy #14183a. A faint film-grain layer at 10% sits above it. The card is two columns inside a 1040px container: a left player column (360px) and a right lyrics panel on a glass surface rgba(10,12,36,.5) with a 1px rgba(255,255,255,.12) border, 28px radius and backdrop blur 20px. Text #ffffff (lines on the blurred background are at least 7:1 after the overlay), inactive lyric lines rgba(255,255,255,.45), accent warm-white #fff3d6.

Typography: "Fraunces" 600 for the song title (32px) and the active lyric line (clamp 28 to 40px); "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for artist, controls, time labels and inactive lines (22 to 30px for lyrics, 14px for UI); tabular numerals for times. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase.

Imagery & art direction: The cover must look like designed album art: build a 600x600 painted square as inline SVG with six overlapping soft radial gradients in the coral, amber, cobalt and mint palette over the navy base, 20% film grain, a vignette, and a hand-drawn paper lantern (a rounded trapezoid with a bright amber glow ellipse inside, thin rib lines, a small string and tassel, and a halo of warm light with a few drifting spark dots) floating low over a dark, glassy water band with a reflected glow. Title lettering "Low Tide Letters" sits bottom-left in small cream caps. The cover has a subtle 2deg tilt shadow and gently pulses its glow (6 seconds) while playing. The same cover SVG, blurred, becomes the page backdrop. Media slots for real material: a 1200x1200 album cover image, and a 1920x1080 blurred or looping cover video background.

Layout: Left column: eyebrow "Now playing", the cover (square, 100% width), title "Paper Lanterns", artist "Maren Vale", a seek slider with elapsed and remaining times, a transport row (Previous line, Play or Pause as a large 64px round button, Next line), and a row of toggles: Like (heart), Karaoke fill (on or off), Text size (A- and A+), and a "Sync" button that appears only when auto-scroll has been interrupted. Right column: the lyrics panel as a scrollable list; each line is a button. Section tags such as "Verse 1", "Chorus" appear as small caps labels. Instrumental gaps longer than 8 seconds show a three-dot pulsing marker that shrinks as the gap ends.

Copy: Original lyric lines such as "The harbour lights are folding into mist", "I carry paper lanterns in my coat", "Each one a name I never learned to say", "So let them drift, let them drift, let them drift", "Past the breakwater, past the bell", "If the tide remembers where we started", "It will tell the sky, it will tell" and at least 14 more across two verses, a chorus repeat and an outro, with timestamps over a 2:48 track.

Interaction: A requestAnimationFrame clock advances the position while playing. The active line is the last line whose start time has passed; it enlarges (scale 1 to 1.12 over 300ms), turns white, and its words fill left to right in warm white as the time passes each word's share of the line (word duration proportional to character count). Lines before it stay at 70% and lines after at 45% with a small blur increasing with distance (reduced motion: no blur). The list auto-scrolls to keep the active line vertically centred (smooth, or instant under reduced motion); if the user scrolls manually it pauses auto-scroll and shows Sync, which resumes it. Clicking or pressing Enter on a line seeks to its start. Space toggles play when no button has focus; Left and Right seek 5 seconds, Up and Down jump to the previous or next line, L toggles like. The like state and text size persist in localStorage inside try/catch. At the end of the track playback stops and the play button returns to the start. The active line is marked aria-current="true" and announced only when the user turns on a "Announce lines" switch (off by default) to avoid noise; the lyric list has aria-label "Lyrics".

Responsive: Under 900px the columns stack with the cover at 240px centred, the lyrics panel is 60vh tall with its own scrolling, and text sizes drop by 4px; no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries, audio files or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with a dark offset. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no glow pulse, no scale, no blur, no smooth scroll). Invented names and original lyrics only.
