Build a single-page "Moon Phase Widget" for an invented almanac called "Lantern Almanac": a calm, photographic night scene with one large, convincingly lit moon that shows the phase for any date the visitor picks. Everything is computed locally from the date; no APIs.

Canvas & base: A full-bleed night landscape fills the page behind the widget: sky graded from #05081a at the top through #0d1840 and #1d3170 to a faint #3a4f9a glow at the horizon, a dense field of stars (about 160, varying radius 0.5 to 1.9px, opacity 0.3 to 1), three layered pine ridges in #1b2a5a, #111c42 and #0a122e with thin blue mist between them, and a near-black foreground band. Over it sits a glass panel #0c1332 at 82% (1px #27336b border, 24px radius, backdrop blur 12px). Text #eef1ff (15:1), secondary #a3afdb (8:1), accent moon-gold #ffe9a8, accent cool #8fb4ff. Invented brand only; no real observatory or agency names.

Typography: "Fraunces" 500 (opsz soft, italic for the phase name) at clamp(40px, 7vw, 72px) for the phase name; "Manrope" 400/500/700 for UI, facts and calendar at 14 to 16px; tabular numerals for percentages, ages and dates. Eyebrow 12px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, #8fb4ff.

Imagery & art direction: The moon is the hero and must read as a photograph of the real moon, not a flat disc. Build it as inline SVG: a base disc with a radial gradient from #f6f1e2 (centre, slightly off to the upper left) to #cfc6b0 at the limb, then painted lunar maria as 8 to 10 blurred dark ellipses (#7e7a6c at 45% opacity, feGaussianBlur 3 to 6px) placed roughly where Mare Imbrium, Serenitatis, Tranquillitatis and Oceanus Procellarum sit, about 14 small crater rings (soft dark circle with a lighter rim arc) and two bright ray systems radiating from a crater lower left. The lit shape is a clip-path built from a limb semicircle plus an elliptical terminator arc (rx = r|cos 2π·phase|), so crescent, quarter and gibbous shapes are exact; the unlit part shows the same texture at 10% opacity as faint earthshine on a #0d1430 disc. A soft outer halo (radial gradient #ffe9a8 at 35% to transparent, 1.6x radius) breathes opacity .8 to 1 over 8 seconds, and a very light bloom sits behind the lit limb. The background is the painted night landscape described above with 15% film grain and a soft vignette, so the moon looks like it hangs over a real horizon. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 moonlit-landscape photo to replace the painted backdrop (with a 35% indigo overlay), and a 1200px square high-resolution moon photograph with transparent corners that can replace the generated disc while keeping the same clip-path for the phase shape.

Layout: Two columns above 900px inside a 1080px container. Left, the stage panel: eyebrow "Lantern Almanac", the big moon (320px, 80vw max), beneath it the phase name in Fraunces italic (New Moon, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full Moon, Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter, Waning Crescent), then a line "73% lit · 11.2 days old", then a control row: Previous day, a native date input, Next day, Today, plus a hemisphere switch (Northern / Southern view; Southern rotates the whole moon 180 degrees as seen from the south). Right, a facts grid of four cards (Illumination %, Moon age in days, Next full moon with date and "in 4 days", Next new moon), then a month calendar (7 columns, Monday first, month name with previous and next month buttons) where every day cell shows a 34px mini moon with the date number, today ringed in gold and the selected day filled, then "Upcoming phases": the next four principal phases (new, first quarter, full, last quarter) with weekday, date and a tiny moon icon.

Copy: Lead under the headline "Look up, then look it up." A footnote "Phases use the mean synodic month of 29.53 days, so times can be off by up to a day." Empty and edge states: invalid or cleared date falls back to today with a polite announcement.

Interaction: Choosing any date (input, arrows, calendar click, Today) recomputes everything: age = ((days since the 6 January 2000 new moon) mod 29.530588853), phase fraction = age / 29.53, illumination = (1 - cos 2π·fraction) / 2. The moon redraws with a 400ms opacity crossfade between two stacked SVGs; the phase name and numbers update with a short count animation (skipped for reduced motion). The calendar is one tab stop with roving tabindex and arrow-key movement (left/right one day, up/down one week, Page Up/Down one month, Home/End start or end of week); Enter or Space selects. Date changes are announced politely ("Waxing gibbous, 73 percent lit, on 14 October"). The hemisphere choice persists in localStorage inside try/catch. Clicking an upcoming-phase row jumps to that date.

Responsive: Under 900px the panels stack, the moon shrinks to min(320px, 78vw), the calendar cells stay at least 40px tall for touch, and there is no horizontal scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffe9a8 outline with a dark offset ring. Phase is always stated in words, never by the picture alone. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no halo breathing, no crossfade, no counting). Invented names and example data only.
