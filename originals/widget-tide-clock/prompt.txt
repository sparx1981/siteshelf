Build a single-page "Tide Clock" widget for an invented harbour town, "Pennick Bay": a live-feeling tide table that shows the current water height, whether the tide is rising or falling, when the next high and low arrive, and a draggable 36-hour curve. All values are computed from a small harmonic model in the page; no API.

Canvas & base: Full-bleed painted dawn seascape behind the widget: sky from #2a3a78 through #7a7fc4 and #f2a6a0 to #fff3d6 at the horizon, a low sun, a glassy sea below grading from #f2a6a0 into #2d5a62 and #173a42 with fine horizontal glints and a distant headland in two blue-grey ridges. Over it, one glass panel #0a2230 at 84% (1px #23506a border, 24px radius, backdrop blur 12px, shadow 0 30px 80px rgba(0,0,0,.45)). Text #e8f6f8 (14:1), secondary #9cc3cf (7:1). Rising colour aqua #5fd6e6, falling colour coral #ff9d7a, high-water gold #ffe29a. Invented place and harbour names only.

Typography: "Sora" 600 for the big height readout (clamp 56 to 88px, tabular numerals) and headings; "DM Sans" 400/500/700 for all UI at 14 to 16px; "DM Mono" 500 for times and table values. Eyebrow 12px, 0.18em tracking, uppercase, aqua.

Imagery & art direction: Make the backdrop feel like a photograph taken from the harbour wall at first light: a calm sea with long horizontal reflections of the sun, a soft haze band at the horizon, a faint headland silhouette with a lighthouse dot, 14% film grain and a gentle vignette. Build it as inline SVG (gradients, rect glints, ridge paths, a feTurbulence grain layer). The tide gauge is the signature illustration: a vertical painted tide pole 0 to 5 m with chunky tick marks every 0.5 m and numerals, a translucent water column whose top edge is a gently animated wave path (two sine layers, 6 second loop), and a small buoy that rides the surface and bobs 2px. Beneath the waterline the column carries a seabed of speckled sand and two pebbles. Media slots for real photography: a 1920x1080 harbour-at-dawn photo for the backdrop with a 40% navy overlay, and an optional 6-second looping clip of lapping water for the gauge column.

Layout: One panel, max width 1020px. Top row: eyebrow "Pennick Bay · Harbour wall", title "Tide Clock", a status chip (Rising or Falling with an arrow) and the local time. Main row (two columns above 820px): left the tide pole gauge (220px wide, 340px tall) with the big height readout beside it ("3.42 m", "Rising 0.8 m per hour"), right the 36-hour curve chart: smooth area under a line, hourly gridlines, labelled high and low markers ("High 4.52 m 14:27"), a gold "now" line and a draggable handle. Under the chart: a Time slider (-6 h to +30 h around now, 5-minute steps) with buttons Now, Next high, Next low. Below: three guidance cards - "Causeway walk" (safe under 1.8 m, with a clear Safe or Not safe label), "Slipway launch" (usable above 2.6 m) and "Next change" (countdown to the next turn) - and a table of the next four turns (type, time, height).

Copy: Guidance sentences like "Safe to walk the causeway for another 1 h 20 min." and "Not safe: water over the causeway until 17:05." Footnote: "Illustrative harmonic model for demonstration, not for navigation."

Interaction: The curve is height(t) = 2.6 + A(t)·cos(2π(t - T0)/12.42 h) + 0.35·cos(2π(t - T1)/6.21 h) with A(t) = 1.7·(1 + 0.18·cos(2π(t - T2)/354 h)) to give a spring and neap swing. Extremes are found by scanning in 2-minute steps. Dragging the handle on the chart (pointer events with setPointerCapture) or the slider changes the shown time; every readout, the gauge level (animated with a 500ms ease), the buoy position, the guidance cards and the status chip update together. The slider and handle are fully keyboard operable (the handle is role="slider" with arrow keys 5 min, Page keys 1 h, Home/End). Now returns to the current time and resumes a live update every 20 seconds; scrubbing pauses live updates. Next high and Next low jump to those moments. Announce changes politely when jumping or when guidance flips between Safe and Not safe.

Responsive: Under 820px stack gauge above chart, the chart keeps a 2.4:1 ratio, the cards go one column, the table scrolls inside its own container; no horizontal page scroll at 390px.

Rules: Single HTML file, Google Fonts link, inline CSS and JS, no libraries or external images. [hidden]{display:none!important}. Visible focus 3px #ffffff outline with dark offset. State is never colour-only (arrows, words, labels). Respect prefers-reduced-motion (static wave and buoy, no height easing). Invented names only.
